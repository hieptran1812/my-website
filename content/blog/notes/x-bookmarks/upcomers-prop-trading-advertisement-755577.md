## Summary

The ad lists lowest funding cost, fast 12-hour payouts, no hidden fees, accounts up to 1 million dollars and up to 99% profit split. It urges readers to join now.

## Background

Prop firms offer funded accounts, usually after a paid evaluation. Terms differ widely, so advertised benefits need checking.

## What I took away

- Read the rules and fees closely.
- Treat ads as marketing.
