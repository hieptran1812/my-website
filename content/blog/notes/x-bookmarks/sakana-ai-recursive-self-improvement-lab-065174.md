## Summary

The author says Sakana consistently has original ideas inspired by biology, and that if any alternative lab can figure out RSI it will be them. The quoted post announces the Sakana AI Recursive Self-Improvement Lab in Tokyo, tasked with redesigning AI development using AI.

## Background

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) means AI systems improving the process that builds AI, so progress could feed on itself. It is a goal and a debated risk. The praise here is opinion.

## What I took away

- RSI labs are becoming an explicit research goal.
- Separate the announcement from the author's enthusiasm.
