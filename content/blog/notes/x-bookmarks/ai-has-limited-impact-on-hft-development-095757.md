## Summary

The author says the impact of AI on HFT development is not great, starting with the lack of resources: no one open sources their order management system, risk engine or pricing modules. The quoted post is the same author's thread of HFT optimizations, such as sending each action over 3 websocket channels at once, which they say to benchmark yourself.

## Background

High-frequency trading relies on very low latency systems. Because firms keep their code private, LLMs have little training data on it and may give generic or wrong advice.

## What I took away

- LLMs are weakest where public training data is scarce.
- Benchmark latency tricks in your own setup.
- Educational context only.
