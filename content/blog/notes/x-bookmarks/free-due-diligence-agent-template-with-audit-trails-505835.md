## Summary

Jerry Liu says they built an AI agent for due diligence with exact audit trails to the source page, usable as a template without paying for PDF parsing. The secret is LiteParse, a free, open source, model-free document parser. The quote is the LlamaIndex demo post on SEC filings.

## Background

An audit trail links each answer to where it came from. In finance this is vital for review. Free local parsing removes per-page costs.

## What I took away

- Use traceable citations for high-stakes document work.
- Free parsers can replace paid ones for text-heavy PDFs.
- Treat the demo as a starting template.
