## Summary

The author claims ChatGPT is cooked and that Gemini 3 has become the biggest edge in crypto research. They say they saved 20+ hours in a week using its new features and promise a thread with tips and free alpha.

## Background

Gemini 3 is a Google AI model. Using an AI for research can speed up reading and summarizing, but results can be wrong. Claims of time saved and edge are the author's, and 'alpha' here means supposed useful information.

## What I took away

- Verify AI-produced research against primary sources.
- Be skeptical of influencer claims of an edge.
- AI is best for speeding up reading, not for decisions.
