## Summary

The author says LongCat is their new favorite AI lab. They say the lab did not have to, but shared its infrastructure and drew it out clearly so others can understand it. They call it a peak research mentality.

## Background

Many labs publish models but not how they train and serve them. Sharing infrastructure details helps the community learn engineering practice. The post is an opinion about openness.

## What I took away

- Infrastructure write-ups are valuable learning material.
- Clear diagrams make technical sharing more useful.
