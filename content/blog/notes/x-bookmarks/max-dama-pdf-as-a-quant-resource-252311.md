## Summary

The author says any list of quant resources must mention the Max Dama PDF, which he calls the original for him. He also recalls another PDF with about 100 brainteasers but cannot find it.

## Background

Max Dama's notes are a well-known free guide to automated trading and quantitative strategies. Brainteasers are common in quant job interviews, which is why such collections get shared.

## What I took away

- Max Dama's PDF is a commonly recommended starting point for quant study.
- Brainteaser practice is part of quant interview prep.
- Save useful PDFs when you find them, since they are hard to find again.
