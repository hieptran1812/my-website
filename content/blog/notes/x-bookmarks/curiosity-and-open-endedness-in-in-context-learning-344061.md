## Summary

@g_lajoie_ notes RL taught sequence models to gather context for extrinsic rewards, then asks about intrinsic rewards from their own predictions. The new paper explores curiosity and open-ended behavior in in-context learning. The quoted post credits a Google team and links arXiv 2606.19476.

## Background

Extrinsic rewards come from the task. Intrinsic rewards come from the agent itself, such as surprise or learning progress. In-context learning means adapting from the prompt without weight updates.

## What I took away

- Curiosity can be built from a model's own predictions.
- In-context learning may support exploration.
