## Summary

The author calls a YouTube tutorial by @yacinelearning on their paper a great one. The quoted post says the 1h20 tutorial covers questions such as what is up with Qwen2.5, whether the GRPO formula is messed up, and why longer wrong chains of thought can be a good thing.

## Background

GRPO is a reinforcement learning method for training reasoning LLMs that compares a group of sampled answers. The paper discussed looks at biases in how it is set up. A video walkthrough can make such papers easier to follow.

## What I took away

- GRPO details such as normalization can affect training behavior.
- Watch the tutorial if you want a guided read of the paper.
- Model choice, such as Qwen2.5, can influence RL results.
