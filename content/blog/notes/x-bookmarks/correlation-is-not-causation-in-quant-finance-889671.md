## Summary

The author says one of the most used phrases in quantitative finance is correlation is not causation. The visible text gives no further detail.

## Background

Two series can move together without one causing the other, for example both reacting to a third factor. In finance, trusting spurious correlations leads to strategies that fail.

## What I took away

- Do not infer causes from correlations alone.
- Test relationships out of sample.
