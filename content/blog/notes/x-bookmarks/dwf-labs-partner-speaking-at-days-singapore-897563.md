## Summary

DWF Labs tells followers not to miss its partner speaking at DAYS Singapore. The quoted post says more than 25 industry leaders will speak at DAYS Singapore on October 6, an event bringing together voices across digital assets, institutional finance and onchain markets.

## Background

This is an event promotion. Industry conferences can be useful for networking, but speaker lineups are marketing and do not by themselves say anything about the quality of any project.

## What I took away

- Treat event promotions as marketing, not information about investments.
- Judge crypto firms by public track record, not stage appearances.
