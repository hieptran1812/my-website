## Summary

@srush_nlp calls the blog post excellent and says it is arguably how people should first learn neural networks. The quoted post asks how much models can remember and promises to show how you could have invented every layer of the transformer from memory.

## Background

Transformers have attention and MLP layers, and one view treats both as forms of memory lookup: attention retrieves from the current context, and MLPs store facts in weights. Building intuition this way can make the architecture feel less arbitrary.

## What I took away

- Thinking of layers as memory helps explain why transformers work.
- Recommended as a first resource for learning neural networks.
