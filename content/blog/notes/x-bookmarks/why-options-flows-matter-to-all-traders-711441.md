## Summary

The author says whether you trade options or not, you should understand their impact on markets. He argues pure directional traders are affected by options positioning and flows, and points to volumes in an attached image.

## Background

Options dealers hedge by buying or selling the underlying asset, which can push prices. Large positioning can therefore shape moves in the stock or index. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Options hedging can move the underlying market.
- Learn the basics even if you only trade the underlying.
