## Summary

The author is building a relative strength rotation strategy for an institutional client. It uses simple indicators, no optimization and quarterly rebalancing across the S&P 500. The author **claims** it still delivers 23% annualized and says profitable technical strategies are still possible.

## Background

Relative strength rotation means holding the assets that have recently performed best versus the rest, and switching as rankings change. Skipping optimization matters because tuned parameters often overfit past data. The figure is the author's claim and the post gives no test period or risk numbers.

## What I took away

- Simple rules with no parameter tuning are less likely to be overfit.
- A headline return means little without the test period, drawdown and costs.
- Quarterly rebalancing keeps turnover and trading costs low.
