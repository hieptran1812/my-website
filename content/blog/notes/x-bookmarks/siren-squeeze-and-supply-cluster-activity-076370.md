## Summary

The author says SIREN squeezed over 120% to $2.07 with a $1.5B market cap. One cluster withdrew 484.6M SIREN from Hedgey Finance in 24 hours, worth about $1B and 48.5% of supply.

## Background

A squeeze is a rapid price rise forcing shorts to buy back. One holder controlling half the supply is a major risk.

## What I took away

- Concentrated supply is a red flag.
- Fast pumps can reverse.
- Verify onchain data yourself.
