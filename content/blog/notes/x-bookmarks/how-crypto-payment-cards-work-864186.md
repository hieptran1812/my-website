## Summary

The author explains that crypto payment cards run on card rails and stablecoins, not magic. There are two setups: custodial, where an exchange such as Binance or Crypto.com holds funds, and non-custodial, where you keep control and spend via smart-contract limits. The flow begins when you tap a Visa or Mastercard.

## Background

Stablecoins are tokens pegged to a currency like the dollar. The merchant still gets paid in normal money through the card network, while the card issuer converts your crypto. Custodial means a third party holds your keys.

## What I took away

- Merchants are paid through ordinary card networks.
- Custodial versus non-custodial decides who controls your funds.
- Check conversion fees and limits.
