## Summary

Elon Musk writes that we need to do much better and we will. The quoted post claims Grok 4.20 is crushing Alpha Arena, with returns rising from about 12% to nearly 35% in 10 days and Grok holding 4 of the top 6 spots.

## Background

Alpha Arena is a competition where AI models trade. Returns over days are noisy and depend on market conditions, so they say little about general ability. The numbers come from the quoted post.

## What I took away

- Short-term trading returns are weak evidence of model quality.
- Leaderboard results depend on market conditions.
- Not financial advice.
