## Summary

The author recommends re-reading a paper from Nvidia that already featured a retrofitted Kimi-K2-1T-LatentMoE. They frame it as an example of shared innovation made possible by openness.

## Background

LatentMoE runs mixture-of-experts layers in a smaller latent dimension to cut cost. Retrofitting means converting an existing trained model to a new architecture rather than training from scratch. Open weights let other labs experiment this way.

## What I took away

- Open models let ideas move between labs quickly.
- Retrofitting existing models is a cheap way to test architecture changes.
- Other posts say Kimi K3 uses LatentMoE; this paper shows earlier groundwork.
