## Summary

@autonity_ invites people who enjoy prediction markets to try the Forecastathon. It moves beyond binary prediction into continuous time-series forecasting and is described as simple, structured and focused.

## Background

Most prediction markets ask yes/no questions. Time-series forecasting asks for a numeric value over time, which is harder and gives richer feedback. This is a promotional post for an event.

## What I took away

- Continuous forecasting is a more demanding test than yes/no prediction.
- Check the event rules and any financial risk before joining.
