## Summary

The vLLM account congratulates the vllm-sr team on Decision 3.0, described as a state-of-the-art multimodal Jev model with open weights on Hugging Face. The quoted announcement says the models range from 0.6B to 27B and rank first on both Jev Decision Index boards in their own evaluation.

## Background

Decision models here are models built to pick among options or route requests, such as choosing which model should handle a query. A semantic router uses such a model to send each request to the right backend.

## What I took away

- The rankings come from the team's own evaluation; look for independent tests.
- Small decision models can make routing cheap in multi-model systems.
