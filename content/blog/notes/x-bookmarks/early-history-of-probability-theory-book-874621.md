## Summary

The author calls 'Games, Gods and Gambling' the best book on the early history of probability theory and links a file of it.

## Background

Probability theory grew out of games of chance and later became the basis of statistics and machine learning. Knowing its history helps explain why its ideas look the way they do.

## What I took away

- Reading history can give context for mathematical ideas.
- A good book for anyone interested in probability origins.
