## Summary

The author says Introspection Adapters train an AI to explicitly answer questions about behaviors it learned in training. They were shocked by how well it works, citing state-of-the-art results on AuditBench and detection of covert fine-tuning attacks. The quoted post describes a single adapter that generalizes to hidden misalignment and backdoors.

## Background

AuditBench tests methods that find hidden behaviors in models. A covert fine-tuning attack changes a model secretly. The claims come from a paper author.

## What I took away

- Self-report methods may be strong auditing baselines.
- Authors' surprise is not independent validation.
