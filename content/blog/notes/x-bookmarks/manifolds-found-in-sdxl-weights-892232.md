## Summary

The author says researchers made an important interpretability breakthrough by finding manifolds in neural net weights for concepts in image generation models like SDXL, such as a pretzel manifold, and steering them to generate varied pretzels. The quoted post is Goodfire's Block-Sparse Featurizers thread.

## Background

A manifold is a curved low-dimensional surface in high-dimensional space. Steering along it changes generated variants. The description is the author's.

## What I took away

- Concepts may form smooth surfaces.
- Steering can use that structure.
- See the Goodfire thread for details.
