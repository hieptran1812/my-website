## Summary

Goodfire announces research on communities of neurons that work together inside LLMs. They say the groups are surprisingly small but powerful, with just a few neurons performing behaviors like arithmetic, ALL CAPS or recalling a date.

## Background

Interpretability studies what units inside a network do. Finding small neuron groups for behaviors suggests parts of a model can be understood and edited separately.

## What I took away

- Behaviors may live in few neurons.
- Small circuits are easier to study.
- Read the research for methods.
