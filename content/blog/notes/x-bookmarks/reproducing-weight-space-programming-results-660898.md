## Summary

@AlexKrentsel says he reproduced the results from the MIT blog post on weight-space programming. He adds that it appears to be a new, step-change capability that only showed up in the latest set of models.

## Background

Weight-space programming means an AI model directly edits another model's weights to change its behavior. An emerging capability is one that appears in newer or larger models but not in older ones. Other bookmarks argue the gains may mostly come from fixing looping behavior.

## What I took away

- Reproduction supports a claim but does not settle how to interpret it.
- Track whether new capabilities only appear in the latest model generation.
- Compare reproductions with critiques before drawing conclusions.
