## Summary

Orchids announces **Bud**, which it calls the first AI Human Emulator. Bud has a full computer with storage, compute and memory, SMS and Telegram, and a browser. It can build and code, manage files, use connected tools and learn custom skills.

## Background

An agent with its own computer can do long tasks by itself, like a remote coworker. This is the company's product pitch, so the claim of being first is its own. The text in the post is cut off.

## What I took away

- Agents are moving to having a full computer environment.
- Treat first-of-its-kind claims as marketing.
- Think about what access you would give such an agent.
