## Summary

@itsharmanjot claims a team open-sourced a Bloomberg Terminal for quant finance that reads papers, blogs, SEC filings and arXiv. It says a Bloomberg seat costs $25,000 a year.

## Background

The post is promotional and the capital letters suggest hype. A research reader tool is not the same as a data terminal.

## What I took away

- Check what the tool really does.
- Treat comparisons to paid terminals skeptically.
