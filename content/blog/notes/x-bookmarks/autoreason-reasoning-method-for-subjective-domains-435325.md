## Summary

The author introduces **Autoreason**, a reasoning method inspired by Karpathy's AutoResearch that extends the idea to subjective domains. The paper was co-written with Hermes Agent by Nous Research using a research-paper-writing skill.

## Background

AutoResearch loops an agent through experiments with clear scores. Subjective areas lack a clear score, so a different approach is needed.

## What I took away

- Subjective tasks lack clear metrics.
- Agents can write papers with skills.
- Read the paper.
