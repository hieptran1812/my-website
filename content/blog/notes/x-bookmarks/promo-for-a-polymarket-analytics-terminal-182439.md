## Summary

The author says they found the perfect Polymarket terminal and calls Bullpen next level for market analysis. Features listed include an analytics tab, a holder view with average entry prices and PnL, and live PnL tracking for traders. The tone is promotional.

## Background

Prediction market terminals add analysis on top of the base site. Posts like this are often promotional, so claims such as nothing comes close are opinion. This is not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Treat enthusiastic tool endorsements as marketing until you try them.
- Check whether the author has a referral or stake.
