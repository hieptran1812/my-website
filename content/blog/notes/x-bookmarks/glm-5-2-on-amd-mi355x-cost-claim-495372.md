## Summary

@jtatarchuk praises the wafer_ai team. The quoted post claims they serve GLM 5.2 on AMD MI355X at 2626 tok/s per node and 213 tok/s single stream, around 80% of B200 throughput at over 2x lower cost than Blackwell.

## Background

Throughput per node and cost per token determine serving economics. AMD GPUs are an alternative to NVIDIA if the software stack is tuned well. These figures are claims.

## What I took away

- Alternative GPUs can be cost competitive.
- Software tuning drives hardware results.
- Check the write-up for benchmark conditions.
