## Summary

The author says MiniMax made OpenClaw accessible to everyone: 10 seconds to deploy, no server setup, and over 10,000 pre-built agents. They say setup that took them 3 hours is now one click. The quoted post is MiniMax's MaxClaw announcement.

## Background

The author is excited about convenience. The figures such as 10 seconds and 10,000 agents come from the author and vendor, not independent tests.

## What I took away

- One-click setups remove friction but need review.
- Try it before relying on claims.
