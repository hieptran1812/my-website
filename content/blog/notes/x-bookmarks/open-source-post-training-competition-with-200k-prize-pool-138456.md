## Summary

The author says the open source post-training competition has a $200K prize pool and gives out $1.5M in compute and API credits, which they call the best part. They say it has 17 tracks from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, Mistral and others, with applications due Nov 30. The quoted post is the official announcement.

## Background

Post-training is the stage where a pretrained model is tuned for tasks and behavior. Credits matter because compute is the main barrier for individuals who want to run such experiments.

## What I took away

- Applications close Nov 30 according to the author.
- The compute credits may be worth more than the prizes for small teams.
- Pick a track that matches a skill you already have.
