## Summary

@TheTuringPost lists the 6 most popular policy optimization algorithms in 2025: PPO, GRPO (Group Relative), GSPO (Group Sequence), DAPO (Decoupled Clip and Dynamic sAmpling), BAPO (BAlanced) and ARPO (Agentic Reinforced). A linked article explains each method.

## Background

Policy optimization is how RL updates a model to make rewarded outputs more likely. PPO is the classic method. The others are newer variants for LLM training that change how rewards, clipping or sampling are handled.

## What I took away

- PPO is the base, and the others are LLM-focused variants.
- Learn the differences in clipping and sampling between them.
- Pick the method based on stability needs and task type.
