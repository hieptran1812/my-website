## Summary

@aiandchai curated 400+ LeetCode problems into 90 patterns and says it helped them get offers at Salesforce, Intuit and Microsoft. The post says LeetCode can overwhelm beginners near interviews and that lists like Blind 75/95 are a start. It links a Google Sheet.

## Background

LeetCode is a site of coding practice problems used for interview prep. Many problems reuse the same patterns, like sliding window or two pointers. Learning patterns lets you solve new problems faster than memorizing answers.

## What I took away

- Study by pattern instead of by individual problem.
- A focused list beats grinding at random.
- Use the sheet as a map and practice actively.
