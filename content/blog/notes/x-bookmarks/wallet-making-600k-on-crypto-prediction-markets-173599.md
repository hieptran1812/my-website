## Summary

The author says they found a wallet that made over **$600k in 3 months** on crypto prediction markets, mostly in markets like whether ETH, BTC or XRP will be above a price on a date. They like its consistent position sizing, which they say suggests discipline. These are the author's claims.

## Background

Prediction markets let people trade on outcomes such as a price being above a threshold. Public wallets let anyone inspect trades, but copying a visible winner ignores luck and hidden risk.

## What I took away

- A good track record over three months can still be luck.
- Consistent sizing is a risk practice worth studying.
- Not advice.
