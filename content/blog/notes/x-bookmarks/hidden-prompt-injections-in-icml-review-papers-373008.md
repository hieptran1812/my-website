## Summary

Paul says ICML editors added hidden prompt injections to every paper sent to reviewers. They tell an AI to use two specific phrases in the review. One reviewer found it and nearly desk rejected, assuming the author did it.

## Background

A prompt injection is hidden text that steers an AI. Here it works as a trap. If a review has the phrases, the reviewer likely pasted the paper into an AI.

## What I took away

- Hidden instructions can serve as detection.
- Check before blaming authors.
