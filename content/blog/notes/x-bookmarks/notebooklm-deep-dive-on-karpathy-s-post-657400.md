## Summary

The author found Karpathy's post interesting and made a NotebookLM notebook from it. He ran a Deep Research task in the app to gather related sources, then generated a slide deck to explore further, calling it an instant knowledge base. The quoted post is Karpathy's note on the space of intelligences.

## Background

NotebookLM is a Google tool that answers questions and creates summaries based on sources you give it. Deep Research collects extra sources automatically. Together they turn a single post into a small study set.

## What I took away

- You can turn one idea into a sourced knowledge base quickly.
- Tools like NotebookLM ground answers in the sources you add.
- Still read the sources it gathers.
