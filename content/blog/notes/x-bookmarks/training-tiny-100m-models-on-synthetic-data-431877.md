## Summary

The author recommends a 45 minute video about creating **synthetic datasets** and training tiny (100M parameter) local language models that specialize in narrow tasks. Code, datasets, models and harnesses are shared in the comments.

## Background

Synthetic data is text generated by a larger model to train a smaller one. Small specialized models can be cheap to run and good enough for a single job.

## What I took away

- Narrow tasks may not need a big model.
- Synthetic data plus a small model is a practical recipe.
- Use the shared code to reproduce the setup.
