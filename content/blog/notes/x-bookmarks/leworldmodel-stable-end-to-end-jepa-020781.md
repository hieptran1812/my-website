## Summary

The author says its time, quoting an announcement that JEPA is now easy to train end-to-end with no tricks. LeWorldModel learns world models from pixels with 15M parameters, one GPU and planning in under a second.

## Background

JEPA predicts in a learned latent space instead of pixels. World models let agents plan by simulating outcomes. Past JEPA training needed heuristics to avoid collapse.

## What I took away

- Small models can learn world models.
- Stability tricks were a barrier.
- Read the paper for the method.
