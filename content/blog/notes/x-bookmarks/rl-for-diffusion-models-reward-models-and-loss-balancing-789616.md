## Summary

@anvilapex shares part 2 of a write-up on RL for diffusion models, covering reward models and loss balancing. He notes how intuitive RL algorithms are and how hard it is to design a proper reward.

## Background

Diffusion models generate images by gradually removing noise. RL can fine-tune them toward outputs a reward model scores highly, such as aesthetics or prompt match. Loss balancing means weighting multiple objectives so none dominates.

## What I took away

- Reward design is usually the hardest part of RL.
- Balancing multiple losses is key when fine-tuning generative models.
- Read part 2 for concrete reward model choices.
