## Summary

The author asks how to remove safety from open-source models and shows a pip tool called gabliteration run on a Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 model from Hugging Face.

## Background

Open weights can be edited, so refusal behavior can be removed, for example by changing internal directions. This shows that safety training in released weights is not a lock. It raises misuse concerns.

## What I took away

- Safety in open weights can be removed by third parties.
- Do not rely on model-level refusals alone for sensitive uses.
