## Summary

Robinhood says users can connect an AI agent to a Robinhood Agentic Account to explore trade ideas, build and rebalance portfolios, program custom tools and place trades. It is rolling out now.

## Background

This lets an AI agent act on a brokerage account, not only give advice. Automation brings risks like errors, bad prompts and loss of control. This is educational context, not advice.

## What I took away

- Agents with trading access need strict limits and monitoring.
- Understand what actions the agent can take before connecting it.
- Test with small amounts and review every trade.
