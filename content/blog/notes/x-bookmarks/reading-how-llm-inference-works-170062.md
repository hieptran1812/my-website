## Summary

The author, in a study log style, says they read the blog post 'How LLM inference works' and links it.

## Background

LLM inference is the process of generating text from a trained model, including processing the prompt (prefill) and generating tokens one by one (decode). Understanding it helps with cost and latency work.

## What I took away

- Learn the prefill and decode phases first.
- A good basics article before serving topics.
