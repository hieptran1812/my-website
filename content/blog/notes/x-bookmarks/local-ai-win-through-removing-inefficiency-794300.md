## Summary

The author says LLMs are full of noisy data and trained with brute-force computation on every layer, like throwing stuff at a wall. Removing these inefficiencies, he claims, shows a clear path for local AI to win.

## Background

The argument is that data quality and smarter compute allocation could shrink the models needed. It is an opinion without evidence in the post.

## What I took away

- Data quality may beat scale.
- Opinion pieces need evidence.
- Efficiency work benefits local AI.
