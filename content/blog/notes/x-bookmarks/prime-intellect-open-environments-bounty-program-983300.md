## Summary

@afurgs says Prime Intellect's Environments Bounty Program stays open to applicants worldwide. Environments are released publicly on the Environments Hub to give good contributor attribution, with eval-based compensation of $1,000 to $5,000 or more.

## Background

An RL environment is a task setup, with rules and a reward, in which a model is trained or evaluated. Companies pay for good environments because they limit what models can learn. A bounty pays contributors who build them.

## What I took away

- Building RL environments can be a paid way to contribute to open AI work.
- Compensation is tied to evaluation results.
