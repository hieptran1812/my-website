## Summary

The author says he is confused by Anthropic's messaging. In his view it is obvious LLMs represent emotions and act as if they have them, since this is a pattern in training data and models roleplay as people. The quote is Anthropic's research announcement on emotion concepts in Claude.

## Background

Interpretability looks for internal representations in a model that explain behavior. The critic argues the finding is expected, and the debate is about whether such work shows anything beyond imitation of human text.

## What I took away

- Representing emotions is not proof of feeling them.
- Ask what a finding adds beyond the obvious.
- Framing and communication shape how research is received.
