## Summary

The author says you can now accurately research anything on X. The quoted post says X revamped its API, and they made a skill that searches X, modeled on Claude Code's research agent, for questions like best clawdbot skills in the last 24 hours or what people say about a ticker today.

## Background

A skill is a reusable bundle of instructions and tools an agent can load. With a usable search API, an agent can gather recent posts and summarize sentiment.

## What I took away

- Agent skills can wrap APIs into reusable research tools.
- Social posts are noisy sources, so verify.
- API access changes decide what tools are possible.
