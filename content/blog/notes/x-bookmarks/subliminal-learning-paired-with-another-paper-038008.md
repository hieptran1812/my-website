## Summary

The author highlights a pairing of papers with seemingly unrelated ideas. The first is Subliminal Learning, in which a teacher model with a trait such as liking owls or being misaligned generates data that passes the trait to a student. The text is truncated.

## Background

Subliminal learning means traits transfer through data that looks unrelated to the trait. It matters for distillation, since a student can inherit hidden behavior.

## What I took away

- Distilled data can carry hidden traits.
- Filtering by content may not be enough.
- Read the second paper for the pairing.
