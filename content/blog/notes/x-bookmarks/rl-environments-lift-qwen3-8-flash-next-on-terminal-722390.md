## Summary

The author stresses the importance of data and environments in model gains. The quoted post says a research team post-trained Qwen3.8-Flash-Next with GRPO in the mini-swe-agent harness, and a fraction of Handshake's coding RL environments raised it from 11.6% to 15.7% on Terminal-Bench 4, beating GPT-6 Luna Max.

## Background

GRPO is an RL algorithm used to train LLMs from rewards. Environments are tasks with automatic checks. The result suggests that high-quality tasks matter as much as the algorithm. The numbers are from the quoted post.

## What I took away

- Quality environments can move benchmark scores.
- Small percentages still matter on hard benchmarks.
- The result comes from the vendor selling the data.
