## Summary

The author says their model is now on TradingView as BTC/Gold Power Law Bands and Quantile Oscillator. The quoted post claims Bitcoin's valuation against gold is at its lowest since 2013, the longest consolidation in its history, near bear-market levels.

## Background

A power-law band fits a long-term growth curve to price and shows where price sits relative to it. Pricing Bitcoin in gold instead of dollars removes some currency effects. These are descriptive models, not predictions.

## What I took away

- Check which asset is the unit of account.
- Models describe history and do not guarantee future moves.
- Not advice.
