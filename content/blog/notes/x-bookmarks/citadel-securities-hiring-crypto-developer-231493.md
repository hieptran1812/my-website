## Summary

The author says Citadel Securities, called the world's largest trading firm, is expanding its crypto team and hiring a crypto quantitative developer for high-performance trading systems, according to a LinkedIn post.

## Background

Quant developers build fast trading infrastructure. A hire signals interest in a market. One job post is a small signal.

## What I took away

- Low-latency systems skills are in demand.
- One job ad is not a trend.
