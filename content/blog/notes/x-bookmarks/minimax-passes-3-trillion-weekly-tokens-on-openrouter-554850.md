## Summary

MiniMax quotes a post saying MiniMax is the first model to break 3 trillion tokens in a week on the OpenRouter rankings, and that Cline is now number 3. MiniMax adds its own celebration.

## Background

OpenRouter is a gateway to many models, and its rankings show token usage by model and app. High usage shows adoption, not quality by itself.

## What I took away

- Token usage is a popularity signal, not a quality benchmark.
- Rankings reflect what routes through one platform.
