## Summary

The author says they have open-sourced multiple generations of on-device audio models through **Audio8** over two months. The portfolio includes ASR models of 0.1B, 0.3B, 0.6B and 3B, and TTS models of 0.1B, 0.3B and 0.6B. They are designed for practical local inference.

## Background

ASR (automatic speech recognition) turns speech into text, and TTS (text to speech) does the reverse. On-device models run locally, which helps privacy, latency and cost. Multiple sizes let you trade quality for speed.

## What I took away

- Small open audio models make local voice apps practical.
- Pick model size based on device limits and accuracy needs.
