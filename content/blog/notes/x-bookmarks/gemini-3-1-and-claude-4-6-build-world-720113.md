## Summary

The author, writing in Vietnamese, says one developer combined Gemini 3.1 and Claude 4.6 to build a real-time world-tracking website, and another developer built a similar World Monitor. They say the speed of AI progress is astonishing.

## Background

These projects show models used together to produce full dashboards from data feeds. They are examples of fast prototyping with AI, not benchmarked results.

## What I took away

- Combining several models can yield capable prototypes.
- Treat the enthusiasm as the author's view.
