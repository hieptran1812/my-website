## Summary

a16z quotes Fei-Fei Li saying the world is not made of words. She says language models have given machines command of concepts and reasoning, but the physical world, virtual or real, runs on a different substrate.

## Background

This is the case for spatial intelligence or world models: systems that learn 3D structure, physics and motion, rather than only text patterns.

## What I took away

- Text alone may not capture physical reality.
- This motivates work on world models.
