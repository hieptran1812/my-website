## Summary

The author calls this the most interesting thing he has seen in a while. He says Karpathy's recipe cuts GPT-2 1.5B training cost from 43000 to 73 dollars, and lists 7 years of improvements over vanilla GPT in 10 points. He starts with the uncommon ones, such as value embeddings, which he had not seen in any LLM.

## Background

GPT-2 1.5B is a well-known language model from 2019. Training recipes improve through better architectures, optimizers, data and hardware. A single recipe that gathers these shows how much progress adds up.

## What I took away

- Collect small training improvements; together they can cut cost by orders of magnitude.
- Read the full thread for the 10 points, starting with value embeddings.
