## Summary

The author says Gemini 3.0 Pro understands context so deeply it feels like mind reading, and claims 95% of users never go past surface level. They offer five prompts that made it click for them.

## Background

Prompt lists like this are common engagement posts. The 95% figure is the author's claim with no source in the post. Good prompts usually give clear context, goals and format requirements.

## What I took away

- Treat the 95% figure as an unsupported claim.
- Give a model detailed context and a clear goal.
- Test any shared prompts on your own tasks.
