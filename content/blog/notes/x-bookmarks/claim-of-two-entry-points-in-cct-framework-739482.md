## Summary

The author says after 5 years of trading he is convinced there are only two accurate places to enter the market. He says viewing markets through the CCT framework reveals both. The post gives no further detail.

## Background

This is a personal opinion about a trading framework, with no evidence given in the text. Claims of only two correct entries are strong and should be tested, not trusted. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of absolute claims about market timing.
- Test any framework on data before using it.
