## Summary

LMSYS says Cosmos 3 is now supported in SGLang-Diffusion and links a cookbook. The quoted post introduces Cosmos 3 as the world's first fully open omnimodel with native vision reasoning, world and action generation, in Super (32B) and Nano (8B) variants.

## Background

An omnimodel handles many input and output types. SGLang-Diffusion is the serving engine's support for diffusion-style generation.

## What I took away

- Day-one serving support eases adoption.
- Cosmos 3 comes in 32B and 8B sizes.
