## Summary

The quoted post says humans are about 1,000,000x more sample efficient than LLMs and claims a big part of the reason is that LLMs are massively underparameterized. It compares the brain's 100T synapses learning from about 200M tokens of language. @NickPlaysCrypto jokes about quadrillion-parameter models matching human neural circuitry.

## Background

Sample efficiency is how much a learner improves per example seen. Parameters in a network are loosely compared to synapses in the brain. Other bookmarks, including a critique by @giffmana, say the quadrillion-parameter conclusion relies on wild extrapolation.

## What I took away

- Brain-to-model comparisons are suggestive, not proof.
- Treat extrapolated scaling claims with caution.
- Sample efficiency is a key open problem in AI research.
