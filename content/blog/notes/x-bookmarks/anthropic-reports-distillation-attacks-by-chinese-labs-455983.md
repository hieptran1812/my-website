## Summary

Anthropic's quoted post says it identified industrial-scale distillation attacks by DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and MiniMax, with over 24,000 fraudulent accounts and over 16 million exchanges. cryptopunk7213 retells this in strong language, saying the labs wanted Claude's coding and reasoning abilities.

## Background

Distillation means training a model on the outputs of a stronger model. It is a normal technique when allowed, but using fake accounts to bypass terms of service is disputed. The accusations are Anthropic's claims.

## What I took away

- Distillation is a main way capabilities spread between labs.
- The figures come from Anthropic's own post.
- API access terms and abuse detection matter for model providers.
