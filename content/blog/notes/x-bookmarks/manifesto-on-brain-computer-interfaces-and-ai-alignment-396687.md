## Summary

The author says hi, welcome to the future. The quoted post says the human brain is approaching its ChatGPT moment and announces Phase Lock, a manifesto written over six months on how brain computer interfaces can help align AI and preserve human agency.

## Background

A brain computer interface (BCI) connects the brain to a computer, reading or writing neural signals. Some argue tight human-AI coupling could keep humans in control of advanced AI.

## What I took away

- BCI-based alignment is a speculative idea; read the argument critically.
