## Summary

The post says a curated list of LLM interview questions, shared by Hao Hoang, is what many candidates wish they had seen earlier. The author offers to send the document to people who follow and comment LLM.

## Background

Interview question lists help candidates see which topics matter, such as attention, fine-tuning and evaluation. The follow and comment offer is an engagement tactic. The document itself is not shown in the post.

## What I took away

- Use question lists to find gaps in your own knowledge, not to memorize answers.
- Be aware that follow and comment offers are engagement tactics.
- Look for the original source of shared lists.
