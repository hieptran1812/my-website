## Summary

The author says video diffusion and world models are becoming synonymous. They call Neural Computers video diffusion world models for terminal and GUI environments. They connect this to talk about automating Manim videos.

## Background

A world model predicts what the screen or world looks like next. A model that draws terminal output frame by frame acts like a simulated computer. Manim is a tool for math animations.

## What I took away

- Screens can be modeled like video.
- The trend is interpretation, not proof.
- Read the paper.
