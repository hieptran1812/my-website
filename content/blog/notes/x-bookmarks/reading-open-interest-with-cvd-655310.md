## Summary

The author says CVD shows aggressor behavior while open interest shows whether positions are opening or closing. He lists four combinations: OI up with CVD up means new longs, OI up with CVD down means new shorts, OI down with CVD up means shorts covering, and OI down with CVD down means longs covering. It answers a quoted question about falling OI with large buy delta.

## Background

CVD is the running total of market buys minus market sells. OI is the number of open contracts. If buying is aggressive but OI falls, the buying is closing shorts rather than opening new longs. This is a general heuristic, not a trading recommendation.

## What I took away

- Rising or falling OI tells you if positions are being opened or closed.
- Buy pressure with falling OI often means short covering.
- Heuristics are not signals to trade on.
