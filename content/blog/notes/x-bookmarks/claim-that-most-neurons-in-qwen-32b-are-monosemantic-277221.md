## Summary

@deedydas claims this is one of the most important results in AI: neural networks are not tangled knots, polysemanticity is wrong, and neurons can be studied directly without first unfolding them with a second model, with 90% of neurons in Qwen-32B having one job. He quotes Nick Cammarata, who calls interpretability perhaps the most important scientific problem and says he has good news.

## Background

Polysemanticity is the idea that each neuron responds to many unrelated concepts, which is why researchers use sparse autoencoders, a second model, to unfold activations into cleaner features. If neurons are mostly monosemantic (one concept each), interpretability becomes much simpler.

## What I took away

- This is a strong claim; other bookmarks here criticize it as overhyped.
- Check how one job is defined and measured before accepting the 90% figure.
- Read the Potts and Kundaje notes for counterpoints.
