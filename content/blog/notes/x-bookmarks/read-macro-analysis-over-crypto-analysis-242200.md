## Summary

The post, written in Vietnamese, says that if you have free time, do not read crypto analysis; read analysis from Ray Dalio, BlackRock, Naval and similar sources instead.

## Background

The author suggests broader macro and investing thinking over narrow crypto commentary. It is an opinion about what to read, and not investment advice.

## What I took away

- Broader sources can give a wider view than niche commentary.
- This is the author's opinion; judge sources on their track record.
- Not advice to buy or sell anything.
