## Summary

Anthropic announces a new engineering blog post, Demystifying evals for AI agents. It says the capabilities that make agents useful also make them harder to evaluate. The post shares evaluation strategies that have worked across real-world deployments.

## Background

An eval is a test that measures how well a model or agent performs. Agents take many steps and use tools, so a single right or wrong answer is not enough to judge them. Good evals are needed to know whether changes actually help.

## What I took away

- Agents need evals that look at multi-step behavior, not just final answers.
- Read the blog post for concrete strategies from real deployments.
- Build evals before making large changes to an agent.
