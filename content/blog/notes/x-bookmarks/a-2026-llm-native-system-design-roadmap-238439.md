## Summary

The author sketches a 2026 LLM-Native System Design Roadmap. Under Retrieval and Knowledge Systems they list RAG vs fine-tuning vs MoM routing, vector DB sharding and hybrid search, knowledge graph plus RAG fusion, and semantic and reasoning cache layers. They then start an Agentic Application Layer section with async items. The text is cut off.

## Background

RAG means retrieval-augmented generation, where a model looks up documents before answering. Hybrid search mixes keyword and vector search. Caching reuses earlier answers to cut cost. The list is one person's view of what to learn.

## What I took away

- Retrieval and caching are core building blocks.
- Compare RAG with fine-tuning for each use case.
- Treat the roadmap as one opinion.
