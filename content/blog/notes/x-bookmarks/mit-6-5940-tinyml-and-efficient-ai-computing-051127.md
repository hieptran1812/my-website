## Summary

The author is co-instructing MIT 6.5940, TinyML and Efficient AI Computing, with @songhan_mit this fall. The course site is efficientml.ai.

## Background

TinyML is about running machine learning on small devices with little memory and power. Efficient AI computing covers pruning, quantization, distillation and efficient inference, which apply to large models too.

## What I took away

- The efficientml.ai materials are a good free resource on model efficiency.
- Efficiency methods for small devices often transfer to LLM serving.
