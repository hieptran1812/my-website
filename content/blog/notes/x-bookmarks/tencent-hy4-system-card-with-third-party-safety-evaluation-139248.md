## Summary

The author is excited that Tencent invested in safety evaluations before releasing Hy4 and worked with a third-party evaluator on loss-of-control risks. One quoted post says it is the first system card from a Chinese frontier lab with an independent pre-release evaluation. The other quote is Tencent announcing open weights and a system card covering safety, security and alignment tests.

## Background

A system card documents a model's capabilities and risks. Third-party evaluation adds independent checks. Loss of control refers to AI systems acting against human oversight.

## What I took away

- Independent evaluation is becoming part of releases.
- System cards are worth reading before using a model.
- Open weights come with published safety tests here.
