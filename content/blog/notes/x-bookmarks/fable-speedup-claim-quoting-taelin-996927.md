## Summary

@scaling01 quotes a claim that in two hours Fable landed a 1770% speedup in one case, 100%+ in four others, and 22% average, beating the author, Opus 4.8 and a swarm of GPT 5.5 agents. The quoted post is Taelin's singularity post.

## Background

The numbers are the author's reported experience on his own code, not a controlled benchmark.

## What I took away

- Treat anecdotes as anecdotes.
- Averages hide large variation.
