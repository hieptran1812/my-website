## Summary

Pika says you can ask your Pika AI Self to join a Google Meet. For other agents, the Skill can be downloaded from the Pika-Skills GitHub repository.

## Background

A skill is a packaged instruction set or tool an agent loads to do a task. This one gives agents a presence in video meetings.

## What I took away

- Skills make capabilities portable across agents.
- Check what permissions a meeting agent needs.
- See the repo for setup.
