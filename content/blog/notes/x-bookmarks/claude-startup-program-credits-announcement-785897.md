## Summary

The author says the Claude Startup Program is open, claiming up to about $25K in API credits for early-stage startups, no VC needed, and selection based on product and real Claude usage. The quoted post makes a similar list for OpenAI startup credits, claiming $100K+.

## Background

API credits let startups use paid model APIs for free up to a limit. Providers use such programs to attract developers. The figures here are the author's claims and should be checked on official pages.

## What I took away

- Verify credit amounts and terms on the official program page.
- Credit programs can cut early prototype costs.
- Do not rely on social media summaries for eligibility.
