## Summary

The post shares an article titled 'Bear Markets Don't Happen When Liquidity's Like This'. Only the headline is given, with no commentary.

## Background

Liquidity here usually means how much money is flowing through financial markets, such as central bank and credit conditions. The headline makes a strong claim that is the article's own. Such market calls often fail.

## What I took away

- Treat strong market-call headlines with skepticism.
- Read the article for its actual reasoning and data.
