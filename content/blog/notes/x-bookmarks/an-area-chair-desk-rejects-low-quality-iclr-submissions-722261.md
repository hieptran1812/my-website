## Summary

@hjy836 says that as an ICLR 2027 Area Chair they recommended desk rejection for 5 out of 14 submissions. They did not use LLM detection tools, which they say are unnecessary and should not be used for desk rejection decisions, and say the submissions shared shockingly basic problems (the text is cut off).

## Background

Desk rejection means a paper is rejected before full review, usually for policy violations or clear quality problems. Area chairs manage reviews for a group of papers. LLM detectors are unreliable, so judging papers on their content is fairer.

## What I took away

- Basic errors can get a paper rejected before anyone reviews the ideas.
- Do not rely on AI-text detectors for important decisions.
- Check submissions for formatting, citations and correctness before sending.
