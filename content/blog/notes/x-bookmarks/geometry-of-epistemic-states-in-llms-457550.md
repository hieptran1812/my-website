## Summary

@jlsteenwyk reports new results that an LLM's guessing, fabricating and step-by-step deriving states live in distinct activation regions, and the geometry is shared across models. The text is cut off.

## Background

Activation space is the internal vector space of a model. If such states are separable, they could be detected to flag unreliable answers.

## What I took away

- Internal states may reveal fabrication.
- Useful for hallucination detection.
