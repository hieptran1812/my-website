## Summary

The author says you can run Kimi k2.5 locally with two Mac Studio machines at 24 tok/sec and predicts web subscriptions will not be needed soon.

## Background

Large models can be split across Macs with unified memory. 24 tokens per second is usable. The prediction is opinion.

## What I took away

- Local large models are becoming feasible.
- Hardware cost is high.
- Speed claims vary by setup.
