## Summary

The author announces a new Penn course, **CIS 6280 World Models**, with a course website. They say LLMs keep amazing them, but there is more to learn than what humans wrote down, by observing and interacting with the world.

## Background

World models learn how the world changes in response to actions, from video and interaction rather than only text. They matter for robotics, games and simulation.

## What I took away

- Check the course site for a structured reading list on world models.
- Learning from interaction is seen as a next step beyond text-only models.
