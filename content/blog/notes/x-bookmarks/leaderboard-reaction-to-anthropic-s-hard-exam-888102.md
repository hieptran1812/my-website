## Summary

@0xShual says that three hours after Anthropic released a notoriously difficult exam, only six people were on the leaderboard, including much of the @paradigm team. He says they had yet to beat Opus 4.5 and that only @gakonst was under the recruiting threshold. The quoted post is Anthropic's blog about redesigning the exam.

## Background

The exam is a performance engineering take-home and a public leaderboard ranks scores. Opus 4.5 is the model that beat the original test. The recruiting threshold is the score that would qualify for a hiring conversation.

## What I took away

- Open challenges can double as recruiting.
- The post suggests the exam is hard even for experts.
- See the Anthropic blog for official details.
