## Summary

@iScienceLuvr recommends a compact video by @jbhuang0604 on progress in self-supervised learning over decades. It covers methods such as MoCo, SimCLR, DINO, BYOL, MAE and ends with LeJEPA in 2025.

## Background

Self-supervised learning trains models on unlabeled data by making up tasks, such as matching two views of an image. It underlies modern pretraining. A timeline video is a quick way to see how the ideas connect.

## What I took away

- Watch for a quick map of the field.
- Note how methods like contrastive learning led to later approaches.
