## Summary

@yacinelearning notes the blog's author now works at OpenAI. The quoted post recommends the blog by Rajan Agarwal to undergrads, grad students and independent researchers, calling it a rare raw look at what happens in most research projects: confusing failures that drag on for weeks.

## Background

Published papers show the final result, not the dead ends. Accounts of the actual process help new researchers set expectations and learn debugging habits.

## What I took away

- Expect long stretches of failure in research; it is normal.
- Writing publicly about your process can open career doors.
- Read the blog for practical lessons.
