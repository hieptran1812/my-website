## Summary

The author shares a failed experiment that still looks cool. Evolution strategy (red) and Hebbian learning (blue) work together, and on Hebbian's turn ES tunes the Hebbian hyperparameters for the most recent weights, but Hebbian crashed. The quoted earlier post says the combination trained a network to 80% accuracy with no backprop.

## Background

Evolution strategies improve a model by trying random weight changes and keeping what works. Hebbian learning strengthens connections between units that fire together. Both avoid backpropagation, which makes them interesting alternatives.

## What I took away

- Share failed experiments; they teach as much as successes.
- Backprop-free methods are an open research area.
