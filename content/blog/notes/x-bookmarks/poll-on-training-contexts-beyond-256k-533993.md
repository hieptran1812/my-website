## Summary

The author asks how valuable training contexts over 256k would be on Tinker and how long users would want. Poll results from 394 votes: 31.5% very valuable or a blocker, 34.3% would be nice, and 34.3% do not care.

## Background

Context length is how many tokens a model can process at once. Training with long contexts costs a lot of memory. Tinker is a fine-tuning service. The poll gauges demand before building.

## What I took away

- Only about a third see long context as a blocker.
- Product teams poll users before heavy investments.
- Long context training is costly.
