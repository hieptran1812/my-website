## Summary

The author says working with the Triton core maintainers has been inspiring, praising the high bar Lei and the team set for architecture, correctness, performance and maintainability, and the value of a lean, high-talent team. The quoted posts celebrate another milestone and recall how the team started building **TokenSpeed**, a new LLM inference engine, from first principles in mid-March despite people saying it was too late in 2026.

## Background

Triton is a language and compiler for writing GPU kernels in Python-like code. An inference engine is the software that runs a trained model efficiently for many users, handling batching, scheduling and memory.

## What I took away

- Small teams with high standards can still build new infrastructure in a crowded space.
- Correctness and maintainability are design goals, not afterthoughts.
- It is rarely too late to try a different architecture if you have a clear reason.
