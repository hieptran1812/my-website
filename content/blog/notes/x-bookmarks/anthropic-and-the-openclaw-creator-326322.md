## Summary

Benjamin wonders if Anthropic regrets not courting Peter Steinberger harder to join Team Claude. He adds that it may have been deliberate, since per an interview on Lex, OpenClaw is deeply unprofitable. Raschka disputes that last point in another post here.

## Background

OpenClaw is an open-source agent tool. Labs often hire or partner with popular open-source builders. Whether such a tool makes money is debated.

## What I took away

- Hiring decisions can be strategic, not only about talent.
- Claims about profitability need data.
