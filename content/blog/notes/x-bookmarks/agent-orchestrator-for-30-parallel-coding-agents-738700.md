## Summary

The team says it open-sourced the system: 40K lines of TypeScript, 3,288 tests and 17 plugins, built in 8 days by the agents it orchestrates. They used Agent Orchestrator to build itself. More numbers follow in the cut-off text.

## Background

Orchestrators assign tasks to many agents and track them. Self-hosting its own development is a demonstration, though numbers are the authors' claims.

## What I took away

- Orchestration is the bottleneck for many agents.
- Self-built tools show capability but verify yourself.
