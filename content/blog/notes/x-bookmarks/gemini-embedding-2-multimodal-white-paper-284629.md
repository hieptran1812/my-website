## Summary

The post shares the Google DeepMind white paper for **Gemini Embedding 2**, called their first native multimodal embedding model. It says GE 2 gives a unified representation for text, audio, video or image input.

## Background

An embedding model turns content into vectors so similar items are close together, which powers search and retrieval. A unified multimodal embedding lets you search images with text or video with audio in one space.

## What I took away

- One embedding space across modalities simplifies retrieval systems.
- Read the white paper for training and evaluation details.
- Consider multimodal embeddings for search over mixed media.
