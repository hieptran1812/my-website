## Summary

@finbarrtimbers says great write up and quotes the post Why Training MoEs Is So Hard, in which the author wanted a small research-focused training repo for experiments with pluggable attention architectures.

## Background

MoE means mixture of experts, where a router sends each token to a subset of expert sub-networks. The quoted post explains why training them is difficult. The reply is a brief endorsement.

## What I took away

- Endorsement from another practitioner is a hint the write-up is worth reading.
- See the original post for the substance.
