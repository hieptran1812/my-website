## Summary

Tencent Hunyuan presents a survey titled Diving into Reliable Self-Evolving Agents. It asks how we know agents actually improved when they modify themselves. The survey maps how agents self-evolve and what evidence is needed to trust each update.

## Background

Self-evolving agents change their own prompts, tools, memory or weights based on experience. Without careful evaluation, such changes can look like improvements while actually overfitting or breaking other behaviors.

## What I took away

- Every self-modification needs an evaluation that shows real improvement.
- Use held-out tests to check that agent updates generalize.
- Surveys are a good starting map before building a self-improving agent.
