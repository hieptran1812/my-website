## Summary

The author says his team worked a long time on GLM-4.7 and is releasing it before Christmas as a gift. The quoted announcement says GLM-4.7 surpasses GLM-4.6 with substantial gains in coding, complex reasoning and tool usage, setting new open-source SOTA, and also improves chat, creative writing and role-play. It is the default model for the Coding Plan.

## Background

SOTA means state of the art. The open-source SOTA claim comes from the vendor and should be checked against independent benchmarks. Tool usage means the model's ability to call external functions correctly.

## What I took away

- GLM-4.7 is a new open model focused on coding and tool use.
- Vendor benchmark claims need independent verification.
- Try it on your own tasks before switching.
