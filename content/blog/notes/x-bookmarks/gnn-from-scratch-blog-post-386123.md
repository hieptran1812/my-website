## Summary

The author finished a blog called GNN from Scratch with decent maths, visualisation and code, and says they would have loved such a blog when starting. The quoted post says that to be great in an ML domain you should replicate old papers and match the results table. They tried the subgraph matching problem from the original Graph Neural Network Model paper by Scarselli et al., 2009.

## Background

A graph neural network learns from data shaped as nodes and edges. Replicating an old paper and matching its results forces you to understand every detail. The 2009 paper is the early form of GNNs.

## What I took away

- Replicate a paper and match its table to learn deeply.
- Start from the original GNN paper for fundamentals.
- Write up what you learn.
