## Summary

The author says their second animated template was accepted to the Framer marketplace at $79 and they are giving it away free for a limited time, about 120 hours of work. To get it you repost and comment Synk.

## Background

Giveaways that need a repost and comment are a way to boost reach. Designers also use them to get early users.

## What I took away

- Engagement giveaways are a marketing tactic.
- Templates are a way designers earn from their work.
