## Summary

Teknium says Anthropic blocked a friend from using a Claude subscription in OpenClaw, so he switched to MiniMax. He calls it a big boost for open models. The quoted posts call it a generational fumble and say they only sent love letters from legal.

## Background

Some tools let people use a consumer Claude subscription as an API substitute, which providers may restrict in their terms. Blocking such use can push developers to cheaper or open alternatives.

## What I took away

- Platform rules can send users to competitors.
- Avoid depending on a single provider.
