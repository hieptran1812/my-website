## Summary

The author says industry classification is one of the most gated datasets in finance, so they built their own and made it available to everyone. The quoted post introduces an Industry and Sector classifier that lets an agent pull every ticker in an industry without a third-party mapping, covering 10,000+ tickers, 10+ sectors and 70+ industries.

## Background

Industry classification systems group companies by what they do, which is used for comparisons, screening and risk analysis. Standard schemes are often licensed and expensive. A self-built version may differ from standard ones, so users should check how it is defined.

## What I took away

- Data access, not models, is often the bottleneck for finance agents.
- Verify how a custom classifier maps companies before relying on it.
- Use such data for research and learning, not as a trading signal by itself.
