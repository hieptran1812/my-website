## Summary

The author says her Complete Guide to Spherical Equivariant Graph Transformers is now on arXiv (2512.13927). She first published it on her Alchemy Bio blog over a year ago and says she is still surprised by how often people refer to it.

## Background

Equivariant graph transformers are models for 3D data such as molecules that handle rotations consistently. Spherical harmonics are the math tool often used to represent directions. Putting a guide on arXiv makes it easy to cite and find.

## What I took away

- Long explainers can find a second life as arXiv papers.
- Read the guide to learn how equivariance is built into models.
- Public writing can build lasting influence.
