## Summary

The Kobeissi Letter lists what Venezuela has. It gives 200 trillion cubic feet of natural gas, 34th largest, 300 billion barrels of crude oil, the largest in the world, 4 billion tons of iron ore worth nearly $600 billion, and 8,000+ tons of gold resources. The list is cut off.

## Background

Reserves are estimates of what can be extracted, and they depend on prices and technology. Having reserves does not mean production is high. Figures here are as stated in the post.

## What I took away

- Reserves and actual production are different things.
- Check figures against primary data sources.
- Context matters for geopolitical posts.
