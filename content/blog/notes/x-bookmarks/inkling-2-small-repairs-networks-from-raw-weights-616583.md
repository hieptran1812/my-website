## Summary

Tinker says its community has had three weeks with Inkling 2 Small and highlights one project that trained it to fix network weights without long reasoning. The quoted post says the model repairs broken networks from raw weights in about 40 tokens with no reasoning, while the untrained model used far more tokens to fix one network.

## Background

Weight-space programming means giving a model a network's parameters as input and asking it to change them. Normally this would need long step-by-step reasoning. A model that does it in a few tokens suggests post-training can teach compact skills.

## What I took away

- Models can be trained to operate directly on weights.
- Short outputs can replace long reasoning for narrow tasks.
- Community writeups show what worked and what did not.
