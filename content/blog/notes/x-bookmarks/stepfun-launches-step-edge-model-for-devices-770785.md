## Summary

The quoted post says StepFun launched Step Edge, a text and vision model for phones, vehicles and other edge scenarios. It claims 0.1s local tool calls, local text, vision and audio processing, cloud-device collaboration, a Step Inference NPU engine, and a top rank across 29 key metrics. The author just calls it interesting.

## Background

Edge models run on the device instead of in the cloud, which reduces latency and keeps data local. An NPU is a neural processing unit, a chip specialized for AI on phones and cars. Cloud-device collaboration splits work between the two.

## What I took away

- On-device models trade capability for latency and privacy.
- Vendor rankings across chosen metrics should be checked independently.
- Fast local tool calls matter for agents on devices.
