## Background

Arbitrage means exploiting price differences, such as when yes and no prices sum to less than one. True risk-free arbitrage is rare and competitive, and fees and execution risk matter. The return claim is the author's.

## What I took away

- Minimal risk is not zero risk.
- Check fees and execution before trusting any edge.
- Not advice.
