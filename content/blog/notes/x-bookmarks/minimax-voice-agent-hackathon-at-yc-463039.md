## Summary

MiniMax invites people to build voice agents at the Conversational AI Hackathon at YC, June 6-7 in SF. Moss retrieval supplies context in time, M3 reasons over it and MiniMax Speech 2.8 Turbo responds in under 250ms across 40+ languages. First place wins an interview with a YC partner.

## Background

Voice agents need low latency, since long pauses break a conversation. That is why retrieval, reasoning and speech output all need to be fast.

## What I took away

- Latency is the key constraint for voice agents.
