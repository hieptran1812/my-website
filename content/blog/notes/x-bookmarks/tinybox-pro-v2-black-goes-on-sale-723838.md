## Summary

tinygrad says it is bringing up its first **tinybox pro v2 black**. The machine is for sale for $160k, shipping in 2 to 4 weeks. GLM-5.2 benchmarks are in the thread.

## Background

The tinybox line is tinygrad's prebuilt AI computers for training and running models locally. GLM-5.2 is an open model from Zhipu AI (Z.ai). Local high-end boxes are an alternative to renting cloud GPUs for teams with steady workloads.

## What I took away

- Compare total cost of owning hardware with cloud rental for your usage.
- Check the benchmark thread for real model throughput numbers.
- Marketing phrases like FOMO are not a reason to buy hardware.
