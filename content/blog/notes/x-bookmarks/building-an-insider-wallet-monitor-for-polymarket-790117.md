## Summary

The author says it took about 4 hours to get their monitor running, including bugs, filters and removing unwanted markets, and they pay $25 per month for Claude. The quoted post says the tool flagged five alerts hours before the Maduro event and he bought on that signal. The text is cut off.

## Background

Fresh wallet monitors watch for newly created accounts making large bets, which may indicate informed trading. AI coding tools make building such monitors cheap. Claims of profit are unverified and this is not advice.

## What I took away

- AI coding makes custom monitoring tools quick to build.
- Signals about insider activity can be false positives.
- Be cautious with trading anecdotes.
