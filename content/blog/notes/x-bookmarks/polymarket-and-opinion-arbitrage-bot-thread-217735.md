## Summary

The author says he built a trading bot that finds hundreds of arbitrage opportunities every day between Polymarket and Opinion. Step 1 is matching markets, since different platforms often list markets for the same event.

## Background

Cross-platform arbitrage means buying the same outcome cheaply on one venue and selling it dearly on another. Matching markets is hard because wording and resolution rules differ, which can turn an apparent arbitrage into a loss. The thread is truncated.

## What I took away

- Matching equivalent markets is the first hard step.
- Different resolution rules can break an arbitrage.
- Claims of hundreds of opportunities do not account for fees and fills.
