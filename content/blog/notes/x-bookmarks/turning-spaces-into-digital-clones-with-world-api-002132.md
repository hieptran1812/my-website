## Summary

The author built an app that turns any space into a digital clone in minutes. As founder of Teleport, an iPhone app that captures 360 panoramas, they had the right input when World Labs released a 3D reconstruction API. The text ends at the first test.

## Background

3D reconstruction builds a navigable 3D scene from images. World Labs works on world models, which generate or model 3D environments. Panoramas supply the images needed.

## What I took away

- 3D reconstruction APIs make spatial capture accessible.
- Good input capture matters as much as the model.
- Check the thread for test results.
