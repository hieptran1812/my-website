## Summary

The author argues that automated research is the means, not just the end, for training superintelligent AI systems. Pointing models at scientific discovery would force them to gain capabilities current LLMs lack, such as long-horizon planning and continual adaptation, among others listed in the blog.

## Background

Long-horizon planning means working toward a goal over many steps and a long time. Continual adaptation means learning from new experience without retraining from zero. The author suggests research tasks are a good training ground for these skills.

## What I took away

- Hard open-ended tasks can drive progress on missing capabilities.
- Long-horizon planning and continual learning remain open problems.
- Read the blog for the full list.
