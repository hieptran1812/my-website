## Summary

Jeff Dean says he and his colleague Sanjay Ghemawat have spent a lot of time tuning the performance of code. A couple of years ago they wrote an internal **Performance Hints** document to capture general principles. The post (as shown) introduces that document.

## Background

Performance tuning means making code run faster or use fewer resources, usually by measuring first and then fixing the biggest costs. Written guidance from experienced engineers helps teams avoid common mistakes and pick good habits. Such general principles apply across many kinds of software, including ML systems.

## What I took away

- Look for general performance principles, not only one-off fixes.
- Write down what your team learns about tuning so others can reuse it.
- Open the link to read the full hints document.
