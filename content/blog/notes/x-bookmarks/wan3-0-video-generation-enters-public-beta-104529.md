## Summary

Alibaba's Wan team introduces **Wan3.0** in public beta. Listed features include native 30-second video generation, reality-grade rendering, and Omni-Reference, which accepts not only text, images, audio and video but also documents, spreadsheets, slides and webpages as input.

## Background

Video generation models create video clips from prompts or reference media. Generating long clips natively, rather than stitching short ones, is hard because the model must keep characters and scenes consistent over time.

## What I took away

- Native clip length is a key spec to compare across video models.
- Using documents and webpages as references points to video generation for business content.
- Public beta results may change; test before relying on it.
