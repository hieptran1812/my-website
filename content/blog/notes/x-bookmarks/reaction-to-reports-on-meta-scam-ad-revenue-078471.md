## Summary

Elon Musk replies No kidding to a quoted thread. That thread says about 10% of Meta's revenue comes from scam ads, that Zuckerberg shut down the anti-scam team, and cites a podcast referencing internal documents leaked to Reuters: roughly $16B per year from scam and banned-goods ads and a third of successful US scams. These are claims in the quoted posts.

## Background

The figures come from secondhand posts citing leaked documents and a podcast, not from the post author. Check the original Reuters reporting before relying on the numbers.

## What I took away

- Verify figures from social threads against original reporting.
- Ad platform integrity is a recurring trust and safety issue.
