## Summary

The author says autoresearchers share experiment notes over a peer-to-peer network, with time for gossip on tech news. He says these are actual agents on their own machines using their own models, and that right now they are brute force. The quoted post is his astrophysics agent setup.

## Background

Sharing notes lets agents avoid repeating experiments others already tried. Brute force here means trying many options without smart guidance.

## What I took away

- Shared notes reduce duplicated experiments.
- The author admits current agents are brute force.
