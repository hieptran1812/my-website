## Summary

The author says every year brings eye-opening cases like this and supports making submissions public at all AI conferences, like ICLR. The quoted post questions whether undergraduate authors who withdrew all 9 ICLR2027 submissions after negative reviews acted rightly.

## Background

Public submissions mean papers and reviews stay visible even after withdrawal, which discourages mass submitting.

## What I took away

- Public submissions add accountability.
- Mass submitting strains review.
- Quality over quantity.
