## Summary

The author says they are hiring exceptional engineers, no degree required, and that total compensation is $550k to $1.1M even though the job ad says something else. The quoted post introduces Polaris, a new Google DeepMind team designing evaluation frameworks for AI coding agents, with autonomy to prototype and publish. It says they care about how you think and build, not your level.

## Background

Evaluation frameworks are the tests and environments used to measure how well AI coding agents perform. Good evals are hard to build and strongly shape which models get improved.

## What I took away

- Eval design for coding agents is a growing career area.
- Compensation figures in posts can differ from official ads; confirm in the process.
