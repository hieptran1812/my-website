## Summary

GoodfireAI shares Atticus Geiger's Stanford guest lecture on causal approaches to interpretability, with timestamps for activation steering (for example Golden Gate Claude) and causal mediation analysis. The quoted post says Goodfire thinks some high-priority interp directions are neglected in teaching, so they are producing lectures and reading lists to help people catch up.

## Background

Interpretability tries to understand what happens inside a neural network. Activation steering changes internal activations to change behavior, and causal mediation analysis tests which parts of the model actually cause an output. These methods move beyond only observing correlations.

## What I took away

- Causal methods test what a component does, not just what it correlates with.
- Use the timestamps to jump to the topic you need.
- More Stanford guest lectures and reading lists are planned.
