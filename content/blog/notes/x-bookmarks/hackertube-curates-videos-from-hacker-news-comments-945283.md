## Summary

@karpathy shares a weekend hack, HackerTube. Annoyed with YouTube's recommendations, he had Claude filter 43M comments from 20 years of Hacker News down to 6,454 videos that keep being recommended. He says it gives a significantly better viewing experience and took about 2 hours with Opus 5.5.

## Background

This is a recommendation system built from community signals instead of engagement algorithms. An LLM helps sift a huge text dataset to find links that people repeatedly recommend.

## What I took away

- LLMs make quick data-mining projects over large text dumps feasible.
- Community recommendations can beat engagement-driven feeds.
- Small weekend projects can produce useful tools in a few hours.
