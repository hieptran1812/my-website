## Summary

Neel Nanda posted a video where he reads the alignment audit section of the Opus 4.5 System Card, in a live paper review style. He adds opinions, analysis and context and assesses where frontier alignment audits stand. His take is that Opus seems decent.

## Background

A system card is a document a lab publishes with a model, describing its testing and safety evaluations. An alignment audit checks whether the model behaves as intended, for example avoiding deception. Outside reviews help readers judge such documents.

## What I took away

- System cards now hold useful detail about safety testing.
- Watching an expert read a document aloud is a good way to learn how to evaluate one.
