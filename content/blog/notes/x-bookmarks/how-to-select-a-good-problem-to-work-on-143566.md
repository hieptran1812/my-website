## Summary

@justinskycak shares advice on how to select a good problem to work on, likely in attached media. The quoted post gives career advice for young people: focus early on getting skilled in technical, writing and people-orchestration skills, and get involved contributing to a mission you are aligned with.

## Background

Problem selection is often more important than effort, because a well-chosen problem lets skills compound. The post connects this to building broad skills early.

## What I took away

- Build technical, writing and people skills early.
- Choose missions you care about so effort compounds.
