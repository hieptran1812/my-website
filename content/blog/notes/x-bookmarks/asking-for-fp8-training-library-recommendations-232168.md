## Summary

The author is compiling a list of good FP8 training libraries: transformer_engine, torchao float8 and MS-AMP. He asks which readers prefer for ease of use, stability and support, and which libraries he is missing.

## Background

FP8 means 8-bit floating point numbers, which speed up training and cut memory use compared with 16-bit formats. The libraries handle scaling and casting to keep training stable.

## What I took away

- Three known FP8 options are Transformer Engine, torchao and MS-AMP.
- Compare libraries on ease of use, stability and support.
- Check replies for libraries not on the list.
