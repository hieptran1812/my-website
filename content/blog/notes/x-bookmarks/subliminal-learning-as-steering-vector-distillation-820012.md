## Summary

@NeelNanda5 recommends commentary comparing his team's work on explaining subliminal learning with prior work. The quoted posts discuss the paper "Subliminal Learning Is Steering Vector Distillation", where authors say interesting LLM phenomena boil down to adding a steering vector.

## Background

Subliminal learning is when a student model picks up traits of a teacher from data that seems unrelated. A steering vector is a direction in activations that shifts behavior. The paper claims training moves the student along this direction.

## What I took away

- Distilled data can carry hidden traits.
- Steering vectors give a mechanistic account.
