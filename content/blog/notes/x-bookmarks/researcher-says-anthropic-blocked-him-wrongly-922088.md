## Summary

@jiahaoshao1 criticizes Anthropic, saying he never distilled Claude: his rollouts came from a strong open-source model and Claude only helped submit jobs and run analysis. He says he was still tagged for training a competitor, and now anything touching RFT, SFT or training larger models gets him blocked.

## Background

Distillation means training a model on another model's outputs. AI providers' terms often forbid using their outputs to train competing models. RFT is reinforcement fine-tuning and SFT is supervised fine-tuning. This is one side of a dispute.

## What I took away

- Read provider terms on using models for ML training work.
- Automated policy enforcement can flag legitimate research workflows.
- This is one person's account; the provider's side is not given.
