## Summary

The author is thinking about a second part of their graph series aligned with distributed systems. They mention the minimum spanning tree, which helps build trees for broadcasting messages or minimizing network costs when syncing. The quoted post is their earlier blog on graphs 101.

## Background

A minimum spanning tree connects all nodes in a graph with the lowest total edge cost and no cycles. In networks it can reduce the cost of sending messages to every node. Graph algorithms are a common bridge between interview prep and real distributed systems work.

## What I took away

- Minimum spanning trees help with cheap broadcasting.
- Graph basics apply directly to networking and distributed systems.
