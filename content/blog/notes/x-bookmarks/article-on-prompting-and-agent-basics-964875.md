## Summary

The author calls an article a banger for vibe coders. It covers prompt chains, temperature in AI, multi-prompting flows, RAG, agents and evals, and guardrails. They suggest bookmarking it.

## Background

RAG means retrieving documents and adding them to the prompt. Evals are tests of model output quality. Guardrails limit unsafe or off-topic outputs.

## What I took away

- Learn evals early; they keep prompt changes honest.
- Use the article as a checklist of topics.
