## Summary

@ysu_nlp says they trained a near-frontier deep research agent on an academic budget: 32 H100s, 8K synthetic samples, fully open training infra and recipe (SFT, mid-training, RL), with models from 2B to 35B ready to use. The text is cut off.

## Background

A deep research agent searches and reads many sources to answer hard questions. Synthetic samples are generated training data. Near-frontier is the authors' claim.

## What I took away

- Small compute and data can train capable agents.
- Open recipes make this reproducible.
