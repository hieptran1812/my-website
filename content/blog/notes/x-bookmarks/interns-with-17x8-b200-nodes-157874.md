## Summary

The author says that at an inference lab you get 17 nodes of 8 B200 GPUs to try things out, around 1.2 EFLOPS FP8 for an intern. It replies to a post arguing compute is an elastic resource and clouds would sell it to whoever pays most.

## Background

B200 is an NVIDIA Blackwell GPU, and FP8 is an 8-bit number format used for fast AI math. An EFLOP is a quintillion operations per second, so the scale is very large for one intern.

## What I took away

- Access to compute differs enormously between teams.
- Numbers here are a rough, joking estimate.
