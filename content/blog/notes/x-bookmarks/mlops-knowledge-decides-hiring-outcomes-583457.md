## Summary

The author says that in about 90% of interviews they have run, MLOps knowledge decided whether a candidate got hired. They promote the quoted post, which lists a deployment with MLOps, agentic AI, an LSTM, Prometheus/Grafana, Redis cache, rate limiting, Qdrant, Kubernetes and Terraform.

## Background

MLOps is the practice of deploying, monitoring and maintaining ML models in production. Skills such as monitoring, data drift detection and infrastructure are often separate from model training.

## What I took away

- Deployment and monitoring skills can set candidates apart.
- Build a project that goes beyond a notebook.
- The 90% figure is the author's own experience.
