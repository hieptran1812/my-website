## Summary

The author says generative AI has two tribes, LLMs and diffusion models, which are both deep generative models with different rules. LLMs use text and code, Transformers, next-token prediction and step-by-step autoregressive generation. The quoted post says Stanford published a 470-page study, The Principles of Diffusion Models, covering the forward noising process and its learned reverse.

## Background

Autoregressive models produce output one piece at a time. Diffusion models start from noise and remove it over many steps. The two are often compared because they scale differently.

## What I took away

- LLMs generate token by token, diffusion refines from noise.
- The 470-page study is a deep reference for diffusion.
- The two approaches are starting to blend.
