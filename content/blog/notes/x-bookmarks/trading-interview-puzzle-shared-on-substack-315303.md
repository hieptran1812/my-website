## Summary

@Lihong062 tells prediction market traders they are missing out by not following a certain author. The quoted author says they wrote a trading-related Substack post about a question that could be asked to early-career candidates or interns as a general test of trading intuition and math skills.

## Background

Trading firms often interview with probability and expected-value puzzles to see how candidates reason under uncertainty. Prediction markets let people trade on the outcome of events, so the same skills apply. This note is educational, not a recommendation to trade.

## What I took away

- Practice expected-value and probability puzzles to build trading intuition.
- Claims of free alpha are marketing language; be skeptical.
- Read the Substack post to see the actual question.
