## Summary

@gemchange_ltd claims someone cracked Polymarket's hourly crypto markets using latency, not analysis, and that one account cashed out $50k PnL in a week. A bot watches real-time feeds. The quoted post describes a liquidity-claimer bot on Bitcoin bracket markets that plays where BTC will not be rather than predicting direction.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market. Latency trading means reacting faster than others to new data. Bracket markets split outcomes into price ranges. These are unverified claims, and not advice.

## What I took away

- Speed edges can matter more than forecasting in short markets.
- Profit claims on social media are unverified.
- Such edges tend to shrink as more bots copy them.
