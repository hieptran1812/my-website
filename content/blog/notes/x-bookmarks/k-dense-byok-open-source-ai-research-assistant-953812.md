## Summary

The author says they open sourced a version of what they call the world's most capable AI co-scientist. It is free, easy to install and uses their Scientific Skills. The quoted post describes K-Dense BYOK as a local research assistant with 170+ scientific skills, 250+ databases and 40+ models, with data staying on the user's computer.

## Background

BYOK means bring your own key: you plug in your own model API keys instead of paying a subscription. A skill here is a packaged instruction set an agent can load for a specific task. The capability claims are the authors' own and not independently verified in the post.

## What I took away

- BYOK tools keep cost and data under your control.
- Skills plus database access is a common pattern for domain-specific agents.
- Treat 'most capable' claims as marketing until you test it on your own task.
