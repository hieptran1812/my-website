## Summary

The author says subliminal steering allows more precise study of mechanisms. When layers are chosen to steer the teacher, the student inherits not just the bias but also a shift in activations at those layers.

## Background

Subliminal learning is when a student model picks up traits from a teacher's outputs even when they seem unrelated. Steering changes activations at chosen layers. This helps locate where traits transfer.

## What I took away

- Distillation can pass hidden traits from teacher to student.
- Layer-level steering is a tool to study that transfer.
