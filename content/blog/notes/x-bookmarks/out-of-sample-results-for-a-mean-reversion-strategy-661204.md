## Summary

@SystematicPeter says he published a mean reversion strategy with a free backtester in January 2025. He reports that over the following 11 months it returned +30.4% versus +24.4% for the Nasdaq, with a -10.2% max drawdown versus -22.8%, a 72% win rate and 83 trades. He then shares the exact system.

## Background

Mean reversion is the idea that prices that move far from their average tend to move back. Out-of-sample means tested on data that was not used to build the strategy, which is a stronger check than a backtest alone. Max drawdown is the largest drop from a peak. These numbers are the author's own report.

## What I took away

- Out-of-sample testing is the honest way to check a backtested idea.
- Compare returns together with drawdown, not alone.
- A short test period and 83 trades is a small sample; this is educational, not advice.
