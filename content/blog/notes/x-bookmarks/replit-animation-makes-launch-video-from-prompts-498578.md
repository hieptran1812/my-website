## Summary

The author says they made a whole launch video with 10 prompts and calls it over. The quoted post introduces Replit Animation, which lets you vibecode a video in minutes, powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro.

## Background

Vibecoding a video means describing it and letting a model generate animation code. The result quality is shown in the video, not text.

## What I took away

- Prompt-driven video is getting practical.
- Judge quality by watching the demo.
