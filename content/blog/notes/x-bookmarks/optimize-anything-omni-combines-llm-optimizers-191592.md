## Summary

The GEPA team announces **optimize_anything omni**, a meta-optimizer pipeline that combines optimizers including GEPA, AutoResearch and Meta-Harness. They find different optimizers have complementary performance profiles. The quoted post says each of GEPA, AutoResearch and Meta-Harness wins on different tasks and no single LLM optimizer wins everywhere.

## Background

LLM-based optimizers use a model to iteratively improve prompts, programs or harnesses based on feedback. GEPA is one such method for prompt and program optimization. A meta-optimizer picks or combines several optimizers since none is best for every task.

## What I took away

- No single optimizer is best for all tasks; try several.
- Combining methods can beat relying on one favorite.
- Automated prompt and harness optimization is a practical tool to explore.
