## Summary

The author calls parameter sensitivity one of the best backtesting techniques. The quoted post says it is one of the best tools in quantitative trading, claims 99% of traders do not understand it, and promises a simple explanation for building better strategies.

## Background

Parameter sensitivity means changing a strategy's settings slightly and checking whether results stay stable. If a small change ruins performance, the strategy was probably overfit to past data. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Test nearby parameter values, not only the best one.
- Fragile results after small changes suggest overfitting.
- Treat the claim that 99% of traders miss this as the author's opinion.
