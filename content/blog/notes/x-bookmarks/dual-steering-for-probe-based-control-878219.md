## Summary

The quote says a standard method adds a probe vector to the representation, but information geometry says to add it in the dual space, called Dual Steering. The paper claims a provably and empirically robust steering strategy.

## Background

Steering changes a model's behavior by editing its hidden activations. A linear probe is a direction that detects a concept. The idea is to respect how the model uses representations.

## What I took away

- Naive vector addition may ignore geometry.
- Look at the arXiv paper for evidence.
