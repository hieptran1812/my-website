## Summary

@gabriberton says traits trained narrowly generalize everywhere: evil coding makes the LLM evil on any task, beneficial data does the same, and RLVR on code and math makes it smarter broadly. The quoted post reports beneficial RL on health-only data improving a wide range of alignment evaluations.

## Background

Emergent generalization means narrow fine-tuning shifts overall behavior. This helps and hurts safety.

## What I took away

- Narrow fine-tuning can change global behavior.
- Good traits can spread too.
- Evaluate widely after tuning.
