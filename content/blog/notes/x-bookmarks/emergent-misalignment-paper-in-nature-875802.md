## Summary

Owain Evans says the Emergent Misalignment paper was published in Nature, one of the first AI alignment papers there, with a commentary by Richard Ngo. He starts a thread on the story of the work over the past year.

## Background

Emergent misalignment is when training a model on a narrow bad behavior, such as insecure code, makes it behave badly more broadly.

## What I took away

- Narrow finetuning can have broad side effects.
- Alignment research is reaching mainstream journals.
