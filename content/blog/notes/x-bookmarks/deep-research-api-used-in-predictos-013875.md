## Summary

The author's company says its AI deep research API is used in a new PredictOS demo by @prediction_xbt. PredictOS is described as an agentic operating system for prediction markets. The company says it keeps integrating protocols like this.

## Background

A prediction market lets people trade on the outcome of real-world events. An agentic system lets AI agents research and act with tools. This is a promotional post about an integration.

## What I took away

- Research APIs are becoming building blocks for trading agents.
- Read integration announcements as marketing until you see the demo.
