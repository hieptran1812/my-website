## Summary

AlexFinn says the Godfather of Vibe Coding bought a Mac Mini to play with OpenClaw and calls local personal AI assistants the future. The quote is Karpathy saying Mac minis are selling like hotcakes and that he is wary of giving private data and keys to 400K lines of vibe coded software.

## Background

OpenClaw is an agent that runs locally and holds credentials. Karpathy's caution shows a security tradeoff the promotional post skips.

## What I took away

- Giving agents your keys carries risk.
- Read both sides before adopting.
