## Summary

The post says GigaBrain-0.5M* is a VLA model trained with world model-based reinforcement learning. It claims near-perfect success on complex manipulation tasks and the number 1 rank on RoboChallenge.

## Background

A VLA (vision-language-action) model sees images and instructions and outputs robot actions. A world model predicts what happens next, so a policy can practice in imagination. Results are as reported in the post.

## What I took away

- World models can cut costly real-robot training.
- Check the benchmark setup behind the ranking.
