## Summary

@__tinygrad__ shares links to the GLM-5 paper on arXiv and the blog posts for GLM-5.1 and GLM-5.2. The card describes GLM-5 as a foundation model moving from vibe coding to agentic engineering.

## Background

This is a reading list for the GLM-5 model line. The card says the models target agentic, reasoning and coding (ARC) abilities. Vibe coding means loosely prompting a model for code, while agentic engineering means models doing longer, tool-using engineering work.

## What I took away

- Use these three links as the entry point to the GLM-5 series.
- Read the paper first for method, then the blogs for updates.
