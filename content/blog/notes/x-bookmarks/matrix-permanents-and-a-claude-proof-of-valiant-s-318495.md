## Summary

The author recalls meeting Van Vu in 2009 and again in summer 2026, when they said permanents were still believed hard to compute. He says we now know they are hard. The quoted post claims an internal Claude model found a proof of Valiant's conjecture, with a 70-page paper credited to Claude and a Lean proof that passed automated checks.

## Background

Valiant's conjecture is the algebraic analogue of P vs NP, about whether the permanent is much harder to compute than the determinant. A Lean proof is machine checked. These are claims from the posts.

## What I took away

- The permanent and determinant look similar but may differ greatly in difficulty.
- Lean checking adds confidence beyond human review.
- Treat the result as claimed until the community confirms.
