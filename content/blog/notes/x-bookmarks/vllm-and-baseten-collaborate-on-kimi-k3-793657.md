## Summary

vLLM points to a write-up and says it is excited to collaborate with the **Baseten** team on supporting Kimi K3 on vLLM. The post text does not include details of the work.

## Background

Baseten is an inference hosting company. Supporting a new large model in a serving engine needs new kernels, configs and testing. Collaboration between engine maintainers and hosting providers speeds up reliable day-0 support.

## What I took away

- Open the linked write-up for technical details.
- Engine and provider partnerships shape how fast new models become usable.
- Check which serving engine your provider uses for a given model.
