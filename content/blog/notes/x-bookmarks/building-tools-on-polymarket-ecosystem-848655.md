## Summary

The author says building on Polymarket is their daily obsession and that they ship features for PolynterNet almost every day. The quoted post says Polymarket reached 200 verified builders, 1.28B dollars plus builder volume, 45K plus active users and 200 verified projects.

## Background

A builder program lets third parties create dashboards and trading terminals on top of a platform. The stats are as claimed in the post. Promotional posts like this should be read with care.

## What I took away

- Ecosystem tooling is an active niche around prediction markets.
- Treat platform milestone numbers as self-reported.
- This is not trading advice.
