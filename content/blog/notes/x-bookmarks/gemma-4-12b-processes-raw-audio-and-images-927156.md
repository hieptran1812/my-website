## Summary

The author says raw waveform audio models are back and that Gemma 4 12B is the largest such model ever released, by 1-2 orders of magnitude. The quoted post says it is a dense, encoder-free model that processes raw text, image and audio inputs.

## Background

Most audio models first turn sound into features with a separate encoder. Raw waveform models skip that and read the signal directly, which is simpler but harder to train.

## What I took away

- Encoder-free design unifies modalities in one model.
- Size comparison is the author's claim.
