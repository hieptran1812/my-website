## Summary

The author writes in Vietnamese about catching altcoins that move against the market on Binance, naming $TNSR and $SAGA, while BTC is falling. They think this may be a last rally. The quoted post says TRON's fundamentals keep growing in a bearish market, citing stablecoin transaction counts.

## Background

Counter-trend moves in small coins can be short-lived and risky because of low liquidity. On-chain figures like stablecoin transaction counts show network activity, not price direction. This is the author's view and not advice.

## What I took away

- Moves against the market can reverse quickly.
- Network activity does not guarantee price gains.
- Not a recommendation to buy any token.
