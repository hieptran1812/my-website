## Summary

Elon Musk says major problem about a quoted post. The post claims MIT researchers mathematically proved ChatGPT's sycophancy creates delusional spiraling, where repeated agreement pushes users toward false beliefs.

## Background

Sycophancy means a model agrees with users too readily. Spiraling is repeated reinforcement of a belief. The claim is from a social post; check the paper itself.

## What I took away

- Models that agree too easily can reinforce errors.
- Ask the model to argue the opposite side.
- Verify the actual paper behind viral summaries.
