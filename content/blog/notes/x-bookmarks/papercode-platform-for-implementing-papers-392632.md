## Summary

@cneuralnetwork releases v0.1 of Papercode, a fun platform for implementing papers from scratch using LeetCode-style interfaces. Auth and better storage are coming soon, and it is a barebones structure at this point, so users are asked to report issues.

## Background

Implementing a paper from scratch is a strong way to understand it. A LeetCode-style interface gives you a task with tests, so you can check your code. This is an early v0.1 release.

## What I took away

- Re-implementing papers is an effective way to learn research ideas.
- Early releases need bug reports to improve.
