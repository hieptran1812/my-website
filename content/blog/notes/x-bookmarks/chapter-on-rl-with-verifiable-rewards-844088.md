## Summary

The author says chapter 6 on RLVR is available. It is essentially GRPO from scratch and his favorite chapter so far. The first 363 pages are done and a follow-up covers more RLVR runs, metrics and policy clipping and KL.

## Background

RLVR trains models with rewards checked automatically, like math answers. GRPO compares groups of samples. Policy clipping and KL keep updates stable.

## What I took away

- Implement GRPO to understand it.
- Verifiable rewards drive reasoning gains.
- Look for the follow-up.
