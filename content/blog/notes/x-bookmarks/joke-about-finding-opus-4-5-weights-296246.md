## Summary

The author jokes that they found the weights for Opus 4.5 and it looks impressive. It is humor, not a real leak.

## Background

Model weights are the numbers that define a model and are not public for Opus. The joke plays on how weight files look like random numbers.

## What I took away

- Do not treat joke posts as news.
- Weights are not human readable.
