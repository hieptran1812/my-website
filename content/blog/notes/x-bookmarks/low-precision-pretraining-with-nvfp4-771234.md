## Summary

Radical Numerics says scaling scientific world models needs co-design of architecture, objectives and numerics. They share the first posts on low-precision pretraining, starting with NVIDIA's NVFP4 recipe for stable 4-bit training.

## Background

Lower precision cuts memory and raises speed, but 4-bit training risks instability, so recipes matter.

## What I took away

- 4-bit training needs careful recipes.
- Numerics is a design axis.
