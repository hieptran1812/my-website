## Summary

The author writes in Chinese that this is more useful than distilling their own skill. The quoted post lists instructions for making Claude Code speak plain language, such as using standard terminology and avoiding metaphors.

## Background

A skill here is a reusable instruction file for an agent. Distilling your own skill means writing one from your past preferences. Sometimes a well-written public set of rules works better than a custom one.

## What I took away

- Try good public instruction sets before writing your own from scratch.
- Simple, concrete style rules tend to work well for agents.
