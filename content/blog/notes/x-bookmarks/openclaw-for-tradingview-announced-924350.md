## Summary

The author says OpenClaw for TradingView has been released, with AI agents analyzing charts and executing trades in real time. No details, results or safeguards are given.

## Background

TradingView is a charting platform. Letting an agent place trades adds risk because mistakes cost real money. Agents need strict limits, testing in paper trading, and human review.

## What I took away

- Test trading agents on paper accounts first.
- Autonomous execution needs hard limits.
- Not financial advice.
