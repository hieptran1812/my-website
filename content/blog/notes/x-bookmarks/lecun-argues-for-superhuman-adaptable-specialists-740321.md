## Summary

The author says a new paper with Yann LeCun argues chasing general AI is a mistake and we should build superhuman adaptable specialists. The quoted post says the biggest LLM is trained on about 30 trillion words, roughly 10 to the power 14 bytes, and compares this with what a 4-year-old takes in over about 16,000 waking hours.

## Background

The argument is that text alone is limited compared with the rich sensory data humans learn from. The numbers are LeCun's comparison as quoted. It supports interest in world models and specialist systems.

## What I took away

- Compare data volume and type, not just size, when judging LLM limits.
- Specialist systems are an alternative goal to general AI.
- These are positions, not settled facts.
