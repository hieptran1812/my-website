## Summary

Tinker says Exa trains Qwen3-4B-Instruct to search using Tinker. The quoted blog post asks how Exa compares to Google for training LLMs to search. It claims that LLMs using Exa during reinforcement learning reach higher performance with 70% less training compute.

## Background

In reinforcement learning, a model learns from reward for outcomes. For search agents, the quality of the search tool in the loop affects what the model can learn. The 70% figure is the blog's claim.

## What I took away

- The tool a model trains against can change training efficiency.
- Check whether the compute savings are measured on your task.
