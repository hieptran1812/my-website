## Summary

The author says current agents are bad at remembering and that RAG no longer works well enough, so they are reading papers on context engineering and persistent memory. They plan to share insights, beginning with a list that includes Google's whitepaper on context. The quoted post just says time to lock in.

## Background

Context engineering means deciding what information goes into a model's limited context window. Persistent memory means storing facts across sessions. RAG retrieves documents at query time and may miss what an agent needs to remember. The RAG claim is the author's view.

## What I took away

- Agent memory is an active research area.
- Study how context is selected and compressed, not only retrieval.
- Follow the thread for the paper list.
