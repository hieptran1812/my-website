## Summary

Qwen says it is thrilled that Qwen3.5 reached a record-breaking 580 tps for agentic workloads on the TokenSpeed engine. It thanks LightSeek, NVIDIA, the Mooncake team and Tri Dao. The quote is the PyTorch Foundation's post about the same optimization.

## Background

Tokens per second (tps) is how fast text is generated. Agentic workloads chain many calls, so low latency matters. The record is as reported by the project and its partners.

## What I took away

- Model teams and engine teams work closely to reach top speeds.
- Many open source parts (engine, cache, kernels) combine for results.
- Read the PyTorch blog for the details.
