## Summary

The author says, sarcastically, that he is done teaching how to automate trading because even the "smartest" quants supposedly recommend 40x leverage. The quoted post replies that the right answer to being liquidated is to use less leverage and trade less, not to automate and lose money faster.

## Background

Leverage means trading with borrowed money, so small price moves can wipe out your account (liquidation). Automation does not change the risk of a strategy. It only makes the same decisions run faster, good or bad.

## What I took away

- Automation amplifies a strategy, including a bad one.
- Heavy leverage is a risk-management problem, not a tooling problem.
- Treat sarcastic trading posts as commentary, not advice.
