## Summary

The author says that if you think better models just need larger datasets or more parameters, DeepSeekMath-V2 is for you. They say it shows a model that can verify its own reasoning can outperform bigger models. The text is cut off.

## Background

Self-verification means the model checks its own proof or reasoning steps and corrects mistakes. Rewarding sound reasoning rather than just correct final answers can improve math ability. The comparison with larger models is the author's claim, so check the paper.

## What I took away

- Training models to verify reasoning is an alternative to simply scaling up.
- Read the paper for the actual results.
