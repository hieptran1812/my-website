## Summary

@paradite_ says Anthropic does not understand machine learning and links its post on detecting and preventing distillation attacks. The quoted author argues that Chinese models are not strong because of distilling US models, and that distilling via API is impossible.

## Background

Distillation means training a smaller or different model on the outputs of a stronger one. Through an API you only see text outputs, not weights or full probabilities, so people disagree about how much can be copied this way. The post is opinion, not evidence.

## What I took away

- These are strong claims from one side of a dispute; treat them as opinions.
- Learn what API outputs can and cannot transfer before taking a side.
