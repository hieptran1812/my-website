## Summary

@XinCynthiaChen is excited the ICML position paper was accepted as an Oral. It asks, when a model appears to deceive or resist shutdown, how we know it is not role-play, instruction following or task completion pressure, noting current evidence often cannot tell them apart.

## Background

Safety evaluations show models doing alarming things, but causes differ. Without separating explanations, conclusions may be overstated. The paper argues for better evidence standards.

## What I took away

- Alarming behavior needs causal explanation.
- Design evals that separate role-play from intent.
