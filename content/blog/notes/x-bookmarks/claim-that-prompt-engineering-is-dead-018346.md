## Summary

The author says in one line that prompt engineering is dead. The post gives no reasons or evidence.

## Background

Prompt engineering means carefully wording instructions to get good output from a language model. Some people say newer models and agent tooling make this less important, while others say it has just changed form into context and harness design.

## What I took away

- Treat one-line claims like this as opinion, not evidence.
- Ask what replaces the skill: better models, better context, or better tooling.
