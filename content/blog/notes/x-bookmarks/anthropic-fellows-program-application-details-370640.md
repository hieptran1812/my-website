## Summary

The post describes a 4-month Anthropic Fellows Program for work on AI safety and security, in London, San Francisco or remote. It lists a weekly stipend of $3,850, $15k per month of compute funding, benefits and mentorship. The deadline is January 20, and a Safety Track is mentioned.

## Background

A fellowship gives funding and compute to researchers outside the company's regular staff. Compute funding matters because experiments with large models are expensive. Figures here are as stated in the post.

## What I took away

- Funded fellowships can combine stipend, compute and mentorship.
- Confirm details on the official application page, since the post is a summary.
- Deadline in the post was January 20.
