## Summary

DeepLearning.AI says Samsung's Tiny Recursive Model (TRM) iteratively refines its answers while keeping a running context of past changes. It is used for Sudoku, mazes and ARC-AGI tasks. The post says it beats many LLMs, including DeepSeek-R1 and Gemini 2.5 Pro, on these benchmarks.

## Background

Recursive models reuse the same small network repeatedly, improving a draft answer each pass. This gives deep reasoning with few parameters. The comparison is on narrow puzzle tasks, so it does not mean a tiny model beats LLMs generally.

## What I took away

- Iterative refinement lets small models do well on structured puzzles.
- The wins are on specific benchmarks, not all tasks.
- Consider recursion when the task has clear verifiable structure.
