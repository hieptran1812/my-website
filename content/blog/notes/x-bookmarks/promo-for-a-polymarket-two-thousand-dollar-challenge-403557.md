## Summary

The author promotes a trader called thegreektrader starting a public $2,000 to Mercedes challenge on Polymarket and says he already turned $150 into $25,000. The quoted post is the trader sharing his challenge wallet and asking people not to copy trade.

## Background

This is promotional content with referral-style links and unverified claims. Public challenges attract attention but do not show repeatable skill. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Challenges are entertainment, not evidence of a reliable method.
- Be wary of copy trading from promoted wallets.
