## Summary

The author wrote a roughly 4000-word article on all the math you need for machine learning and urges readers to bookmark it. The link card is titled The Roadmap of Mathematics for Machine Learning.

## Background

ML relies on linear algebra, calculus, probability and statistics. A roadmap shows what to learn and in what order so you avoid wasting time on unneeded topics.

## What I took away

- Learn math in a planned order rather than at random.
- Focus on linear algebra, calculus and probability first.
