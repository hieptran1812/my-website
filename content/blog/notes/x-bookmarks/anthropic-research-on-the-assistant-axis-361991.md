## Summary

@AnthropicAI announces research called the Assistant Axis. It says that when you talk to a language model, you are talking to a character it plays, the Assistant. It asks who the Assistant is and what happens when that persona wears off.

## Background

Language models can simulate many characters. The Assistant is the helpful persona trained into chat models. If the persona slips, behavior can become less reliable or safe. Studying it is part of understanding model character.

## What I took away

- Chat models are playing a persona.
- Persona stability is a safety question.
- Read the research for the method and findings.
