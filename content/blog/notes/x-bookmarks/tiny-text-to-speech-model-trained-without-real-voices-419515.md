## Summary

The author says they trained a text-to-speech model that has never heard a real human voice. It has 4.5M parameters, takes 18 MB, runs on a CPU, and is called tiny-voca. The text is cut off.

## Background

Text-to-speech models usually train on recorded human speech. Training on synthetic data avoids collecting voices and can make small models cheap to produce.

## What I took away

- Synthetic data can train small models.
- Very small TTS models can run on a CPU.
- Check the repo for quality samples.
