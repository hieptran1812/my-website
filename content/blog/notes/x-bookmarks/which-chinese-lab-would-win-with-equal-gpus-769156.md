## Summary

@eliebakouch asks who would produce the best model if the top 50 people at DeepSeek, Kimi, Z.ai and MiniMax had the same GPUs for 6 months. Final poll results of 1,450 votes: DeepSeek 48.8%, Z.ai 30.1%, Moonshot (Kimi) 18.9%, MiniMax 2.2%.

## Background

The poll is opinion, not performance data. It shows community perception of lab quality. Voters are followers of the account.

## What I took away

- Polls show perception, not capability.
- DeepSeek led the vote.
