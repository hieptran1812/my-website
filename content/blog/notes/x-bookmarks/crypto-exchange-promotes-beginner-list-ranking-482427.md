## Summary

The author says it is not too early to set 2026 trading goals. They say they are proud to earn a spot on a Coinpedia list of best crypto exchanges for beginners in 2026 and promote BingX AI.

## Background

This is marketing for a crypto exchange. Lists like this are often promotional, and being listed does not show quality or safety. It is not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Treat exchange promotions as advertising.
- Check an exchange's security, fees and regulation yourself.
