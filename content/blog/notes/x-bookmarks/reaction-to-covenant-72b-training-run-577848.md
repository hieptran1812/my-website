## Summary

The author says this is the coolest thing he has seen all year. The quoted post announces Covenant-72B, a 72B-parameter model trained on about 1.1T tokens on Bittensor subnet 3, permissionless and over commodity internet.

## Background

Decentralized training lets many independent GPU owners contribute to one model instead of relying on a single lab's cluster.

- Community excitement is about open access to training compute.
