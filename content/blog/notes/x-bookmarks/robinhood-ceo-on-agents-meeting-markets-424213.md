## Summary

Robinhood's CEO posts a short line, agent, meet markets, quoting the official announcement. That post says users can connect an AI agent to an Agentic Account to explore ideas, rebalance portfolios, build custom tools and place trades, rolling out now.

## Background

The product connects AI agents to live brokerage actions. It signals how mainstream finance is embracing agents. This is not advice to use it.

## What I took away

- Financial firms are productizing agent access.
- Keep human review in the loop for money actions.
- Read the terms and safeguards first.
