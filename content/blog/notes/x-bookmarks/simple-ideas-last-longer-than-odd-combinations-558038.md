## Summary

The author says anti-intuitive ideas or unrelated combinations can always produce surprising work, but work that stands the test of time is usually very simple. The quoted post finds weak-to-strong OPD interesting and calls it an anti-human idea, adding that testing it requires writing the codebase first.

## Background

OPD likely refers to on-policy distillation, where a student model learns from a teacher on the student's own outputs. Weak-to-strong means a weaker model supervising a stronger one, which runs against the usual teacher-student direction.

## What I took away

- Surprising results are worth testing, but favor simple methods for the long run.
- Build a small codebase to test an idea before trusting it.
- Weak-to-strong setups are an open question worth watching.
