## Summary

The author praises Jacob as an exceptional talent for building a chip design company. The quoted post is Jacob's announcement of a long-form blog on AI chip architectures covering NVIDIA, AMD, TPUs, Trainium, Cerebras and Groq across architecture, scaling and software stacks.

## Background

AI chips differ in how they handle compute, memory and interconnect, and in how mature their software is. A single post comparing them side by side is useful background for anyone choosing hardware or reasoning about inference costs.

## What I took away

- The underlying blog post is the useful part; read it for chip comparisons.
- Endorsements are signals of reputation, not technical evidence.
