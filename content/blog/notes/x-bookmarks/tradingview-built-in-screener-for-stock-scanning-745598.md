## Summary

The author says TradingView's built-in screener is far better than any other paid screener, and that 75% of their scanning is done there. They like it because it gives easy-to-scroll chart visuals and saves time. These are their opinions.

## Background

A screener filters many stocks by rules like volume or price. Linking the results to charts lets you review candidates quickly.

## What I took away

- Compare free tools before paying for a screener.
- Fast visual review saves time.
