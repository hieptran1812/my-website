## Summary

The author open sourced what he calls the largest synthetic parallel multilingual OCR dataset: **1M+ document images in 22 languages**, including Arabic, Bengali, German, Hindi, Japanese and more. The quote calls it a multilingual, multimodal, multitask document corpus.

## Background

OCR reads text from images. Synthetic data is generated programmatically, so labels are exact. Parallel means the same content appears across languages. Many languages lack good OCR training data, so this helps.

## What I took away

- Synthetic data can fill gaps for low-resource languages.
- Parallel data helps compare quality across languages.
- Check the license and how realistic the images are.
