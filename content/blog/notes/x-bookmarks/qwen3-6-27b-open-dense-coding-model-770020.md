## Summary

Alibaba Cloud introduces Qwen3.6-27B, a dense open-source model said to pack flagship-level coding power. It highlights agentic coding, strong reasoning across text and multimodal tasks, thinking and non-thinking modes, and an Apache 2.0 license.

## Background

A dense model uses all its parameters on every token, unlike mixture-of-experts. At 27B it can run on a single high-end GPU. Apache 2.0 allows commercial use. Performance claims are the vendor's.

## What I took away

- A 27B dense open model is practical to self-host.
- Apache 2.0 makes commercial use easy.
- Verify coding claims on your own tasks.
