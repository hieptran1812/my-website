## Summary

The author is hiring on their OpenAI team for Research Engineer, Scientist and Software Engineer roles. They say a key question is how to train AI to help people over time, worked on through RLHF, post-training, reward modeling and related areas.

## Background

RLHF trains a model using human preference feedback. Reward modeling learns a score for good responses. Helping people over time is harder than single-reply quality because effects accumulate across conversations.

## What I took away

- Long-term helpfulness is an active research direction.
- RLHF and reward modeling skills map to these roles.
- See the post for the full listing.
