## Summary

The author says they built a side project, Gradient Bang, which they believe is the first massively multiplayer, completely LLM-driven game. They invite people to play and try to beat them on the leaderboard. They mention sub-agents in latent space.

## Background

In an LLM-driven game, models run the world and characters. Sub-agents are smaller agents that a main agent delegates to. First-of-its-kind is the author's belief.

## What I took away

- Games are a good testbed for agent systems.
- Try it to see multi-agent design.
- First claims are unverified.
