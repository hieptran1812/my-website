## Summary

The post says AI can build financial models like Goldman Sachs analysts for free. It offers 12 Claude prompts it claims replace $150K per year investment banking work.

## Background

Financial models require correct inputs, assumptions and review. Prompts help draft but do not replace judgment. The tone is engagement bait.

## What I took away

- Check every number in AI models.
- Be skeptical of replace-a-job claims.
