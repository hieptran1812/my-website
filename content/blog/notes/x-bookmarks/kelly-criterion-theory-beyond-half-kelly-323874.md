## Summary

The author announces a new article that claims to do better than Half-Kelly and other heuristics by explaining the theory behind the Kelly Criterion. It covers practical use through Bayesian inference to update odds and shrinkage for stability. The link is in the profile bio.

## Background

The Kelly Criterion is a formula for how much of your capital to bet to maximize long-run growth, given your edge. Half-Kelly is a common safer fraction. Uncertain edge estimates can make full Kelly too aggressive, so Bayesian updating and shrinkage pull estimates toward safer values. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Kelly sizing depends heavily on how well you estimate your edge.
- Shrinkage reduces the risk of overbetting on noisy estimates.
- Understand the theory before using a rule of thumb like Half-Kelly.
