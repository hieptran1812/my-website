## Summary

The post claims that Claude Opus 4.5 for Polymarket is a cheat code. There is no further detail in the text.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people bet on event outcomes. Using an AI model to analyze or automate such bets is the idea. The claim is promotional and unproven here, and betting involves risk.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of cheat code claims about markets.
- Models can help research but do not remove risk.
