## Summary

The author calls RN1 one of the most profitable bots he has seen on Polymarket. Despite more than $2M in PnL, it lost about $20k today on an unfavorable outcome. A link to its profile is included.

## Background

PnL means profit and loss. Even strong strategies have losing days, so a single loss says little about the whole. This is a description, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Profitable strategies still have losing days.
- Judge strategies over many trades, not a single day.
