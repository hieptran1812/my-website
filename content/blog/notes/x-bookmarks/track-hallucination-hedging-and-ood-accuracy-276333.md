## Summary

@beanie0__0 loves the recommendation to track more than in-distribution accuracy: the hallucination rate, the amount of hedging (epistemic verbalization), and out-of-distribution accuracy. The post gives no further context.

## Background

In-distribution accuracy measures performance on data like the training set. Hedging means the model expresses uncertainty in words. Out-of-distribution data differs from training and reveals fragility.

## What I took away

- Add hallucination, hedging and OOD metrics to evaluation.
- Accuracy alone can hide problems.
