## Summary

@Aakshayy12 says the best way to get into HFT is to take part in IICPC Quantfest or Codefest and reach the top 50 ranks, after which HFT firms will shortlist you for internships. They cite 25+ finalists and regionalists getting job and intern offers this year.

## Background

HFT means high-frequency trading, where firms trade very fast using software. These firms recruit through competitions that test coding and quantitative skills. The statistics are the author's own claim.

## What I took away

- Competitions can be a direct recruiting route into quant firms.
- Check the figures with the event organizers.
