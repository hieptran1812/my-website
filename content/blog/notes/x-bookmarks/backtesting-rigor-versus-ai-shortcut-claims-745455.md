## Summary

The author says quant funds spend $25,000 a month and 3 weeks before a strategy goes live, with out-of-sample validation, Monte Carlo over 10,000 scenarios and clean tick data. They say this is the standard, not paranoia. The quoted post claims AI will kill manual backtesting within 18 months and that Horizon cut 3 weeks to 2 minutes.

## Background

Out-of-sample validation tests a strategy on data it was not tuned on, to avoid overfitting. Fast tools can make weak testing look rigorous. Both posts are opinion.

## What I took away

- Skipping validation invites overfitting.
- Speed claims need the same rigor as slow methods.
- Not trading advice.
