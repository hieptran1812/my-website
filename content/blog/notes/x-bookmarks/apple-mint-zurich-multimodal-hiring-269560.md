## Summary

The author announces hiring for MINT-Zurich at Apple, interns and full-time, to build multimodal foundation models. Research spans pre-training, post-training, agents and world models. Applicants should email a CV and relevant work and apply on the Apple jobs page.

## Background

Multimodal foundation models handle text, images and more in one system. This is a job post, useful for people in the field.

## What I took away

- Open roles in multimodal research are available.
- Include relevant work with a CV.
