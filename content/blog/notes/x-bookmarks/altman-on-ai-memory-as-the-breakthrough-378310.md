## Summary

The author promotes his blog as a way to see the future five months earlier. The quoted post says Sam Altman believes the real breakthrough will be total memory: an AI remembering every conversation, email and document in a person's life and finding patterns people never express.

## Background

Long-term memory would let assistants personalize responses using a lifetime of data. It raises privacy and storage concerns. The Altman claim is secondhand in the quoted post.

## What I took away

- Memory is seen as a key product feature for assistants.
- Persistent memory raises privacy questions.
- Check the original source of the Altman quote.
