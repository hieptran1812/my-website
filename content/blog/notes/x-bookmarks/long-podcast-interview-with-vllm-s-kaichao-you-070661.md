## Summary

The author is excited about a podcast featuring **Kaichao You**. The linked episode is a 3-hour Chinese-language interview covering open source infrastructure and model co-design. Its title includes a quote saying that if vLLM failed, they would regret it for life.

## Background

Kaichao You is a core contributor to vLLM, the open source LLM serving engine. Model co-design means designing model architecture together with the inference system so both work well together. Long interviews often reveal design reasoning not found in docs.

## What I took away

- Listen for how infra builders think about model and system co-design.
- Chinese-language podcasts are a source of insight on the open model ecosystem.
- Open source infra success depends on community and persistence.
