## Summary

@prz_chojecki says the Apodex agent is impressive, with up to 200 research steps, good at retrieval and IMO-level math, and free for now, though he has not tested it properly.

## Background

Research agents plan and search over many steps. Free access may be temporary.

## What I took away

- Test agents on your own tasks.
- Early impressions are not benchmarks.
