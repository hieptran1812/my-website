## Summary

The author introduces Situation Monitor, a dashboard with a global activity monitor, a TBPN livestream, a tech, finance and politics news feed, stocks and crypto, Polymarket predictions and a tech layoffs tracker. It also has AI race news, a Fed printer indicator and a Venezuela and Greenland section.

## Background

A monitor dashboard gathers many live sources on one screen. Polymarket is a prediction market where people bet on event outcomes. Such pages are quick to build and host as static sites.

## What I took away

- Aggregating feeds on one page can save time.
- Check the quality and sources of each panel yourself.
- Static pages on GitHub Pages are an easy way to share tools.
