## Summary

The authors say they have sat on Ethena's risk committee for over 18 months and watched it scale and adapt its strategy. They claim Ethena is no longer a tokenized delta-neutral strategy, and that even crypto-native people still hold that false belief. The post is cut off after mentioning a great insight by someone else.

## Background

A delta-neutral strategy holds offsetting positions so price moves cancel out. A tokenized version packages that strategy as a crypto token. The point is that labels about a protocol can be out of date. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Protocol descriptions can go stale as strategies change.
- Prefer primary sources and recent disclosures when assessing crypto products.
- Claims here come from the authors' own committee role.
