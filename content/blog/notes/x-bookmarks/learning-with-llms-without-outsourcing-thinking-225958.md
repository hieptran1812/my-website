## Summary

@jwuphysics shares a new blog post asking what learning looks like in the era of LLMs. It asks what happens when we are overconfident in our understanding due to AI sycophancy, and whether LLMs can help education without outsourcing our thinking.

## Background

Sycophancy means a model agrees with you or flatters you instead of correcting you. If a learner trusts it, they may think they understand something they do not. The post explores how to use LLMs without losing the effort that learning needs.

## What I took away

- Ask an LLM to challenge you, not just to agree.
- Test your understanding without the model.
- Learning needs effort that you should not outsource.
