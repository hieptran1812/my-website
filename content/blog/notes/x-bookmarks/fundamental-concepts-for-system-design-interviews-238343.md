## Summary

The author, who says they are a principal engineer who passed system design rounds at Amazon, Atlassian, Walmart, Salesforce and Deliveroo, says learning system design is not hard. They list fundamental concepts with links, starting with load balancing and CDNs (the list is cut off).

## Background

System design interviews test how you would build large, reliable services. Load balancing spreads requests across servers, and a CDN (content delivery network) caches content close to users to cut latency.

## What I took away

- Build system design skill from basic building blocks.
- Learn why each component exists, not only its name.
- These same ideas apply to LLM serving systems.
