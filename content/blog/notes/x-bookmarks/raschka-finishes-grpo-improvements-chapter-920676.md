## Summary

Raschka says he finished Ch07 on Improving GRPO. It builds on the GRPO intro and adds clipped policy ratios, a KL term, format rewards and a couple of other improvements, with a notebook on GitHub.

## Background

GRPO is a reinforcement learning method for training reasoning in LLMs. A KL term keeps the model near its original behavior. Clipping limits large updates.

## What I took away

- Work through the notebook to learn GRPO.
- Clipping and KL stabilize training.
