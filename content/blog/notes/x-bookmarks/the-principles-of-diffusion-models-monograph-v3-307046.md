## Summary

The authors released v3 of **The Principles of Diffusion Models** on arXiv ahead of its MIT Press publication in 2027. New content includes a Road Ahead chapter on discrete diffusion, major restructuring including a chapter on diffusion solvers, and reader guides. The quoted original post presents it as a systematic, fundamental reference so readers do not need to keep going back to original papers.

## Background

Diffusion models generate data by learning to reverse a gradual noising process. Solvers are numerical methods for running that reverse process in fewer steps. Discrete diffusion applies the idea to tokens like text instead of continuous pixels.

## What I took away

- A single monograph can save time versus reading scattered original papers.
- Discrete diffusion for text is a growing research direction.
- Solver choice strongly affects diffusion speed and quality.
