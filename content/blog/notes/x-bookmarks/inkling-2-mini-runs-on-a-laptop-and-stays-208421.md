## Summary

The author says Inkling 2 Mini is a looped transformer that runs on a laptop and that looping lets a model think more and write less. At this scale its thought process remains monitorable, though this may not stay true. They also share a variable-loop research model. The quote describes a 26B model scoring 20.1 on AA Index.

## Background

Looping lets reasoning happen inside the network instead of in written chain of thought. That makes it harder to read what the model is doing, which is a safety concern. Researchers want models whose reasoning can be monitored.

## What I took away

- Less written reasoning can reduce monitorability.
- The authors warn the property may not persist.
- A variable-loop model is shared for study.
