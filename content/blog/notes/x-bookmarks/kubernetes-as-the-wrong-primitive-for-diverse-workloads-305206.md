## Background

Kubernetes is a widely used system for scheduling and running containers across a cluster. A primitive here means the basic building block you design your infrastructure around. AI workloads like training, batch jobs and low-latency inference have very different needs, which some argue fit poorly into one container scheduler.

## What I took away

- Open the link to read the actual argument before forming a view.
- Ask whether your scheduling layer matches the shape of your workloads.
- One-size-fits-all infrastructure can be a hidden cost for mixed AI workloads.
