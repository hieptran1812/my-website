## Summary

The author says he cannot do what was asked because he is not a financial advisor and cannot give financial advice. He has thought about raising a $100M fund and running his algos for investors, which he says would be the only way to use strategies for others. The quoted post suggests he build a ready-to-use trading app.

## Background

Rules on investment advice differ by country, and sharing signals can create legal liability. A fund is a regulated route to manage other people's money.

## What I took away

- Giving signals can have legal implications.
- Productizing a strategy needs legal structure.
