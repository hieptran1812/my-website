## Summary

The post says backtesting got cooked: you can test simple buy-and-hold, trend following, asset allocation and intraday strategies without writing strategy code. It is powered by OpenAlgo and VectorBT. The text is cut off.

## Background

Backtesting runs a strategy on past data to see how it would have done. It is easy to overfit, meaning to tune to the past so it fails live.

## What I took away

- Easy backtesting makes overfitting easier too.
