## Summary

The author says he has struggled with the noise in LLM evaluations and did a deep dive into practical, applied statistics for evals. He will publish his learnings on his blog the next morning.

## Background

Eval scores vary with sampling, small test sets and prompt choices. Statistics such as confidence intervals tell you whether a score difference is real or just noise.

## What I took away

- Report uncertainty, not only a single eval score.
- Small differences between models may be noise.
- Look for the full blog writeup.
