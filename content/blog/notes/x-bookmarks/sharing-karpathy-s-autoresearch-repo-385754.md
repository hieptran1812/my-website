## Summary

The author highlights a new drop from Karpathy. The quoted post says autoresearch was packaged into a self-contained minimal repo, based on the nanochat training core, as a single-GPU, one-file version of about 630 lines.

## Background

Autoresearch is about letting an AI agent iterate on training experiments. A tiny repo lowers the barrier to try it. See the original post for the exact workflow.

## What I took away

- Small self-contained repos are the easiest way to learn new ideas.
- Check the original post for details on the loop.
