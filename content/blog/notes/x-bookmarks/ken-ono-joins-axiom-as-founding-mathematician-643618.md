## Summary

Ken Ono says he has joined Axiom as Founding Mathematician, working with his former student Carina Hong, and mentions an article on AI for mathematical discovery in the WSJ. The quoted post from Axiom says AxiomProver keeps getting stronger and that Ono left his tenured position at UVA to help build an AI mathematician as employee number 15.

## Background

AI for math means systems that find or verify proofs, often with formal proof assistants. Axiom is a company working on this. A senior academic moving to a startup shows how seriously the field is taken.

## What I took away

- AI mathematics is attracting senior researchers.
- Watch AxiomProver for progress claims.
- Academia to startup moves signal where talent is going.
