## Summary

The author says a German lab appeared from nowhere with a state-of-the-art open-weight model trained from scratch that beats Qwen, Mistral and Nemotron models of similar active size. The quoted post announces Kolibri: 78B parameters, 3.46B active, up to 1M tokens of context, built in Europe, released under Apache 2.0.

## Background

A mixture-of-experts model with very few active parameters per token can run fast on modest hardware while storing a lot of knowledge. Apache 2.0 is a permissive license allowing commercial use.

## What I took away

- Small active parameter counts make big MoE models cheap to serve.
- Aleph Alpha is an established lab, not new; the from nowhere framing is the poster's.
