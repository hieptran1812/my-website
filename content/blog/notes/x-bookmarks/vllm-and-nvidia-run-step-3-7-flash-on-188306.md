## Summary

Kaichao You says working with NVIDIA is pleasing, as the team helps validate vLLM across many hardware types, including datacenter GPUs and DGX Station. The quoted post says Step-3.7-Flash runs with vLLM on the DGX Station locally or as a NIM container.

## Background

vLLM is a popular open source engine for serving LLMs efficiently. DGX Station is NVIDIA's desktop AI workstation. Hardware validation means the engine is tested to work well on each chip.

## What I took away

- Open source inference engines depend on vendor collaboration.
- The same engine can run locally and in production via NIM.
- Check hardware support before picking a serving stack.
