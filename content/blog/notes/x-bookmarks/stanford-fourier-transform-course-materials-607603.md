## Summary

The post shares Stanford's course "The Fourier Transform and Its Applications" (EE261). It includes videos, problem sets and more, available online at the linked page.

## Background

The Fourier transform breaks a signal into the frequencies it contains. It is used in audio, images, signal processing and many parts of machine learning. A full course with problems is a structured way to learn it.

## What I took away

- Free university courses with problem sets are good for deep study.
- Fourier ideas show up in signal processing, audio and ML.
- Do the problem sets, not just the videos.
