## Summary

NVIDIA says it loves open source. The quoted post calls it the King of American Open-source AI, noting it crossed 1,000 public repositories on Hugging Face (820 models, 249 datasets and 57 spaces) with almost 60,000 followers, plus the top trending model LocateAnything and PiD at number five.

## Background

Hugging Face is the main hub for sharing models and datasets. Counts of repositories show how much NVIDIA is releasing openly.
