## Summary

The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative say that on November 20 Levent Alpoge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis as a Lean library of 17 million lines of code. They released a joint statement on its verification after a day of review.

## Background

Lean is a proof assistant, and Mathlib is its math library. A Lean proof is checked by a small trusted kernel, so correctness depends on the statement being right. The rest of the statement is not included in the text.

## What I took away

- Formal verification checks logic mechanically.
- The statement of the theorem still needs human review.
- Read the full statement at the source.
