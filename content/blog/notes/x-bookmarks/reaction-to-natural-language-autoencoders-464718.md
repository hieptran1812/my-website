## Summary

The author reacts with a single word, woah. The quoted Anthropic post says models like Claude talk in words but think in numbers, and that they trained Claude to translate its activations into human-readable text.

## Background

Activations are the numbers inside a network as it computes. Making them readable is a goal of interpretability research.

## What I took away

- Interpretability aims to make internal computation readable.
- Several researchers flagged this work as notable.
