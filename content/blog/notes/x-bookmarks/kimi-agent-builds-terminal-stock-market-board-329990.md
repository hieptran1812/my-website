## Summary

The Kimi product account says its agent made a Bloomberg-style stock market board in one shot. It shows stocks, price action and real-time context through terminal commands, and needs only a prompt. A demo link is included.

## Background

One-shot means the result comes from a single prompt without edits. Such demos show code generation and tool use, but quality depends on data sources and testing. This is a product demo, not financial advice.

## What I took away

- Agents can scaffold dashboards from a single prompt.
- Check where the data comes from and how fresh it is.
- Demos hide the failures, so try your own case.
