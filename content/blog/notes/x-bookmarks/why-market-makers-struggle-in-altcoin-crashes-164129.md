## Summary

The author's Attack on MM 2 asks why things go wrong right after buying altcoins. It also asks why market makers with large capital could not absorb selling during the 10/11 event. The quoted post is the same text in another translation.

## Background

Market makers post buy and sell quotes to provide liquidity. In a sharp crash, they may pull quotes or run out of risk capacity, so prices gap down. The post is analysis of order books, not advice.

## What I took away

- Liquidity can vanish in crashes even where market makers exist.
- Understand order book depth before trading thin altcoins.
