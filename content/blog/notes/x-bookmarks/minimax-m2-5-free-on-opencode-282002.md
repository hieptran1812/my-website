## Summary

Niels Rogge says MiniMax M2.5 is freely available in OpenCode. He calls it a good chance to try the power of open models for coding.

## Background

OpenCode is a coding agent tool that can connect to different models. Open models are models whose weights are released, so anyone can run or host them. Free access lowers the cost of comparing them with closed models.

## What I took away

- Try an open model in your coding agent to compare with your current one.
- Free access is a cheap way to test quality before paying.
