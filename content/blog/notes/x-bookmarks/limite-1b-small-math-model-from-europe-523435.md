## Summary

The quoted post introduces **Limite 1B - Violetto**, a model for high-frequency mathematical intelligence. The author says it reaches SOTA math from scratch on 300B tokens and that Europe can build very good small models that will grow.

## Background

Training from scratch means no starting from another model's weights. 300B tokens is a modest pretraining budget by current standards. Small specialized models can be cheap to run at high volume, which is what high-frequency usage implies.

## What I took away

- Focused data can make a small model strong in one domain.
- Small models are a practical path for teams with limited compute.
- Check which benchmarks the SOTA claim refers to.
