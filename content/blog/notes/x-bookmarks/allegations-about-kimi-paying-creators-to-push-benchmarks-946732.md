## Summary

@giadotai calls the so-called Kimi files possibly the biggest scandal in benchmaxxing history and asks for the list of involved creators to be released. The quoted post is from a creator who says they received campaign documents from Kimi AI after the Kimi K3.1 pro release, describing the narrative creators were asked to push, and says they refused because they only share opinions on things they tested.

## Background

Benchmaxxing means tuning or marketing a model to look strong on benchmarks rather than in real use. Paid creator campaigns can blur the line between honest reviews and advertising. These are allegations from the posts, not confirmed facts.

## What I took away

- Treat influencer model reviews with care, especially right after a launch.
- Test models on your own tasks before trusting public praise.
- Look for disclosure of sponsorship in reviews.
