## Summary

The quoted post says some AI capabilities are both helpful and dangerous, such as virology knowledge, which can help make vaccines or pathogens. It introduces GRAM, a training method that puts dual-use capabilities into removable modules. Anthropic says it collaborated with AE Studio on this research.

## Background

Dual-use means a capability can serve both good and harmful ends. Normally such knowledge is spread across all weights, so it is hard to remove. Isolating it in a separate module would let a provider switch it off for general users and on for trusted ones.

## What I took away

- Modular training can make capability removal cleaner than post-hoc filtering.
- Access control at the weight level is an alternative to refusal training.
- Read the research for how well removal holds up.
