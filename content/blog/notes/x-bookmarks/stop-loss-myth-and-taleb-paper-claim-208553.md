## Summary

The author says three years ago they wrote a lesson called **The Stop Loss Myth**, which uses probability distributions to argue against the classical view of static stop losses. They say Nassim Taleb is now following with a paper, linking to his post saying a new paper is coming.

## Background

A static stop loss sells when price falls to a fixed level. Critics say it can lock in losses when prices are noisy. The link to Taleb's paper is the author's assumption, since his post gives no topic.

## What I took away

- Question simple trading rules by looking at the distribution of outcomes.
- Do not assume a teaser paper supports your view.
- Educational content, not advice.
