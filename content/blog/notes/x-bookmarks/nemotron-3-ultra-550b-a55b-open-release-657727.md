## Summary

The author announces Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B-A55B), Nvidia's strongest open-weight model and full training recipe so far. It emphasizes real-world inference efficiency for long-context agentic workloads. Base, post-trained and reward checkpoints plus NVFP4 quantized versions are all open.

## Background

550B-A55B suggests 550 billion total parameters with 55 billion active per token, as in mixture-of-experts models. Releasing reward checkpoints and the recipe is unusual and useful for researchers.

## What I took away

- Open release includes base, post-trained, reward and NVFP4 weights.
- Training recipes help others reproduce results.
