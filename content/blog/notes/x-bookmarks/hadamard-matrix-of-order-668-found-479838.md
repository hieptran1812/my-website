## Summary

The author announces that a Hadamard matrix of order 668 has been found by a researcher, resolving the smallest unresolved case of the Hadamard conjecture after about 21 years. They note they had previously tested GPT 5.6 Sol Max Reasoning and Claude Fable on this problem as part of HorizonMath. The quoted post is a long string of plus and minus signs, representing the matrix.

## Background

A Hadamard matrix is a square matrix of +1 and -1 entries whose rows are all mutually orthogonal. The Hadamard conjecture says one exists for every order that is a multiple of 4, and 668 was the smallest such order where none was known. Such matrices are used in coding theory and signal processing, and Hadamard transforms also appear in LLM quantization.
