## Summary

@gdb says it took 6 days and $40,000 of compute with GPT-5.6 Sol to find a critical Chrome vulnerability, earning Google's first full-chain reward of 2026 for a bug affecting an estimated 1 billion users, and that this shows the opportunity for defenders. The quoted XBOW post says Google awarded a $250,000 bounty for a full-chain exploit, only the second time in Chrome's history, and tells the story of the challenge set at the start of 2026.

## Background

A full-chain exploit links several vulnerabilities to go from a web page to full control, escaping the browser sandbox. Bug bounties pay researchers who report such bugs responsibly. AI agents are now able to do parts of this work.

## What I took away

- AI can find serious security bugs at a cost far below a bounty payout.
- Defenders should adopt the same tools attackers will use.
- Budget compute for security testing like any other engineering cost.
