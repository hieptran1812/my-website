## Summary

MiniMax says this marks the end of the M2 series and MiniMax-M3 is coming. The quoted post says they consolidated the work behind M2 into an arXiv paper, just over six months after first open sourcing M2 on December 23 last year, and that many of their ideas and systems have been broadly adopted.

## Background

Publishing a consolidated paper documents the design choices for others. Model series are numbered releases, and M3 would be the next generation. No M3 details are given.

## What I took away

- Read the M2 paper for practical lessons from a full model series.
- A new generation is announced without details.
- Open-sourcing leads to ideas being adopted widely.
