## Summary

Fei-Fei Li celebrates her team at World Labs releasing **Atlas**, described as a first-of-its-kind multimodal world model trained from scratch. The quoted announcement says Atlas generates image and video frames with pixel-perfect camera control and reconstructs them in 3D, letting users model the world, move the camera and simulate space and time.

## Background

A world model learns how a scene looks and changes so it can generate consistent views and predict what happens next. Camera control means you can choose the viewpoint precisely, and 3D reconstruction turns generated frames into geometry. These matter for simulation, games and robotics.

## What I took away

- World models combine generation with 3D consistency, not just pretty frames.
- Precise camera control is a key feature for using generated scenes in real tools.
- The first-of-its-kind claim is the company's own framing.
