## Summary

Jianlin Su shares a blog post titled Deconstructing Scaling Laws: The Triad of Optimization, Architecture, and Data. It is hosted on his site kexue.fm. The post text gives only the title.

## Background

Scaling laws describe how model loss improves as you add compute, data or parameters, usually following a power law. Breaking them into optimization, architecture and data asks which factor drives each part of the curve. Jianlin Su is known for technical posts such as the RoPE positional encoding work.

## What I took away

- Scaling curves reflect choices in optimizer, model design and data, not just size.
- Read the post for a structured way to think about scaling.
- Changing one factor can shift the whole scaling curve.
