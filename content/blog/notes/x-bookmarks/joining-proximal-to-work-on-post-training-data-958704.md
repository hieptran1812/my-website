## Summary

The author invites people interested in post-training, data research or large-scale infra to reach out. The quoted post announces they joined Proximal in San Francisco, saying there are many open problems in data and post-training.

## Background

Post-training data, such as preference pairs and RL environments, is a major driver of model quality. Companies focusing on it are a growing career area.

## What I took away

- Data research is a distinct and growing role in AI.
- Reach out directly to researchers if a team interests you.
