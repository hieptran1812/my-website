## Summary

The author, describing a Polymarket quant, says to stop vibe-coding Polymarket bots until you learn three main prediction-market formulas. The first is the Avellaneda-Stoikov optimal spread, described as the foundation framework of modern market making. The rest of the thread is cut off.

## Background

Avellaneda-Stoikov is a classic market-making model that sets bid and ask quotes around a reference price, adjusting for inventory risk. Market makers earn the spread but carry the risk of holding positions.

## What I took away

- Learn market-making basics before automating a trading bot.
- Avellaneda-Stoikov is a good starting paper.
- Educational only, not advice.
