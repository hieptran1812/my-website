## Summary

The author says quantum hype disappeared once agents emerged and was once confused with quantitative research. He points to a paper using GA plus QAOA whose best backtest result has a Sharpe of a low value (cut off in the text).

## Background

QAOA is a quantum optimization algorithm and GA is a genetic algorithm. Sharpe ratio measures return per unit of risk.

## What I took away

- Quantum finance hype often outruns results.
- Sharpe ratio shows risk-adjusted performance.
- Read the paper for the exact figures.
