## Summary

PaddlePaddle announces the official release of **PaddleOCR-VL 1.6**. It claims a new state-of-the-art 96.33% on OmniDocBench, outperforming open-source and proprietary solutions in text, formula and table recognition. The text is cut off.

## Background

OCR-VL models read documents with a vision-language model, handling text, formulas and tables. OmniDocBench is a benchmark for document parsing. The record is the vendor's claim.

## What I took away

- Document parsing models are improving quickly.
- Benchmark scores may not transfer to your documents.
- Test on tables and formulas if those matter to you.
