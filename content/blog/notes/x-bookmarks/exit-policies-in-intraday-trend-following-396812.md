## Summary

The author recommends Concretum as a must-follow source of practical quant research. The quoted post says intraday trend-following is often presented as if only the entry signal matters. It argues that the exit policy is a less discussed source of performance differences, and begins an ATR-based study.

## Background

Trend following means buying strength and selling weakness. ATR (average true range) is a common measure of recent price movement, often used to place stops. The idea is educational: how you exit a trade can change results as much as how you enter.

## What I took away

- When testing a strategy, evaluate the exit rule separately from the entry rule.
- Volatility-based measures like ATR are common for sizing exits.
- This is research discussion, not a trading recommendation.
