## Summary

The author calls a new app named rent a human weird. Humans create profiles with skills, location and ratings, agents find them via MCP or API and give instructions, humans do tasks in person and get paid instantly in stablecoins.

## Background

MCP is a protocol that lets AI agents call external tools. Here the human worker becomes the tool for physical-world actions. Stablecoins are crypto tokens tied to a currency, which allow fast payments.

## What I took away

- Agents may increasingly delegate physical tasks to people.
- Think about safety and accountability when agents give instructions.
- The post describes the idea, not a verified service.
