## Summary

The author says prompt engineering is officially outdated. He says Anthropic released The Complete Guide to Building Skills for Claude, a 30+ page deep dive that shifts the conversation away from prompt engineering.

## Background

Skills are packaged instructions and files an agent loads when relevant. They reuse know-how across tasks. The claim that prompt engineering is outdated is the author's framing.

## What I took away

- Package repeatable workflows as skills.
- Read the official guide for details.
