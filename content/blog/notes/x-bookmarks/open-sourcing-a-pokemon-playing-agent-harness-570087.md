## Summary

Clad3815 says that after nearly a year of building Pokemon-playing agents, he is open-sourcing everything. He claims GPT-5.2 beat Pokemon FireRed from start to finish, fully autonomous, with no human input, and releases the whole harness.

## Background

A harness is the code around a model that gives it observations, tools, memory and loops. Games are good tests of long-horizon agents. The result is the author's claim, and the harness is the reusable part.

## What I took away

- The harness matters as much as the model for long tasks.
- Study open harnesses to learn memory and planning patterns.
