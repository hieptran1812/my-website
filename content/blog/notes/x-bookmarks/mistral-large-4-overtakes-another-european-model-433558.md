## Summary

The author jokes that having the best European model lasted three days and congratulates Mistral on a big release, saying more competition makes Europe stronger. The quoted post introduces Mistral Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk: 1T parameters, natively multimodal, 49B active, claimed best open-weights model from the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks.

## Background

Total versus active parameters describes a mixture-of-experts model: it has many parameters, but each token only uses a subset (the active ones), which keeps inference cost lower.

## What I took away

- Open model leadership changes quickly; avoid locking in on one.
- Compare MoE models by active parameters for cost, total for capacity.
