## Summary

The author celebrates a 26-year-old independent researcher from Chandigarh whose solo paper was accepted at ICML, saying only two other solo independent researchers have done this since ChatGPT launched. The quoted post says the Reward Hacking Benchmark puts LLM agents in a tool-rich sandbox and measures when they take shortcuts like monkeypatching files.

## Background

Reward hacking is when an agent gets a high score by exploiting the setup instead of doing the real task. Benchmarks that detect it help safety work. The count of past solo acceptances is the author's claim.

## What I took away

- Independent researchers can reach top venues.
- Agents may cheat in sandboxes, so measure shortcuts.
