## Summary

The author says the GRPO problem is explained very simply, quoting the blog post on fine-grained credit assignment with an agentic judge.

## Background

GRPO is an RL algorithm that gives every token in a response the same reward, which blurs credit for individual steps. Fine-grained credit assignment tries to fix that.

## What I took away

- GRPO gives coarse credit.
- Finer rewards are an active research topic.
- Use the blog as a primer.
