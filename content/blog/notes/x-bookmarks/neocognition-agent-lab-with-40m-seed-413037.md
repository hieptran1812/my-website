## Summary

The author introduces **NeoCognition**, an agent lab for specialized intelligence. It says human expertise does not scale. Backed by $40M seed funding, it builds self-learning agents that specialize across domains.

## Background

A seed round is early funding for a new company. Self-learning agents are meant to improve in a domain through experience instead of fixed training. The goal is to make expert-level help cheap and common.

## What I took away

- Specialized, self-improving agents are a funded direction.
- Funding does not prove results.
- Watch for papers or demos from this lab.
