## Summary

Prajwal Tomar claims AI can design and that Cursor with Opus 4.5 built a landing page with scroll animations in under 10 minutes. The quoted post says he replicated a $5K scroll animation, and that Gemini 3 or Opus 4.5 is enough for shipping an MVP. These are his claims.

## Background

Scrollytelling ties animations to scrolling. Coding assistants can generate such front-end code quickly, but polish still takes review.

## What I took away

- Good workflow matters more than blaming AI.
- Check generated designs yourself.
