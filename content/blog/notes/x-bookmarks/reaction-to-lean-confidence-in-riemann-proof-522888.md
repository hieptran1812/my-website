## Summary

The author excitedly shares the joint statement in which the Lean and Mathlib communities say they have full confidence that Alpoge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is correct. The quoted post is the statement announcing the verification of the 17 million line library.

## Background

Lean and Mathlib are a proof checker and its math library. Their confidence means the formal proof was checked. The post is mostly a reaction.

## What I took away

- The key source is the joint statement.
- Enthusiasm is not evidence; read the statement.
- Formal proofs can change how math is verified.
