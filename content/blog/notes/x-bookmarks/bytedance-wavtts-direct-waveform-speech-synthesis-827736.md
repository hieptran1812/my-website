## Summary

The author says ByteDance's team proposed WavTTS, like a TTS version of WavFlow, generating audio waveforms directly, in the lineage of E2-TTS and F5-TTS. The quoted post gives the paper title, WavTTS: Towards High-Quality Zero-Shot TTS via Direct Raw Waveform Modeling.

## Background

TTS is text-to-speech. Zero-shot means cloning a voice from a short sample without training on it. Most systems produce spectrograms first, so direct waveform output removes a stage.

## What I took away

- Direct waveform modeling skips intermediate features.
- Read the arXiv paper for results.
