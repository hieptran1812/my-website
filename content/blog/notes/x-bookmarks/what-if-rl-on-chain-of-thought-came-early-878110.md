## Summary

@hughbzhang asks what if we had known o1-style RL on chain of thought early, and reports SFT plus a bit of RL on GPT-2 almost matches fine-tuned GPT-3 12b on GSM8K, which used >100x the pretraining compute.

## Background

Chain-of-thought RL rewards correct reasoning steps. Small models gain a lot.

## What I took away

- Small models plus RL can rival much larger ones.
- Algorithms matter as much as scale.
