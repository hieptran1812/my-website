## Summary

The author says their note-taking system helped them write 4 papers in the first 2 years of their PhD. The linked article describes an academic system built around sources, topics and arguments.

## Background

Researchers read many papers, and notes that are only summaries are hard to reuse when writing. Organizing notes by source, then by topic, then by argument makes it easier to turn reading into a paper. This is the author's claim about their own workflow, not a tested result.

## What I took away

- Separate notes about a source from notes about a topic and notes about an argument.
- Structure your notes so they can be reused directly when writing.
- Treat the paper count as one person's experience, not a guarantee.
