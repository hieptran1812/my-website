## Summary

The author says a friend put 50 quant formulas into Claude and built a risk calculator in one prompt. His first discovery was that his profitable strategy was bleeding 12% a year to variance drag; he had been up 30% one month and down 30% the next. The numbers are the author's anecdote.

## Background

Variance drag means volatile returns compound to less than their average. Up 30% then down 30% leaves you about 9% down, not flat. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Arithmetic average returns overstate compounded growth.
- Up 30% then down 30% does not return to zero.
- Lower volatility helps long-term compounding.
