## Summary

Elon Musk replies 'You really will. It's awesome.' to a post saying try it, you'll like it. The quoted author says a 4 hour demo drive in a new Model Y left them buzzing, and they did not understand FSD until experiencing it.

## Background

FSD is Tesla's driver-assist software. The post is anecdote and promotion from the company's CEO.

## What I took away

- Anecdotes are not safety data.
- Check independent evaluations of driver-assist.
