## Summary

The author announces applications for the Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon for people building agents that move money. It offers Airwallex APIs, $100K in prizes and credits, and a demo day in San Francisco.

## Background

Agentic banking means AI agents that can make payments or financial operations through APIs. This raises hard questions about authorization, limits and fraud prevention.

## What I took away

- Agents that move money need strict permissions and audit trails.
- Hackathons are a low-cost way to try new APIs.
