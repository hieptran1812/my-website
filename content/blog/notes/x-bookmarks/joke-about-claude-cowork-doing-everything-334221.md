## Summary

The author jokes that after installing Claude Cowork it freed 14GB, sped up boot, doubled battery life, cleared the inbox and filed taxes. The quoted post is the announcement of Cowork, described as Claude Code for the rest of your work and for non-technical tasks. The list is satire.

## Background

Cowork brings the coding-agent workflow to general desktop tasks. The exaggeration pokes fun at overstated agent hype.

## What I took away

- Read hype lists with humor and skepticism.
- Cowork aims at non-technical tasks.
