## Summary

The post advertises Serus, which claims to scan more than 100 billion dark web records to find leaked passwords, IP addresses, phone numbers and emails about you in seconds. It offers a free trial.

## Background

Breach-check services compare your details against leaked databases. Entering your personal data into any such tool carries its own privacy risk.

## What I took away

- Check a leak service's reputation before entering personal data.
- Use a password manager and unique passwords regardless.
