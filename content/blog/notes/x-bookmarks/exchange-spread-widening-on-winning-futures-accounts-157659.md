## Summary

The author says futures trading on an exchange feels like a casino. After winning a lot, they claim the exchange automatically widens the spread on their account. They say the exchange's business contact told them to open another account, and that even a 10 USDT trade now gets about a 1% spread. They add that the feature is called spread control.

## Background

The spread is the gap between the buy and sell price, and it acts as a hidden trading cost. A wider spread makes every trade more expensive. This is one person's claim about one exchange and I cannot verify it from the post.

## What I took away

- Check the real spread and fees on an exchange before sizing trades.
- Treat single-user claims about exchange behavior as unverified.
- Hidden costs like spread can matter more than the visible fee.
