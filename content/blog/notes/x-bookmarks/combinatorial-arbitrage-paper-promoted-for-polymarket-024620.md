## Summary

@bored2boar calls a paper a "$40M blueprint" for Polymarket builders and says it is a must for any vibe coder. The post claims combinatorial arbitrage turns mispricing into profit and that readers can use the loophole too. It links an arXiv paper.

## Background

Arbitrage means profiting from price differences between related markets. Combinatorial arbitrage looks at markets whose outcomes are logically linked, so their prices should be consistent. Real opportunities are usually small and quickly competed away, and the profit claims here come only from the post.

## What I took away

- Read the paper itself, not the promotional framing.
- Dollar claims in social posts are unverified.
- Prediction markets carry real risk; this is not advice.
