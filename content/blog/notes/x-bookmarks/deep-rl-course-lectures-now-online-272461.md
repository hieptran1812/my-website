## Summary

@chelseabfinn says all of her Deep RL course lecture videos from Spring 2025 are now online, and links a YouTube playlist.

## Background

Reinforcement learning (RL) trains an agent by trial and error using rewards. Deep RL uses neural networks inside that loop. A full university course on video is a structured way to learn the field.

## What I took away

- A full, free university course on deep RL is available as a playlist.
- Pair video lectures with small coding exercises to retain the material.
