## Summary

Maxime Labonne says he is proud of the release and calls it the first step towards agents running on device. He says the team learned a lot post-training the model and to stay tuned. The quote announces LFM2.5-8B-A1B, an 8B MoE with 1.5B active, 128K context, for phones, laptops and robots.

## Background

On-device agents run without a cloud server, which helps privacy and latency. Post-training is the stage after pretraining where the model learns to follow instructions and use tools.

## What I took away

- Post-training lessons for the model are promised later.
- On-device agents need small, efficient MoE models.
- Watch for the team's write-up.
