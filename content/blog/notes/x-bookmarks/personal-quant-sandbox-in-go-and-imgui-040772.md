## Summary

The author is mapping out standalone tools to practice Go and imgui architecture and to learn market mechanics, as a personal quant sandbox. The goal is to merge the best modules into a personal terminal. He asks which of the tools people would want open sourced.

## Background

imgui is an immediate-mode GUI library that is popular for fast, tool-like interfaces. Building small separate modules helps learn both software design and market structure. Merging them later into one terminal is a common approach.

## What I took away

- Small standalone modules are a good way to learn a stack.
- Build tools to understand market mechanics, not only to trade.
- Ask your audience which parts are worth open sourcing.
