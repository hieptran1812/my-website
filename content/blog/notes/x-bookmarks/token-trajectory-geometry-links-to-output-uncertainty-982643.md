## Summary

The author announces ICML 2026 work asking how uncertainty in LLM output shows up in internal representations. They report that the shape of internal token trajectories provides a direct geometric link to behavioral uncertainty, measured as output entropy. A thread follows.

## Background

Output entropy measures how spread out a model's next-token probabilities are. Linking it to internal geometry could give cheap signals of when a model is unsure.

## What I took away

- Internal geometry may reveal uncertainty before the output.
- This could help detect likely hallucinations.
