## Summary

@TraderJonesy says every trader wants to win but few learn the lesson that finally made them profitable. They claim most traders lose money for years because they trade without a foundation and jump between ideas, indicators and strategies. The post is cut off before the lesson itself.

## Background

This is a common pattern in trading content: a story about a hidden lesson. The text gives no concrete method, and it is personal opinion. It is not advice.

## What I took away

- Switching strategies constantly is a commonly cited trader mistake.
- Be skeptical of posts that tease a secret lesson without evidence.
