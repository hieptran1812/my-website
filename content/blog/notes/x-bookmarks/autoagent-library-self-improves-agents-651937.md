## Summary

The author introduces **AutoAgent**, which lets an agent improve another agent on any domain. After 24 hours of optimization it claims #1 on SpreadsheetBench (96.5%) and the top GPT-5 score on TerminalBench (55.1%), while other entries were human-engineered.

## Background

Agent performance depends on prompts, tools and loops, not just the model. Automatic optimization searches over those parts. Benchmark results are the author's claims.

## What I took away

- Agent harnesses can be optimized automatically.
- Compare with human-built baselines on the same benchmark.
- Check for overfitting to a benchmark.
