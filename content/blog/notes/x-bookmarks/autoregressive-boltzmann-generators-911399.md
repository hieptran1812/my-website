## Summary

@AlexanderTong7 introduces ArBGs, framing Boltzmann Generation as autoregressive next-token prediction. The quoted thread says that by discretizing space into bins, ArBGs generate equilibrium peptide structures atom by atom.

## Background

Boltzmann generators sample molecular structures with correct physical probabilities. Using a language-model style approach replaces continuous flows or diffusion.

## What I took away

- Discrete tokens can replace continuous generation.
- Autoregressive modeling applies to science.
