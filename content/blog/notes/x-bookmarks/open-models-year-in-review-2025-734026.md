## Summary

@natolambert shares an open models year in review with an updated builder tier list, top models of the year and 2026 predictions. The first winners listed are DeepSeek R1, which he says transformed the AI world, and the Qwen 3 family.

## Background

Open models are those whose weights anyone can download and run. A yearly review helps engineers see which labs are releasing useful models. The tier list reflects the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- DeepSeek R1 and Qwen 3 are named as leading open models of 2025.
- Year-in-review posts are a quick way to catch up on the field.
- Tier lists are opinions; test models on your own tasks.
