## Summary

The quoted post is Josh Engels explaining he left DeepMind's AGI safety team for METR. Neel Nanda, his former manager, says he enjoyed managing him and was sad to lose him but thinks it is the right move. He argues talented safety experts are needed to hold AGI labs to account.

## Background

METR evaluates frontier models from outside. A manager publicly backing a departure signals that even lab insiders value strong external oversight.

## What I took away

- Independent oversight of labs is seen as important even by people inside them.
- Good managers can support people leaving for the right reasons.
