## Summary

The author says inference is the main cost driver in AI. Revenue grows fast but costs grow faster, labs operate at heavy losses, and inference is 70%+ of total cost. The quoted post says inference gets 10x cheaper but usage rises 30x, driven by harnesses like OpenClaw and Hermes.

## Background

Inference is running a trained model for users. Training is a one-time cost, while inference scales with usage. Cheaper tokens can still raise total spend if people use far more.

## What I took away

- Cost per token and total spend differ.
- Agents burn many tokens.
- Figures are the authors' claims.
