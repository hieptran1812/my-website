## Summary

Google Research introduces **TimesFM-3**, a time series foundation model that does multivariate forecasting in a single forward pass. Google says it significantly outperforms other forecasting models across major benchmarks, with more details on its blog.

## Background

A time series foundation model is pretrained on many sequences of numbers over time so it can forecast new series without training from scratch. Multivariate means it forecasts several related variables together. A single forward pass means one run of the model produces the forecast, which is fast.

## What I took away

- Foundation models now cover forecasting, not just text and images.
- Try a pretrained forecaster as a baseline before building a custom model.
- Benchmark wins may not transfer to your own data, so test on it.
