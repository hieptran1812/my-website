## Summary

The author claims a study proved $40 million was extracted from Polymarket in one year using a single mathematical formula. They say they found a wallet using it on Iran war markets that made $1.4M in one week. The text is cut off and the claims are unverified.

## Background

Prediction markets can have mispricings, such as related outcomes whose prices do not add up. Posts like this are often hooks for paid content, and the figures cannot be checked here.

## What I took away

- Treat large profit claims with skepticism.
- Ask for the actual study and data.
- Not financial advice.
