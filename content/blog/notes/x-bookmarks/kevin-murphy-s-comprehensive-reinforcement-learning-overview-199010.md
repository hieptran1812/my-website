## Summary

The author calls this the most comprehensive RL overview they have seen, written by Kevin Murphy of Google DeepMind. They say it bridges classical RL with the modern LLM era, including a chapter dedicated to LLMs. The post text is cut off after that.

## Background

Reinforcement learning trains agents by reward. Modern LLM post-training uses RL to improve reasoning and behavior. A resource covering both old and new ideas helps connect the theory to current practice.

## What I took away

- Use it as a single reference from classical RL to LLM training.
- Read the LLM chapter if you care about post-training.
- Citation counts are the author's argument for credibility, not proof of quality.
