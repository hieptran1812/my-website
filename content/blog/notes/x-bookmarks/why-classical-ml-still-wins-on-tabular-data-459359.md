## Summary

The author says transformers are often better than classical ML models, but tabular ML is one field where classical ML still dominates most of the time. They begin to break down why, explaining tabular ML deals with CSV or tabular data. The text is cut off.

## Background

Tabular data is rows and columns such as customer records. Gradient boosted trees such as XGBoost are strong on it, and deep learning often does not beat them. This is a common practical lesson.

## What I took away

- Try gradient boosted trees first on tabular data.
- Bigger models are not always better.
- Pick the tool to match the data type.
