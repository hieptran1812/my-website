## Summary

The post says the secret to algorithmic trading is a scientific approach and points to a book that explains how it works with Python code.

## Background

A scientific approach means forming hypotheses and testing them carefully, including avoiding overfitting. The book is a learning resource, not a promise of profit.

## What I took away

- Test hypotheses rigorously and out of sample.
- Books with code help you practice.
- Educational only, not advice.
