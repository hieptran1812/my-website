## Summary

The author recommends a visual guide that starts from attention and GPT-2 and builds up to Kimi-K3. The quoted creator says they made a breakdown of how LLMs scaled since 2019 and a visual website to explain it, which took a lot of effort.

## Background

Modern LLMs add many changes on top of GPT-2, such as new attention variants, positional encodings and mixture of experts. A guided path through these changes helps engineers understand current models.

## What I took away

- Use this guide to learn architecture changes step by step.
- Understanding GPT-2 well makes newer models easier to follow.
