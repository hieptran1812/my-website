## Summary

The post lists parts of a Claude 4.6 prompt. First is Task: say what you want and what success looks like. The author says role prompts like act as a senior expert are over. Second is Context Files: upload files with your expertise and rules. The list is cut off.

## Background

Clear goals and success criteria help models produce usable results. Providing files gives grounded context. These are the author's recommendations.

## What I took away

- State the task and success criteria.
- Supply context files instead of role prompts.
- Test the template on your own work.
