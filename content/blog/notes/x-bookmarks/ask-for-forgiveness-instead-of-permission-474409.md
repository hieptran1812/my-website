## Summary

The post says only: always ask for forgiveness instead of permission. There is no further context in the text.

## Background

This is a common saying meaning it is often better to act first and apologize if needed than to wait for approval. It is usually applied to work and research culture.

## What I took away

- Bias toward action can speed progress, but judge the cost of mistakes first.
- This works best for reversible decisions, not risky or irreversible ones.
