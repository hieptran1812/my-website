## Summary

The author shares AutoScientists. The quoted post describes it as a decentralized team of AI agents for long-running scientific experimentation, powered by ClawInstitute. It contrasts with AI scientist agents that run a single reasoning thread or use a central planner, saying real research is not like that.

## Background

Most agent systems have one thread or a central coordinator. A decentralized design lets agents pick tasks and share results more like a real lab. It is a design claim that needs testing.

## What I took away

- Self-organizing agents are an alternative to central planners.
- Long-running experiments need persistence and coordination.
- Read the paper for evaluation results.
