## Summary

The author shares a blog on learnings after coding GPT and Llama from scratch. They say it contains almost everything they learned, including fundamental but subtle points to keep in mind. It is a 4 minute read with the link in the replies.

## Background

Implementing GPT and Llama yourself shows how the architectures differ in details such as normalization and position encoding. Small mistakes there can silently hurt results. Short lessons-learned posts are a fast way to avoid them.

## What I took away

- Reimplementing known models exposes subtle details.
- A short checklist of pitfalls is valuable before your own attempt.
- The link is in the replies, not the post.
