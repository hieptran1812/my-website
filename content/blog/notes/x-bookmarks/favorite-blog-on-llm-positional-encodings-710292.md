## Summary

@jino_rohit calls a linked item by far their favorite blog on positional encodings for LLMs. The post gives no other detail.

## Background

Transformers process tokens in parallel, so they need positional encodings to know word order. Methods include sinusoidal, learned and rotary encodings. Understanding them helps with long-context topics.

## What I took away

- Positional encodings tell a Transformer the order of tokens.
- Learn rotary embeddings to follow modern LLMs.
- Open the link for the specific blog.
