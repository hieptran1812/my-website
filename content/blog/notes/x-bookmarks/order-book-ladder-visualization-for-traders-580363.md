## Summary

Marketlens says two traders highlighted the Ladder/DOM view as their go-to for order book data. Their version aggregates across multiple exchanges and adds executed orders on timeframe rotation. They ask what else to add for pull and stack visualization.

## Background

A ladder or DOM (depth of market) lists resting buy and sell orders by price level. Pulling means orders being removed and stacking means orders being added, and both can hint at intent. This describes a tool, not trading advice.

## What I took away

- A ladder view is a common way to read order book depth.
- Aggregating exchanges gives a broader picture than one venue.
