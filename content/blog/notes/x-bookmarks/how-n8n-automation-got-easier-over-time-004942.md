## Summary

The author lists the evolution of n8n automation by year. In 2019 it launched, 2020 brought self-hosting guides taking 2 days, 2021 needed about 40 hours learning nodes, 2022 templates cut it to about 10 hours, and 2023 let ChatGPT debug errors. The 2024 and later entries are cut off.

## Background

n8n is a workflow automation tool where you connect steps (nodes) such as API calls and data transforms. The post argues AI assistants have steadily lowered the effort needed to build automations. The hour counts are the author's illustration, not measured data.

## What I took away

- Templates and AI debugging have lowered the barrier to automation.
- The time figures are rough, not measured.
- Starting from a template is faster than building from scratch.
