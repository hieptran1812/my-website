## Summary

@finkd announces Muse Spark Open, with weights releasing the same day. He says it runs well on new Mac Studios thanks to work with John Ternus and Apple.

## Background

Open weights let anyone download and run a model locally. Mac Studios have large unified memory shared by CPU and GPU, which makes them good for running big models at home. Hardware partnerships can bring specific optimizations.

## What I took away

- Large unified memory makes Macs practical for local LLMs.
- Check the license terms that come with open weights.
- Vendor collaborations can bring early optimizations for specific hardware.
