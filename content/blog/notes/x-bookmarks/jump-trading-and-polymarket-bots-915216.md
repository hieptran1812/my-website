## Summary

The author says Jump Trading, a large crypto HFT firm, joined Polymarket as a market maker a few days ago. Since then six-figure bots on 5 and 15-minute crypto markets increased. He hints at a link but says to figure it out yourself.

## Background

A market maker posts buy and sell quotes to provide liquidity. More liquidity can attract bots. The link is the author's suggestion and is not proven.

## What I took away

- Correlation is not causation.
- Professional market makers make thin edges harder for small bots.
