## Summary

The author claims TradingView is cooked because QuantCharts offers free footprint charts for stocks and crypto, runs Pine Script indicators without code changes, and includes CME data with paid plans. The quoted post is LuxAlgo's launch announcement.

## Background

Footprint charts show traded volume at each price inside a bar. Accounts with names tied to a product often post promotional content, so claims may be biased.

## What I took away

- Read hype posts as marketing and compare features yourself.
- Free data tiers may have limits not shown in posts.
