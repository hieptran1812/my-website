## Summary

@princeofprawns0 says many people talk about space compute but no one has broken down the physics, energy, cooling, economics and work involved. They built a first-principles model at astrocompute.dev.

## Background

Space compute means data centers in orbit. Cooling is hard because there is no air, so heat must be radiated away. A model lets you test assumptions about cost and feasibility.

## What I took away

- Cooling and power are the main limits of space compute.
- Try the model with your own assumptions.
- Check inputs before trusting any conclusion.
