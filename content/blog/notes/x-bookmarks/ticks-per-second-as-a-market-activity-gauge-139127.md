## Summary

The author calls TPS (ticks per second) a favorite tool, the heartbeat of the market. They say low TPS means quiet accumulation or decay, while spikes suggest sudden aggression, algo dumps or a liquidity hunt, and that this matters for scalping.

## Background

A tick is one price update or trade. Counting ticks per second gives a simple measure of how active the market is right now. It is a descriptive tool, not a prediction on its own.

## What I took away

- Activity measures can add context to price charts.
- Spikes do not say direction by themselves.
- Educational, not advice.
