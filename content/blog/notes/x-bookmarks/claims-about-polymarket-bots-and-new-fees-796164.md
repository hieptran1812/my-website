## Summary

The author claims the Polymarket bot era is over because temporal arbitrage is cooked, and that Polymarket quietly rolled out fees. They cite a bot that allegedly turned $313 into $414k in one month farming 15-minute BTC markets. The quoted post describes the bot as running one simple strategy repeatedly.

## Background

Arbitrage means profiting from price differences, and temporal arbitrage here exploits timing. Fees reduce or remove thin edges. Huge profit claims like this are unverified and often used to attract followers.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of dramatic profit claims; survivorship bias is common.
- Fees can erase strategies with thin margins.
- Do not treat such threads as advice.
