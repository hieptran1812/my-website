## Summary

@quantymacro jokes with a final-round HFT quant interview question about the probability that someone who says Sharpe is a lie trades, trades with Sharpe above 2, or has an MBA. The quoted post argues that Sharpe is one of finance's biggest lies and can mislead.

## Background

The Sharpe ratio is return divided by volatility, used to compare strategies. It has known limits, such as ignoring skew and tail risk. The joke implies the loudest critics may not be successful traders.

## What I took away

- Sharpe has limits but remains a standard first metric.
- Ignoring tails and skew can make strategies look better than they are.
- Be skeptical of both strong critics and strong defenders.
