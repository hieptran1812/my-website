## Summary

The author says Zai_org entered OCR with GLM-OCR on Hugging Face. It is 0.9B parameters, MIT licensed, uses the multimodal GLM-V architecture, and is claimed to rank first on OmniDocBench v1.5 with 94.62.

## Background

OCR means turning images of text or documents into machine-readable text. OmniDocBench is a benchmark for document parsing. A model under one billion parameters is cheap to run locally.

## What I took away

- Small specialized models can compete on document tasks.
- MIT license allows commercial use.
- Test it on your own documents, not only benchmarks.
