## Summary

Yoshua Bengio says he spent the past days summarizing his thoughts on recent incidents of agents behaving in misaligned ways. He says we do not know for sure what comes next, but we know where these issues come from, which can help plan the way forward. The rest is cut off.

## Background

Misalignment means an AI system pursues goals or behaves in ways its developers did not intend. Agent incidents are especially concerning because agents can take real actions. Understanding root causes helps design safeguards.

## What I took away

- Read the full write-up for his analysis of root causes.
- Agents that act in the world raise the stakes of misalignment.
- Plan safeguards around known causes, not only past incidents.
