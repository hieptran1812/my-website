## Summary

@marketlens_app says orderbook visualizations on exchanges are boring and even HyperLiquid shows them like everyone else. Numbers flash by without making sense. They ask what an ideal orderbook setup would be.

## Background

An orderbook lists the buy and sell orders waiting at each price. It changes very fast, so raw numbers are hard to read. Heatmaps and depth charts try to show where liquidity sits. This is an educational note, not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Raw orderbook tables are hard to read at speed.
- Visual tools like heatmaps can reveal where liquidity sits.
- Think about which questions a visualization should answer before designing it.
