## Summary

The author lists the best Polymarket tools in one place. Examples include Hashdive, a dashboard with lots of info, Polysights, which finds clusters of identical behavior patterns such as insider wallets, and Gondor, offering 2x leverage on bets. The list is cut off.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market, and its trades are public on-chain. Analysis tools mine that data for patterns. Leverage increases both gains and losses. This is not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Public on-chain data enables wallet analysis.
- Leverage greatly increases risk.
- Verify any tool before trusting it.
