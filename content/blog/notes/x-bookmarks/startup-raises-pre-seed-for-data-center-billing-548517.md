## Summary

@omaikasei announces @netbackyard has raised $4.5M pre-seed to build the financial backbone of the global compute economy, starting with billing for data centers. The author says $1.2B flows into compute every day, almost entirely debt-financed.

## Background

AI data centers rent out compute and need accurate usage billing. The post suggests that financial tooling for this market is immature. The figures are the founder's claims in an announcement.

## What I took away

- The AI compute boom creates demand for supporting business tools like billing.
- Funding announcements are promotional; check the figures independently.
