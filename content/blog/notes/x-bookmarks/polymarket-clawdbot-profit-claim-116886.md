## Summary

The post claims a leaked five-minute Polymarket setup used Clawdbot to manage execution and reach nearly $150K in returns from a modest start. It says there was no insider access. These are the author's claims with no proof in the text.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on event outcomes. Clawdbot is an AI agent tool used here to automate trades. Posts with big profit numbers and urgent words like leaked are common marketing hooks.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of posts that promise large returns with a leaked recipe.
- Ask for verifiable records before believing profit claims.
- This is not trading advice.
