## Summary

The quoted post reports that Tower Research hired Marvin Arteaga from Millennium as head of mid-frequency trading strategies, after 12 years at Millennium and seven at Bank of America. @Vexxter4sure argues that MFT is the true test of an edge: in HFT, mediocre alpha can work with great execution because it is a race for fills, while MFT needs strong conviction in the edge, especially for long/short or arbitrage.

## Background

HFT (high-frequency trading) holds positions for very short times and depends on speed. MFT (mid-frequency trading) holds positions for minutes to days, so the prediction itself (alpha) matters more than speed. Long/short means holding both long and short positions to hedge market moves.

## What I took away

- Different trading horizons reward different skills: execution versus prediction.
- This is one trader's opinion, offered as education, not advice.
- When evaluating a strategy, separate execution quality from signal quality.
