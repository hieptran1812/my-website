## Summary

@teortaxesTex says faster algorithmic progress could have made things weirder. The quoted post says SFT plus a bit of RL on GPT-2 almost matches fine-tuned GPT-3 12b on GSM8K, a model with >100x the pretraining compute.

## Background

GSM8K is a grade-school math benchmark. RL on chain of thought improves reasoning cheaply.

## What I took away

- Algorithmic ideas can beat compute.
- Hindsight experiments show missed opportunities.
