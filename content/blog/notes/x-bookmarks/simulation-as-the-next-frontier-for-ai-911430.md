## Summary

The author says simulation is clearly the next frontier for AI. He notes the most impressive AI feats, like beating Le Sedol at Go, winning IMO gold and writing apps from scratch, happened where there is a clear environment and reward. A linked article is titled Simulation: The Next Frontier for AI.

## Background

Reinforcement learning works best when an agent can try actions in an environment and get a reward signal. Simulations could create such environments for more areas, such as social or real-world settings.

## What I took away

- Clear environment plus reward has driven AI's biggest wins.
- Building good simulators may unlock new domains.
