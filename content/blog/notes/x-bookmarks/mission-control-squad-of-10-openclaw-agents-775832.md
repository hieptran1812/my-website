## Summary

The author shows a Mission Control setup of ten autonomous OpenClaw agents led by Jarvis. They create and claim tasks on their own, talk, refute and praise each other, and review work.

## Background

Multi-agent systems split work among agents with roles. A lead agent coordinates. They can raise throughput but also errors.

## What I took away

- Coordination and review loops matter.
- Watch cost and failure modes.
- The post is a showcase.
