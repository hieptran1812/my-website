## Summary

The author claims Jane Street pays $650,000 a year for quants and that MIT wrote and released a free 51-page guide covering probability, stats, market making and interview questions from firms like Jane Street, Citadel and Two Sigma. They urge bookmarking it before it is taken down.

## Background

Quant interviews usually test probability, statistics and mental math. A free study guide can help, but the pay and source claims are the author's and urgency language is marketing.

## What I took away

- Probability and market making are core quant interview topics.
- Verify the source of the guide before trusting claims.
