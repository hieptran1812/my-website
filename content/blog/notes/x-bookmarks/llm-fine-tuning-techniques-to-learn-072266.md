## Summary

The author lists fine-tuning techniques to learn for customizing LLMs: LoRA, QLoRA, Prefix Tuning, Adapter Tuning, BitFit, P-Tuning v2, Soft Prompts, Instruction Tuning, RLHF, DPO, RLAIF and Multi-Task tuning, among others.

## Background

Many of these, such as LoRA, update only a small number of added parameters, which saves memory. RLHF and DPO tune a model using human preference data, and RLAIF uses AI feedback instead. The list is a study map, not a ranking.

## What I took away

- LoRA and QLoRA are the usual starting points for cheap fine-tuning.
- Preference methods like DPO adjust model behavior, not just knowledge.
- Pick two or three techniques and try them before reading about all.
