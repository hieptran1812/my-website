## Summary

Prime Intellect says its RL Residency gives independent researchers compute grants and mentorship to build open evals and training environments for overlooked problems. In 2026 residents shipped projects across robot control, medical imaging, gameplay and GPU kernels.

## Background

RL environments and evals define what a model practices and how it is measured. Residencies with compute help people without lab resources contribute.

## What I took away

- Compute grants lower the barrier for independent research.
- Overlooked problems can be good targets.
- See the highlights for example projects.
