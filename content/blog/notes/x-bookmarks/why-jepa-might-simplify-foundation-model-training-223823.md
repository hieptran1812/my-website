## Summary

The author says they finally understand Yann LeCun's JEPA approach and why he may have quit Meta. They think it might fix one of the most hacky parts of training foundation models, and ask whether 90% of the tricks used to train big models are just complicated workarounds.

## Background

JEPA predicts abstract representations rather than raw data. The claim is that a cleaner objective could remove tricks used to keep training stable. The reason for LeCun leaving Meta is the author's speculation.

## What I took away

- Training tricks may hide a weak objective.
- Treat the Meta departure remark as speculation.
- Read about JEPA to judge if it simplifies training.
