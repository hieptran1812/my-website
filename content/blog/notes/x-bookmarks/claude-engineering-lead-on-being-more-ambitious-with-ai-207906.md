## Summary

@cgtwts points out the author is an Anthropic lead engineer. The quoted post is from Felix, an engineering lead on Claude, who says he loves watching people do things they never thought possible, notes that the advice to be more ambitious with AI is hard to follow, and shares a tutorial on how to do it.

## Background

As AI tools get more capable, the limit is often what people think to ask for. A practical guide to ambition helps users attempt bigger projects with models.

## What I took away

- Try tasks with AI that you would have assumed were out of reach.
- Read the tutorial for concrete steps.
