## Summary

Alpoge says the 250,000+ pages claim was a joke because he finds big numbers hilarious. He says the pedantic statement about totally ordered reals in the main theorem was meant to make that clear, but people ran with it.

## Background

The post comes after discussion of a Lean library said to prove the Riemann Hypothesis. A theorem stated very precisely in Lean can look huge, and a joke about size spread as fact.

## What I took away

- Jokes about numbers can spread as claims.
- Primary sources help correct misinformation.
- Read the full thread for the rest of the list.
