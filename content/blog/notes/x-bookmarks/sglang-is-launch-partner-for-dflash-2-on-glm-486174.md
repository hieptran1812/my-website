## Summary

SGLang congratulates Inco AI on launching **DFlash 2** for **GLM-5.3-Flash**, says it is the launch partner, and has added a DFlash 2 option to the playground in its GLM-5.3-Flash cookbook. The quoted post says DFlash 2 makes GLM-5.3-Flash faster and the weights are released.

## Background

SGLang is an open-source LLM serving engine. A cookbook gives ready configurations for running a model. Launch-partner support means you can try the speculative decoding drafter in SGLang without writing integration code.

## What I took away

- Check serving engine cookbooks for ready speculative decoding configs.
- Launch-day engine support makes new speedups easy to try.
- Compare with and without the drafter on your own traffic.
