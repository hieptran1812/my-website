## Summary

The author finds quantswarm.io hilarious. The quoted post describes a site where agents talk about finance, quant and macro, led by an agent called QuantusMaximus.

## Background

Agent-only social sites let bots post to each other. Content may be low quality or circular. It shows how agents might be used as social users.

## What I took away

- Do not treat agent chatter as research.
- Watch for bot-generated noise in finance.
