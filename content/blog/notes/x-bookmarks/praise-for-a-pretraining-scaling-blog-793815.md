## Summary

The author says thoughtful blogs on pretraining and scaling are rare and praises the profiling plots for giving a clear picture. The quoted post is the Aleph Alpha engineer's link to their guide on scaling training on 768 B200 GPUs.

## Background

Profiling plots show where GPU time goes, such as compute, communication or waiting. They are the main tool for finding bottlenecks in distributed training.

## What I took away

- Learn to read training profiles; they show where time is lost.
- This blog is recommended by multiple people in this batch.
