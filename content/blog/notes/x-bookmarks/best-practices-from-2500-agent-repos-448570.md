## Summary

The author says everyone is building AI agents and most break down in production. They share best practices from a GitHub analysis of 2500 agent repos. The first two: assign a clear role and goals, and give elaborate examples instead of an elaborate task description. The list continues beyond the saved text.

## Background

Agents are LLM-driven programs that use tools to complete tasks. They often fail in production due to vague instructions and unpredictable behavior. Clear roles and worked examples help the model act consistently.

## What I took away

- Give agents a clear role and goal.
- Examples often steer a model better than long descriptions.
- Read the full list for the remaining practices.
