## Summary

The author claims a publicly accessible Microsoft web page confirms OpenAI has used looped Transformers in the GPT-6 series, matching earlier reporting by The Information. They say GPT-6.1 Sol uses 2 inference passes, with a passing mention of instead of three.

## Background

A looped Transformer runs the same block of layers several times per token, adding compute without adding parameters. The number of passes trades speed for reasoning depth.

## What I took away

- This is a claim based on a passing mention; treat it as unconfirmed.
- See the teortaxes reply in this batch for a skeptical reading.
