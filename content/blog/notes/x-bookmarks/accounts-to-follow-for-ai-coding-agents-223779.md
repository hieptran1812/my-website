## Summary

The author lists voices he thinks produce the best AI coding agent content: Karpathy on vibe coding workflows, swyx on agent architectures, Alex Albert on Claude's development process, and McKay Wigley building apps live on stream. The list continues beyond the shown text.

## Background

Vibe coding means building software by describing what you want to an AI and iterating, with less manual code. Following practitioners is a quick way to learn which workflows work.

## What I took away

- Follow practitioners who show real workflows, not just opinions.
- Agent architecture and live builds teach different things.
