## Summary

The author asks if understanding AI models makes them safer, an assumption behind interpretability research. The survey of mechanistic interpretability for safety notes that subfields operationalise this intuition in different ways. The post is truncated.

## Background

Mechanistic interpretability tries to reverse-engineer the internal computations of neural networks. The survey examines how that work links to safety goals such as detecting deception.
