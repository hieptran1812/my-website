## Summary

The author is happy that people were already offering Kolibri-1 commercially 10 hours after release. The quoted post says Kolibri 1 is live on Nodion EU AI Inference at over 165 tokens per second single-stream, as a 78B MoE with 3.46B active for German and English, EU-hosted with zero data retention, priced at 0.15 euro in and 0.60 euro out per 1M tokens.

## Background

Open weights let anyone host a model, so providers can compete on price, speed and data location. ZDR means zero data retention, where prompts are not stored.

## What I took away

- Open licenses enable fast third-party hosting.
- EU hosting and ZDR matter for data-sensitive users.
