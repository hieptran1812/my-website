## Summary

The author published a data dump of full order book data from Polymarket. It is maximally granular, with no filtering, saving every order book change and trade across all markets. It updates hourly and each snapshot has about 30M rows.

## Background

Order book data lists buy and sell orders at each price. Granular data helps research and backtesting but is big to store and process.

## What I took away

- Check disk and compute needs for 30M-row snapshots.
- Useful for market microstructure research.
