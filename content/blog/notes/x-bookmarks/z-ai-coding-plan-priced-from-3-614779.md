## Summary

The post asks whether you are done overpaying for coding assistants and says Z.ai offers the same top performance at a way lower price. The link card says plans start from $3.

## Background

This is an advertisement. The same performance claim is the vendor's and has no benchmarks in the post.

## What I took away

- Compare coding plans on real tasks, not on slogans.
- Check what limits apply to a low entry price.
