## Summary

@Prof_Oak_ announces in a playful three-part reveal that Anthropic acquired a one-person biotech company, and that the person is the account owner. After six years of loose anonymity, this is the post where they reveal who they are and promise to explain the deal.

## Background

Large AI labs sometimes acquire very small companies mainly to bring in a person or a specific capability, often called an acqui-hire. A one-person biotech being bought by an AI lab suggests interest in applying AI to biology and drug discovery.

## What I took away

- A solo founder with deep domain skill can be an acquisition target for AI labs.
- AI labs are expanding into science domains like biotech.
