## Summary

The author jokes that this could be the most influential paper in the history of AI. The quoted post says Meta sent a memo that tracking software will be installed on computers of all US employees so it can train its AI, on a list of work-related tools.

## Background

Training agents to use computers needs data on how people do real work, such as clicks and keystrokes. Collecting it from employees raises privacy and consent questions. The details come from the quoted post only.

## What I took away

- Computer-use data is valuable for training agents.
- Workplace data collection raises privacy questions.
- Check the original report for the exact scope.
