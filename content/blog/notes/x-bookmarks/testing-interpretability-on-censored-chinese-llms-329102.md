## Summary

Neel Nanda says rigorous interpretability needs ground truth, which is hard to find. His MATS scholars suggested extracting CCP-censored facts from Chinese LLMs. The quoted post says these models know sensitive facts but are trained to hide them, and shares transcripts and an evaluation of current techniques.

## Background

Interpretability tries to understand what a model knows internally. A model that hides a known fact offers a natural check on whether a technique can reveal it.

## What I took away

- Realistic secret-keeping is a useful testbed.
- Evaluate interpretability methods against known ground truth.
