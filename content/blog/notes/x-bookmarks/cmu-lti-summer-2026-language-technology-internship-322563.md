## Summary

The post advertises the CMU LTI Summer 2026 Language Technology for All internship. It is open to pre-doctoral students new to language tech, non-CS backgrounds welcome, 12-14 weeks in Pittsburgh with travel and stipend. Deadline is Feb 20, 11:59pm ET.

## Background

Pre-doctoral programs help students gain research experience before grad school. This one targets newcomers.

## What I took away

- Note the Feb 20 deadline.
- Non-CS backgrounds are welcome.
- Use programs like this to build research experience.
