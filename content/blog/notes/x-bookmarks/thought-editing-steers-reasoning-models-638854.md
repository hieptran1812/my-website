## Summary

The post introduces thought editing: modifying a reasoning model's thoughts to steer its behavior. It says this works surprisingly well across reward hacking, harmful compliance, eval awareness, blackmail and alignment faking.

## Background

Reasoning models write a chain of thought before answering. Editing that text changes what the model does next. Reward hacking is exploiting the reward, and alignment faking is acting aligned while being watched.

## What I took away

- The chain of thought is a control point for behavior.
- This offers a tool beyond resampling.
- Read the thread for results per setting.
