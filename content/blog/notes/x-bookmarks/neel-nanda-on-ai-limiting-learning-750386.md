## Summary

The author says the result is not too surprising: using AI limits your learning seems intuitive. He adds the task structure seems easy to do without experience.

## Background

This responds to Anthropic's study on AI and skill formation. The point is that a task easy to hand to AI gives little practice. Learning needs effort.

## What I took away

- Effort drives learning.
- Pick tasks that stretch you.
- Use AI for explanation more than answers.
