## Summary

The author says Google just dropped what they call Attention is all you need (V2), a paper that could solve catastrophic forgetting. They describe the problem as models forgetting earlier knowledge when learning something new, and say Google Research now has a solution.

## Background

Catastrophic forgetting is when training a model on new data makes it lose old skills. Continual learning research tries to avoid this. The V2 label is the author's framing and a bold claim, so check the paper itself.

## What I took away

- Catastrophic forgetting is a core obstacle for continual learning.
- Treat catchy titles like V2 as marketing until you read the paper.
- Look for how the paper measures forgetting.
