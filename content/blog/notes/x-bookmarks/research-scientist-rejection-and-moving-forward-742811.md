## Summary

@wjqkoko shares being rejected for a Research Scientist role at a frontier AI startup while staying excited about what comes next. He is a PhD researcher at CUHK, previously interned at Tongyi Qwen and MiniMax, and works on RL, LLM post-training and on-policy distillation.

## Background

Post-training is the stage after pretraining where a model is tuned for helpfulness and skills, often with RL. On-policy distillation trains a student model on its own outputs with guidance from a teacher. These are in-demand research areas.

## What I took away

- Rejections happen even to strong candidates with top internships.
- Sharing your research focus publicly can open new opportunities.
- RL and post-training remain hot research areas for hiring.
