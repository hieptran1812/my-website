## Summary

The author admires how Karpathy simplifies complex ideas, and says Claude Code plus $10 on RunPod for one H100 gave him a world class ML researcher on autopilot. The quoted post packages autoresearch as a single-GPU, one-file repo of about 630 lines based on nanochat training core.

## Background

Autoresearch lets an agent edit a training script, run experiments and keep improvements while a human guides through instructions. A small single-GPU setup makes it cheap to try.

## What I took away

- A small, self-contained repo makes agent research easy to try.
- Rented GPUs can make such experiments cost only a few dollars.
- The quality claim is the author's own opinion.
