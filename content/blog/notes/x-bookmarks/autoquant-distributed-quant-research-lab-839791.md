## Summary

The author announces Autoquant v2.6.9, a distributed quant research lab that applies Karpathy's autoresearch loop to quantitative finance, mentioning 135 autonomous agents (text cut off). The quoted post, Autoskill v2.6.5, applies the same pattern to a distributed skill factory.

## Background

Autoresearch loops need a measurable score, and in finance, backtests provide one but can mislead through overfitting. Results are the authors' claims.

## What I took away

- The same loop pattern is being reused across domains.
- Be cautious with backtest-driven claims.
- Educational only, not advice.
