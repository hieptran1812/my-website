## Summary

The author added their Strong Stocks Screener to TradingView and calls it a game-changer for finding leaders. Their first criterion is massive relative strength: stocks already 70% or more above their 52-week lows. The rest of the list is cut off.

## Background

A screener filters stocks by rules, here by strength measured from the 52-week low. The idea is that leaders tend to stay strong, but that is the author's approach. This is not advice and filters like this carry risk.

## What I took away

- Screeners turn a trading idea into explicit rules.
- Do not treat a screener as a recommendation list.
- Test the rule against history before relying on it.
