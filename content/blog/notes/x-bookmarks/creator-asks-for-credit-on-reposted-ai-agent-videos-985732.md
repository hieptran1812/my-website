## Summary

The author says he is the person who made the AI agent explainer videos that other accounts keep stitching together without asking, often calling him an ex-Google engineer or this guy. He links the original post and invites people to follow him directly. The quoted post is an aggregator account praising his videos for making complex agent concepts easy to understand, without jargon.

## Background

Aggregator accounts often repost others' content for engagement without credit. For learners, going to the original creator usually gives the full and most accurate material.

## What I took away

- Look for the original source of educational content you find through aggregators.
- Credit creators when sharing their work.
