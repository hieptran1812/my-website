## Summary

The author says Meta released not just SAM-3 but also SAM-3D for objects and humans. SAM 3D Body is a promptable model for recovering full-body 3D human meshes from a single image and seems to be the new SOTA.

## Background

A 3D mesh is a surface model of a body made from points and triangles. Getting it from a single photo is hard because depth is ambiguous. Promptable means you can guide the model with hints. SOTA is the author's impression.

## What I took away

- Single-image 3D recovery is improving quickly.
- SOTA here is the author's view, so compare on your data.
- Check the release for license and demos.
