## Summary

Z.ai introduces GLM-OCR with only 0.9B parameters. They claim state-of-the-art results across document understanding benchmarks including formula recognition, table recognition and information extraction.

## Background

Document understanding includes reading formulas, tables and extracting fields from files. A small model is cheap to run. Claims come from the developer.

## What I took away

- Small models can be effective for OCR.
- Test on your own document types.
- Benchmarks are vendor reported.
