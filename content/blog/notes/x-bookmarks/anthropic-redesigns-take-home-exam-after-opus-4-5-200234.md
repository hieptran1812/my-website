## Summary

@AnthropicAI posts on its Engineering Blog about a hard take-home exam for performance engineering candidates. It says the exam worked well until Opus 4.5 beat it. The post explains how they designed and redesigned it, under the title Designing AI resistant technical evaluations.

## Background

A take-home exam is a test candidates do on their own time. If an AI model can solve it, the test no longer separates strong candidates. This is a real example of AI forcing changes in hiring evaluations.

## What I took away

- AI progress can invalidate old hiring tests.
- Evaluations need redesign as models improve.
- Read the blog post for the design lessons.
