## Summary

The author says they made a breakthrough in self-evolving AI scientists, moving from search to principled discovery. They argue scientific discovery requires that the search space itself changes, and an AI scientist must notice this without intervention. They say they built an AI that achieves this.

## Background

Most automated search explores a fixed space of options. Real science often redefines the space, for example by finding new variables. The post is cut off, so details are in the linked work.

## What I took away

- Changing the search space is a harder problem than searching within it.
- This is the author's claim; look for the paper.
