## Summary

The post announces Agentic General Intelligence v3.0.10: the Karpathy autoresearch loop is now generic, so anyone can propose an optimization problem in plain English and the network spins up a distributed swarm to solve it with no code. It also claims intelligence compounds across domains. It quotes the Autoquant release.

## Background

A swarm means many agents working on parts of a problem in parallel. The key requirement is still a measurable objective. The cross-domain claims are the author's.

## What I took away

- Problems need a clear metric for a swarm to work.
- Plain-English interfaces lower the barrier to optimization.
