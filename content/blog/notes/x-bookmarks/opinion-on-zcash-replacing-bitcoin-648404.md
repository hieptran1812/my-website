## Summary

The author argues that if ZEC truly replaces BTC, with people saying we now have encrypted bitcoin, then no one would take any crypto asset seriously as a store of value again. They ask readers to think about why.

## Background

A store of value is meant to hold worth over time, which depends on belief that it is the scarce leader. If a newer coin can displace the leader, that belief is weakened. This is a speculative opinion, not a prediction or advice.

## What I took away

- Store-of-value claims depend on trust in a lasting leader.
- This is one person's opinion on a market narrative.
- Not investment advice.
