## Summary

The author claims that combining Clawdbot with MakeUGC yields 600 AI-made UGC ad videos a day. It lists about $1 per video, minutes to produce, and 24/7 operation. These are marketing claims.

## Background

UGC means user-generated content, such as short videos that look like real people reviewing a product. AI tools can now generate these automatically. Quality, disclosure rules and platform policies are the open questions.

## What I took away

- Cheap, endless ad content raises questions about quality and trust.
- Check platform rules on AI-generated ads before using such tools.
