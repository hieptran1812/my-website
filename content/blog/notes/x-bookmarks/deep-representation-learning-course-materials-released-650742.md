## Summary

The author finished teaching a course on Deep Representation Learning and says all lecture slides and video recordings are now on the book website. They say that with the book, these help students clarify basic concepts and principles of intelligence.

## Background

Representation learning is about how models learn useful internal features from data. The course is tied to a book, so slides and videos follow it. Free university material is a good way to learn at depth.

## What I took away

- Slides, videos and a book are all freely available.
- Good for building principled understanding beyond tutorials.
