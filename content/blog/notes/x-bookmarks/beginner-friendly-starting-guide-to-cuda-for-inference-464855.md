## Summary

@dhananjaya163 says they started learning CUDA a few months ago to work on inference and found most good blogs assumed existing knowledge. They wrote the starting point they wished they had, aimed at building a mental picture of the whole topic.

## Background

CUDA is NVIDIA's platform for writing programs that run on GPUs. Inference optimization often means writing or tuning GPU kernels, small programs that run in parallel on thousands of threads. A good mental model covers threads, blocks, memory types and how data moves.

## What I took away

- Start with the overall model of GPU execution before diving into kernels.
- Writing a guide for your past self is a good way to learn.
- Open the link for the guide.
