## Summary

@ProfTomYeh wrote a short story for students about the evolution of PPO, DPO, GRPO and GDPO, NVIDIA's new paper. The story follows his own journey learning to cook as the family chef. The post starts with PPO and the text is cut off.

## Background

These are algorithms for tuning LLMs with feedback. PPO is a classic RL method. DPO learns from preference pairs without an RL loop. GRPO scores groups of answers against each other. Analogies like cooking make the ideas easier to remember.

## What I took away

- Each method simplifies or improves the previous one.
- Analogies help learn RL ideas.
- Read the thread for the full story.
