## Summary

SemiAnalysis congratulates RadixArk on its launch. The quoted post announces Miles v0.1, an open-source RL framework for LLMs and multimodal models that aims to help runs stay correct, efficient and stable at scale.

## Background

RL frameworks coordinate generation, reward computation and model updates across many GPUs. Because RL runs are hard to debug, tooling focused on correctness and efficiency is valued in post-training work.

## What I took away

- See the original Miles announcement for technical details.
- Industry attention suggests RL tooling is becoming a key area of infrastructure.
