## Summary

The author says his team has done interpretability work much faster using their new platform internally, and that a private beta is open. The quoted post from Goodfire presents Silico as like having a team of AI researchers to run experiments such as replicating J-space on GLM 5.2 or training a reward model to reduce hallucinations.

## Background

Interpretability research studies how models compute their outputs. A platform that automates setup, experiments and analysis could shorten the cycle from idea to result.

## What I took away

- This is an insider endorsement; weigh it accordingly.
- Faster experiment loops are the main promise of research automation tools.
