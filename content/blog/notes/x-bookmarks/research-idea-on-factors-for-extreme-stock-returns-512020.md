## Summary

The author shares a research idea from the paper Designing Factors for Extreme Outcomes with Machine Learning. It revisits an older Beneish-style idea from 2001 that fundamentals matter most in the tails of the return distribution, not around the mean.

## Background

A factor is a measurable stock characteristic used to explain returns. Most models focus on average returns, but extreme winners and losers matter a lot for portfolios. The claim here is that fundamentals are most informative for those extremes. This is educational and not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Model tails separately, since signals may differ from those around the mean.
- Old finance ideas can be revisited with machine learning.
- Check the original paper before building on a summary.
