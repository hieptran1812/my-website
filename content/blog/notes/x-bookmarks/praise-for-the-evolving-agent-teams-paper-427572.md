@lxe quotes the introduction of Evolving Agent Teams (EAT), where a supervisor diagnoses stalled agent teams and restructures them for long-running optimization tasks. He calls it top-tier alpha, meaning very valuable insight.

## Background

Alpha here is slang for an edge or insight others have not yet absorbed. The underlying work is about using a supervisor agent to reorganize teams of agents when they get stuck.
