## Summary

The author says to check the ARENA curriculum update for learning pragmatic interpretability, with new coding tutorials and utilities for putting it in an LLM prompt. The quoted post announces 8 new exercise sets on alignment science, interpretability and AI safety, each 1 to 2 days of hands-on content replicating key papers, open source on GitHub.

## Background

Interpretability studies what happens inside neural networks. ARENA offers structured coding exercises that replicate key papers, which is a practical way to learn.

## What I took away

- Use ARENA exercises for hands-on interpretability learning.
- Replicating papers builds real skill.
- Material is free and open source.
