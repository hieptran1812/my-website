## Summary

Jeff Dean announces he is co-founding **Discovery Loop** with Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le. It is a Public Benefit Corporation, and its mission, as far as the post text goes, is to automate machine learning research or discovery (the text is cut off).

## Background

A Public Benefit Corporation is a for-profit company legally committed to a stated public benefit alongside profit. Automating research means using AI systems to run parts of the scientific loop, such as forming hypotheses, running experiments and analyzing results.
