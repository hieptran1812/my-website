## Summary

The author welcomes a researcher to the team at GDM and Gemini. The quoted post is the researcher's personal announcement that earlier this year he left Thinking Machines, where he helped build Tinker, and joined Google DeepMind a few months ago.

## Background

Researcher moves between labs are common and signal where talent and projects are heading. Tinker is described in the quote as a new product category that was built from the ground up.

## What I took away

- Talent moves between top labs often.
- Founding-team experience can lead to roles elsewhere.
- Treat such news as career context, not technical results.
