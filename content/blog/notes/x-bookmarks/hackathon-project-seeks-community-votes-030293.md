## Summary

The author says their project made the Top 12 out of 92 projects and 244 builders in their 41st hackathon, Project G.U.D by Pushchain. Community voting is open, and they ask for likes and comments for APT-Casino. The quoted post describes it as a universal, provably transparent casino using on-chain randomness.

## Background

A hackathon is a timed building event with prizes. Community voting often decides finalists. On-chain randomness means random numbers generated and verified on a blockchain so outcomes can be checked.

## What I took away

- Hackathon experience builds a portfolio.
- Voting asks are promotion, so evaluate the project on its own.
- Gambling apps carry legal and risk concerns.
