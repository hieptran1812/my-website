## Summary

The author shares a Substack post titled 'When quantum optics meets diffusion'. They describe it as a simple blog on one of the papers they loved.

## Background

Diffusion models generate data such as images by learning to reverse a gradual noising process. Quantum optics is a branch of physics about light and its quantum behavior. Posts linking the two show how ideas from physics can inspire generative modeling.

## What I took away

- Physics ideas often inspire new generative modeling methods.
- A blog summary is a gentle way into a paper before reading it fully.
