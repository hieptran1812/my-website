## Summary

@Mr_Buzzoni says someone connected @openclaw to @Polymarket with $100 and let it trade 15-minute BTC up/down markets overnight. The post claims it woke up with about $347. The listed takeaways say the trades were automated and used X sentiment and news.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on event outcomes. A 15-minute up/down market asks if BTC will rise or fall in a short window. A single overnight result is an anecdote and says nothing about repeatable skill.

## What I took away

- One lucky run is not evidence of an edge.
- Unverified profit claims deserve skepticism.
- Automation can lose money as fast as it makes it.
