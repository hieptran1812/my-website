## Summary

The post says that in the model's learned representations, human and robot images become more aligned as pre-training is scaled up. To a scaled-up model, human videos look like robot demonstrations.

## Background

A representation is the internal vector a model uses to describe an input. If human videos and robot videos map to similar representations, then data of people can help teach robots. This matters because human video is far more plentiful than robot data.

## What I took away

- Scale can make human video usable as robot training data.
- Representation plots are a way to check transfer between domains.
- This could reduce the cost of collecting robot demonstrations.
