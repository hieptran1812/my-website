## Summary

The author shares new OpenAI research on how CoT monitorability, including no-CoT capability and CoT controllability, evolves during training. The findings are described as unsurprising but sobering as models become more intelligent. The post text is cut off.

## Background

Chain-of-thought (CoT) is the reasoning text a model writes before answering. Monitorability is how well humans or tools can detect problems by reading it. If models can reason without CoT or control it, monitoring weakens.

## What I took away

- CoT monitoring is a safety tool that may weaken with scale.
- Track capability to reason without visible CoT.
