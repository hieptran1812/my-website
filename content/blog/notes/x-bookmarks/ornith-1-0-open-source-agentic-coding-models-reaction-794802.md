## Summary

@ryaneshea reacts strongly and claims this is the first open-source model from a US lab that codes at frontier levels. The quoted post introduces Ornith-1.0, open-source LLMs specialized for agentic coding, in 9B Dense, 31B Dense, 35B MoE and 397B MoE sizes. It claims state-of-the-art among open models of comparable size.

## Background

Agentic coding models are trained to work through multi-step programming tasks with tools. Dense models use all parameters per token, while MoE models activate only a subset. The frontier-level claim is the author's opinion and should be checked against independent benchmarks.

## What I took away

- Ornith-1.0 spans small dense to large MoE sizes.
- The frontier claim is the author's, not independently confirmed here.
