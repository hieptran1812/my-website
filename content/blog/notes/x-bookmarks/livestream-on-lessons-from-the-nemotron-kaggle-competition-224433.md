## Summary

@kagglingdieter says he will be on a livestream discussing learnings from the Nemotron competition hosted by NVIDIA on Kaggle. The linked video is titled What 5,000 Kagglers Taught Us About Improving AI Reasoning, about the NVIDIA Nemotron Model Reasoning Challenge.

## Background

Kaggle competitions crowdsource solutions from thousands of practitioners. A reasoning challenge asks participants to improve how a model solves multi-step problems, often through fine-tuning or data work. Recaps surface what worked across many teams.

## What I took away

- Competition recaps distill many experiments into practical lessons.
- Watch the video for reasoning-improvement techniques.
- Crowdsourced results are a cheap way to see what works.
