## Summary

@_avichawla notes that Google Maps uses graph ML for ETA and that Netflix, Spotify and Pinterest use it for recommendations. The thread then presents 6 must-know ways for graph feature engineering with code.

## Background

Graph ML works on data made of nodes and edges, like roads or users and items. Feature engineering turns graph structure into numbers a model can use, such as node degree or neighbors. It matters for recommenders and routing.

## What I took away

- Graph structure can be turned into model features.
- Graph ML powers many large products.
