## Summary

The author says you can now do weight-space programming without an LLM at all, and presents the method called AHA in a blog with a collaborator. They report big jumps on GSM8K, and the text is cut off.

## Background

Weight-space programming means producing changes to a model's weights directly for a task. Doing it without a helper LLM would make it cheaper. Results are the authors' claim.

## What I took away

- Weight edits can be generated without a helper LLM.
- Results on GSM8K are self-reported.
- Read the blog for method details.
