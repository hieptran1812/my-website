## Summary

@jianfengchi agrees with a statement that Meta is no longer a great place for academic research. The quoted post says the author's paper won an ICML award after being rejected from NeurIPS, and that ICML gave only 7 awards among 24k submissions.

## Background

Conference awards are rare, and reviews can be noisy. A paper rejected at one venue can do well at another after small cleanups.

## What I took away

- Do not give up on a rejected paper; revise and resubmit elsewhere.
- Review outcomes are noisy, so one rejection says little.
- Industry labs may be shifting how much open academic work they support.
