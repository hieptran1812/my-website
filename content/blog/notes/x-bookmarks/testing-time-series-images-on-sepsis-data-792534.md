## Summary

The author saw a post about the GAF method and asked their advisor why they do not use it on pediatric sepsis time-series data. The advisor was skeptical and said she tried it during her PhD. The author, calling themselves a delusional optimist, commits 40 hours to experiment with it.

## Background

The Gramian Angular Field (GAF) turns a time series into an image so image models can classify it. Sepsis is a dangerous reaction to infection, and time series from patients are used to predict it. A skeptical advisor is a useful reminder that new tricks often do not beat strong baselines.

## What I took away

- Time-boxing an experiment, such as 40 hours, is a good way to test an idea.
- Compare against a strong baseline before believing in a transformation.
- Listen to skeptics and still test for yourself.
