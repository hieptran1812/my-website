## Summary

The author says to spend one hour building Obsidian plus Claude Code as a personal JARVIS. It captures everything, connects ideas and thinks alongside you, using what they call the most powerful model.

## Background

A second brain is a notes system that links your ideas. An AI helps search and connect them. The JARVIS comparison is hype.

## What I took away

- Notes plus an agent are practical.
- Start small.
- Hype claims need testing.
