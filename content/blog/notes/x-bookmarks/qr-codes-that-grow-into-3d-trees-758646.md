## Summary

The author likes the design and plans to use it at offline events so people scan to follow them. The quoted post shares a tool that grows a 3D tree from a URL's QR code, where the ground grid is the real scannable code. It offers Spring, Summer and Autumn looks and runs fully in the browser with nothing uploaded.

## Background

A QR code encodes data in a grid of squares that phones can scan. Because scanners only need the grid, the rest of the image can be decorative. Running in the browser means your URL is not sent to a server.

## What I took away

- QR codes can be decorated heavily as long as the grid stays readable.
- Client-side tools avoid sending your data to a server.
- Test scannability on several phones before printing.
