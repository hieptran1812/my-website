## Summary

The quoted post says Gemini 3 flash achieved 90% accuracy at 1 million context on OpenAI's MRCR benchmark, per contextarena.ai, which it calls SoTA across all models, noting most top models cannot go past 256k. The author replies that his team developed powerful new techniques for Gemini 3 long-context reasoning and congratulates the team.

## Background

Context length is how much text a model can read at once. MRCR tests whether a model can find and distinguish specific items in a very long context. Staying accurate at 1 million tokens is hard because standard attention loses precision as context grows.

## What I took away

- Long-context accuracy is a separate skill from context window size.
- A Google author confirms new techniques were used, without details.
- Check MRCR results yourself for your use case.
