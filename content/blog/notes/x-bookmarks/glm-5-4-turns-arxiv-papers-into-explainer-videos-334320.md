## Summary

The author jokes that 3Blue1Brown walked so we could run. The quoted post says GLM-5.4 can turn any arXiv paper into a 2-minute 3Blue1Brown-style video, and that trending papers on alphaXiv now have videos on the homepage. It argues models should also be good at explaining research.

## Background

AI generated explainers can help readers triage papers faster. Quality depends on how accurate the generated explanation is, which readers should verify.

## What I took away

- Short videos can speed up paper triage.
- Verify AI-made explanations against the paper.
- Explanation skill is a useful model capability.
