## Summary

The author says no one really knows what environment research needs. Competition students have strong coding skills that are valued in both quant and LLM work, but the author questions whether strong coding equals strong vibe coding equals strong research. The quoted post links a Zhihu article, partly agrees with it, and criticizes an environment in China where one view is treated as the only answer.

## Background

Competition programming trains speed and algorithmic skill. Research also needs problem choice, taste and patience with ambiguity. Vibe coding refers to building with heavy AI assistance.

## What I took away

- Coding skill is necessary but not sufficient for good research.
- Research environments that allow disagreement may produce better ideas.
- Think about which skills AI coding tools make less scarce.
