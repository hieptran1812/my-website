## Summary

The ICML conference says authors can get feedback on their ICML 2026 submissions from a Google Gemini-based paper assistant. It is live now, with details in a blog post.

## Background

LLM-based reviewers can catch unclear writing or missing details before submission, but should not replace human judgment.

## What I took away

- Use the tool for early feedback.
- Treat LLM feedback as advisory.
