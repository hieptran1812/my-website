## Summary

The OpenClaw account says the project went from Clawd to Moltbot to OpenClaw. It claims 100k+ GitHub stars and 2M visitors in a week. Tagline is your assistant, your machine, your rules.

## Background

OpenClaw is an open-source personal AI assistant that runs on your own hardware. Renames signal fast growth.

## What I took away

- Names changed; mind older tutorials.
- Stars are a popularity signal.
- Review permissions before running.
