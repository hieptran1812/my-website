## Summary

The author is annoyed that papers cite everyone but them, though their paper came out at the same time as DSpark and earlier than DFlash2. They wonder if the phone-friendly format looks less serious. The quoted post says Tencent released DSpine for Qwen3.8-27B and claims it beats DFlash2.

## Background

Speculative decoding has a small draft model propose tokens that the big model verifies in one pass. Citation gaps are a common frustration in fast-moving fields.

## What I took away

- Fast-moving areas produce citation disputes.
- Presentation can affect perceived seriousness.
- Check related work yourself.
