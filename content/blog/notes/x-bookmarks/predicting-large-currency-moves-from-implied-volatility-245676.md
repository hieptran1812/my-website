## Summary

The post quotes a paper called 'Large Moves in the Foreign Exchange Market'. It says the timing of large currency moves, such as three-standard-deviation events, is predictable in real time via the slope of the term structure of option-implied volatility. A link to the SSRN paper is included.

## Background

Implied volatility is the market's expected size of future price swings, backed out from option prices. The term structure shows it across different maturities. A steep slope means traders expect a different level of risk in the near term versus later. The finding is the paper's claim.

## What I took away

- Option prices contain information about expected volatility.
- The shape of the volatility curve may signal large moves.
- Read the paper before using the idea.
