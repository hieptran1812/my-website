## Summary

The post says Anthropic released a 33-page cheat sheet for building Claude skills and links the PDF on resources.anthropic.com.

## Background

Skills are reusable packages of instructions, and sometimes scripts, that Claude loads when relevant. A guide helps structure them well.

## What I took away

- Read the guide before writing your first skill.
- Skills are a lightweight way to customize agents.
