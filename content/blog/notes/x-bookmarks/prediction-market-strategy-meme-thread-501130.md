## Summary

The post is written as a story: a Jane Street intern making $350k finds prediction markets, models mispriced probabilities with five formulas in a Google Sheet, and claims 87% of wallets bet on feelings. It mentions a ceasefire contract at $0.30 and Bayes. The text is cut off.

## Background

Prediction markets price event outcomes as probabilities. Spotting mispricing needs real edge, and the stats in the post are unsourced. The tone is promotional.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of unsourced stats and 'easy edge' stories.
- Bayes updating is useful, but markets can be efficient.
- This is educational context, not advice.
