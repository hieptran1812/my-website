## Summary

The author says xAI uses war rooms for its highest priority projects. The team crams into a conference room, sits side by side and sprints until it ships. He calls it intense but fun, says there are many active war rooms, and counts 12 days until end of year.

## Background

A war room is a co-located focus mode used to speed up delivery. It trades work-life balance for speed. The post is the author's own view of the culture.

## What I took away

- Some labs use intense co-located sprints for key launches.
- Consider the trade-off between speed and sustainability.
