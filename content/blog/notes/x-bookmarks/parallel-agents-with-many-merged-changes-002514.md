## Summary

The author asks how a developer manages 4 parallel agents, 5 in draft and 23 merged. The quoted post says it is already burning the house down and calls it incredible. Few details are in the text.

## Background

Running agents in parallel means several AI coding sessions work on separate tasks at once, each producing changes for review. Throughput depends on good task splitting and review.

## What I took away

- Parallel agents need well-scoped, independent tasks.
- Review capacity becomes the bottleneck.
