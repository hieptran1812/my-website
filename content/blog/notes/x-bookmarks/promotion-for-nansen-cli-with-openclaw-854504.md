## Summary

The author says that if your OpenClaw is trading onchain and you are not using a particular CLI from Nansen you are clinically insane, linking to a Nansen post. It reads as promotion.

## Background

The hyperbolic wording is marketing style. Letting an agent trade with real funds carries risk.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of hype in tool promotion.
- Never give an agent control over funds without limits.
