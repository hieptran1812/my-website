## Summary

Alibaba Qwen introduces **Qwen-Audio-3.0-TTS**, a text-to-speech model in two versions: Flash for real-time interaction and Plus for high-quality generation. New features include fine-grained inline tags such as [whisper], [angry], [breaths] and [laughs]. It also mentions free-style natural-language control (text cut off).

## Background

Text-to-speech (TTS) turns written text into spoken audio. Inline tags let you mark exactly where the voice should change style or add sounds. Offering a fast and a high-quality variant lets developers trade latency for quality.

## What I took away

- Inline style tags give fine control over generated speech.
- Pick the real-time variant for voice agents and the quality variant for content.
- Consider misuse risks of expressive voice generation.
