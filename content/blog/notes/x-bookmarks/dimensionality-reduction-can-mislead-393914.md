## Summary

The author warns that dimensionality reduction can see structures that do not exist and miss structures that exist. They add that the simplest explanation is not always the best one. The post starts a thread.

## Background

Methods such as t-SNE and UMAP squeeze many dimensions into two for plotting. This distorts distances, so clusters or gaps in the plot may be artifacts. Treat such plots as hints and check with other analysis.

## What I took away

- Do not trust clusters in a 2D plot blindly.
- Verify structure with other methods.
- Parameter choices can change what you see.
