## Summary

@xerocooleth shares the Anthropic Fellows Program, AI Safety and Security, as an opportunity for people needing money or a job. Listed benefits are direct mentorship from Anthropic researchers, a weekly stipend of 3,850 USD, 2,310 GBP or 4,300 CAD plus benefits that vary by country, and about $15k per month for compute and other research expenses. Locations include London, Ontario, San Francisco and remote in the US.

## Background

Fellowship programs fund people to do research for a fixed period with mentorship from a lab. They are a common entry point into AI safety research for people outside academia or big labs.

## What I took away

- Fellowships are a realistic path into AI safety research.
- Check the official listing for current terms and eligibility.
- Compute funding matters as much as the stipend for research output.
