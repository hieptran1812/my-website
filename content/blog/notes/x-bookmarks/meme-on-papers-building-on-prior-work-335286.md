## Summary

The post is a meme: starting a new paper knowing Zhang et al. and Wang et al. already did 80% of the thinking.

## Background

Researchers often build on prior papers. Citing and extending is normal, and the joke is about the feeling of being second.

## What I took away

- Build on prior work and add something clear.
- Do literature review early.
