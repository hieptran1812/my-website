## Summary

@aakashgupta says a San Francisco startup claims it turned mercury into real gold and that, by its own math, gold is one of the cheapest things the machine can make, while targeting other metals could make a cancer drug ingredient worth tens of billions of dollars per gram. The quoted company post says it demonstrated high-rate transmutation beating CERN's record by 10x and enriched isotopes in a powerful plasma centrifuge, beating a 40-year-old Soviet record.

## Background

Transmutation changes one element into another by altering its nucleus, which takes nuclear reactions. Gold from mercury has been done in labs for decades, but only in tiny amounts. Isotope enrichment separates atoms of the same element by mass and matters for medicine and energy.

## What I took away

- The records and values are the company's claims and need independent review.
- Medical isotopes may be a more realistic market than gold.
- Separate the science result from the economic story when reading such posts.
