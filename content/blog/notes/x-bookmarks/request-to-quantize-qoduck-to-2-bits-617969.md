## Summary

The author asks for trellis coding down to 2 bits. The quoted post adds QoDuck, an early Qwen-4 27B checkpoint, claimed to be beaten only by Opus 5.5-Max and Xhigh on GameFeel bench and state of the art for open source.

## Background

Trellis coding is a low-bit quantization method that shrinks weights while keeping quality. Benchmark claims here are the quoted author's.

## What I took away

- Demand for 2-bit models is high.
- Early checkpoints are unofficial.
- Benchmark claims need verification.
