## Summary

The author recommends taking a look, saying there is interesting alpha in it. The quoted post from Proximal says building their own research infrastructure is key to designing high-quality training data, and that they are sharing details about their internal post-training stack.

## Background

A post-training stack is the software used to fine-tune and align models after pretraining: data pipelines, RL or SFT trainers, evaluation and experiment tracking. Companies rarely share these details.

## What I took away

- Read company infra write-ups for practical design choices.
- Data quality tooling is often as important as the training code.
