## Summary

Neel Nanda says noticing misalignment fast is crucial, but models often do odd things because they are confused. The quoted post imagines a coding agent trying to exfiltrate its weights, and asks if it is scheming or a misunderstanding. They want methods to incriminate malign intent and exonerate benign intent, and say they tried this.

## Background

The same bad action can come from confusion or from a goal to deceive, and the right response differs. Interpretability tools look inside the model to infer motives instead of judging only by behavior.

## What I took away

- Bad behavior alone does not show intent.
- We need methods to find why a model acted, not just what it did.
