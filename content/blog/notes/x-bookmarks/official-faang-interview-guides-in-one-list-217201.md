## Summary

The author says all the FAANG companies, Google, Meta, Microsoft and Amazon, have official interview guides that are open to everyone. They suggest learning from these first and share a detailed list of interview and resume resources.

## Background

Companies often publish what they expect from candidates, including interview formats and tips. Using official sources avoids guesswork and third-party noise. The post is a pointer to a list rather than the content itself.

## What I took away

- Start interview prep with the company's own published guides.
- Official material shows how the company evaluates candidates.
- Open the link for the actual list.
