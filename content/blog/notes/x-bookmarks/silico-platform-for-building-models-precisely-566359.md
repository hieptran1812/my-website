## Summary

Goodfire introduces Silico, a platform for building AI models with the precision of written software. It lets researchers and engineers see inside models, debug failures and intentionally design them from the ground up. Early access is open.

## Background

Today models are mostly trained and then tested from outside. Silico aims to use interpretability so builders can inspect and edit internals. Its real capability is not shown in this post.

## What I took away

- Interpretability may become part of the standard model workflow.
- Judge it by demos and independent use.
