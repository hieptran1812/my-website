## Summary

The quoted post introduces **ApprenticeBench**, a benchmark for computer use and continual learning on a real job. It claims Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra can now learn on a job and surpass human professionals, and that agents deploy themselves without FDEs. The author frames this as a move from professional tasks to virtual employees.

## Background

Computer use means an agent operates software through a screen, keyboard and mouse. Continual learning on the job means the agent improves from experience during the task. FDEs are forward-deployed engineers who usually set up AI systems for customers.

## What I took away

- Benchmarks are shifting from single tasks to sustained jobs.
- Treat surpass-human claims as the authors' result until replicated.
- Look at how the benchmark measures learning over time.
