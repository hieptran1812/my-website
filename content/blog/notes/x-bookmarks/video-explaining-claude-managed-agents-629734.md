## Summary

The author says this may be the clearest breakdown of Claude Managed Agents. The 12-minute video explains what it is, who needs it, what the live console looks like and why the pricing is, in their words, crazier than most think.

## Background

Managed agents are hosted agent setups run by the provider. Pricing may differ from plain API use. The praise is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Check pricing before building.
- Watch if you evaluate hosted agents.
- Hype wording is the author's.
