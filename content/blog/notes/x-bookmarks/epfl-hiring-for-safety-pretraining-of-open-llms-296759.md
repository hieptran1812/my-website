## Summary

EPFL has open positions for AI research engineers working on safety pretraining and alignment for LLMs, funded by a $1M award. The work contributes to **Apertus**, a fully open LLM trained on 10k+ GPUs. The listing mentions pretraining and RL post-training at scale, synthetic data, evaluation and red-teaming.

## Background

Safety pretraining means building safety into the base training data and process, not only adding it later with fine-tuning. Fully open models release data and code as well as weights, making research reproducible.

## What I took away

- Academic labs offer roles in large-scale open model training.
- Safety work is moving earlier into pretraining.
