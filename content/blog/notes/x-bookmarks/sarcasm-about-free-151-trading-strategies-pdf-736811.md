## Summary

@quantian1 replies with wow, genius stuff, saying he cannot believe academics give this away free. The tone reads as sarcastic. The quoted post claims a paper unlocks every algorithm used by hedge funds with 151 strategies in a 361-page PDF.

## Background

Surveys of trading strategies exist and are public, but listing strategies does not give an edge. Hedge funds do not run on public textbook strategies alone. Claims that a paper unlocks every fund algorithm are hype.

## What I took away

- A catalog of strategies is not a profitable system.
- Be wary of unlock every algorithm claims.
- Read it as study material, not as advice.
