## Summary

The author recommends following John Kim, an L7 engineer at Meta, noting his video on how he uses Claude Code has half a million views. In the quoted post, John introduces himself as a Senior Staff engineer at Meta who makes AI tech content on YouTube and Substack, has worked on Reels and Meta AI on Messenger, now works on Threads, and teaches agentic topics.

## Background

Claude Code is an AI coding agent that works in a terminal. Senior engineers sharing how they actually use such tools can be a practical source of workflows, because they work on large real codebases.

## What I took away

- Learn agent coding workflows from practitioners who use them on real projects.
- View counts show popularity, not necessarily depth; judge the content itself.
