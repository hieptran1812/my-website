## Summary

The author says their LP bot farming against another LP farming bot in the same orderbook creates a very ugly book and a very inefficient market.

## Background

LP (liquidity provider) farming means placing resting orders mainly to earn rewards or points, not to trade for profit. When two bots do this against each other, orders cluster oddly and prices can look unnatural. It shows how reward programs can shape market behavior.

## What I took away

- Incentive programs change how participants behave in a market.
- Bots competing for rewards can lower real market quality.
- Check the orderbook before assuming displayed liquidity is meaningful.
