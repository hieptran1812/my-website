## Summary

The author calls this an underrated release, saying Anthropic gave Claude the ability to control lab equipment such as microscopes, robotic arms, liquid handlers and lasers, and that QuEra had Claude fix quantum computer lasers. The quoted Anthropic post announces the first phase of a research preview of the **Model Hardware Standard (MHS)**, a standard for AI agents to safely operate physical equipment in research and advanced manufacturing.

## Background

A standard like this defines how an agent talks to hardware, similar to how MCP defines how agents call software tools. Safety matters more for physical equipment because mistakes can damage gear or harm people.

## What I took away

- Agents are moving from software tools to physical instruments.
- A shared standard can make equipment usable by many agents.
- Safety controls matter more when actions affect the physical world.
