## Summary

@emilyyvuong writes in Vietnamese that the IMF is warning about a new macro risk from stablecoins. The author says the biggest story of this season is not BTC or any altcoin but the least noticed one, stablecoin, which in 2025 for the first time in history passed something. The text is cut off.

## Background

Stablecoins are crypto tokens designed to keep a steady value, often pegged to the US dollar. Regulators like the IMF watch them because they can affect financial stability if they grow large or lose their peg. Details of the comparison are not in the visible text.

## What I took away
