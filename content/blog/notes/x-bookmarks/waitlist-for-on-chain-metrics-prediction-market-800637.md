## Summary

The post invites readers to join a waitlist for what it calls the first prediction market for every on-chain metric, offering early access. It is promotional and has little detail.

## Background

On-chain metrics are numbers derived from blockchain activity. A prediction market lets people bet on such values. Invites like this are marketing.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of early-access promotions.
- Research any platform before depositing funds.
