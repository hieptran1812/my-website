## Summary

The post says AI can now build ML models like a Goldman Sachs trading desk for free. It offers 12 Claude prompts it claims replace $400K per year quant researchers.

## Background

A quant researcher builds statistical models of markets. Prompts can help write code and ideas, but they do not supply data, validation or live risk control. The post uses urgent words and big salary figures as hooks.

## What I took away

- Prompts do not replace data, backtesting discipline or risk management.
- Be skeptical of replace-a-professional claims.
