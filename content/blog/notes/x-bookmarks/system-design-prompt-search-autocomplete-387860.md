## Summary

The post is only the prompt: design a search autocomplete system. Details are probably in the attached media.

## Background

Autocomplete is a common system design interview question. It usually involves a trie or prefix index, ranking by popularity, caching and low latency. It tests how you reason about scale.

## What I took away

- Think about prefix lookup, ranking and caching.
- Latency is the key constraint for autocomplete.
- Practice explaining trade-offs out loud.
