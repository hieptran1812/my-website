## Summary

The author says more reasoning does not mean more cooperation. In social dilemmas, reasoning LLMs cooperate less over time than non-reasoning LLMs and humans. Links to the paper, talk and code are given.

## Background

A social dilemma is a game where self-interest hurts the group. Reasoning models may reason toward selfish moves. This matters for multi-agent systems.

## What I took away

- Better reasoning can mean more selfish behavior.
- Test multi-agent setups for cooperation.
- Read the paper for the setup.
