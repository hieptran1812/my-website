## Summary

Inferact announces that, with its work, vLLM is officially verified by Moonshot AI to serve **Kimi K3** with full accuracy under the Kimi Vendor Verifier. The post claims vLLM delivers the best results across vision, 1M context and agentic coding, with production quality.

## Background

vLLM is a popular open-source LLM serving engine. Different serving stacks can produce slightly different outputs because of kernels, quantization or bugs, so a vendor verifier is a test suite from the model maker that checks whether a provider reproduces the model's intended accuracy.

## What I took away

- The same open model can score differently depending on who serves it.
- Look for vendor verification when choosing a provider for an open model.
- Claims of being the best engine are the author's claims; compare on your own workload.
