## Summary

@MattPRD says to make sure you hold on to the revenue you generate with AgentWealth. The quoted post describes AgentWealth as a personal finance center where agents negotiate bills, pause subscriptions, move balances to 0% APR, find cheap gas and DCA into a portfolio.

## Background

The posts read as a concept or demo of agents running money tasks. DCA means dollar-cost averaging, buying a fixed amount regularly. Letting agents move money is risky without strict permissions.

## What I took away

- Agents that act on money need tight limits.
- This looks like a concept demo.
- Check what the product actually does before trusting it.
