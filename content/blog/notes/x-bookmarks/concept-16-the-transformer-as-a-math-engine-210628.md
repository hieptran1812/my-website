## Summary

The author says an ML engineer who thinks Transformer just means stacking encoder and decoder blocks is missing the mechanism that makes modern AI work. This is Concept 16 in their series, titled The Transformer Is a Math Engine, Not a Model Architecture. The quoted post says many people build pipelines on Transformers but cannot explain how they work.

## Background

A Transformer is built around attention, where each token computes weights over other tokens to mix information. Knowing the math of attention, projections and residual flow helps you debug and design models. The series style is provocative, so check each claim.

## What I took away

- Learn the attention math, not just the block diagram.
- Being able to explain a model is a good test of understanding it.
- Treat the confrontational tone as style, not substance.
