## Summary

@ramin_m_h shares open-sourcing more wisdom on evaluations. The quoted post releases IFStruct to measure how well models generate structured outputs, and says a 350M model trained on it outperforms models more than 10x its size.

## Background

Structured output means JSON or other fixed formats that programs can parse. A small model trained for the format can beat bigger general models.

## What I took away

- Narrow training can make small models excellent at format.
- Check what the benchmark covers.
