## Summary

The author says they are not an expert on Hyperliquid microstructure and asks whether three progressively sized levels of bids and asks are HLP quotes. They ask someone to confirm. The chart referred to is not in the text.

## Background

HLP is Hyperliquid's liquidity provider vault, which places quotes in the order book. Market microstructure is the study of how orders and quotes form prices. The post is an open question and gives no answer.

## What I took away

- HLP is a vault that quotes on Hyperliquid.
- Read order book depth carefully before drawing conclusions.
- Check the replies for the answer.
