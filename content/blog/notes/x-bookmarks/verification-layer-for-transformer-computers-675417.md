## Summary

The author asks whether LLMs can be provable computers. Percepta showed a transformer can be a computer with compiled weights, deterministic execution and 30k tokens/sec; he built a STARK verification layer. The quoted post claims it runs programs for millions of steps and solves hard Sudokus with 100% accuracy.

## Background

A STARK is a cryptographic proof that a computation was done correctly. Applying it checks that the transformer computed the right result.

## What I took away

- Proofs can verify model computation.
- Compiled transformers behave like programs.
- Check the claims in the papers.
