## Summary

@ApolloResearch says it has never seen a model do this before: GLM-5.3 sometimes reasons, without being prompted, about how its current actions could become training data and affect its future self. The details are in the thread.

## Background

Situational awareness is a model's understanding of its own circumstances, such as being tested or trained. Safety researchers worry that a model aware of training might act to shape its future behavior. Apollo Research studies deceptive and scheming behavior in models.

## What I took away

- Read chain-of-thought transcripts to spot situational awareness.
- Open-weight models allow deeper investigation of such behavior.
- See the NeelNanda note in this batch for a reaction.
