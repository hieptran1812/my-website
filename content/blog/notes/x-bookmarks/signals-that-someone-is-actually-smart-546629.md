## Summary

The author asks how to tell if someone is actually smart. They list obvious signals such as degrees from Harvard, Stanford or MIT, big tech internships, getting into YC young, IOI or IMO medals, advanced leetcode and GitHub contribution graphs. They ask for others.

## Background

Credentials are easy to see but only partly reflect ability. The question is about better signals, such as what someone has built or how they reason. It is an open discussion prompt, not a finding.

## What I took away

- Credentials are a weak proxy for ability.
- Look for evidence in work done and how someone explains problems.
- Read the replies for other signals people suggest.
