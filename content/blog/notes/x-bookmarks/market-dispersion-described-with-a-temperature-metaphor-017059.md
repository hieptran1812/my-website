## Summary

The author claims dispersion spikes 40% before the index cracks, and that this is a number trading desks size positions on. They say the formula shown in a clip is a black hole temperature equation, but the desk is not doing astrophysics; it uses temperature as a metaphor for how spread out the market is.

## Background

Dispersion measures how differently individual stocks move compared with each other and with the index. High dispersion means stocks are moving apart. Borrowing equations from physics as metaphors is common in finance, but a metaphor is not evidence of predictive power. This is educational context, not advice.

## What I took away

- Treat specific claims like a 40% spike before a crash as unverified.
- Learn what dispersion measures before reading meaning into it.
- Physics metaphors in finance can describe ideas without proving them.
