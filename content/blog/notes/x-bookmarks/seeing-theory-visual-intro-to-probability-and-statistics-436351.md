## Summary

The author enthusiastically shares seeing-theory.brown.edu. The link card describes it as a visual introduction to probability and statistics.

## Background

Seeing Theory uses interactive visualizations to teach core ideas such as distributions and inference. Seeing the concepts move helps many learners more than formulas alone.

## What I took away

- Use interactive visuals to build intuition for statistics.
- Good foundation for ML and quant work.
