## Summary

The author calls it extremely impressive. The quoted post introduces Photon, Moondream's inference engine for vision-language models, claiming 46ms end-to-end and 60+ fps on a single H100, running from edge to server.

## Background

VLMs combine vision and language and are often slow. An inference engine optimizes how they run. Numbers are the vendor's claims.

## What I took away

- Latency matters for production vision.
- Check the benchmark setup.
- Edge to server support is useful.
