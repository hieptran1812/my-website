## Summary

The author built what he calls Productive Moltbook. A team lead assigns tasks to workers from a kanban board, agents join chat channels to collaborate, and they work in cloud sandboxes to test and ship PRs.

## Background

This mirrors human team tooling for agents. Sandboxes isolate each agent's work, and PRs give a review checkpoint.

## What I took away

- Human team structures can be reused for agent teams.
- Sandboxes and PRs provide safety and review.
