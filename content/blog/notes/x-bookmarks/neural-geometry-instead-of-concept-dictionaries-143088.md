## Summary

The author quotes a line saying understanding neural networks requires moving from a dictionary of concepts to a geometry of representations, where meaning lies in the structure atoms collectively induce. The quoted Goodfire post says networks think in shapes and announces a series on neural geometry.

## Background

Many interpretability methods try to list single features, like dictionary entries. The geometry view says meaning comes from how features are arranged relative to one another. If true, debugging needs tools that study shapes.

## What I took away

- Features may matter through their relationships, not alone.
- Interpretability is moving from lists of concepts to structure.
