## Summary

@rasbt says the Big LLM Architecture Comparison article has been updated and has more than doubled in size since the initial version in July 2025. It is available on his magazine.

## Background

The article compares the designs of recent open LLMs, such as attention types and MoE layouts. It is a good single place to see how architectures differ.

## What I took away

- Use it to compare modern open model designs.
- It is regularly updated.
- Read it alongside model config files.
