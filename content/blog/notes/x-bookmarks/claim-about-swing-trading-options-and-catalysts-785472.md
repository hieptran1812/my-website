## Summary

The author says this man is smart and that this is how they made millions swing trading options. They tell readers to pay close attention to the catalyst part. The method is in the linked media.

## Background

Swing trading holds positions for days or weeks. A catalyst is an event expected to move a price. The profit claim is unverified and this is not advice.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of personal profit claims.
- Catalysts are a common framework for timing trades.
- Options can lose all their value; do not copy without understanding risk.
