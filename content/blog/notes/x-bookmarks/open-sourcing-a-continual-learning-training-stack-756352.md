## Summary

Day 3 of Trajectory's launch week: the team is **open sourcing a training stack for continual learning** together with SkyRL and Anyscale. Its stated mission is to bring continual learning to every team and company. The quoted post is the official Day 3 announcement.

## Background

Continual learning lets a model keep improving as it sees new data, instead of staying fixed after training. SkyRL is an RL training framework and Anyscale runs distributed compute. Combining them aims to make this practical outside big labs.

## What I took away

- Continual learning is moving from research toward tooling.
- Look at SkyRL and Anyscale integration if you train agents with RL.
- Open stacks let you inspect how the learning loop works.
