## Summary

The author shares a summary by a colleague on how Harvey optimizes agent harnesses for domain-specific legal tasks. He links a Karpathy post that appears to be the auto-research inspiration.

## Background

An agent harness is the code around a model: prompts, tools and loops. Auto-research means an AI loop that tries changes to improve something and keeps what scores better. Applying it to a harness tunes it for a domain like law.

## What I took away

- Harnesses can be tuned automatically, not only by hand.
- Domain-specific evals are the key ingredient.
- Look at the linked thread for the method.
