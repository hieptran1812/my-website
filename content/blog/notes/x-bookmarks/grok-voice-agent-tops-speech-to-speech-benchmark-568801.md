## Summary

Elon Musk posts the single word Grok above a quoted post. The quoted post says xAI's new Grok Voice Agent is the leading speech-to-speech model, beating Gemini 2.5 Flash Native Audio and GPT Realtime on Big Bench Audio with a score of 92.3%.

## Background

Speech-to-speech models take spoken audio in and produce spoken audio out directly, without a text step in between. Big Bench Audio is a benchmark that tests reasoning from audio input. A lead of a few points on one benchmark may not hold in real use.

## What I took away

- Speech-to-speech models are now competing on reasoning benchmarks.
- Single benchmark leads are narrow, so test with your own audio.
- The 92.3% score is as reported in the quoted post.
