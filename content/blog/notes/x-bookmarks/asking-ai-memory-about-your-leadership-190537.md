## Summary

@danifesto says you can ask your memory how you lead your team, with the answer based on real episodes from Slack, Gmail and more.

## Background

AI memory tools index your work messages and documents so an assistant can answer questions about your own history. This helps with reflection but also means sensitive data is gathered in one place, so privacy and access control matter.

## What I took away

- Personal memory layers can turn scattered messages into reflection.
- Consider what data you are giving such a tool access to.
