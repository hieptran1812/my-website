## Summary

Tencent Hunyuan announces Hyra 2.0, the next evolution of its Hunyuan Research Agent. It moves from solving individual problems to building reusable tools, models and workflows that accelerate future discoveries. They link a perspective on the next chapter of AutoResearch.

## Background

AutoResearch refers to AI agents doing research tasks with little human help. Making reusable outputs means each task leaves behind assets that help the next one, so the system compounds over time.

## What I took away

- Design agents to leave reusable artifacts, not just answers.
- Research agents are a growing area for large labs.
- Read the linked perspective for their roadmap.
