## Summary

The author announces Cofounder 2: run an entire company with agents. They call it infrastructure for the one-person billion dollar company, orchestrating agents across engineering, sales, marketing, ops and design.

## Background

Multi-agent orchestration assigns roles to AI agents and coordinates them. The billion dollar company idea is a marketing aim, and real results depend on reliability and oversight.

## What I took away

- Agent products are targeting whole business functions.
- Look for evidence of reliability before relying on them.
