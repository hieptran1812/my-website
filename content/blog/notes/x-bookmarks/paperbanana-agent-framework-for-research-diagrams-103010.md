## Summary

The authors introduce PaperBanana, an agentic framework from PKU and Google Cloud AI. It aims to remove the burden of crafting diagrams and plots so researchers can focus on ideas.

## Background

An agentic framework uses multiple LLM steps to plan, draw and refine outputs. Figures take lots of researcher time. Quality still needs human check.

## What I took away

- Figures are a time sink worth automating.
- Check generated figures for accuracy.
- Read the thread for the pipeline.
