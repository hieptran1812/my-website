## Summary

The author says that in multimodal settings even the discrete versus continuous divide is a distraction. The real challenge is bridging the semantic gap between high-level language tokens and low-level perceptual representations. He is replying to posts arguing autoregression versus diffusion is a false dichotomy and that diffusion fits a FLOPS-rich future.

## Background

Autoregression predicts tokens one by one, and diffusion refines outputs in steps. Discrete tokens are symbols like words, continuous ones are real-valued vectors.

## What I took away

- Framing matters: semantic gap over modeling style.
- These are opinions in a debate.
