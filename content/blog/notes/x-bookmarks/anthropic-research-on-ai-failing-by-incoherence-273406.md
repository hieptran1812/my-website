## Summary

The author shares the new Anthropic alignment research. It says AI might fail not through systematic misalignment but through incoherent, self-undermining behavior that does not optimize a consistent goal, like humans often do. The quoted post is the Fellows announcement.

## Background

Systematic misalignment means pursuing a consistent wrong objective. Incoherence means behavior without a stable objective. The difference shapes which risks to prepare for.

## What I took away

- Failure may be messy, not scheming.
- Safety work should cover both modes.
- Read the linked write-up.
