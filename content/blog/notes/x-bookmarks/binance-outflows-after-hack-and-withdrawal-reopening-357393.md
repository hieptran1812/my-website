## Summary

@WhaleInsider reports that Binance has reportedly seen over $4 billion in asset outflows since reopening withdrawals following the $6.3 billion hack.

## Background

Outflows are funds users withdraw from an exchange. After a hack, users often move assets out to reduce risk. The figure is reported, not confirmed, in the post.

## What I took away

- Large outflows after a security incident signal shaken user trust.
- Treat breaking news accounts as unverified until confirmed.
- Self-custody versus exchange custody is a key risk decision in crypto.
