## Summary

The author claims a Chinese man built a Second Brain in Obsidian and gets 3 trading ideas each morning that brought him $180,000 in 6 months. It says he runs six N8N workflows that pull articles, podcasts and voice notes into a shared Obsidian vault. These are unverified claims.

## Background

Obsidian is a markdown note app and N8N is a workflow automation tool. Collecting notes automatically is useful, but it does not by itself produce profitable trades. Posts quoting big returns are often promotional.

## What I took away

- Automating note capture is a real productivity idea.
- Be skeptical of unverified profit claims.
- This is not trading advice.
