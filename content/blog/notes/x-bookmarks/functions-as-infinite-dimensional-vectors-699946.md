## Summary

The author recommends the blog post Functions are Vectors by Max Slater. It shows that viewing functions as infinite dimensional vectors unlocks powerful linear algebra tools. They say this perspective is under-emphasized in intro linear algebra classes.

## Background

A function can be treated like a vector with one entry per input value. Then ideas such as inner products, bases and projections apply to functions. This view underlies Fourier series and kernel methods.

## What I took away

- Think of functions as vectors to reuse linear algebra.
- It connects to Fourier analysis and kernels.
- Worth reading to deepen intuition.
