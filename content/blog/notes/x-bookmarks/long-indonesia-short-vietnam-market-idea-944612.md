## Summary

The post, in Indonesian, proposes long Indonesia and short Vietnam. It says Vietnam was seen as a favorite in the US-China trade war because factories moved there, and its stocks have risen, so expectations may be too high. It quotes a post removing the paywall on a 2026 trades report.

## Background

A long/short pair idea bets one market will beat another. The argument is that good news is already priced in. This is one author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Pair trades express a relative view, not a market direction.
- One opinion is not advice.
