## Summary

The author built Research Loop, 7 open skills giving AI agents a flight plan: frame, evidence, test, execute, verify, synthesize. Every claim traces to a source, failed checks stay on record and Unresolved is a valid landing. The quoted post recommends Anthropic's How researchers use Claude analysis of 200,000 conversations.

## Background

Skills are reusable instruction sets for agents. Requiring sources and allowing unresolved answers reduces made-up conclusions.

## What I took away

- Process structure makes agent research more honest.
- Allow unresolved as a valid result.
- Trace claims to sources.
