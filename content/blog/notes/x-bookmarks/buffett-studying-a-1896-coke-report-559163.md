## Summary

The author says Buffett spends his time studying annual reports. He claims Buffett was reading the 1896 Coke annual report.

## Background

Annual reports show how a company describes its business and results. Reading old ones reveals how a firm evolved. The anecdote is used to promote deep study over quick tips.

## What I took away

- Primary documents beat summaries.
- Reading history can build long-term judgment.
