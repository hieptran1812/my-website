## Summary

Sergio Paniego offers holiday reading with blogs he contributed to this year. They cover VLMs in TRL, VLMs in 2025, tokenization in transformers v5 and faster transformers.

## Background

VLMs are vision language models that take images and text. TRL is Hugging Face's library for post-training. These are practical write-ups from the people who maintain the tools.

## What I took away

- Use the blogs to catch up on vision language models and tokenization.
- Library authors' posts show how the tools are meant to be used.
