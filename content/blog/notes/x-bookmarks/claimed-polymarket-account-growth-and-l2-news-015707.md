## Summary

The author says an account called coffeemachine turned about $2,000 into $123,000 in one month on Polymarket. The quoted post says Polymarket confirmed migrating to its own L2 and that the app drives about 25% of its current network's TVL and gas.

## Background

L2 here means a layer-two blockchain, a separate chain built to scale a main chain. The profit story is an unverified claim with a referral-style link. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Be cautious about referral-link success stories.
- Check on-chain data yourself before believing a claim.
