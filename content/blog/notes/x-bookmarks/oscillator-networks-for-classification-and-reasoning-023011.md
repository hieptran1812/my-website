## Summary

@jm_alexia shares another paper in the same oscillator vein, but for classification and reasoning. It seems oscillators give good performance at much smaller parameter counts. The author finds this a very exciting line of work. The quoted post introduces Un-0, an oscillator-based image generator.

## Background

Coupled oscillators are units that swing and influence one another, used here as a replacement for ordinary neural layers. Fewer parameters would mean cheaper models, if the performance claim holds. The claim comes from the author's reading, not a verified result.

## What I took away

- Oscillator networks may reach good accuracy with small models.
- Read the linked project page to check the claim.
