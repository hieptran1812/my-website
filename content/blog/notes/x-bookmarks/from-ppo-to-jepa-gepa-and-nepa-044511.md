## Summary

The author jokes that after the PPO, DPO, SimPO and GRPO era, we are now in the JEPA, GEPA and NEPA period. It is a quip about the pace of acronyms in AI research.

## Background

PPO, DPO, SimPO and GRPO are RL and preference-training methods for LLMs. JEPA, GEPA and NEPA are newer approaches covering predictive embeddings and prompt evolution. The joke is that the field keeps renaming its focus.

## What I took away

- Research trends shift between acronyms quickly.
- Focus on the underlying ideas, not the names.
