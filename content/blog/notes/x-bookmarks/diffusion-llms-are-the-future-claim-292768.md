## Summary

The author calls something the most important reading of the month or year and quotes a post declaring that diffusion LLMs are the future, promising to return in a couple of years to collect an I-told-you-so award. It is a prediction, not a result.

## Background

Diffusion language models generate text by refining many tokens in parallel instead of one at a time. They may be faster but are less proven than standard autoregressive models.

## What I took away

- Diffusion LLMs are an active but unproven alternative.
- Treat the bold prediction as opinion.
