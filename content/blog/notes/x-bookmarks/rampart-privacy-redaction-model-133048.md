## Summary

@ClementDelangue jokes about the US government moving from regulating open models to releasing them. The quoted post releases Rampart, a 14.7MB model that redacts personal information in the browser before data goes to any server.

## Background

PII redaction removes names and numbers. Running locally keeps data private.

## What I took away

- Small models can run privately in browsers.
- Redact before sending to servers.
