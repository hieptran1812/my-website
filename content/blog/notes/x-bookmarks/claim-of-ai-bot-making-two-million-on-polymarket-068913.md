## Summary

The author claims AI will kill Polymarket and says a trader made $2.2M in two months with an account run entirely by a bot using probability models. They say the news will blow up the internet. The post is cut off.

## Background

This is a dramatic, unverified claim. Prediction markets are risky, and stories of huge bot profits often leave out losses, luck and other accounts that failed. It is not advice.

## What I took away

- Treat headline profit claims as unverified.
- Ask for evidence, risk data and how many similar bots lost money.
