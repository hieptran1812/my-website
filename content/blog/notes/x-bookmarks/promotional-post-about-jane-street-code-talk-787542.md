## Summary

The author claims a Jane Street Head of Technology showed the code that generates $13B profit and that watching a 50-minute vibe coding video will help you build your own hedge fund. The quoted post claims an ex-Citadel trader testified to the US Senate that the market is rigged. These are the authors' claims.

## Background

Vibe coding means building software by prompting an AI and accepting its output. Posts like this use big profit numbers to attract clicks. A talk cannot hand you a firm's edge, which comes from data, infrastructure and risk control.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of posts promising to replicate a top fund.
- Profit headlines rarely transfer to a hobby project.
- Check primary sources for any testimony or fines cited.
