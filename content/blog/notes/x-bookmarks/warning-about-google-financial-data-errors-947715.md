## Summary

@jartine says not to get financial data from Google. They claim Google has a 20x underreporting error on a company under review by MSCI and NASDAQ for index inclusion, in both Search and Finance, and that the numbers are not internally consistent.

## Background

Consumer finance pages may pull data from different sources that can disagree. For decisions involving money, primary filings or dedicated data providers are safer. This is the author's claim about one case.

## What I took away

- Cross-check financial data against primary sources.
- Inconsistent numbers on one page are a warning sign.
- Do not make decisions from a single aggregator.
