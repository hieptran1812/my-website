## Summary

The post says they completed the largest decentralised LLM pre-training run in history. Covenant-72B was trained permissionlessly on Bittensor subnet 3 with 72B parameters and about 1.1T tokens over commodity internet. There was no centralized cluster or whitelist, and anyone with GPUs could join or leave.

## Background

Pretraining normally needs fast links between GPUs in one site. Doing it over the open internet requires communication-efficient methods and tolerance for nodes joining or leaving.

## What I took away

- Open participation in pretraining is now claimed at 72B scale.
- The size record is the team's own claim.
