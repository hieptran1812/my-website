## Summary

The author says the linked arXiv paper (2507.06203) is a perfect illustration of why token-level reasoning is bad. The post does not explain the paper's argument.

## Background

Token-level reasoning means a model reasons by writing out words one token at a time. Some researchers prefer reasoning in latent or embedding space instead. This is the author's opinion, so read the paper to judge.

## What I took away

- There is a debate between token-level and latent reasoning.
- Read the cited paper to see the evidence.
- One paper is not proof of a general rule.
