## Summary

The author calls the quoted Slack-for-coding-agents project a wild way to orchestrate agents. He asks if the future is replicating our tools, taskboards and org charts for agents, or giving agents entirely different communication systems.

## Background

Multi-agent systems need coordination. Human-style structures are familiar, but they may not be optimal for machines.

## What I took away

- Question whether human workflows fit agents.
- Experiment with both familiar and new coordination designs.
