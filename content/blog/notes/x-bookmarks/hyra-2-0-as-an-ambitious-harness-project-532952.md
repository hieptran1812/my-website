## Summary

Tencent Hunyuan introduced Hyra 2.0, the next version of its Hunyuan Research Agent, which moves from solving problems to building reusable tools, models and workflows that speed up future discoveries. @teortaxesTex calls Hyra a very ambitious harness project.

## Background

An AutoResearch agent tries to carry out research tasks on its own. Building reusable tools means the agent's past work makes future work faster, similar to a team building internal libraries.

## What I took away

- Reusable artifacts make agents improve over time.
- Research agents are becoming a major harness engineering focus.
- Read Tencent's write-up for the design details.
