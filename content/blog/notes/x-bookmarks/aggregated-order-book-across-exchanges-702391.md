## Summary

The author added an aggregated view to his orderbook imgui widget. It combines the order books from all exchanges into one stacked visualization so you can see where liquidity sits across venues at each price. Hovering shows a breakdown, such as $26M on Binance perp bids, $8M on Bybit spot asks and $4M on Hyperliquid bids.

## Background

An order book lists the buy and sell orders at each price. Liquidity is how much can be traded without moving the price. Combining venues gives a fuller picture than any single exchange.

## What I took away

- Cross-venue aggregation reveals liquidity that one book hides.
- Hover breakdowns help identify which exchange supplies it.
