## Summary

Greg Brockman says welcome to the team. The quoted author is joining OpenAI Codex to work on agentic development. They say that at Cursor they saw the shift from autocomplete to agents, and the next step is an Agent Development Environment (ADE) for orchestrating agents.

## Background

An ADE would be tools to manage, run and review many agents, not just edit code. It is the author's vision for the next tooling step.

## What I took away

- Tools are shifting from editors to agent orchestration.
- ADE is a concept to watch.
