## Summary

The author asks why FVGs often act as magnets for price. They say fair value is where both sides can trade, and that when an FVG forms only one side participated. The text is cut off.

## Background

A fair value gap is a price range crossed quickly with little trading in it. Some traders expect price to revisit it. This is a popular but contested idea.

## What I took away

- FVG theory is a trading concept, not a proven rule.
