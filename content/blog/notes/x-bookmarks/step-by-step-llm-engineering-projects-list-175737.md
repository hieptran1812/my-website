## Summary

@TheAhmadOsman posts a list of step-by-step LLM engineering projects, with each project teaching one concept the hard, real way. It begins with tokenization and embeddings: build a byte-pair encoder and train your own subword vocab, write a token visualizer, and compare one-hot with learned embeddings.

## Background

Byte-pair encoding builds a vocabulary of subwords by merging common pairs. A learned embedding maps each token to a vector the model can train. Building these by hand shows what libraries hide.

## What I took away

- Learn a concept by building a small version of it.
- Tokenization and embeddings are the right place to begin.
