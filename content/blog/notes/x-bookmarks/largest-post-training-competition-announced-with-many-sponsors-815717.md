## Summary

White Circle announces what it calls the largest post-training competition ever, run together with a long list of partners including OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Mistral. The visible text mentions $1.5m in compute for participants and more than $200k in prizes, and links to a sign-up page.

## Background

Post-training means improving a model after pretraining, using methods like supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. Competitions with free compute let individuals try this work that would otherwise be costly.

## What I took away

- Competitions can provide free compute for post-training experiments.
- Check the sign-up page for deadlines and rules.
- Sponsor lists show which labs care about open post-training work.
