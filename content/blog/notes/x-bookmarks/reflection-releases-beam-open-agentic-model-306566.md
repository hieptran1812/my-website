## Summary

Graham Neubig calls it an impressive first release by Reflection, joking that good things come to those who wait. The quoted post introduces Beam, an agentic open model with 501B total and 23B active parameters, trained from scratch, claimed to advance the Western open frontier on coding and agentic tasks, with full weights due this month.

## Background

An agentic model is tuned to use tools and take multi-step actions. The low active parameter count means each token uses only a small part of the model, keeping serving cheaper.

## What I took away

- Check whether weights are actually released before planning on it.
- Western open models are getting more competitive in agentic coding.
