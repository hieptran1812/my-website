## Summary

Ziming Liu announces an update to his Physics of AI blog. It covers learning sparse attention, which has a sticky plateau, representation collapse and branching dynamics. The details are in the linked blog post.

## Background

Sparse attention lets a model look at only some tokens instead of all, saving compute. A training plateau is a long stretch where loss barely improves. Studying small toy cases helps explain why such training can be hard.

## What I took away

- Training plateaus can be studied as phenomena with their own dynamics.
- Representation collapse is a known failure mode worth watching for.
- Read the blog for the actual experiments.
