## Summary

@LiorOnAI says Andrew Ng released an Agentic Reviewer for research papers. The post claims it reached near human-level agreement after training on real ICLR 2025 reviews. It argues that paper review is slow, with each cycle taking about six months.

## Background

Peer review is the process where experts judge papers before publication. An agentic reviewer is an LLM system that reads a paper and writes feedback. Agreement with human reviewers is one measure, but it does not prove the feedback is correct.

## What I took away

- Fast AI feedback could shorten paper revision cycles.
- Agreement with humans is not the same as being right.
- Check how the agreement was measured.
