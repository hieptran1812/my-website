## Summary

Neel Nanda says Summer MATS applications are open. Participants do full-time research on a mech interp paper under his supervision, and applications are due Dec 23. All backgrounds are welcome. He says he has supervised 40+ papers, 17 at top conferences.

## Background

MATS is a program that pairs researchers with mentors for AI safety work. Mechanistic interpretability, or mech interp, tries to reverse-engineer what neural networks compute internally. This is an opportunity for people wanting to enter the field.

## What I took away

- The deadline is Dec 23.
- Applicants from varied backgrounds are welcome.
- Mech interp is a way into AI safety research.
