## Summary

Paula Barta says LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct from Liquid AI runs at over 239 tokens per second on an AMD CPU, over 82 on a mobile NPU, and under 1GB RAM. This means no cloud bills or internet. These are the author's figures.

## Background

Small models run on edge devices, offering privacy and offline use at some capability cost.

## What I took away

- Small models can run on-device.
- Measure on your own hardware.
