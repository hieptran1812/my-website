## Summary

@soldni asks what if you never stop training and announces Olmo 3.1 32B Think and Instruct. The quoted post says they extended their strongest RL run and scaled their instruct recipe to 32B, calling them their most capable models yet.

## Background

Olmo is a family of fully open language models. Think models are tuned for reasoning with RL, and Instruct models for following instructions. Extending an RL run means training longer to keep improving.

## What I took away

- Longer RL runs can keep improving a model, per the authors.
- Olmo releases are open, so you can study them.
- Try both variants for your use case.
