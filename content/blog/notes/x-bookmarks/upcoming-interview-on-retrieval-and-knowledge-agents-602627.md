## Summary

The author will interview @mrdrozdov from Databricks about retrieval. Topics include RL for knowledge agents, learning representation programs for retrieval, and where the harness fits. They invite questions on retrieval.

## Background

Retrieval means finding relevant documents to give a model as context. Knowledge agents search and reason over sources to answer questions. Training them with RL and designing their harness are active research areas.

## What I took away

- Look for the interview later for practical retrieval insights.
- Retrieval quality often limits agent quality more than the model does.
