## Summary

The author says they nodded along to everything in a paper until the GSPO-token part, and now have no idea what is going on. The quoted post is a light note about reading a nice paper with headphones while the house is quiet.

## Background

GSPO is a reinforcement learning method for LLMs related to GRPO, working with sequence-level rather than token-level ratios. It is a recent and subtle area, and token-level variants can be confusing. The post is a humorous reaction rather than a technical claim.

## What I took away

- Even experienced researchers get stuck on RL method details.
- Token-level and sequence-level objectives behave differently in LLM RL.
- It is fine to reread a tricky section slowly.
