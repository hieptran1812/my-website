## Summary

@annanay jokes that HFT market making is out and being a 5-second-horizon superforecaster at Kalshi or a financial-markets post-training neolab is in. The quoted post says an analysis of over 1 billion Kalshi trades found that about 15,000 superforecasters, specifically 14,694 people, have significantly better predictive ability than other traders and largely explain market accuracy.

## Background

Prediction markets let people trade contracts on event outcomes, and prices act as probability estimates. Superforecasters are people with consistently better forecasting records. This is educational context, not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Market accuracy may come from a small group of skilled participants.
- Measuring forecasting skill needs many resolved predictions.
- Joke career takes are not career advice.
