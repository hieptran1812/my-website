## Summary

The author says an open-source agent named **Feynman** takes a topic, searches papers, synthesizes findings and verifies claims against real sources. It returns a cited research brief, and he claims it does in seconds what takes PhDs hours.

## Background

Research agents chain search, reading and writing steps. Verifying claims against sources aims to reduce made-up facts. The speed claim is promotional and quality should be tested.

## What I took away

- Check citations yourself, even from verifying agents.
- Try the tool on a topic you know to judge quality.
- Treat PhD-hours comparisons as marketing.
