## Summary

SPAR is a virtual, part-time program that connects students and professionals with established researchers. Projects last 3 months and take 5 to 20 hours a week. The post lists 130+ projects across AI safety, policy and security, with an apply-by date of January 14.

## Background

Programs like this let people gain research experience without leaving a job or school. Mentored projects are a common way to enter AI safety work. The deadline in the post is for the January 2026 round.

## What I took away

- Part-time mentored programs are a low-cost way to try AI safety research.
- Check deadlines early; this one closed January 14.
- Projects cover policy and security as well as technical work.
