## Summary

NVIDIA says SynthID Detector is now available to everyone. They watermark content generated by NVIDIA Cosmos on build.nvidia.com, so anyone can upload it to check it came from their models. The quoted Google post says the detector checks content from Google AI and partners including OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao and, soon, Apple.

## Background

SynthID is a watermarking method that hides a signal in AI-generated images, video, audio or text so it can later be detected. Shared detection across companies makes provenance checks more useful.

## What I took away

- Watermark detection only works for content from participating providers.
- Provenance tools help but do not prove content is human-made.
