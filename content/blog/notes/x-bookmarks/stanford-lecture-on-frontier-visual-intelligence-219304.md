## Summary

The author shares Session 4 of the Stanford frontier systems lecture, Frontier Visual Intelligence Systems with Andi Blattmann from Black Forest Labs. Timestamps cover GANs to latent diffusion and the Stable Diffusion breakthrough.

## Background

Latent diffusion generates images in a compressed space, which made Stable Diffusion practical. The lecture covers that history.

## What I took away

- Good overview of image generation history.
- Use timestamps to jump.
- Free learning resource.
