## Summary

The author compares specs between Hopper, Blackwell and Rubin. Rubin NVLink bandwidth is faster than H100's HBM bandwidth, and this omits a 3-5x FP4 FLOPS increase from Blackwell to Rubin. The quoted post marvels at the Hopper to Vera Rubin jump, including 48x memory bandwidth and 31x NVLink bandwidth.

## Background

HBM is the fast memory attached to the GPU, and NVLink connects GPUs to each other. When the link is faster than older memory, multi-GPU systems behave more like one big chip.

## What I took away

- Interconnect bandwidth is growing as fast as compute.
- These are the authors' reported figures.
