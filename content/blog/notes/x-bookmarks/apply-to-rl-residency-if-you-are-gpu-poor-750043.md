## Summary

The author says that if you are GPU poor you can just do things, and invites people to apply to the RL Residency for compute grants. The quoted post is the program announcement.

## Background

GPU poor means having little access to compute. Programs that provide grants let people run experiments they otherwise could not.

## What I took away

- Compute access need not block research.
- Apply and propose a concrete project.
- Check eligibility on the program page.
