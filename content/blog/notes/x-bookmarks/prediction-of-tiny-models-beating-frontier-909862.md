## Summary

The author says imagine a 1B model beating Opus 5.5 and calls it the future. The quoted post argues LLMs are full of noisy data and brute-force training, and that removing inefficiencies gives a path for local AI to win.

## Background

The claim assumes training efficiency gains can make small models rival frontier ones. It is a prediction, not a result.

## What I took away

- Small local models could improve a lot.
- This is a forecast, not evidence.
- Watch for data quality gains.
