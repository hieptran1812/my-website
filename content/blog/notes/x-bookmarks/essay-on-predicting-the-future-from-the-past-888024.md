## Summary

Mihika Kapoor calls an essay mandatory holiday reading. She highlights the line that the future is often difficult to predict because it always disguises itself as the past. She links it to the saying that people overestimate short-term impact of new technology. The post is cut off.

## Background

The saying she refers to is commonly about overestimating short-term change and underestimating long-term change. It is a reminder that new technology is often seen through old frames.

## What I took away

- New technology is often judged by old frames.
- Expect slow short-term change but big long-term change.
