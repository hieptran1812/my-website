## Summary

The author invites people to join Anthropic and help Claude learn real-world knowledge work. The role owns the strategy for domains such as finance, legal and automation: sourcing high-value tasks, designing RL environments and reward signals, and evaluating capability gains. They are looking for Staff+ research engineers; the link goes to an Anthropic job board.

## Background

An RL environment is a setup where a model attempts tasks and gets a reward signal for good results. Designing realistic environments and rewards for fields like law and finance is a key way labs improve models on professional work.

## What I took away

- Domain-specific RL environments are a major lever for model capability.
- Reward design is a specialized skill in demand at labs.
- Open the job link for exact requirements.
