## Summary

The author claims, in all caps, that a crawler bot plus Jev and Grok bots turned $100 into $23,879 while they slept, and that their previous crawler post got 1.2M views. The text describes the crawler as a bot handling scam coins and cuts off.

## Background

Posts that show huge returns from bots, with no verifiable track record, are a common pattern in crypto engagement farming and scams. Small accounts with screenshots are easy to fake.

## What I took away

- Treat unverifiable profit claims as marketing, not evidence.
- If a strategy really made 200x, the author has little reason to share it publicly.
- Never connect wallets or pay for bots promoted this way.
