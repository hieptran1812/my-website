## Summary

The author says their team at HRT is hiring to build LLMs for trading from scratch. They claim it is one of few places to do real frontier LLM research, from pretraining to reasoning, on a B200 GPU cluster. They quote a colleague's post announcing a new role on the same LLM team.

## Background

A B200 is a recent NVIDIA data-center GPU used for large model training. Training from scratch means not starting from an open model's weights. The post is a recruiting message, so the claims about being frontier-level are the author's own.

## What I took away

- Some quant firms run serious in-house pretraining programs.
- Compute access is a main selling point in AI hiring.
- Treat recruiting claims as marketing and check details yourself.
