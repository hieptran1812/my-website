## Summary

Tongyi Lab says the Qwen3 Technical Report and Group Sequence Policy Optimization (GSPO) were recognized in the Hugging Face Daily Papers Wrapped 2025. They thank the Qwen team and the community for votes. The quoted post lists the top 10 most upvoted papers of the year.

## Background

GSPO is a reinforcement learning method for training language models that works on whole sequences rather than single tokens. The Hugging Face Daily Papers page ranks papers by community upvotes, so it is a rough signal of attention, not quality.

## What I took away

- Upvote lists are a quick way to find the year's notable papers.
- Qwen3 and GSPO are worth reading if you follow open models and RL for LLMs.
