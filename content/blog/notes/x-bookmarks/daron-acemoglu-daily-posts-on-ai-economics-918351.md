## Summary

The author recommends Nobel economist Daron Acemoglu's daily posts on AI as useful for thinking about the issues. The quoted post asks whether the AI boom can continue without a large rise in inequality, citing a paper by Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh on how much revenue the AI industry needs to recover its investment.

## Background

AI investment is huge, and repaying it requires large revenues. Economists ask who captures those gains and how it affects workers and inequality.

## What I took away

- Follow economists for perspectives beyond the tech industry.
- Revenue needed to justify AI capex is a useful lens on hype.
