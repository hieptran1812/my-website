## Summary

@lateinteraction asks why retrieval people are not working on sparse attention, saying all the techniques look like poor man's IR. The quoted post explains a new sparse attention method combining parts of existing ones such as DSA's top-k indexer.

## Background

Sparse attention picks a subset of tokens to attend to, like retrieval picks documents. Indexers resemble search ranking.

## What I took away

- Sparse attention overlaps with information retrieval.
- Cross-field ideas may help.
