## Summary

@akshay_pachaar says the RL framework behind GLM-5.2 is open source. According to the post, GLM-5.2's full post-training ran on it in about two days, and the same stack backs the GLM series from 4.5 to 5.1. It is called slime and keeps a single RL kernel. The text is cut off after that.

## Background

Post-training with RL improves a pretrained model using reward signals, often for reasoning and coding. A framework like slime handles rollouts, scoring and weight updates. Having it open source lets others reproduce the training setup.

## What I took away

- The RL training stack of a frontier open model is available to study.
- Treat the two-day figure as the author's claim.
