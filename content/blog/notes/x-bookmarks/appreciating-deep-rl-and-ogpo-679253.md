## Summary

@max_simchowitz says he was told to avoid deep RL as hacks and alchemy, but came to appreciate the thoughtful research on why things work. The quoted post introduces OGPO for robot learning with no per-interaction compute limit, accepted to ICML26.

## Background

Robot learning is limited by costly real-world interaction. Off-policy methods reuse old data. OGPO asks what RL looks like if compute per interaction is free.

## What I took away

- Deep RL has more principled work than its reputation.
- Real-world interaction is the bottleneck in robotics.
