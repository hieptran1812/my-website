## Summary

The author says they basically figured out the prompts and approach for this scenario, an ink-wash wuxia action game. The quoted post says a mecha game video was made with GPT-image-2 and Seedance2.0, from character selection to skills, gameplay footage and cutscenes, all produced by AI.

## Background

GPT-image-2 generates images and Seedance is a video model. Chaining them can produce game-style trailers without a game engine. The result is video, not a playable game.

## What I took away

- Image models plus video models can make polished concept footage.
- Consistent prompting is the key skill.
