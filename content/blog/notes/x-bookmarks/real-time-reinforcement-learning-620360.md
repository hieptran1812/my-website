## Summary

@aneeshers introduces Real-time RL, noting that in the real world time is not free and the environment moves while you compute your next action. They show RL agents learning to adaptively think in real-time games.

## Background

Most RL assumes the world pauses while the agent decides. In real-time settings, longer thinking costs missed opportunities, so agents must balance thought and action.

## What I took away

- Thinking time has a cost in live settings.
- Agents can learn when to think more or less.
