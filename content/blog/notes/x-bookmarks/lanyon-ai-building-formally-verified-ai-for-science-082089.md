## Summary

Jonathan Gorard says he has been quiet because he is building a new kind of formally verified AI for science, math, engineering and more at Lanyon AI. The quoted post from Lanyon AI says the company will gradually come out of stealth over the coming days and weeks, with a message from its CEO, Gorard.

## Background

Formal verification means proving with a machine-checked system that a result or program is correct, rather than trusting it. Combining it with AI aims to get outputs that are not just plausible but provably right.

## What I took away

- Formal verification is a growing direction for making AI outputs trustworthy.
- Details are not in the post; wait for the company's technical material before judging.
