## Summary

The author says FreeVideo and FreeToken will enable a fully automatic short-drama pipeline: VLMs write prompts and storylines, FreeVideo generates videos and assets, and FreeToken orchestrates. The quoted post introduces FreeVideo, which brings MiniMax H3 and Video DeltaNet to consumer hardware with as little as 8GB VRAM and 16GB RAM, with ComfyUI integration.

## Background

Running video generation locally depends on memory-saving tricks like offloading and efficient attention. ComfyUI is a popular node-based interface for building image and video generation workflows.

## What I took away

- Video generation is now feasible on consumer GPUs per the authors.
- Chaining VLMs and video models is a practical pipeline pattern.
