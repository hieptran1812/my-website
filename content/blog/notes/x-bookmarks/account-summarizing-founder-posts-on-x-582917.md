## Summary

The author promotes an account that summarizes the greatest founder posts on X and says it is free. The quoted post claims to show how to launch an AI company from $0 to $1M+ from a founder with $30m ARR.

## Background

Summary accounts curate long threads into short posts. Such posts often promise big results from a simple recipe. Success stories are usually survivor-biased.

## What I took away

- Be wary of zero-to-millions recipes.
- Read the original source rather than a summary of a summary.
- Pick lessons you can test cheaply.
