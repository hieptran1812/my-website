## Summary

The list: Kimi K2.5 (Feb 2), GLM 5 (Feb 12), MiniMax M2.5 (Feb 12), ByteDance Seed-2.0 (Feb 13), Nanbeige 4.1 3B (Feb 13), Qwen 3.5 (Feb 15), and Cohere's Tiny Aya (Feb 17). He hopes DeepSeek V4 comes soon.

## Background

Open-weight models are released with downloadable weights. A dense month of releases shows how fast the field moves.

## What I took away

- Use release lists to keep track of open models.
- Evaluate on your own tasks, not release hype.
