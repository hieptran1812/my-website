## Summary

@anshulkundaje says Goodfire has a history of massively overhyped claims, and that the ones in his own field, a new biomarker for Alzheimer's and annotating VUS variants, are overblown. He puts the new interpretability claim in the same category. He quotes Christopher Potts's posted comments on the same interpretability piece.

## Background

VUS stands for variants of uncertain significance, genetic changes whose effect on disease is unknown. A biomarker is a measurable signal of a disease. Domain experts are often best placed to judge AI claims in their field.

## What I took away

- Check how domain experts react before accepting AI-for-science claims.
- A lab's track record is useful context for new claims.
- This disputes the enthusiastic neuron-interpretability posts in this batch.
