## Summary

The post claims one Polymarket account turned $50 into $435,000. It says the owner reverse-engineered the strategy and had AI build a similar bot in 40 minutes. The bot supposedly exploits price lag, runs locally in Rust, and makes $400-700 a day. These are the author's unverified claims.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on event outcomes. A latency or price-lag strategy tries to trade before prices update. Posts like this are often promotional and do not show verified results, fees, risk or capacity limits.

## What I took away

- Treat huge profit claims from a single post as unverified marketing.
- Ask about fees, slippage, capacity and survivorship before believing a bot story.
- This is not trading advice.
