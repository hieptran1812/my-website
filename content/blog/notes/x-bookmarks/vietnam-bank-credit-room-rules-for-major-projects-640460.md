## Summary

@Steven_Research reports, in Vietnamese, that the State Bank of Vietnam reportedly agreed to exclude outstanding debt of 18 projects of Vingroup, Sun Group and Masterise from credit room calculation. The quoted post says Circular 25/2026/TT-NHNN raises the cap on short-term funds used for medium and long-term loans from 30% to 40% from 01/07/2026.

## Background

Credit room is the limit on how much a bank can lend. Excluding some loans frees capacity for more lending. This is described as reported news, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Regulatory changes affect bank lending capacity.
- Check official sources, since the first claim is reported.
