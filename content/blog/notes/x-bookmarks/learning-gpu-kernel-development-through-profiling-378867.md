## Summary

After more than 6 months of on-and-off study, the author says **profiling should be the starting point** for learning GPU/TPU kernel development. The method is to profile, ask questions, look for answers, and read along the way. The post links a blog about the journey.

## Background

A profiler shows where a program spends time and what the hardware is doing. For kernels, that tells you whether you are limited by compute or memory. Starting there gives you concrete questions to study.

## What I took away

- Learn kernels by profiling real code, then asking why.
- Let measurements drive what you read next.
- Profiles reveal bottlenecks that theory alone does not.
