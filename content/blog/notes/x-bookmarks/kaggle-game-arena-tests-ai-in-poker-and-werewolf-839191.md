## Summary

Google says it is advancing AI benchmarking by letting models play games such as Poker and Werewolf in the Kaggle Game Arena. The quoted post says results for Werewolf, Poker and Chess were added, testing contextual communication, building consensus and handling ambiguity.

## Background

Games give a controlled sandbox with clear outcomes, which is hard to get in open-ended tasks. Werewolf and Poker involve hidden information and persuasion, so they test social skills as well as logic. This complements standard benchmarks.

## What I took away

- Games can test skills that static question sets miss.
- Hidden information tasks probe communication and deception handling.
