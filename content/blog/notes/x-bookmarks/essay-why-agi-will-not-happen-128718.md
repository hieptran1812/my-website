## Summary

@hardmaru recommends Tim Dettmers' essay Why AGI Will Not Happen. It discusses diminishing returns and risks of scaling, and contrasts the Western winner-takes-all approach of building the biggest thing with a long-term focus on practicality in the East.

## Background

Scaling means making models and training runs bigger to improve results. Diminishing returns means each extra unit of compute yields less gain. The essay is an opinion piece arguing these limits matter.

## What I took away

- Scaling may hit limits in cost and returns.
- Practical deployment and scaling are different strategies.
- Read the essay to judge the argument yourself.
