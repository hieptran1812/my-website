## Summary

@tomaarsen agrees that machine learning is not just text generation. The quoted post celebrates Google's Embedding Gemma 2 trending on Hugging Face and argues that media and Silicon Valley talk only about reasoning LLMs while many other useful models exist, like embeddings, decision models, audio and video.

## Background

Embedding models turn text or images into vectors so similar items have similar vectors. They power search, recommendation and retrieval for RAG systems. They are small, cheap and widely deployed.

## What I took away

- Small specialized models like embeddings often deliver more value per dollar.
- Do not ignore non-generative models when designing systems.
- See the DigUp note in this batch for an embedding app example.
