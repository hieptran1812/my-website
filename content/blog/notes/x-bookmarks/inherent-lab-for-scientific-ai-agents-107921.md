## Summary

Louis Kirsch announces he co-founded **Inherent**, which he calls a recursively self-improving lab for scientific AI. He cites prior work automating AI research and building AI Scientists at DeepMind. The quoted announcement says Inherent is designed from scratch to build agents that discover new knowledge.

## Background

AI scientist agents try to form hypotheses, run experiments and learn from results. Recursive self-improvement means the system helps improve itself. These are goals stated by the founders, not proven results.

## What I took away

- More startups are betting on automated scientific discovery.
- Treat self-improving claims as goals until results are shown.
- Watch for published outputs from the lab.
