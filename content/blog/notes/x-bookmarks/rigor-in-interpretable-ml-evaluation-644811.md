## Summary

@giangnguyen2412 notes that Doshi-Velez and Kim wrote a mostly math-free paper with a taxonomy of application-grounded, human-grounded and functionally-grounded evaluation. The quoted post introduces the Standard Interpretable Model, a user-aware general theory for designing interpretable methods.

## Background

Interpretable ML tries to make model decisions understandable to people. Debate persists on how to define and evaluate interpretability. A taxonomy of evaluation types helps researchers say what a claim actually proves.

## What I took away

- Define who the explanation is for before evaluating it.
- Know the three evaluation types.
