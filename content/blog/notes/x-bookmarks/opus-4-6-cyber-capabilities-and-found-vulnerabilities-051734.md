## Summary

The author says Opus 4.6 is a step up in cyber capabilities and that Anthropic found 500 validated high-severity vulnerabilities in open source code with its models, then worked to disclose and patch them. They say we are in the middle of a security inflection point. The quoted post announces Claude Opus 4.6 with 1M token context in beta.

## Background

Models that can find bugs help defenders but also attackers. Responsible disclosure means telling maintainers privately so they can fix issues before details go public.

## What I took away

- AI vulnerability finding is now at scale.
- Disclosure and patching matter as much as finding.
- The count is the author's reported figure.
