## Summary

Sergey Levine announced that the latest Deep RL lectures are online as a YouTube playlist. Thanks to Seohong Park, CS185/285 for Spring 2026 is available for anyone to watch, with a course website at rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse. He apologizes for a few recording glitches.

## Background

Deep reinforcement learning combines neural networks with learning from rewards through trial and error. It underpins robotics research and much of modern LLM post-training, so a strong foundation helps with understanding methods like RLHF and GRPO.

## What I took away

- A free university course is a structured way to learn RL fundamentals.
- Pair lectures with the course website's assignments for real understanding.
