## Summary

Karpathy says he is interested in what a era of highly bespoke software looks like. As an example he set up a regimented experiment to lower his resting heart rate from 50 to 45. The rest is cut off.

## Background

With LLMs, small custom tools for a single person become cheap to build.

## What I took away

- Build small tools for your own needs.
- Custom software becomes viable.
