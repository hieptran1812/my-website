## Summary

@shiyi_c98 ends the thread on Evolving Agent Teams (EAT, NeurIPS'26) by pointing to more results in the GitHub repo and the open evolution traces on Hugging Face. The repo description says a supervisor agent evolves teams of agents.

## Background

Evolution traces are records of how the agent teams changed over time during optimization. Releasing them lets others study what the supervisor changed and why, not just the final scores.

## What I took away

- Open traces make agent research easier to audit and learn from.
- Check the repo for setup details before trusting headline results.
- Studying how a system changed over time can teach more than final numbers.
