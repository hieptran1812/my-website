## Summary

The author built an arbitrage bot for Polymarket using Google's Antigravity. The bot works, finds setups and sends transactions, but execution is brutal. The quoted post lists 17 Polymarket trading bots he has been watching, mostly on 15-minute Bitcoin Up/Down markets, and he plans to study them and build his own.

## Background

Arbitrage means exploiting price gaps for low-risk profit. Finding a gap is easy in theory, but orders must fill at the right prices quickly, and that is where bots often fail. Slippage and latency can erase the edge.

## What I took away

- Spotting an opportunity is not the same as capturing it.
- Execution quality is often the real bottleneck for trading bots.
- Copying other bots' behavior still requires careful testing.
