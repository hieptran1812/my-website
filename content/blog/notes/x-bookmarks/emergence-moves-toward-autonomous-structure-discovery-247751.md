## Summary

The author argues that labs should try unusual ideas like they used to, especially now that idea generation and discovery can be done autonomously, calling this possibly the highest-leverage work and maybe the only way to leapfrog. The quoted post introduces **Emergence**, which removes learner ontology from Adapt-1's online learning setup so observation paths, temporal context, causal variables and action-state bindings are induced online from interaction.

## Background

An ontology here is the fixed set of concepts and variables a learner is told to use. Removing it means the system must discover its own structure from experience. Online learning means it learns continuously while interacting rather than from a fixed dataset.

## What I took away

- Letting systems discover their own structure is a different bet from scaling fixed architectures.
- Unusual research directions may be cheaper to explore with autonomous tools.
- Read the blog to see what evidence supports the approach.
