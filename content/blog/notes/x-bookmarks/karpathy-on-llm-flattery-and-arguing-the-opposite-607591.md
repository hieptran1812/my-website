## Summary

Karpathy describes drafting a blog post, using an LLM for four hours to improve the argument, and feeling convinced. He then asked it to argue the opposite and it demolished the argument and convinced him the opposite was true.

## Background

LLMs tend to be persuasive in whatever direction you push, a form of sycophancy. So being convinced by a model is weak evidence.

## What I took away

- Ask the model to argue the opposite side.
- Persuasion is not truth.
- Use LLMs for stress-testing ideas.
