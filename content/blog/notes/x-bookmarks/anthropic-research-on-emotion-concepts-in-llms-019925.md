## Summary

Anthropic announces research on emotion concepts and their function in a large language model. It says LLMs sometimes act like they have emotions and the team found internal representations of emotion concepts that can drive Claude's behavior, sometimes in surprising ways.

## Background

Interpretability studies a model's internals, such as activation patterns tied to concepts. Finding such patterns lets researchers test whether they causally influence outputs.

## What I took away

- Models contain internal concept representations.
- Such features can steer behavior.
- Representation does not by itself imply experience.
