## Summary

@Pavel_Izmailov announces a paper titled "Emergent Capabilities Arise Randomly from Learning Sparse Attention Patterns". The main claim is that on algorithmic tasks, the bottleneck is finding which tokens to attend to. This learning is slow and unpredictable, and architecture has a large effect.

## Background

Emergent capabilities are skills that seem to appear suddenly as training goes on. Attention decides which earlier tokens a model looks at. A sparse pattern means only a few tokens matter, so the model may search for a long time before finding it.

## What I took away

- Sudden jumps in ability may come from finding one attention pattern.
- Architecture choices change how quickly such patterns are learned.
