## Summary

The author read the paper Chain-of-Visual-Thought (COVT) and says it teaches VLMs to see and think at the same time, using continuous visual tokens instead of words. They argue that forcing models to reason in words loses visual information. The text is cut off.

## Background

A VLM is a model that handles images and text. Standard chain of thought writes reasoning in words. Reasoning in visual tokens aims to keep details like shape and position.

## What I took away

- Text-only reasoning can lose visual detail.
- Read the paper for how visual tokens are trained.
