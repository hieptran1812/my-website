## Summary

The author lists four arbitrage types on prediction markets and says he mostly uses two because he trades only on Polymarket. The first, in-market arbitrage, is one of the most common in his trading. The rest of the thread is cut off.

## Background

In-market arbitrage means exploiting prices inside a single market, for example when YES and NO prices sum to less than $1. Other types usually span multiple venues or related markets. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Arbitrage can occur within one market or across markets.
- Fees and fills decide whether a gap is actually profitable.
