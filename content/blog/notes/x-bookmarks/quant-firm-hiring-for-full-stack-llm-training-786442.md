## Summary

The author says their team is adding people to its LLM group quickly. They already build models of markets and ask why the world needs another LLM in 2026. They say they have a unique story and are hiring across the **full training stack**, from pre-training to post-training, with big resources and ownership.

## Background

Pre-training teaches a model from raw data. Post-training shapes it afterwards, for example with fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. Trading firms are interested in LLMs because they work on large, noisy data. A job post like this shows where talent demand is moving.

## What I took away

- Trading firms are now hiring for end-to-end LLM training, not just using APIs.
- Full-stack experience (pre to post training) is a marketable skill.
- Ask what a new model offers that existing ones do not before building it.
