## Summary

The author says that after 6 years and hundreds of backtests, almost every profitable strategy falls into four types. He explains how each works and when, with examples.

## Background

A backtest tests a strategy on historical data. Strategy categories such as trend following or mean reversion organize ideas. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Classifying strategies helps organize ideas.
- Backtests can overfit.
- Know when a strategy works, not just how.
