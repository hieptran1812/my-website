## Summary

The author says latent structure learned from audio is more likely to match real physical structure, because audio is tied to the arrow of time. The quoted post says audio is probably the highest fidelity recording of physical phenomena, while images are lossy 2D projections of 3D space.

## Background

Representation learning builds internal features from data. The argument is that audio is causal and temporal, so it may teach world structure better than images.

## What I took away

- Time-ordered data may capture causality.
- Modality choice affects what models learn.
- This is an opinion, not a result.
