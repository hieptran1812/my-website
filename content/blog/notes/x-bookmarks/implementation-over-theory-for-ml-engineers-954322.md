## Summary

The author says that for an ML engineer implementation beats everything: knowing is theory, implementing is understanding. They list topics in the resource, including reinforcement learning with PPO and DQN, transformers from classical ones to Retro, Switch and GPT models, and diffusion models such as Stable Diffusion, DDPM, DDIM and U-Net.

## Background

PPO and DQN are common reinforcement learning algorithms. DDPM and DDIM are diffusion sampling methods, and U-Net is the network often used in them. Re-implementing such methods is a hands-on way to learn how they work.

## What I took away

- Implement an algorithm to really understand it.
- Pick one topic from the list, such as PPO or DDPM, and code it from scratch.
- Open the link to see the full topic list.
