## Summary

The author writes about MiMo-V2.6 and the difficulty of scaling RL. They say it is very likely one of the largest single RL runs by compute that any open-source model team has done. Despite scarce compute, they dedicated a team of several dozen people to it; the rest is cut off.

## Background

RL post-training improves reasoning by rewarding good outputs, and it has become a major share of total training compute. Large RL runs are hard because they mix generation and training and can become unstable.

## What I took away

- RL compute is now a big part of frontier model budgets.
- Read the full write-up for lessons on stability at scale.
- Dedicated teams per run show how engineering-heavy RL has become.
