## Summary

@YiMaTweets announces that lectures for his course Principles of Deep Representation Learning (HKU DATA8014, Fall 2026) are on YouTube, with more being added. He plans to open-source the slides on the textbook website, and the textbook is open access.

## Background

Representation learning studies how neural networks learn useful internal features from data. A principled course connects deep learning to math such as compression and geometry, which helps explain why architectures work.

## What I took away

- A free university course and textbook are available for self-study.
- Learning the theory behind representations helps when reading research papers.
- Check back for slides as they are released.
