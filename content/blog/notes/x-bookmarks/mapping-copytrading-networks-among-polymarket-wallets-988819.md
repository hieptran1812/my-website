## Summary

The author says that instead of building another copytrading bot, coldvisionXYZ built a system to expose who is copying who. They used Louvain clustering and Jaccard similarity to map 300 top Polymarket wallets into a hypergraph. The text ends before the result.

## Background

Louvain clustering finds communities in a network. Jaccard similarity measures overlap between two sets, such as markets two wallets traded. Together they can reveal groups that behave alike.

## What I took away

- Graph methods can reveal hidden relationships in public data.
- Similar behavior does not prove coordination.
- The result is not stated in the post.
