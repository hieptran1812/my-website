## Summary

The author invites people to join the AI recommendations and advertising engineering team. The quoted post asks engineers with ads experience to apply for greater impact and says the current team has just 18 engineers.

## Background

Ad and recommendation systems are large machine learning stacks that rank content and ads. A job post like this signals the company is investing in that area and is looking for experienced people.

- Ads experience is the stated requirement.
