## Summary

Elon Musk says AI content will vastly exceed all human content. The quoted post claims AI-written output exceeded human-written output in 2025.

## Background

The claim depends on how output is counted. It raises questions about training on AI-made data.

## What I took away

- Check how such totals are measured.
- AI text may feed future training data.
- Trend claims need sources.
