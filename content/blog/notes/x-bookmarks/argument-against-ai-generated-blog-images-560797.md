## Summary

The author points to the number one Hacker News story, which argues against using AI-generated images for blogs. They urge companies to hire a freelance illustrator, noting The Consensus can afford it, and ask developers simply not to use AI images.

## Background

Many blogs use AI image generators for header images. Critics say these images look generic, can lower reader trust, and take work from illustrators.

## What I took away

- Visual choices on a blog affect how readers judge its quality.
- Consider simple diagrams or real illustrations instead of generic AI images.
