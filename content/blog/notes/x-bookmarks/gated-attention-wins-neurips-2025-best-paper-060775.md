## Summary

The author congratulates Alibaba. The quoted post announces that the paper 'Gated Attention for Large Language Models: Non-linearity, Sparsity, and Attention-Sink-Free' received the NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper Award.

## Background

Gated attention adds a learned gate that scales the attention output, adding non-linearity and sparsity. An attention sink is when a model puts excess attention on the first token, which the title says this approach avoids. NeurIPS is a top machine learning conference.

## What I took away

- Small architecture tweaks like gates can matter at scale.
- The paper title names its three claims: non-linearity, sparsity, no attention sink.
