## Summary

The author lists everything he uses to trade, grouped into analysis, execution, news, organization and comms. Analysis tools include TradingView for market scans and plans, Aggr v3 for tape reading and real-time flows, and MMT for liquidity scans. The list is cut off after that.

## Background

Traders combine charting, order flow and liquidity tools to see market state. This describes one person's setup, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Separate tools by job: analysis, execution, news.
- Open the post for the full list.
- A tool stack does not create an edge.
