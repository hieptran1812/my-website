## Summary

The author says understanding this article will teach you more about inference than 90% of people, and that it is only the first resource in the AI performance engineering repo. The quoted post from Wafer introduces their series and calls part 1, All About Transformer Inference, the starting point.

## Background

Inference performance depends on memory bandwidth, compute, batching and parallelism. Foundational articles explain how to estimate these costs from first principles, which is useful before tuning real systems.

## What I took away

- Start inference learning with a first-principles article before tools.
- Use the Wafer series as a structured reading list.
- Ignore the percentage claims; focus on actually working through the math.
