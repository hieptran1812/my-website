## Summary

The author lists that the product supports LoRA, full fine-tuning, SFT and pre- and mid-training, under the slogan own your intelligence. The quoted post introduces Model Factory to train, deploy and continuously improve models on one stack, on demand, on dedicated clusters or bring your own.

## Background

LoRA trains small adapters, FFT updates all weights, and SFT trains on examples. Offering all on one platform targets teams that want their own models instead of relying on APIs. It is a product announcement.

## What I took away

- Teams can choose between LoRA and full fine-tuning.
- One stack may ease train and deploy loops.
- Treat it as a product pitch.
