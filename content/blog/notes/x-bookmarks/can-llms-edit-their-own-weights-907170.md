## Summary

@minhnguyen asks if anyone has tried using open-source LLMs of different sizes to edit their own weights. He reasons that big LLMs are smarter while small ones have simpler weights, and wonders about a middle ground. He links a draft, Towards Self-Programmable Interpretable LLMs, describing an interpretable MoE LLM whose weights can be programmed in natural language.

## Background

Self-editing means a model changes its own parameters to alter its behavior. Interpretable designs aim to make weights understandable, which would make such edits safer and easier. This is an open research idea, not a result.

## What I took away

- Model size trades off capability against weight complexity for editing tasks.
- Interpretable architectures could make weight editing practical.
- This is a research question worth testing with small open models.
