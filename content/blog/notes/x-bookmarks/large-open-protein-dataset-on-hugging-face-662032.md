## Summary

The author says open source has caught up with closed source by a huge margin and credits two people at Litefold. The quote says they released the biggest protein data collection on Hugging Face, after more than 3 weeks of curating, filtering, splitting, sharding and analyzing raw data.

## Background

Protein models like structure predictors need large, clean datasets. Releasing curated data openly lets more groups train and compare models. The claim of being biggest is theirs.

## What I took away

- Data curation is major work behind open models.
- Open datasets let others reproduce and extend results.
- Check dataset licenses before use.
