## Summary

The author jokes that project number 16 on the list should be an AI-native gateway that filters slop on Twitter, claiming it reduces time to first brain tumor by 98%. The quoted post is the list of AI infrastructure projects that starts with tuning vLLM or SGLang configs and claims default configs waste 60% of VRAM.

## Background

The joke plays on TTFT (time to first token), a standard LLM serving latency metric. It pokes fun at listicle-style career advice posts.

## What I took away

- Treat must-build lists with some humor and skepticism.
- TTFT is a real serving metric worth knowing.
