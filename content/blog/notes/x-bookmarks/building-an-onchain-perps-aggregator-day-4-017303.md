## Summary

The author posts a day-4 build log: fixed an Ostium integration (46 markets live), added dark mode, and got QFEX pulling data, with 100+ tradfi perp markets across 11 DEXs planned. The quoted post pitches onchainmarkets.fun as an aggregator for onchain perps on stocks, commodities, forex, indices and pre-IPO assets.

## Background

A perp (perpetual future) is a derivative that tracks an asset price with no expiry date. Onchain means these trade on decentralized exchanges (DEXs) instead of traditional brokers. An aggregator collects many venues in one place so users can compare markets.

## What I took away

- Public build logs are a simple way to keep momentum on a project.
- Aggregators add value by solving discovery across many venues.
- This is a project update, not trading advice.
