## Summary

vLLM lists four things one model can do: text to video with synced sound, first or first-plus-last frame to video with audio, image plus audio to a lip-synced clip, and green-screen footage plus background video to an auto-relit composite. It thanks MiniMax for mentioning vLLM-Omni. The quoted MiniMax post introduces Awesome MiniMax H3 Integrations, an index of tools built around H3, from 24GB VRAM local ComfyUI setups to enterprise SGLang and vLLM-Omni serving.

## Background

A single multimodal video model can handle many conditioning inputs, such as text, frames, audio or video. vLLM-Omni serves such models in production. ComfyUI is a node-based tool for running generation models locally.

## What I took away

- One video model can cover several tasks depending on inputs.
- An integrations index is a quick way to find setups for your hardware.
- Local runs on 24GB GPUs are listed as possible for H3.
