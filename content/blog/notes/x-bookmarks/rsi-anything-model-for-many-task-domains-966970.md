## Summary

The author introduces **RSI-Anything**, described as one RSI model that works across coding, GUI, planning and science tasks. The post links a paper on arXiv and code on GitHub, with tags for world models, agents, AI for science and JEPA.

## Background

RSI stands for recursive self-improvement, where a system improves its own abilities over repeated rounds. JEPA is a family of models that learn by predicting representations of missing parts of the input rather than raw pixels or tokens.

## What I took away

- Read the paper to see what RSI means concretely in their setup.
- Broad claims across many domains deserve a close look at the evaluation.
