## Summary

The author says happy Olmo day. The quoted announcement introduces Olmo 3, a fully open LM suite for reasoning, chat and tool use that shares the whole training flow, not just final weights. It claims the best fully open 32B reasoning model and best 32B base model.

## Background

Fully open means data, code, checkpoints and recipes are shared, so others can reproduce and study the work. The best-in-class claims are from the announcement. This makes Olmo useful for research.

## What I took away

- Fully open releases let you study the whole training pipeline.
- The 32B best claims are the team's own.
- Good base for learning how models are built.
