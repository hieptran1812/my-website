## Summary

The post claims the weights for the X algorithm have been publicly released and that the highest boost to a post comes from copying its link to share it. It invites readers to try it on this post for a surprise.

## Background

Social media ranking algorithms score posts based on engagement signals like likes, replies and shares. The invitation to try it for a surprise suggests the post may be a playful engagement prompt rather than a careful technical claim.

## What I took away

- Treat claims about ranking algorithms skeptically unless you can check the source.
- Posts asking you to act for a surprise are often engagement bait.
