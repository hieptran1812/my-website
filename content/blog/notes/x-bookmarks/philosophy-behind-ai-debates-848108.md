## Summary

The author says it is a golden age for philosophy, or should be. The quoted post shares a guide to philosophical questions shaping AI debates. It argues most AI debates are not about evidence, so people fall back on philosophy, political intuitions or tribal identity.

## Background

Many AI disagreements, such as whether models understand or what risk is, cannot be settled with data yet. The guide tries to separate the underlying assumptions. Seeing them helps you argue more clearly.

## What I took away

- Identify the assumption behind an AI disagreement before arguing about facts.
- Some debates are value or philosophy questions, not measurement questions.
