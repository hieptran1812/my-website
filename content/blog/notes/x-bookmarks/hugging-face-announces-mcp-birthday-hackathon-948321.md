## Summary

Hugging Face says this may be the biggest AI hackathon ever, with more than 6,300 registrants, running two weeks from Nov. 14 to 30, open to anyone virtually. It offers $20,000 in cash prizes and $3.5M+ in sponsor credits, hosted by Anthropic and Gradio with 10 sponsors. The quoted post invites people to the kickoff for MCP's first birthday.

## Background

MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is a standard way for AI apps to connect to tools and data. A hackathon lets developers build with it quickly. The size claim is the poster's.

## What I took away

- MCP has reached its first anniversary.
- Hackathons are a way to try new tooling and win credits.
- Check the rules and dates on the official page.
