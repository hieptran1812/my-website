## Summary

The author claims some coders quietly make $5k to $100k per month on Polymarket with spike-hunting bots. The idea is that news hits, the market panics, the price jumps 1 to 4% within seconds and then calms, and the bot captures the bounce.

## Background

A spike-hunter trades short-lived price moves caused by news. The earnings figures are unverified, and the strategy competes with other fast bots. Posts like this often highlight winners and skip losses.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of unverified profit claims.
- Short-term edges are crowded and execution-sensitive.
- Ask what the losing trades and costs look like.
