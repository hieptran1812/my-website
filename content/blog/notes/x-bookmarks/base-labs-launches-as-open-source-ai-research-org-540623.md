## Summary

Base Labs announces a dedicated research organization focused on open-source AI. It says it wants a healthy open frontier model ecosystem and lists blue-sky research on continual learning, the science of RL, and how models learn. The post is cut off after the first item.

## Background

Continual learning means a model keeps learning new things after deployment without forgetting old skills, which current LLMs do poorly. Blue-sky research is open-ended work without a direct product goal. New open labs matter because most frontier work happens in closed companies.

## What I took away

- Continual learning and the science of RL are named as open research gaps.
- Watch whether new open labs publish models, papers, or both.
- Open frontier ecosystems depend on organizations willing to fund basic research.
