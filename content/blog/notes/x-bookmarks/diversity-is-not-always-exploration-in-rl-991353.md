## Summary

@sheriyuo likes the idea that not all diversity is exploration. The paper argues diverse trajectories can come from new reasoning paths or reshuffled memorized patterns, and treating both as exploration may waste RL compute.

## Background

In reasoning RL, models sample many answers and learn from rewards. Encouraging diversity is meant to improve exploration. If variety is only surface-level, compute is wasted.

## What I took away

- Measure real novelty, not surface variety.
- Do not equate diversity with exploration.
