## Summary

The author says Claude Opus 4.6 destroyed every benchmark. He lists 76% on MRCR v2 versus 18.5% for Sonnet, top Terminal-Bench 2.0, 144 Elo above GPT-5.2 on GDP-val, a 1M token context and 128k output tokens. These are the author's figures.

## Background

MRCR tests whether a model can retrieve details from very long context. Terminal-Bench measures command-line agent tasks. Elo is a rating that compares models head to head. Benchmarks are useful but do not predict every real task.

## What I took away

- Check numbers against the official model card.
- Benchmarks and real work can differ.
