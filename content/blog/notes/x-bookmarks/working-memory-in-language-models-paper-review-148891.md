## Summary

@NeelNanda5 calls the paper excellent and says he wrote a review at Anthropic's request. He has long suspected models keep working memory for intermediate variables during a forward pass. The quoted Anthropic post says only a small fraction of what happens inside Claude is accessible in a workspace, like conscious thought in people.

## Background

A forward pass is one run of the model through its layers. Working memory here means a place to hold intermediate values while computing. Finding such a structure would help explain how models reason.

## What I took away

- Models may have a limited shared workspace that mirrors a human-like divide.
- Interpretability work can test long-held intuitions.
- Read the paper and the review for the evidence.
