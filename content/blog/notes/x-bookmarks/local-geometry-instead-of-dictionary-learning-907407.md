## Summary

The author asks whether people still use SAEs and says it is time to move from dictionary learning to local geometry. The quoted thread says leveraging local geometry gives a natural region-based decomposition of LM activations with better steering and localization.

## Background

Sparse autoencoders learn a dictionary of features from activations. Steering means changing model behavior by editing activations. A region-based view studies local neighborhoods of activation space instead.

## What I took away

- SAEs are not the only way to decompose activations.
- Better steering is the claimed benefit.
- Read the thread for the method.
