## Summary

The author admits bias but thinks this method or a close variant can give the interpretable, mechanistically faithful components of an arbitrary neural network that people have long sought. The quoted post describes decomposing a model's weights, including attention.

## Background

Mechanistic faithfulness means the components truly explain how the model computes, not just correlate with outputs. Finding such parts has been a main goal of interpretability.

## What I took away

- Faithfulness is the key test for any interpretability method.
- Authors of a method are biased judges; look for independent replication.
