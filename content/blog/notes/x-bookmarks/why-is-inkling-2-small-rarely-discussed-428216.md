## Summary

The author asks whether he is in the wrong twittersphere, since few talk about Inkling 2 Small, which can be downloaded since yesterday and has interesting bits. He notes people analyze Chinese models for features like DeepSeek Sparse Attention, HCA, Attention Residual, mHC and iHC. The link card points to the Hugging Face model page.

## Background

Open weights let people study architecture details. Attention variants and residual changes are active areas, and each new model may add ideas worth reading.

## What I took away

- Open weights invite architecture study.
- Read the model card for design details.
- Do not ignore western open models.
