## Summary

The author says OpenAI ran a hiring challenge, Parameter Golf, but the top candidate was one they could not hire: their autonomous research agent, Aiden. Aiden ran 22 days and outperformed all 1,016 other researchers. This starts a thread.

## Background

Parameter Golf is a challenge about getting strong results under tight model-size limits. An agent that runs experiments around the clock can try many ideas, but this result is the author's claim.

## What I took away

- Long-running research agents are being tested against humans.
- Read the thread for how fair the comparison is.
