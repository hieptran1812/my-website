## Summary

The author claims quants are cooked because someone one-shotted an arbitrage tool. It says Polymarket, Kalshi, DraftKings and FanDuel often price the same event differently, and buying both sides locks in guaranteed profit. The post is cut off.

## Background

Arbitrage means profiting from price differences across venues. In practice, fees, limits, settlement rules, execution delays and account restrictions can erase the gap. Guaranteed profit is the author's claim.

## What I took away

- Check settlement rules and fees before assuming risk-free profit.
- Be skeptical of guaranteed profit claims.
- Educational only.
