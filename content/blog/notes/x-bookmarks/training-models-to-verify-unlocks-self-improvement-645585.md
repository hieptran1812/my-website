## Summary

@AdtRaghunathan says verification is easier than generation and explicit training for it unlocks self-improvement, with claimed 14x boost in test-time refinement and 30% gain beyond the RL plateau. The quoted post says models can be trained to pinpoint their own errors.

## Background

If a model can judge its own work, it can revise it. Numbers are the authors' claims.

## What I took away

- Train the verifier skill too.
- Verification enables iteration.
