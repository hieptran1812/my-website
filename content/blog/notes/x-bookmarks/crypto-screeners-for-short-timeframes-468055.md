## Summary

The author shares an article on crypto screeners. He says he cannot imagine trading altcoins on 1 minute without a screener giving a directional bias, a regime bias and alerts for good setups.

## Background

A screener filters many markets by rules. Regime means the current market state, such as trending or ranging. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Screeners save attention but do not remove risk.
- Short timeframes bring high costs and noise.
