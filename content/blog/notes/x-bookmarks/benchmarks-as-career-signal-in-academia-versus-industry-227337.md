## Summary

The author says creating a benchmark can be a negative signal in academia, since people assume you may churn out low-quality papers, though this is a bias. In industry it is a positive signal and brings exposure. The quoted post argues a PhD can mostly build benchmarks now, and doing it well teaches problem definition.

## Background

A benchmark is a standardized test for comparing models. Building a good one needs a clear question and careful design. Perceptions differ by community, so the value depends on your audience.

## What I took away

- Benchmark building teaches problem definition.
- Signals differ between academia and industry.
- A good benchmark can bring visibility.
