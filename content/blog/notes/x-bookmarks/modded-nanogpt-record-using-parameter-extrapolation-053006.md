## Summary

@yacinelearning shares for those curious about the harness used for this SOTA. The quoted post says tensorqt reached a 2925-step run, 5 fewer than the previous record, by adding a late parameter-space extrapolation step.

## Background

Modded-NanoGPT is a speedrun benchmark to train a small GPT to a target loss in the fewest steps. Parameter-space extrapolation pushes weights further along their recent direction. The harness mention suggests an automated search setup.

## What I took away

- Small tweaks still move speedrun records.
- Late extrapolation is a cheap trick to try.
