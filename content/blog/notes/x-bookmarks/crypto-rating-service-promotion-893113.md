## Summary

The post advertises Coira's Research, which scores 400+ cryptos on six fundamentals: sustainability, transparency, revenue, innovation, community and tokenomics. It implies LUNA losses could have been avoided by checking the sustainability score. It is an ad.

## Background

Rating services are marketing and may not predict outcomes. Hindsight claims about past failures are easy to make.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of ads that use past losses as a hook.
- No score replaces your own research. This is not advice.
