## Summary

Karpathy says people have poor intuition about how large the space of intelligences is. Animal intelligence, the only kind we have known, is a single point that arose from a very specific kind of optimization, one fundamentally distinct from how AI systems are built.

## Background

Animal minds were shaped by evolution for survival. LLMs are shaped by different pressures, such as predicting text and being rated by humans. So we should not assume they think or fail like animals or people.

## What I took away

- Do not map human intuitions directly onto LLMs.
- Different training pressures produce different kinds of minds.
- This view helps explain odd model strengths and weaknesses.
