## Summary

The author summarizes Bybit's response over the exchange withholding a relative's profit of 35k to 1 billion VND. The exchange said the trader violated trading policy without explaining why, then asked them to accept getting principal back and giving up profit. The quoted post makes similar angry accusations. These are one side's claims.

## Background

Exchanges can freeze funds for policy violations, and disputes are hard to settle. These posts are one-sided and unverified.

## What I took away

- Exchange counterparty risk is real.
- Keep records and read terms of service.
- Unverified accounts need caution.
