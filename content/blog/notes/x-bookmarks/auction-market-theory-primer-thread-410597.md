## Summary

@DailyProfilerCo says that after studying auction theory and TPO with other traders, they found 30-minute TPO charts showed repeatable daily distributions between 09:30 and 16:00, set by the market environment. The thread is a primer on Auction Market Theory. The quoted line, attributed to J. Dalton, says price is an advertising mechanism, time regulates opportunities, and volume measures their success or failure.

## Background

Auction Market Theory views a market as an auction where price searches for fair value. TPO (time price opportunity) charts show how much time price spends at each level. This is educational background about a charting method, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Auction theory looks at price, time and volume together.
- TPO charts show where the market spent time during the day.
- Claims of repeatable patterns should be tested before relying on them.
