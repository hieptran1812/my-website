## Summary

LuxAlgo announces QuantCharts for everyone. It lets you run any Pine Script indicator outside TradingView, offers L1 orderflow for crypto, US equities and futures on all plans including free, and includes a coding agent to build, backtest and journal strategies on charts.

## Background

Pine Script is TradingView's language for indicators and strategies. L1 orderflow means best bid and ask data, which shows short-term buying and selling pressure.

## What I took away

- Being able to move scripts off one platform reduces lock-in.
- Backtests are only as good as the data and assumptions behind them.
- Educational context only.
