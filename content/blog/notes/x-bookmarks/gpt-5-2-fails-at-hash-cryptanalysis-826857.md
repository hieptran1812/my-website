## Summary

@Teknium replies that it might be the last thing you want. The quoted post says GPT-5.2 in deep research mode ran for 27 minutes on cryptanalysis of a hash function and produced nonsense. It was wildly over-confident and claimed false discoveries.

## Background

Cryptanalysis means looking for weaknesses in cryptographic functions. It is a hard task where false claims are easy to make. The example shows models can sound sure while being wrong, especially on tasks with no easy way to check.

## What I took away

- Long thinking time does not guarantee a correct answer.
- Over-confident output on hard technical tasks needs verification.
- Prefer tasks where results can be checked automatically.
