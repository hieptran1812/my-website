## Summary

The author recommends a clearly written LLM eval guide that uses a customer-service assistant example to explain how to test, what to test and how to interpret numbers. The quoted post says it is for engineers, product managers and CEOs in plain language.

## Background

LLM evals measure whether an AI feature actually works. Understanding the numbers prevents false confidence.

## What I took away

- Evals are for everyone shipping LLMs.
- Examples make concepts concrete.
- Bookmark for product work.
