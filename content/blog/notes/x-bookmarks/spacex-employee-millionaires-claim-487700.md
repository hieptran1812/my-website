## Summary

@heyshrutimishra claims 4,400 SpaceX employees became millionaires in one day, 400 worth over $100 million, including welders, technicians and cafeteria staff, because the company paid all levels in equity for two decades.

## Background

The post is about employee equity, which pays workers in company ownership. These numbers are the author's claim and are not verified here. It is a business story, not a technical one.

## What I took away

- Verify viral numbers.
- Broad equity ownership is a notable compensation idea.
