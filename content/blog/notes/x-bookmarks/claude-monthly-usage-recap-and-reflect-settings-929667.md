## Summary

Anthropic introduces a way to reflect on how you use Claude. A monthly recap shows when you use Claude most and what you worked on. It offers options to set quiet hours and nudges to take breaks, found in Settings under Reflect.

## Background

Usage dashboards help people see their habits with a tool. Quiet hours and break nudges are wellbeing features meant to prevent overuse.

## What I took away

- Reviewing your own AI usage patterns can show where it helps most.
- Quiet hours can protect focus time from constant tool use.
