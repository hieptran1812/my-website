## Summary

Binance Research says oil's ceiling is in, with $110 Brent pricing in a month-plus closure of the Strait of Hormuz, and that policy and market mechanisms such as strategic reserves have yet to engage. The quoted Reuters-linked post says the G7 will discuss a joint release of emergency oil reserves.

## Background

Strategic reserves are stockpiles governments can release to cool prices during supply shocks. This is a market view, not advice.

## What I took away

- Policy actions like reserve releases can cap price spikes.
- Treat price forecasts as opinion.
