## Summary

Alibaba Cloud introduced Wan3.0 in public beta. It offers 30-second video generation, what it calls reality-grade rendering, and omni-reference inputs across text, images, audio and video. Listed API pricing is $0.05 per second at 480p, $0.10 at 720p and $0.20 at 1080p, with API access coming soon.

## Background

Video generation models create clips from prompts and reference media. Omni-reference means the model can be conditioned on several input types at once. Per-second pricing makes cost estimates easy: a 30-second 1080p clip would cost about $6 at the listed rate.

## What I took away

- Estimate video generation costs by multiplying clip length by the per-second rate.
- Beta quality claims should be tested with your own prompts.
