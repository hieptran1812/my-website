## Summary

The post claims a 79% win rate on prediction markets from 25 years of data. It lists 38 indicators including moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger bands, volatility, OBV, momentum, sentiment and insider activity. The win rate is the author's claim.

## Background

A win rate alone says little. Backtest results can be inflated by overfitting, leakage of future data, or ignoring fees and odds. Many indicators on one dataset raises overfitting risk.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of high win rates without out-of-sample testing.
- Win rate is not profit; payouts and costs matter.
- This is educational, not a recommendation.
