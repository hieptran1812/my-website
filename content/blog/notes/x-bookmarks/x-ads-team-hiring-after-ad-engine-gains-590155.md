## Summary

The author asks engineers with ads experience to apply, saying the team has just 18 engineers. They quote a post saying the X ad team made drastic improvements to the ad engine, making it that user's best performing channel of the year.

## Background

An ad engine decides which ads are shown to whom and at what price. Improving it directly affects advertiser results, which is why such a team would recruit.

## What I took away

- The performance praise comes from one advertiser, so it is anecdotal.
- Job posts reveal where a company is investing.
