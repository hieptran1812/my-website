## Summary

The author says MiniMax-M2.5 MLX runs in LM Studio on an M3 Ultra Mac Studio with 512GB. He says it is fast enough out of the box to host OpenClaw, n8n workflows and Open WebUI for a team.

## Background

MLX is Apple's framework for running models on Apple chips. Large unified memory lets big models fit locally. LM Studio is an app to run models.

## What I took away

- Big-memory Macs can serve large open models locally.
- Test speed on your own workload.
