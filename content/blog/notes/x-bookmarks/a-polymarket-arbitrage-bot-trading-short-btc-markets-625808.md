## Summary

@lorden_eth says they spent 48 hours finding a bot wallet on Polymarket. They say it trades only BTC Up or Down (15 min) markets with the same strategy, waiting until one outcome falls below 35 cents and then buying more. The rest of the thread is not in the text.

## Background

Prediction market shares for Yes and No outcomes are priced between 0 and 1. In an arbitrage setup, a trader tries to buy both sides at a combined price that is lower than the payout. This is the author's reading of one wallet, and nothing here is advice.

## What I took away

- Prediction market bots often follow simple, repeatable rules.
- Treat posts about profitable wallets skeptically; they are unverified.
- This is described for education, not as a recommendation.
