## Summary

@natalieyeo says they flew 20+ hours from Singapore to hack at the xAI hackathon with 500 top developers, jet lagged but determined to ship. The quoted post says it is live with over 500 handpicked developers, and that first-place winners in each track win an all-expenses trip to a Starship launch.

## Background

Hackathons let people build prototypes in a short time with prizes as motivation. This post just documents attendance at the event.

## What I took away

- Hackathons are a way to build quickly and meet other builders.
- The effort people put into attending shows how competitive the event was.
