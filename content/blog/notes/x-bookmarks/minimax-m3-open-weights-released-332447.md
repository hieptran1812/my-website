## Summary

@SkylerMiao7 says MiniMax M3 weights are live and calls it the number one open-weights model on the AA Intelligence Index. The quoted post lists about 428B total parameters, 23B active, and links to MiniMax Sparse Attention.

## Background

Total versus active parameters are typical of MoE models, where only a part runs per token. The ranking is the author's claim from a third-party index.

## What I took away

- Active parameters drive inference cost.
- Rankings are claims; test yourself.
