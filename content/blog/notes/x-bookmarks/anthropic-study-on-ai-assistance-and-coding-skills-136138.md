## Summary

Anthropic notes that AI speeds work but may make learning new skills harder. In an experiment with software engineers, coding with AI led to a decrease in mastery, but this depended on how people used it.

## Background

Mastery means actually understanding a skill, not just getting the task done. Using AI to hand off thinking may skip the learning. Using it to ask and explain may differ.

## What I took away

- How you use AI affects what you learn.
- Try to understand code before accepting it.
- Speed and learning can trade off.
