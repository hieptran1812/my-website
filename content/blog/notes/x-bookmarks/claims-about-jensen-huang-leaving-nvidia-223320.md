## Summary

The author tells a dramatic story that Jensen sacrificed his role after being falsely guilted over a spy and honeypot story, and says the board did not vote him out per the 8-K. The quoted post says NVIDIA filed an 8-K on Nov 12 saying he resigned as CEO and director, with no vote held.

## Background

An 8-K is a US filing that companies use to announce major events. Its text is the reliable source. The surrounding narrative in the post is the author's interpretation and may be speculation.

## What I took away

- Prefer the filing over commentary.
- Dramatic narratives may be unsupported.
- Verify news with primary sources.
