## Summary

The quoted FBI post says North Korea was responsible for the theft of more than $6 billion in virtual assets from Binance on or about October 29, 2026. @cz_binance thanks the FBI, USSS, IRS and others for tracking and freezing funds, and says much of the credit for why bitcoin did not drop more goes to them.

## Background

State-backed hacker groups have targeted crypto exchanges for years. Attribution and fast freezing of funds can limit both losses and market panic.

## What I took away

- Exchanges are high-value targets for state-level attackers.
- Law enforcement coordination can reduce market impact of hacks.
- Treat market-impact claims as the author's view.
