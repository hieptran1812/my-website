## Summary

The author says they caught up with Karpathy for a No Priors episode covering the phase shift in engineering, AI psychosis, claws, AutoResearch, a SETI-at-Home style movement in AI, the model landscape and second-order effects. Timestamps are listed, such as what capability limits remain.

## Background

SETI-at-Home was a project where volunteers donated spare computer time. The idea here is a similar volunteer network for AI work. Autoresearch is a loop where agents run experiments automatically.

## What I took away

- Karpathy's views on autoresearch are a good entry point to the topic.
- Volunteer compute is an idea to keep an eye on.
