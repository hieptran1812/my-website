## Summary

@therobotjames says individuals trying to make money trading should read the quoted piece, which he wrote himself. It argues that you should stop trying to beat Djokovic at tennis: there is no beginners' market, so you compete for good prices against the best, and many people are better at markets than you.

## Background

Markets have no beginner tier, so new traders face professionals directly. The essay is an opinion about why individual trading is difficult. It is educational and not advice.

## What I took away

- Ask who is on the other side of your trade and what edge you have over them.
- Self-recommended content is still the author's opinion.
