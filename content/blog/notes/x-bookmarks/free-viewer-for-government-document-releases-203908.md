## Summary

The author says they made a viewer for the Epstein files because the government's website is poor. It is hosted for free on Vercel, and the post gives the link.

## Background

Public document releases are often hard to search and browse. Small independent front ends can make the same data easier to explore. Free hosts like Vercel let a person ship such a tool quickly.

## What I took away

- A better interface over public data can be a useful small project.
- Free hosting makes weekend tools easy to share.
- Check the source of any third-party viewer before relying on it.
