## Summary

@teortaxesTex jokes that a Brazil municipal employee found a 1000x faster way to finetune LLMs with a weird trick, which is insane and frontier labs hate him. The quoted post is the claim that Rio 3.5 is about 0.6 Nex N2 Pro plus 0.4 Qwen 3.5.

## Background

The joke imitates clickbait ads to mock the story. The point seems to be that blending two existing models is not a breakthrough in finetuning. Read it as satire, not a technical claim.

## What I took away

- Spot satire when reading AI news.
- Merging existing models is not new training research.
