## Summary

The author is releasing 53 slides on post-training, covering core algorithms like DPO and GRPO, data quality, synthetic data pipelines and on-policy training. They presented it as a guest lecturer in Cambridge, UK.

## Background

Post-training adapts a pretrained model with methods such as DPO (learning from preference pairs) and GRPO (a reinforcement learning method that compares groups of sampled answers). Data quality and synthetic data are major factors in results.

## What I took away

- Slides are a compact overview of DPO, GRPO and data pipelines.
- Data quality is as important as the algorithm.
- On-policy training uses samples from the current model.
