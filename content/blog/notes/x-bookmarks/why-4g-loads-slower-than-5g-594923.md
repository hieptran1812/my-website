## Summary

The post asks why the same website takes 3 seconds on 5G and 30 seconds on 4G with the same content.

## Background

Possible causes are bandwidth, latency, many round trips, large assets, and connection setup. A tenfold gap suggests heavy pages or many requests.

## What I took away

- Measure latency and bandwidth separately.
- Reduce requests and asset sizes.
