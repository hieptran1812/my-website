## Summary

The author lists a long satirical chain of programs and employers (MATS, SPAR, BlueDot, big tech, publishing) and says if you missed this it is over. The quoted post complains about a former boss with a thin resume.

## Background

The post jokes about credential chasing in AI safety. It is satire and not literal advice.

## What I took away

- Do not take satire as career advice.
- Credentials are not the only path.
- Focus on real work.
