## Summary

The author built a tool because he was tired of scrolling Twitter for alpha. It tracks the consensus of favorite analysts in one place and provides daily market summaries in 17 languages at unbias.fyi.

## Background

Alpha means trading ideas that may beat the market. Aggregating many analysts can reduce noise, but consensus is not proof that a call is right. This is a product announcement, not advice.

## What I took away

- Aggregating opinions saves time but does not validate them.
- Treat analyst consensus as information, not a signal to act on.
