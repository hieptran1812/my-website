## Summary

The author introduces Lattice Deduction Transformers, an 800k-parameter looped transformer that reasons like a SAT solver. They claim 100% on Sudoku-Extreme with only 15 minutes of training. It is a collaboration between Axiom Math, Amherst College and Barnard College.

## Background

A looped transformer reuses the same layers repeatedly, like iterating a reasoning step. SAT solvers search by deduction, and Sudoku is a classic constraint problem.

## What I took away

- Small looped models can solve structured reasoning tasks.
- This is a claim on one benchmark.
