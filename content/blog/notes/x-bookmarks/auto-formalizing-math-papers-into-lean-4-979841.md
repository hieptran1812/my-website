## Summary

The author says mathematical papers need formal validation, usually done informally by a referee. He asks whether auto-formalization into Lean 4 could be a more robust approach, where the referee only checks that the formulations are stated correctly.

## Background

Lean 4 is a proof assistant, a language where proofs are checked by a computer. Auto-formalization means an AI translates a human-written paper into Lean. If the proof compiles, the logic is verified, and humans only need to check that the definitions and theorem statements match the intent.

## What I took away

- Formal proofs move the checking burden from proof details to statements.
- Auto-formalization could change how math is reviewed.
- Humans still need to verify that the formal statements are faithful.
