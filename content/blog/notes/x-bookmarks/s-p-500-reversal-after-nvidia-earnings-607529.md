## Summary

The post says the S&P 500 lost about $2 trillion of market cap in 5 hours, its fastest reversal since Liberation Day. It says Nvidia went from up 6% to down 3% after reporting record revenue of $55 billion, without any new headlines, and promises an explanation.

## Background

Market cap is the total value of a company's shares. A sharp reversal after strong earnings suggests investors were worried about something else, such as an AI spending bubble. The figures are the author's, and this is news, not advice.

## What I took away

- Good earnings do not always mean the stock rises.
- Nvidia's results are watched as a signal for AI spending.
- Not a recommendation to trade.
