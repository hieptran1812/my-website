## Summary

The author says Anthropic is launching an openclaw competitor: Dispatch lets you text Claude to do work while away and agents do it, with persistent memory also launched. The quoted post says Dispatch in Claude Cowork is a research preview: one persistent conversation that runs on your computer, messaged from your phone.

## Background

The agent runs on your computer while you send instructions from your phone. Persistent memory means it keeps context across sessions. It is a research preview, so features may change.

## What I took away

- Phone-to-desktop agent control is becoming standard.
- Research preview means expect rough edges.
