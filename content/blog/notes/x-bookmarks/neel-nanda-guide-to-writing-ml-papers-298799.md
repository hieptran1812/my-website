## Summary

Ahead of the ICML deadline, Neel Nanda shares his post on writing good ML papers. The quoted post says that after supervising 20+ papers he wrote the guide to make the process less opaque, emphasizing scientific integrity. It may help NeurIPS submissions too.

## Background

Writing a paper well is a skill rarely taught. A guide helps with framing, evidence and honesty.

## What I took away

- Read the guide before submitting.
- Scientific integrity is part of paper quality.
