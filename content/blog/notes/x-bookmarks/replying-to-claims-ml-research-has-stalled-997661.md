## Summary

The author replies that you may just be missing cool papers and links two arXiv papers. The quoted post is a controversial take that ML research has not been great lately and that papers are minor tweaks of old ideas.

## Background

Feeling that research is repetitive is common when you follow only popular feeds. Good work can be hidden among a large volume of papers, so curation matters.

## What I took away

- Read beyond trending papers to find strong work.
- Open the two arXiv links for the recommended papers.
