## Summary

The post says they are indexing the full SEC corpus: every 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K over 30+ years. They expect agents soon to answer questions about any public company, with search coming soon.

## Background

10-K, 10-Q and 8-K are US company filings: annual, quarterly and event reports. Indexing them lets agents retrieve exact passages. It is a product that has not launched yet.

## What I took away

- Filing search for agents is a growing area.
- Check accuracy when it launches.
