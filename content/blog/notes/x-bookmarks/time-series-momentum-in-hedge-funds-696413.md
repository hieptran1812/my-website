## Summary

The author says a 23-page research paper reveals the number one method hedge funds use to beat the market: time series momentum. The post introduces a thread that explains how it works. The thread content is not included in the text.

## Background

Time series momentum means going long an asset whose own past return was positive and short one whose past return was negative. It differs from cross-sectional momentum, which ranks assets against each other. The claim that it is the top hedge fund method is the author's.

## What I took away

- Time series momentum looks at each asset's own trend.
- Be careful with claims that one method is number one for all funds.
- Read the original paper before relying on a thread summary.
