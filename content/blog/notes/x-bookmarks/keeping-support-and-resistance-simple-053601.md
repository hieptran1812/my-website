## Summary

The author says most traders overcomplicate support and resistance. They keep it simple and focus only on swing highs and lows. More detail is in the attached thread.

## Background

Support and resistance are price levels where a market has tended to stop falling or rising. A swing high or low is a local peak or trough in price. This is chart-reading education, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Simple level definitions are easier to apply consistently.
- Swing highs and lows give objective reference points.
