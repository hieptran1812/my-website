## Summary

The author says GeoSpy has evolved into Raven, a frontline visual intelligence system. From a single image it turns pixels into actionable intelligence in seconds, with no metadata required.

## Background

Image geolocation infers where a photo was taken from visual clues alone. Such tools raise privacy concerns because they can locate people from ordinary photos. Capabilities here are the company's claims.

## What I took away

- Photos can leak location even without metadata.
- Consider privacy before posting images.
