## Summary

The post, in Vietnamese, says over 14 billion USD of BTC options expire the next day at 15:00 on Friday. It gives the Max Pain price as 73,000 USD for BTC and 2,300 USD for ETH.

## Background

Max Pain is the price at which option holders lose the most at expiry. Some traders watch it, but its predictive value is debated. This is information, not advice.

## What I took away

- Max Pain is a watched, debated level.
- Expiries can raise volatility.
- Do not treat it as a signal.
