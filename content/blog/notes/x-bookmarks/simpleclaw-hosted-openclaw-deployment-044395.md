## Summary

The author introduces simpleclaw.com, which they say deploys your own always-on OpenClaw instance in under a minute. It targets non-technical people and has limited capacity.

## Background

OpenClaw is an open source personal AI assistant that runs on your own machine. Hosting it normally needs server setup. This service wraps that into a one-click flow, which is a convenience trade-off.

## What I took away

- Hosted wrappers trade control for convenience.
- Check what data a hosted agent can access.
- Promotional post; verify before use.
