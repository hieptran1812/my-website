## Summary

The author says Qwen3.6 35B A3B cannot fill out a paper form alone. With NVIDIA's LocateAnything-3B, the number one trending model on Hugging Face, as its eyes, the two small models succeed. The test is placing each element at the right pixel position on a blank form image.

## Background

Language models are weak at exact pixel locations. A small grounding model finds where things are, and the language model decides what goes there.

## What I took away

- Pair a reasoning model with a specialized perception model.
- Small models can combine to do tasks one cannot.
