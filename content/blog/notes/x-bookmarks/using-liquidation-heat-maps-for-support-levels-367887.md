## Summary

The author says heat maps can help identify support and resistance areas by showing where many liquidations may occur, using leverage and entry data. They also use them with an order-book profile to see where cascades might happen. A longer explanation follows in the thread.

## Background

A liquidation happens when a leveraged position is force-closed. Many liquidations at one price can push the market further in a chain reaction, called a cascade. Heat maps estimate where such positions sit, but the data is inferred.

## What I took away

- Liquidation maps are estimates, not exact data.
- Educational idea only, not a recommendation.
- Combining several signals is the author's suggested approach.
