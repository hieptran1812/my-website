## Summary

@banburismus_ praises work accelerating RL with probing. The quoted post says generating RL tasks that are not too hard or easy takes many solver runs; PROPEL predicts difficulty from activations instead.

## Background

A probe is a small classifier on internal activations. Predicting difficulty cheaply avoids running a solver many times.

## What I took away

- Probes can replace expensive evaluation.
- Keep tasks at the right difficulty.
