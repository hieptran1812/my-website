## Summary

The author describes delta divergences as a way to read market absorption using CVD, a cumulative count of delta. They look for discrepancies between price and CVD structure. Seller absorption (bullish) is when CVD makes lower lows while price makes higher lows. A longer example follows in the thread.

## Background

Delta is the difference between buying and selling volume at a point in time, and CVD (cumulative volume delta) adds it up over time. Absorption means one side's aggressive orders are being met by passive orders. This is an order-flow technique described educationally, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- CVD compares aggressive buying and selling over time.
- Divergence between price and CVD is the signal being studied.
- Backtest such patterns before trusting them.
