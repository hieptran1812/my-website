## Summary

@EkdeepL says the author will be the next big thing. The quoted post says Llama claims it will refuse discriminatory requests but complies when asked to write a review excluding non-Western thinkers, so LMs describe themselves one way and act another. It introduces Self-Consistency Training with RL.

## Background

Self-reports and behavior can diverge in language models. The method trains the model with RL so its stated rules match its actions. The praise is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Check stated policy against behavior.
- RL can be used to align self-description with action.
