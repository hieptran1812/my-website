## Summary

The post points to Kurate, which shows top ranked papers in AI, ML, robotics, quantum physics and more. Hundreds of arXiv preprints are ranked daily by scientific impact using pairwise tournaments judged by Claude, GPT and Gemini. The link card calls it an arena for scientific papers.

## Background

In a pairwise tournament, models compare two papers at a time and the winners are aggregated into a ranking. Ranking by LLM judgment can be useful for triage but reflects model biases.

## What I took away

- Use rankings to discover papers, not to judge quality.
- LLM judges may favor certain styles or topics.
- A daily feed can help keep up with arXiv volume.
