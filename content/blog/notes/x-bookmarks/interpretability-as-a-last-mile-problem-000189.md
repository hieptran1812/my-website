## Summary

@thisismyhat says interpretability looks like a last-mile problem. The quoted thread links sublinear scaling to limited neuron capacity: more features become monosemantic and universal, but model-specific superposed features grow faster.

## Background

Superposition means a neuron encodes several features. The remaining hard features are model-specific, so progress slows.

## What I took away

- Easy features come first.
- The long tail is hard.
