## Summary

The author reacts with Oh my god. The quoted post introduces Silico, a platform for building AI models with the precision of written software, letting researchers see inside models, debug failures and design them intentionally. Early access is open.

## Background

The idea is to treat model building like software engineering, with debugging tools. How well it works is not shown in this post.

## What I took away

- Interpretability is being productized as debugging tooling.
- Early access means claims are not yet widely tested.
