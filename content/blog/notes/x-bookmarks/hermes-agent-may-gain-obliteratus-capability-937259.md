## Summary

The author teases what Hermes-Agent will soon be able to use. The quoted post introduces OBLITERATUS, an open-source toolkit for removing refusal behaviors from open-weight LLMs, with a pipeline of summon, probe, distill, excise, verify and rebirth.

## Background

Refusal behavior is when a model declines certain requests. Removing it from open weights shows that safety tuning can be undone. This has safety implications.

## What I took away

- Safety tuning in open weights can be stripped.
- Consider this when evaluating open-model risk.
