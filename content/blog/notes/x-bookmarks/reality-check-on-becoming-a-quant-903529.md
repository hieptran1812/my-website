## Summary

The author says 99.9 percent will not become a quant, and that they are walking this path now. They note that a plan of 18 months at 2 hours a day sounds like selling a course, and that you must know which quant role you want, such as trader or developer.

## Background

Quant roles differ: traders, developers and researchers use different skills. The percentage is a rhetorical claim. The post pushes for clarity on direction.

## What I took away

- Pick a specific quant role before studying.
- Be wary of simple time-based promises.
- Consistent effort matters more than a plan.
