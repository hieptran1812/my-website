## Summary

The author says Yanik published a guide on scalping with PolyScalping and asked him to share the story behind the project and what comes next. It is aimed at readers interested in scalping, Polymarket tools or the project's future. It reads as promotion of a product.

## Background

Scalping means making many quick trades for small gains. Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on event outcomes. Tools for it are new, and fees and slippage can erase small edges. This is not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Treat guides tied to a product as promotional.
- Small-edge strategies are very sensitive to fees and liquidity.
- Never trade based on a post alone.
