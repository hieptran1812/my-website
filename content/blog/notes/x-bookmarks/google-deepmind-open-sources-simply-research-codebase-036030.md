## Summary

The author replies that they are open sourcing part of their infrastructure for automated research, used for Gemini to evolve itself, in the google-deepmind/simply repository. It is more complex than the nanochat setup but closer to state-of-the-art LLM pre- and post-training while staying as minimal as possible. More is promised.

## Background

JAX is a Python library for fast numerical computing on accelerators. A minimal research codebase is easy to read and modify. Automated research means using AI systems to run and propose experiments.

## What I took away

- Simply is a minimal and scalable JAX research codebase worth reading.
- It sits between toy setups like nanochat and full production stacks.
- Small readable codebases make experiments faster to iterate.
