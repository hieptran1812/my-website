## Summary

Devpost says the Gemini 3 hackathon with Google DeepMind and the Gemini App is live. It invites people to build with Gemini 3's enhanced reasoning and multimodal skills and with Antigravity. Prizes total $100,000, and the post mentions a deadline.

## Background

A hackathon is a timed building contest. Multimodal means the model handles more than text, such as images. Events like this are a low-risk way to try a new model on a real project.

## What I took away

- Hackathons are a quick way to learn a new model.
- Check the deadline and rules on the event page.
