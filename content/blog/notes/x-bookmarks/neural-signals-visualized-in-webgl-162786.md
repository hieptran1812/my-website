## Summary

@alexnortn asks how a brain makes a mind and shares a WebGL demo of neural signals. He says it was inspired by conversations at the Seung symposium, which celebrated research from Sebastian Seung's lab.

## Background

WebGL lets browsers draw graphics on the GPU. Here it is used to animate neural activity, a visual way to explain neuroscience. It is a creative demo more than a technical result.

## What I took away

- Browser GPU graphics are enough for rich science visuals.
- Open the link to explore the demo.
