## Summary

Turing Post praises Sebastian Raschka for a calm debunking. In the quoted post, Raschka notes a wave of hype about OpenAI's **Astra** model, traced to an article in The Information saying Astra is a recurrent-depth or looped transformer. He says new approaches are interesting to read about, but the post is cut off before his full argument.

## Background

A looped or recurrent-depth transformer reuses the same stack of layers several times on its own output, so it can spend more compute per token without adding parameters. The idea is not new, which is part of why claims that it explains a model's strength deserve care.

## What I took away

- Treat secondhand reports about a closed model's architecture as unconfirmed.
- Looped transformers are an older research idea, not a sudden invention.
- Read the original analysis rather than relying on timeline hype.
