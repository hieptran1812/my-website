## Summary

Kaggle announces the Build the Future with Gemini 3 Pro hackathon with Google DeepMind, running December 5 to 7, 2025. It celebrates the launch of Gemini 3 Pro, described as Gemini's most intelligent, capable and versatile model yet.

## Background

Hackathons let developers try a new model quickly and get feedback. Vendors use them to drive adoption. The description of the model is the announcement's own claim.

## What I took away

- Hackathons are a low-cost way to try new models.
- Check dates and rules on the Kaggle page.
