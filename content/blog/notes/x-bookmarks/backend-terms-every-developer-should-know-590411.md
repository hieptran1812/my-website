## Summary

The author lists 10 backend terms, including idempotency, backpressure, circuit breaker, eventual consistency, distributed lock, delivery semantics, cold start, high cardinality metrics and sharding. The quoted post lists AI terms: token, context window, prompt, embedding, temperature, top-p, hallucination, LLM, RAG and inference.

## Background

Idempotency means repeating a request has the same effect as once. Backpressure slows producers when consumers are overwhelmed.

## What I took away

- Check which terms you can explain.
- Learn both backend and LLM basics.
