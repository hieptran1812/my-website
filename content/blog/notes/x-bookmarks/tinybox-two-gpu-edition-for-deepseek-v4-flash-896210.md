## Summary

tinygrad claims **245 single-user tok/s** on DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 using only 2 RTX 6000 Blackwell GPUs. To mark this, the company is launching a 2 GPU edition of its tinybox. It says all hardware to install 4 GPUs is included and tested.

## Background

Single-user tokens per second measures how fast one user sees text generated, which reflects latency rather than total throughput. Running a large open model on just two workstation GPUs makes local deployment cheaper. The tinybox is tinygrad's prebuilt AI computer.

## What I took away

- Single-user speed and multi-user throughput are different metrics; know which one you need.
- Efficient open models are making small local setups viable.
- Buying a box with room to expand can lower upgrade costs later.
