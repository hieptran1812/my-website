## Summary

The author says an ML engineer who does not understand how the Jacobian of the network evolves in training is missing the mechanism controlling feature learning, signal flow and training stability. This is Concept 14, The Jacobian Flow of Deep Networks.

## Background

The Jacobian is the matrix of how the outputs change when the parameters or inputs change. Its size and shape affect whether gradients vanish or explode. Ideas like this sit behind initialization and normalization choices.
