## Summary

The author says that if you have seen cool shader tweets and do not know what a shader is, they wrote something for you. They link a chapter on shaders at makingsoftware.com.

## Background

A shader is a small program that runs on the GPU, often to decide the color of pixels or position of vertices. It is the basis of real-time graphics and also related to general GPU programming.

## What I took away

- Shaders are small GPU programs.
- A friendly entry point into graphics and GPU code.
