## Summary

@zhihanyang_ is excited that a state-of-the-art diffusion LLM may be fully open in weights, code and data. The quoted post gives a sneak peek of the diffusion LLM to be released soon, and notes presentations at ICML 2026.

## Background

A diffusion LLM generates many tokens in parallel by refining a whole sequence, instead of one token at a time. Fully open means anyone can reproduce training, not just use the weights.

## What I took away

- Fully open releases include data, which is rare.
- Diffusion LLMs are becoming a serious alternative to autoregressive ones.
- Wait for the release to check the SOTA claim.
