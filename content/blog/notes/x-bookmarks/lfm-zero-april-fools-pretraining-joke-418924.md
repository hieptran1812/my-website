## Summary

Maxime Labonne calls it a massive unlock: pre-training for $0. The quoted post introduces LFM-Zero as the first foundation model trained on 0 tokens with no pretraining, finetuning or data. It was posted on April 1, so it reads as an April Fools joke.

## Background

Pretraining normally needs trillions of tokens and huge compute. A model trained on nothing would be a joke. The date suggests satire.

## What I took away

- Check the date before believing launches.
- Real pretraining needs data and compute.
- Extraordinary claims need evidence.
