The author says the youngest-ever Citadel managing director explains his process for quantifying and systematizing a discretionary trader's approach. The content is in the attached video or link.

## Background

A discretionary trader decides using judgment, while a systematic approach uses written rules. Turning judgment into rules lets you test it on past data. The post describes the idea only at a high level.
