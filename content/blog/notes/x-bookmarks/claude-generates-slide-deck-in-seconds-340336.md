## Summary

The author says Claude makes a PowerPoint presentation in less than 5 seconds and adds that it is so over. The demo is in the attached media, and the claim is the author's.

## Background

LLMs can produce slide structure and files quickly. Speed does not equal quality, so slides still need review for accuracy and design.

## What I took away

- Fast generation still needs human review.
- Use AI for the first draft of slides.
- Hype lines like so over are not evidence.
