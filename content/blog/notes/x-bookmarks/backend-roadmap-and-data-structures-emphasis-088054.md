## Summary

The author says backend work is more than APIs and CRUD and shares a roadmap that companies like Google, Meta and Amazon expect. They claim that failing data structures and algorithms (DSA) rounds means projects do not matter, and that mastering it opens every door. The rest is cut off.

## Background

DSA covers things like arrays, trees, graphs and sorting, and is the usual topic in big-tech coding interviews. The strong claim that projects do not matter is the author's opinion. Hiring practices vary across companies and roles.

## What I took away

- Big-tech interviews usually test DSA heavily.
- Treat absolute career claims with caution.
- Balance interview practice with real project experience.
