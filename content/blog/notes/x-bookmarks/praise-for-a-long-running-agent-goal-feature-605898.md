## Summary

The author claims the /goal feature lets you turn AI agents into 24/7 workers that run for hours without manual intervention. They call it the most powerful AI feature release of the month and urge people to try it.

## Background

A goal-style command gives an agent an objective and lets it loop on its own until done. Long-running agents can save effort but also risk drifting or wasting tokens without oversight. The post is promotional and gives no evidence or details.

## What I took away

- Goal-driven agents are an emerging pattern: set an objective, let it iterate.
- Unattended runs need guardrails like budgets and checks.
- Hype posts need your own testing before you trust them.
