## Summary

Artificial Analysis announces agentic performance benchmarking for Speech to Speech (S2S) models. It uses tau-Voice to measure tool calling and customer interaction in realistic customer service scenarios. The post text is cut off after saying even the strongest S2S models face difficulty.

## Background

Speech-to-speech models listen and answer in audio directly, without a separate text step. Voice agents must also call tools such as looking up an account, which is harder in real time. Benchmarks like this show where voice agents still fail.

## What I took away

- Voice agents need to be tested on tool use, not just speech quality.
- Benchmarks in realistic scenarios reveal gaps leaderboards hide.
