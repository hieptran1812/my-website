## Summary

Beff Jezos says RenTech used probabilistic graphical models for 20 years before ML was common and points to THRML from his team at Extropic for experimenting with them on specialized accelerators. The quoted post is a conspiracy theory that RenTech found LLMs in the 90s.

## Background

Probabilistic graphical models encode dependencies among variables. The RenTech claim is speculation.

## What I took away

- Graphical models are an older toolkit.
- Conspiracy claims are unverified.
