## Summary

@rasbt jokes that LatentMoE was probably inspired by MLA, which came from LoRA, then SVD, then eigendecomposition.

## Background

LoRA, MLA and SVD all use low-rank structure to compress matrices. LatentMoE is likely similar for experts.

## What I took away

- Low-rank ideas keep reappearing.
- Basics of linear algebra pay off.
