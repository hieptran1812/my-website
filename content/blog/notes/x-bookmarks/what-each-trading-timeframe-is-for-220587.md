## Summary

@RebellioMarket lists timeframes from 3M, 1M, 1W, 1D, 4H, 2H, 1H, 30m, 15m, 5m, 3m, 1m and tick, and explains what each does. For example, 3M shows big macro cycles and long-term capital rotation, 1M shows major trends and institutional positioning, and 1W shows primary direction.

## Background

Different timeframes show different kinds of price structure. Longer ones show trend and context, shorter ones show timing. This is an educational framing from the author, not advice.

## What I took away

- Use longer timeframes for context and shorter for timing.
- Check that signals agree across timeframes.
- Pick a timeframe that fits your style.
