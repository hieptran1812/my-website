## Summary

The author says you can now upload and parse documents with Firecrawl using a new Rust-based parser with layout awareness, smart OCR routing and 5x higher speeds, supporting PDFs, xlsx, docx and html. The quoted post says the endpoint turns files into Markdown or JSON, preserves reading order and tables, and supports Zero Data Retention.

## Background

Agents need clean text from messy files. OCR reads text from images. Preserving tables and reading order keeps the meaning. The 5x speed claim is the vendor's.

## What I took away

- Good parsing is a key step before RAG or agents.
- Check table handling on your own documents.
