## Summary

The author gives a humorous recipe: have a problem, be delusional enough to think you are smart enough to build a database, start, accidentally get into YC, make the fastest graph database in the world, and reach product-market fit.

## Background

The post jokes about how daunting building a database company is. Product-market fit means customers actually want what you built. The tone is humor, not a guide.

## What I took away

- Hard infrastructure problems start with someone willing to try.
- It is humor and should not be read as a plan.
- Product-market fit is the real test.
