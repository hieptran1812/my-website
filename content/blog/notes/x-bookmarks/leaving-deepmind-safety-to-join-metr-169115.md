## Summary

The author left Google DeepMind's AGI safety team three weeks ago to join METR. They enjoyed their work at GDM and turned down offers from Anthropic and OpenAI, which surprised friends and family. They say they chose this because of how high they see the stakes; the rest is cut off.

## Background

METR is an organization that evaluates AI models for dangerous capabilities from outside the labs. Some safety researchers believe independent evaluation is needed to hold labs accountable.
