## Summary

The author says Qwen 3.5 runs fully locally on an iPhone 17 in airplane mode, so AI subscriptions become optional. The quoted post says the model runs on iPhone 17 Pro, beats models four times its size, offers visual understanding and toggled reasoning, using a 2B 6-bit model with MLX.

## Background

6-bit quantization compresses weights to run in limited memory. MLX is Apple's framework for Apple Silicon. A 2B model is far smaller than cloud models, so quality differs.

## What I took away

- Small quantized models can run on phones.
- Subscriptions optional is the author's claim; test quality yourself.
- Privacy is a benefit of local inference.
