## Summary

The author published a blog post explaining Anthropic's Optimization team take-home exam, which was retired because Claude Opus 4.5 effectively solved it. The quoted Anthropic post describes how they designed and redesigned the exam.

## Background

A take-home exam is a hiring task candidates do at home. When an AI model can solve it, it no longer separates strong candidates. Anthropic's write-up covers designing tests that stay hard for AI.

## What I took away

- Hiring tests need updating as models improve.
- The blog is a walk-through of the performance task itself.
- Read Anthropic's engineering post for design lessons.
