## Summary

The author is excited about a wafer mention. The quote welcomes Wafer to Infron as a provider, saying Wafer's AI agents automatically do what used to take a team of performance engineers, optimizing GPU inference across hardware. Infron is described as a unified AI gateway.

## Background

An AI gateway routes requests to many model providers through one API. Automatic inference tuning means agents search for the best serving configuration. The claims are vendor claims.

## What I took away

- Inference optimization is being automated by agents.
- Gateways make it easy to switch providers.
- Test tuned configs against your own baseline.
