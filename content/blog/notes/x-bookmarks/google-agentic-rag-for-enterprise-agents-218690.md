## Summary

The author says Google introduced an agentic RAG framework for Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Traditional RAG searches once, grabs a few documents and generates. The quoted post says the multi-agent workflow breaks down complex queries and searches iteratively for enough context before answering.

## Background

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) means fetching documents and giving them to the model to answer from. Agentic RAG lets the system plan, search several times and check coverage first, which helps with complex questions.

## What I took away

- One-shot retrieval often misses context on hard questions.
- Iterative search plus a sufficiency check is the core idea.
