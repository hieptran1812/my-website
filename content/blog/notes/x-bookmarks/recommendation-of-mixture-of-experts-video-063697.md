## Summary

Mishig praises the creator as the 3blue1brown of deep learning and recommends his videos. The quoted post announces a video on Mixture of Experts covering FFN roles, scaling without slowing down, load balance and stability.

## Background

MoE models route each token to a few expert networks, so they have many parameters but use little compute per token. Load balancing keeps experts evenly used.

## What I took away

- MoE scales parameters without scaling compute.
- Load balance is a key stability issue.
- Watch the video for a visual intro.
