## Summary

@zhyncs42 says TokenSpeed launched about six weeks ago and now powers part of Together AI's serverless production traffic. The author says it balances performance and developer experience. The quoted post announces a Together AI workshop at AI Engineer World's Fair on how inference engines work for agentic coding workloads.

## Background

An inference engine is the software that runs a model efficiently for many users. Agentic coding creates long, tool-heavy sessions that stress these engines differently from chat. Production use is a stronger signal than a launch announcement.

## What I took away

- New inference engines can reach production quickly.
- Agentic workloads change serving requirements.
