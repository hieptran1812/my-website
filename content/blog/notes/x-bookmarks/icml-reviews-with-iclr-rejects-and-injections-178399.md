## Summary

The author says 3 of the 6 ICML papers he reviews are ICLR rejects. He also says every paper has a prompt injection to detect LLM use by reviewers.

## Background

ICML and ICLR are major ML conferences. Resubmission after rejection is common. The injection matches the trap in another post here.

## What I took away

- Resubmission is common in ML.
- Reviewers should know policy on AI use.
