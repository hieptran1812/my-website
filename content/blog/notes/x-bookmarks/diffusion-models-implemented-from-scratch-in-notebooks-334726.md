## Summary

@ludocomito shares notebooks implemented from scratch to learn major concepts of diffusion models step by step. The implementations are kept simple and derived from first principles, starting with an unconditional DDPM.

## Background

A diffusion model generates data, such as images, by learning to remove noise step by step. DDPM (denoising diffusion probabilistic model) is the basic version. Unconditional means it generates samples without any text or label guidance.

## What I took away

- Building small versions from scratch is a good way to understand diffusion.
- Start with plain DDPM before moving to conditional or advanced variants.
