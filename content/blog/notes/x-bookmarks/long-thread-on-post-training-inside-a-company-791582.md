## Summary

The author announces a big thread about post-training, written to sort out for themselves how they would do it inside a company. It covers why bother at all, how to measure, which data, and which model, in that order.

## Background

Companies often ask whether to fine-tune a model or just prompt a good one. Measurement and data come before picking a model, because without evals you cannot tell if tuning helped.

## What I took away

- Start by asking whether post-training is needed.
- Define measurement before training.
- Choose data before choosing the model.
