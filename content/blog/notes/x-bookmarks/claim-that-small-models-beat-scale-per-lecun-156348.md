## Summary

@heyshrutimishra claims Yann LeCun was right and that 109 million parameters beat 72 billion. She says LeCun spent years arguing scale is not what makes a model understand the world, while Meta bet the other way, and that he left in November to start his own lab.

## Background

LeCun argues that models should learn world models, internal predictions of how the world works, rather than just scaling up text prediction. The post does not say which benchmark the comparison used, so the claim needs context.

## What I took away

- Ask which task and benchmark a small-beats-large claim is based on.
- Architecture and objective can matter as much as size for some tasks.
- Treat framing like X was right as opinion, not evidence.
