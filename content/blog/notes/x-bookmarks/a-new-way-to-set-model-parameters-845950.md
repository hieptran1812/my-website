## Summary

@t_andy_keller calls the work a very clever take on a classic problem and credits @johnjvastola. The quoted post announces a NeurIPS 2025 paper. It says AI models tune millions of internal settings, usually by trial and error, and the team took a different approach.

## Background

Training a neural network means adjusting millions of numbers called parameters. How those adjustments are chosen has mostly been found by experiment. A principled method could make training more predictable. The post text does not state the actual method.

## What I took away

- Many training choices are still made by trial and error.
- Open the OpenReview link to learn the paper's actual method.
- Look for principled alternatives to hand-tuned settings.
