## Summary

The author says free blogs can be very valuable. They found a blog by Sergi, described as the top trader on Darwinex, explaining how to evaluate whether a filter actually improves a trading strategy, and used it to build a model that does this systematically. The text cuts off there.

## Background

A filter in trading is an extra rule that blocks some trades, such as only trading in certain volatility regimes. Filters easily overfit past data, so a careful test of whether they truly help is important.

## What I took away

- Test filters out of sample to avoid curve-fitting.
- Free practitioner blogs can be high-value learning sources.
- Educational only, not a recommendation.
