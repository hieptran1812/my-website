## Summary

@ZenomTrader describes a pool of 50,000 prop firm traders. The red mass falling represents the 96.5% of traders, and the six bright lines going up are random luck with no edge and the same negative expected value. They say outliers are what everyone focuses on.

## Background

This is survivorship bias. If many people trade with no edge, some will get lucky streaks. Looking only at winners makes luck look like skill. This is the author's illustration.

## What I took away

- Winners in a large group can be pure luck.
- Ask how many people tried before trusting a success story.
- Edge needs evidence beyond a good run.
