## Summary

The author is excited about a project to be released in the coming weeks. The quoted essay argues for putting more computation, in the form of intelligence, into backprop, the most general object in neural network training. It says interpretability is key and relevant to alignment.

## Background

Backpropagation computes how to change weights to reduce error. The idea is to guide training using understanding of the model's internals, so training steers toward safer behavior.

## What I took away

- Interpretability may feed into training, not just analysis.
- The project is not yet released.
