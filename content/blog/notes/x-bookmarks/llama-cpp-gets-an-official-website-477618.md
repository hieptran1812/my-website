## Summary

The author simply says they love llama.cpp. The quoted post announces an official website at llama.app. It says the goal is to make local AI accessible and mentions a single-line cross-platform installer on the landing page.

## Background

llama.cpp is a widely used open source engine for running LLMs on local machines, including laptops. Easier installation lowers the barrier for people who are not systems engineers.

## What I took away

- llama.cpp now has an easier install path.
- Local AI usability is a priority for the project.
- Check llama.app for the official install instructions.
