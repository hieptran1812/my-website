## Summary

The author says let's build a real-time audio transcription system that runs 100% locally. The business problem is that voice assistants and transcription tools usually send audio to a cloud server for processing.

## Background

Running locally keeps audio on your device, which helps privacy and works offline. It needs a speech model small enough to run on your hardware. The post introduces the idea and the full steps are in the thread.

## What I took away

- Local processing avoids sending audio to the cloud.
- Privacy is the key reason to run speech models on-device.
- Follow the thread for the build steps.
