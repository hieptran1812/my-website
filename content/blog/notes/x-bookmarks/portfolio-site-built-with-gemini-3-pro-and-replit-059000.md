## Summary

The author says they built their portfolio in 30 minutes using Gemini 3 Pro and Replit Design Mode. They say it avoids AI slop and violet gradients and looks good. They ask people to comment portfolio and follow to get the instructions by DM.

## Background

AI slop here means generic, template-looking design. The comment-and-follow prompt for a DM is an engagement tactic. The result is the author's claim, shown in the media.

## What I took away

- AI tools can speed up a first version of a site.
- Judge design quality by looking at the result.
- Engagement bait often hides simple instructions.
