## Summary

The author finished editing an interview with the first author of the Dr. GRPO paper. Topics include LLM post-training oddities, whether self-reflection is real, the state of GRPO, and simplicity in algorithm design. They recommend it for RL fans.

## Background

GRPO is a reinforcement learning method used to train reasoning LLMs by comparing groups of sampled answers. Dr. GRPO is a variant proposed in a paper that adjusts parts of GRPO. Hearing a paper's author discuss it gives context that papers often leave out.

## What I took away

- Interviews with paper authors reveal motivation and doubts missing from the paper.
- Simple algorithms are often preferred in RL post-training.
- Question whether apparent self-reflection in models is real reasoning.
