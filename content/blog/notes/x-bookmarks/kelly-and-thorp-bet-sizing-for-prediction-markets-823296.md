## Summary

The post says the bet size in prediction markets is already solved by Kelly-Thorp strategies. It lists the inputs: p for probability of winning, q as 1 minus p, and b for payout odds.

## Background

The Kelly criterion picks a fraction of capital to bet to maximize long-run growth given an edge. It depends on correctly estimating p, which is hard. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Kelly needs p, q and b.
- Estimation errors in p can make full Kelly too risky.
- Many practitioners use a fraction of Kelly.
