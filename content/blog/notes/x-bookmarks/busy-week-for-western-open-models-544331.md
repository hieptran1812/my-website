## Summary

Nathan Lambert calls it a chonky week for Western open models. The quoted post introduces Mistral Large 4, aka Le Chonk, with 1T parameters, 49B active, natively multimodal, and claimed best open-weights model from the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks.

## Background

Western open models compete with strong Chinese open releases. Several large releases in one week (Mistral Large 4, Beam, Kolibri in this batch) signal more choice for self-hosting.

## What I took away

- Track open model releases by week; the landscape shifts fast.
- Benchmark claims are self-reported at launch.
