## Summary

The author says Fibonacci is a probability tool, not magic, and lists topics: Fibonacci, breakout, reversal, Elliott Wave, failure value gap, candlesticks, Heikin Ashi, moon phases, Renko, harmonic patterns, support and resistance, dynamic S&R, trend lines, Gann angles and momentum.

## Background

These are chart-reading methods. Many have weak or mixed evidence, and several are subjective, so results vary by who applies them.

## What I took away

- Test any chart pattern rather than trust it.
- Subjective methods are hard to backtest.
- Not advice.
