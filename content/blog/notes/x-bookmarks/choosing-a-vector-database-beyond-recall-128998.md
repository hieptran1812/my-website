## Summary

The post sets a Meta ML interview question on how to choose a vector database. The author says answering that you would benchmark recall and latency is only 20% of the problem and starts a thread breaking it down.

## Background

A vector database stores embeddings and finds similar ones quickly. Beyond speed and recall, choices depend on cost, scale, filtering, updates and operations. The 20% figure is a rough rhetorical estimate.

## What I took away

- Consider cost, filtering and update patterns, not just recall.
- Ask about scale and workload in interviews.
- Open the thread for the full breakdown.
