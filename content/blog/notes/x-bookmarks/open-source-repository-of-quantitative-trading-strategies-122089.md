## Summary

The author says the Chinese open-source community has shared outstanding research across many fields and highlights a repository they found. The link card points to **fmzquant/strategies**, a collection of quantitative trading strategies written in JavaScript, Python, C++ and PineScript.

## Background

A quantitative trading strategy is a set of rules, coded as a program, for deciding when to buy or sell. Public strategy collections are good for learning how strategies are structured, but they rarely include proof that they work after costs.

## What I took away

- Use public strategy repos to learn structure and code patterns, not as ready-to-trade systems.
- Always backtest with realistic costs before trusting any strategy.
- This is educational material, not a recommendation to trade.
