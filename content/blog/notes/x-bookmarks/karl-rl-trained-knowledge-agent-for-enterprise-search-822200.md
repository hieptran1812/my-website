## Summary

The author recommends following j_nadan_chang for cutting-edge RL science. The quoted post announces KARL, a knowledge agent trained with reinforcement learning that, per the claim, beats Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.2 on enterprise search at a fraction of the cost and latency.

## Background

Enterprise search means finding answers across company documents. Training a smaller agent with RL on this task can beat general models on that narrow task. Results are the authors' claims.

## What I took away

- Task-specific RL can beat larger general models on narrow tasks.
- Check benchmarks and costs in the thread.
- Follow researchers who publish RL results.
