## Summary

Axiom announces AXLE, the Axiom Lean Engine, which serves its core infrastructure for formal proving at scale. The post says these are the same Lean metaprogramming tools behind AxiomProver, which won Putnam and cracked research conjectures, and are available to anyone.

## Background

Lean is a proof assistant where proofs are checked by a computer. Formal proving lets AI math systems verify their results. Win claims are from the company.

## What I took away

- Lean tooling is now available for building proof systems.
- Formal verification gives checkable AI math results.
- Claims of Putnam wins are the company's.
