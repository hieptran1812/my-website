## Summary

The author previews a new **grid** mode for their market-making bot. They say it ends in one of two outcomes: positive profit and loss (PnL) or a stop loss (SL). If prices range around the start price, it should profit, and if prices move away and the order is canceled, it closes out the exposure.

## Background

Market making means placing buy and sell orders around the current price to earn the spread. A grid places orders at several price levels. The claim of guaranteed outcomes is the author's own and depends on fees, fills and fast price moves.

## What I took away

- A grid earns when price oscillates and loses when price trends.
- Be careful with the word guarantee in trading; check what assumptions sit behind it.
- A stop loss caps loss but can still be crossed in fast markets.
