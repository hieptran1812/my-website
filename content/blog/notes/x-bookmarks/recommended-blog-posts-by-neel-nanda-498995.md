## Summary

@dejavucoder says they read 10-15 blog posts by @NeelNanda5 and liked them. They recommend two of them, Mini Blog Post 11 on living a life you feel excited about and Mini Blog Post 7 on problems being for fixing, then following the linked posts if interested.

- Reading a researcher's advice posts can help with motivation and approach.
- Following links from one post to related posts is a good way to go deeper.
