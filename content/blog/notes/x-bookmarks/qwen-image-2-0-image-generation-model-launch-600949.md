## Summary

Alibaba Qwen introduces **Qwen-Image-2.0**, a next-generation image generation model. It claims you can type a paragraph to get a professional slide, describe a scene to get photoreal 2K output, and add text without glitchy letters. The post lists key upgrades beyond the shown text.

## Background

Image models have long struggled to draw readable text. Better typography lets them produce slides, posters and infographics. Such claims are usually based on the vendor's own examples.

## What I took away

- Text rendering inside images is a key quality area.
- Try it on your own prompts before relying on demos.
- Slide and infographic generation is a practical use.
