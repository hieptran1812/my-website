## Summary

@FEijkelboom praises a reframing of symmetry discovery: instead of finding generators, learn which transformations in a large Lie group keep the data distribution unchanged. The quoted post announces LieFlow at ICML 2026.

## Background

Symmetry as an inductive bias helps models but must be known. LieFlow discovers it from data.

## What I took away

- Learn symmetries instead of assuming.
- Works for discrete and continuous groups.
