## Summary

The author invites people to join. The quoted post says Thinking Machines is growing its evaluation team and cares about whether models are actually useful and whether measurements are good enough to serve as the y-axis for scaling research.

## Background

Evaluation is the work of measuring model ability with tests and benchmarks. Scaling research plots performance against compute or data, and the y-axis is the eval score, so a noisy or wrong eval leads to wrong conclusions about scaling.

## What I took away

- Good evals are the foundation of scaling research, not an afterthought.
- Ask whether a metric reflects real usefulness before optimizing it.
- Eval roles are a growing career path in AI labs.
