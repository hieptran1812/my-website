## Summary

@predict_addict highlights Speech and Natural Language Processing, a free book of more than 620 pages from Stanford by Jurafsky, calling him the NLP OG.

## Background

The book is a long-standing textbook that covers language processing from basics to modern neural methods. A free, updated textbook is a good way to build foundations.

## What I took away

- Use it as a reference for NLP fundamentals.
- It is free to read online.
- Pair reading with small coding exercises.
