## Summary

The author shares aisafety.fund and lists 2026 figures: $1.5B from philanthropy, $346M from governments and $2.1B from frontier AI companies. He compares this with $98B on AI capabilities (25x safety) and $868B on datacenters.

## Background

The comparison shows how small safety funding is relative to capabilities. The figures are from the post and the tracker.

## What I took away

- Safety funding is small relative to capabilities.
- Check the tracker for definitions.
- Companies provide the largest safety share.
