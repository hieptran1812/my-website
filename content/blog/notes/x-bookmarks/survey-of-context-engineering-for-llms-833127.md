## Summary

The post calls A Survey of Context Engineering for LLMs a must-read. It covers why performance is shaped at inference time, what lies beyond prompt design, core components such as retrieval and generation, processing, and memory and compression, and system implementations like RAG.

## Background

Context engineering is deciding what information goes into a model's context window. RAG means retrieving relevant documents and adding them to the prompt. Good context often matters more than clever wording.

## What I took away

- Think of the prompt as one part of the whole context.
- Retrieval, memory and compression are core context tools.
- Use the survey as a map of the area.
