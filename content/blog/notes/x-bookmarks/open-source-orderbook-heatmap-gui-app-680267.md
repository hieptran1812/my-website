## Summary

The author is finally open sourcing a heatmap and orderbook GUI app, partly to include it with a resume. The repo link is in the replies and a few persistent issues are noted in the readme.

## Background

An orderbook lists buy and sell orders at each price. A heatmap shows liquidity over time. Open sourcing a project is a good portfolio item.

## What I took away

- Open source projects can support job applications.
- Document known issues honestly.
