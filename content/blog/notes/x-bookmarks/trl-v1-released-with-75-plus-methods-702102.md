## Summary

Clement Delangue announces TRL v1 with 75+ methods including SFT, DPO, GRPO and async RL, after six years of development. The quoted post says Intercom joins other firms finding it better, cheaper and faster to train open models themselves than use APIs.

## Background

TRL is a library for post-training: SFT is supervised fine-tuning, DPO learns from preference pairs, and GRPO is a reinforcement learning method. Teams fine-tune open models for specific tasks to cut cost.

## What I took away

- TRL covers many post-training methods in one library.
- Open models can beat APIs on cost for narrow tasks.
- Install with pip install trl.
