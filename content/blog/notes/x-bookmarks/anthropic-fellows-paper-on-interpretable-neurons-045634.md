## Summary

@tszzl says neural nets are already clean. The quoted post announces a paper from January's cohort of Anthropic Fellows showing that individual neurons may be a stronger basis for interpretability than commonly assumed, amid recent debate on how interpretable LLM neurons are.

## Background

Interpretability research asks what internal parts of a model represent. A common view is polysemanticity: each neuron mixes many unrelated concepts, so tools like sparse autoencoders are used to untangle them. This paper argues raw neurons are more meaningful than that view suggests.

## What I took away

- The debate on neurons versus learned features is active and unsettled.
- Related bookmarks in this batch include both strong praise and criticism of similar claims.
