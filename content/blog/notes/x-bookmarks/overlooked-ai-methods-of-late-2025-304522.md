## Summary

The post lists methods to know at the end of 2025: switching BF16 to FP16 precision, Modular Manifolds, XQuant and XQuant-CL, multimodal fusion such as Mixture of States (MoS), Mixture-of-Recursions (MoR) and Causal Attention with Lookahead Keys (CASTLE).

## Background

BF16 and FP16 are 16-bit number formats used in training, with different range and precision trade-offs. XQuant relates to quantization, which stores values in fewer bits. MoR and CASTLE are architecture ideas for using compute or attention more efficiently.

## What I took away

- Number formats like FP16 vs BF16 still matter for training stability.
- Use the list as a reading queue for recent methods.
- The post is a list with little detail, so read the papers.
