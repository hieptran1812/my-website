## Summary

The author wanted a better mental model of transformer architectures, so they reimplemented GPT2 from scratch using Neel Nanda's tutorial. They say much of the magic comes from understanding small details, and the more they learn, the more they realize this is a deep topic.

## Background

GPT2 is a small, well-documented transformer. Rebuilding it by hand shows how attention, embeddings and layers fit together, which reading alone often does not.

## What I took away

- Reimplementing a small model is a strong way to learn transformers.
- Details like shapes and masks matter.
