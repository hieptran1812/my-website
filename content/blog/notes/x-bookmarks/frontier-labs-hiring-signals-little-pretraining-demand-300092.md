## Summary

@teortaxesTex asks whether there is no hiring for pretraining and wonders if labs think they have enough. The quoted post points to OnlyLabs.fyi, which tracks what frontier lab hiring signals for RL environment companies.

## Background

Pretraining is the large first phase of training a language model on raw text. RL environments are the simulated tasks used to train models with reinforcement learning. Hiring patterns can hint at where labs put effort, though they are an indirect signal.

## What I took away

- Hiring data is a rough signal of lab priorities, not proof.
- RL environments appear to be a growing area of demand.
