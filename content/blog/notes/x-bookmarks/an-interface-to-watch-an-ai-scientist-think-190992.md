## Summary

@belindmo asks what it would be like to watch an AI scientist think. They built an interface that makes Sakana AI Labs' AI Scientist-v2 interpretable, showing every hypothesis, failed experiment and 'aha' moment. The post starts a thread of 8 on what they learned.

## Background

AI Scientist-v2 is an agent system that tries to run research experiments automatically. Such agents produce long chains of steps that are hard to follow. A viewer that lays out the hypotheses and failures helps humans check whether the process makes sense.

## What I took away

- Long agent runs need good visualizations so humans can audit them.
- Failed experiments are as informative as successful ones.
