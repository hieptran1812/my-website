## Summary

The author says every millionaire trader they studied looks for tight, coiled price action. They compare price to a spring that stores energy when it compresses: tight range, low volume, undercuts and reclaims, and higher lows. The quoted post argues that the edge in technical analysis is how price behaves around indicators, not the indicators themselves.

## Background

Coiled price action means the price trades in a narrowing range before a possible larger move. An undercut and reclaim is when price dips below a prior low and then climbs back above it. This is a discretionary chart-reading idea, not a proven rule, and the claim about millionaire traders is the author's.

## What I took away

- The idea is to read how price behaves, not just which indicators are on the chart.
- Treat this as a pattern to study, not a guaranteed signal.
- Test any pattern on historical data before trusting it.
