## Summary

The author asks whether anyone wants a video on the topic of their profiling blog post. The plan is to go through the post, open the profiles, zoom in and out, add commentary and show bonus profiles not in the blog. The quoted post is the original blog announcement on learning kernels via profiling.

## Background

Profiler traces are dense and hard to read cold. A guided walkthrough shows how experts scan them. Creators often poll their audience before investing time in a video.

## What I took away

- Reading profiles is a skill best learned by watching someone do it.
- Look for the blog post and the follow-up video if published.
- Bonus profiles can show cases the blog skipped.
