## Summary

Banghua Zhu calls the post a comprehensive walkthrough of dLLM inference support and credits Zehuan and Jinwei for leading it. He says Jinwei is applying to PhD programs this year and highly recommends him. The quoted post is the LMSYS announcement of Day-0 LLaDA 2.0 support in SGLang's dLLM framework.

## Background

dLLM means diffusion large language model, which generates text by iterative refinement instead of one token at a time. The SGLang team built serving and RL tooling for them. Public endorsements like this are a common way researchers support students.

## What I took away

- Detailed walkthrough blogs make good entry points to a new inference area.
- Public credit helps early-career engineers get noticed.
