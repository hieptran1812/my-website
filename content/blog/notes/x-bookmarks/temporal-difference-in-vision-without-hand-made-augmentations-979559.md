## Summary

@AlexiGlad argues AI progress comes from weaker assumptions, but representation learning relied on strong ones like augmentations, masking and cropping. He introduces Temporal Difference in Vision (TDV). The text is cut off.

## Background

Representation learning builds useful features from unlabeled data. Augmentations encode human assumptions about what stays the same. Temporal difference learning predicts from time structure in video instead.

## What I took away

- Fewer assumptions may scale better.
- Time in video can replace hand-made augmentations.
