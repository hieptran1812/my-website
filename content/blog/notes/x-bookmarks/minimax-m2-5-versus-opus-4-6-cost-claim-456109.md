## Summary

The author claims MiniMax M2.5 is way better than Opus 4.6 and over 90% cheaper. He says 100 output tokens per second costs about $1 per hour of nonstop work. These are his claims after reading specs and comparisons.

## Background

Cost per hour of agent work depends on output speed and price per token. Cheaper models can suit long agent loops. Claims of being better than a top model should be checked on your own tasks.

## What I took away

- Estimate cost per hour, not only per token.
- Do not trust better-than claims without your own tests.
