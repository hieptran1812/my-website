## Summary

The author says Claude Sonnet 4.6, when asked in Chinese what model it is, confidently replies that it is DeepSeek. They point out the model's maker had just accused DeepSeek of industrial-scale distillation attacks.

## Background

Models do not reliably know their own identity. If their training data includes outputs from other models, they may name them. A single example does not prove how the model was trained.

## What I took away

- Do not trust model self-identification.
- One example is anecdote, not proof.
