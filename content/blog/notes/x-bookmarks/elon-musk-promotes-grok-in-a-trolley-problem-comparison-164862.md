## Summary

@elonmusk writes only 'Grok' as a reply to a quoted post titled 'The trolley problem, ChatGPT vs. Grok'. The post suggests the quoted comparison favors Grok, but the text gives no details.

## Background

The trolley problem is a classic ethics thought experiment about choosing between harms. People use it to compare how chatbots reason about moral dilemmas. One example prompt does not measure a model's overall quality or safety.

## What I took away

- A single viral prompt is weak evidence about a model.
- Check the actual comparison before drawing a conclusion.
