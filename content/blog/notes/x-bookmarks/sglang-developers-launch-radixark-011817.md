## Summary

@ibab says SGLang is the best inference framework for LLMs and that xAI used it heavily because it ran large models faster than anyone else. He notes the developers have now started RadixArk to serve infrastructure needs. The quoted post says RadixArk was started by many core developers of SGLang and its ecosystem, including slime and AReaL, and that the author left xAI in August.

## Background

An inference framework is software that runs trained LLMs to answer requests quickly and cheaply. SGLang is a popular open-source one. The 'best' and 'faster than anyone' statements are the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Inference frameworks can make a large difference in speed and cost.
- Open-source infrastructure projects often grow into companies.
- Benchmark frameworks on your own workload.
