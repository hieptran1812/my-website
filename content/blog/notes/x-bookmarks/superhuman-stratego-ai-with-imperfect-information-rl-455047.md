## Summary

The author calls it a big result in RL for domains with imperfect information that humans dominated, adding that there are no human-dominant domains in the long run. The quoted post says their Nature paper introduces the first superhuman Stratego AI, built with general RL and test-time compute techniques for imperfect information.

## Background

Imperfect information games hide some state from players, like hidden pieces in Stratego. They require reasoning about what the opponent might know, which is harder than chess or Go.

## What I took away

- Techniques for hidden-information games may transfer to negotiation or security.
- Test-time compute helps beyond perfect-information games.
