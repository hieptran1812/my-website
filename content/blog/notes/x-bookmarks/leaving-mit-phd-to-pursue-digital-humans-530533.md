## Summary

The author says they are leaving MIT and not continuing into a PhD because AI is coming too fast for humans to keep up. They believe digital humans are more possible than most think. With AI researchers helping, they say it might cost $10B, take under 10 years, and use 50k H100s.

## Background

Digital humans here means AI copies of human minds or abilities. The H100 is a common high-end training GPU. The cost and timeline are the author's rough estimate, not a result.

## What I took away

- This is a personal and speculative claim.
- Career choices around AI speed are a live debate.
- Treat the cost and timeline as guesses.
