## Summary

The author releases the Ultimate guide to RL environments. They say definitions differ widely in the LLM era, so they spent a month building several environments across 6 frameworks, domains and complexities to map which are easiest to build.

## Background

An RL environment is the task setup where a model acts and receives rewards. For LLMs it can be a coding sandbox or a tool-use task. The right framework choice saves time.

## What I took away

- Compare frameworks by how easy they are to build in.
- Start with a simple environment before scaling up.
