## Summary

The author says volatility targeting sets position size as target volatility divided by current volatility, so you size down in volatile markets and up in calm ones. He claims it minimizes all risk and turned $50 into $1,690 in a week.

## Background

Volatility targeting is a real risk-management idea, but it does not remove risk. The profit claim is unverified and looks like promotion.

## What I took away

- No formula brings risk to zero.
- Be skeptical of fast profit claims.
- The sizing idea itself is valid, educationally.
