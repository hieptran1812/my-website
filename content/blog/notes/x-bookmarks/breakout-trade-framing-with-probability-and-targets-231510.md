## Summary

The author explains breakout trades: you can enter at a key resistance level such as the previous day's high, with an X% chance the level breaks and reaches your take profit, and a 1-X% chance it holds and hits your stop loss. They prefer entering when several factors line up. The quoted post shows a trade that hit +1.59%.

## Background

This framing treats each trade as a bet with a probability and payoffs. Whether it is profitable depends on the real value of X and the reward-to-risk ratio. A single winning example proves little.

## What I took away

- Think in expected value: probability times payoff.
- One winning trade is not evidence.
- Educational, not advice.
