## Summary

The author lists a one-line narrative for each of several crypto projects. Examples include Bitcoin as digital gold, Litecoin as digital silver, Ethereum as the world computer, Solana as internet capital markets and Dogecoin as internet-native money. It continues with Polkadot, Celestia, Filecoin and Cosmos (text cut off).

## Background

In crypto, a narrative is the story people use to explain what a project is for and why it has value. Narratives shape attention and prices but are marketing summaries, not technical analysis. Some listed projects, like Celestia and Filecoin, focus on data availability and storage.

## What I took away

- Slogans simplify projects; look at actual usage and technology.
- Narratives can drive hype independent of fundamentals.
- This is educational context, not investment advice.
