## Summary

Lossfunk says world models are probably what is missing to reach AGI. A researcher there summarizes their history, future and remaining open questions in a new post.

## Background

World models learn how an environment works so an agent can predict outcomes and plan. The AGI claim is the author's opinion. The post is a survey-style overview.

## What I took away

- World models are a popular candidate for the next step in AI.
- Read it for history and open questions, not for settled answers.
