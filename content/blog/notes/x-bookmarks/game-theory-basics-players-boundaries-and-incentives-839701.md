## Summary

The author argues you win a game by understanding the incentives that drive players, not just the visible rules. They share a video where Prof. Jiang breaks down three elements of game theory: players, boundary conditions and incentives, as a way to predict human behavior.

## Background

Game theory studies how people or agents make decisions when outcomes depend on others' choices. Looking at who the players are, what limits them, and what they want often explains behavior better than formal rules.

## What I took away

- When predicting behavior, ask what each player is rewarded for.
- Constraints shape which strategies are even possible.
- Apply the same lens to markets, teams and negotiations.
