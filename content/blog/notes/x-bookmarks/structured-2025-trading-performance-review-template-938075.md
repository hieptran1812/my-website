## Summary

The author says a trader's year-end review comes down to three questions: what worked, what did not and what is next. He shares a structured 2025 Trading Performance Review PDF covering edge decay, execution, psychology and more.

## Background

Edge decay means a strategy's advantage fades over time. A regular structured review helps separate skill from luck and find mistakes. This is a self-improvement tool, not advice.

## What I took away

- Review trades yearly with fixed questions.
- Track edge decay and execution separately from psychology.
