## Summary

The author shares a short LessWrong post, Understanding and Controlling LLM Generalization, that distills their long-term research agenda and current thinking. They welcome takes on it.

## Background

Generalization is how a model behaves on inputs it was not trained on. Understanding and steering it is a central concern for safety, since small training changes can shift behavior in surprising ways. The post is an agenda, not a result.

## What I took away

- Generalization is a key lever for safe model behavior.
