## Summary

The author says a new neo-lab was announced by ex-OpenAI and ex-Anthropic founders and congratulates three people. The quoted announcement says Core Automation aims at systems that optimize and automate work, starting with research itself.

## Background

A neo-lab is a new, well-funded AI research startup. Automating research means AI systems that run experiments and propose ideas. If it works, progress could speed up.
