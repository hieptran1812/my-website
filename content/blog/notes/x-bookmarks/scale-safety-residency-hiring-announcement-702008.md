## Summary

The author says they are hiring and asks people to apply for a funded safety research opportunity, with the first cohort starting February 2027. The quoted post announces the Scale Safety Residency for researchers to tackle open AI safety questions with Scale's Preparedness and Policy research team.

## Background

Residencies are short, paid programs that let researchers work on a topic with a host team. They are a common entry route into a field.

## What I took away

- Funded residencies can be an entry route to safety research.
- Check the application deadline at the link.
- The cohort begins in February 2027.
