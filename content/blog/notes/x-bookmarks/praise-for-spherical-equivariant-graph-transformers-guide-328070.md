## Summary

Pranam praises his lab member Sophia Tang, saying her article on the Alchemy Bio Substack convinced him of her brilliance. He is proud she finalized it and released it on arXiv. The quoted post is her announcement that her Complete Guide to Spherical Equivariant Graph Transformers now has a permanent home on arXiv (2512.13927).

## Background

Equivariant graph transformers are neural networks for 3D structures such as molecules that respect rotations and other symmetries. A guide that explains them clearly helps newcomers. Moving a blog post to arXiv makes it citable.

## What I took away

- Good blog explainers can become citable papers.
- Public writing can showcase talent.
- Read the guide to learn equivariance for molecular ML.
