## Summary

Musk says forcing AI to read every demented corner of the internet is a sure path to madness. The quoted post says his warning about training on the conformist status quo of Wikipedia and Reddit is now an academic paper about a False-Correction Loop in large language models.

## Background

LLMs are trained on huge web scrapes, so data sources shape their views and errors. Critics argue about bias in sources, and the claims here are opinion. The paper's findings are not described in the post beyond its title.

## What I took away

- Training data sources shape model behavior.
- Read the actual paper before accepting claims about it.
- Opinions on bias differ and need evidence.
