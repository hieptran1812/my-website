## Summary

The author read and annotated the 300+ page Messari 2026 thesis on a long flight and shares thoughts. It took about three hours back to back. His favorite essays included crypto money and tradfi x crypto. The quoted post announces the free Messari Theses for 2026.

## Background

Messari is a crypto research firm, and its yearly Theses report gives forecasts and themes. These are opinions about the next year, not guarantees.

## What I took away

- Annotated summaries save time on long reports.
- Treat annual crypto theses as perspectives, not predictions.
