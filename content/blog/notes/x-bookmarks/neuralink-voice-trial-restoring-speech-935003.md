## Summary

Elon Musk shares Neuralink's VOICE clinical trial. The quoted post says ALS took Kenneth's ability to speak and the interface translates thought to speech to help restore autonomy.

## Background

A brain-computer interface reads neural signals and decodes intent. ALS is a disease that weakens muscles including those for speech.

## What I took away

- BCIs aim at assistive speech.
- Clinical trials are early evidence.
