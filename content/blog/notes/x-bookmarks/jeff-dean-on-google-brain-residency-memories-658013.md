## Summary

@JeffDean says he loves seeing what Google Brain Residents and AI Residency cohorts have gone on to do. Prompted by a post from @hyhieu226, he shares a blog post, '7 months in and looking ahead', and a video from that era. The quoted post recalls the first class as a good year when people did what they liked without promotion tricks or politics.

## Background

A research residency is a program where people spend a year doing research at a lab. The first Google Brain Residency class produced many researchers who later worked across the field. The quoted nostalgia is a personal memory.

## What I took away

- Residency programs can launch research careers.
- Early research eras often favor curiosity over metrics.
