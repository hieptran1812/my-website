## Summary

The post says DraftedAI turns a few inputs, such as bedroom count and open kitchen, into floor plans and matching 3D exterior images in seconds. It is tagged as sponsored.

## Background

Generative design tools take a short brief and produce visual options. They help people see ideas early. A sponsored tag means the author was paid, so the description is promotional.

## What I took away

- Note the sponsored label when judging a tool recommendation.
- Use such tools for early ideas, then verify with a professional.
