## Summary

Lewis Tunstall jokes that the team ran a distillation attack on the **Kimi K3 technical report** by reading it in parallel at the Hugging Face Journal Club. The video is on YouTube. Their main takeaway is that there does not seem to be any secret sauce behind frontier performance; K3 is instead described as something more (text cut off).

## Background

A journal club is a group that reads and discusses papers together. A technical report describes how a model was built and trained. The claim of no secret sauce suggests frontier results come from many well-executed pieces rather than one hidden trick.

## What I took away

- Frontier performance may come from careful execution of known ideas.
- Group paper readings are an efficient way to digest long reports.
- Watch the video for the specific components they highlight.
