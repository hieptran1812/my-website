## Summary

Karpathy shares a fireside chat at Sequoia AI Ascent 2026. His first theme is that LLMs are about more than speeding up existing work like coding, and gives examples such as menugen. The quoted host says vibe coding raised the floor, while agentic engineering raises the ceiling, and Karpathy has never felt more behind as a programmer.

## Background

Vibe coding means prompting an AI to write code. Agentic engineering means using agents carefully in professional work. The talk frames how developer work is shifting.

## What I took away

- Look for new things LLMs enable, not just faster old tasks.
- Agentic engineering is a skill beyond vibe coding.
