## Summary

The author claims to have turned $10k into $10,520 in 15 minutes on Polymarket, repeatedly. They say they spent over 6 months studying the platform and that split and merge are the entire game.

## Background

On Polymarket, splitting turns collateral into outcome shares and merging turns them back. The post suggests exploiting that structure. The profits are self-reported and no risks or costs are given. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of quick profit claims.
- Claimed edges disappear when others copy them, and fees and risks may be omitted.
