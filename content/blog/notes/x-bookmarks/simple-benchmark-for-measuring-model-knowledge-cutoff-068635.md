## Summary

The quoted post describes a simple benchmark to measure the actual knowledge cutoff of models. Its author finds that OpenAI and Anthropic are the only big labs keeping models fresh, while others, including Chinese labs, are at least 12 months behind. The author of this post likes such simple projects and compares it to a paper estimating black-box LLM parameter counts via factual capacity.

## Background

A knowledge cutoff is the latest date of information in a model's training data. Stated cutoffs can differ from real ones. Probing a model with dated facts reveals what it actually knows.

## What I took away

- Simple probes can reveal hidden properties of black-box models.
- Do not trust a model's stated cutoff; test it on recent facts.
- Findings come from one simple benchmark; treat them as indicative.
