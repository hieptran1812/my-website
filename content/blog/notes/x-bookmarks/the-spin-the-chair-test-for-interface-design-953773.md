## Summary

A designer with 20+ years of experience shares the **Spin The Chair Test**. You put your design on screen, move your seat back, turn around, and spin, then look again. The text is cut off after step four.

## Background

Breaking your focus and returning lets you see the design as a new user would, noticing what stands out first. This is a cheap way to check visual hierarchy.

## What I took away

- Look away, then re-look to judge hierarchy.
- Check what your eye lands on first.
