## Summary

Lookonchain says the whale who previously manipulated $JELLY is now long $ZEREBRO, with 85.86M tokens worth $3.42M across two wallets. They say he pushed the price up over 300% and has over $2.3M unrealized profit. The quoted post describes the earlier JELLY case, which caused the Hyperliquidity Provider to face a $12M loss.

## Background

Price manipulation in thin markets means moving a token's price with large trades. The HLP vault takes the other side of trades on Hyperliquid, so it can lose money. These are on-chain analytics claims and not advice.

## What I took away

- Low-liquidity tokens are easy to move.
- On-chain trackers show wallet activity but not intent.
- Not a trading signal.
