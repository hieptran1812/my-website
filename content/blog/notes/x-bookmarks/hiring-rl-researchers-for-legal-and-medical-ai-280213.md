## Summary

The author is hiring RL researchers to build logic into legal and medical AI and asks for resumes by DM. The quoted post is from an investor saying they back founders building improbable things across many sectors.

## Background

Legal and medical tasks need careful reasoning and verifiable correctness, which makes them a target for RL with checkable rewards.

## What I took away

- RL for high-stakes domains is an active hiring area.
- Reward design is the hard part when correctness is hard to verify.
