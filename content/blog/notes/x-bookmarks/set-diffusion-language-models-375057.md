## Summary

@mariannearr announces Set Diffusion for ICML 2026, a faster and more flexible alternative combining AR and diffusion by interpolating generation orders. It follows Block Diffusion LMs, adopted by dLLMs at Google, NVIDIA, Meta, etc.

## Background

Autoregressive models write left to right. Diffusion LMs refine many tokens at once. Interpolating orders mixes the two for speed with flexibility.

## What I took away

- Hybrid orders trade between AR and diffusion.
- Check the thread for results.
