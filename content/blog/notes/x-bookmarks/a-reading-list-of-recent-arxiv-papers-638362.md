## Summary

The author says they will spend time trying things and digesting a few papers, and lists six arXiv links. The link preview shows one of them: a paper proposing a method to estimate how much a language model knows about a data point, used to measure model capacity.

## Background

Memorization research asks how much of the training data a model stores verbatim versus generalizes from. Measuring capacity helps explain privacy risks and how much information fits in a given number of parameters.

## What I took away

- Curated reading lists from practitioners are a cheap way to find relevant papers.
- Memorization and capacity measurement is a useful lens for understanding model size versus knowledge.
- Pick one paper from the list and read it end to end rather than skimming all six.
