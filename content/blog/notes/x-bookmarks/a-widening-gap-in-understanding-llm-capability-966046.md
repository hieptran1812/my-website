## Summary

Karpathy revisits an April post and says the gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is widening. He describes it as a sharp funnel rather than two groups talking past each other, with napkin math of around 6B people (about 75% of the population) having barely used these tools. The quoted post argues many people judged AI from the free tier of ChatGPT a year ago and let that shape their views too much.

## Background

How good an LLM seems depends heavily on which model tier and which tools you use, and how recently. Someone who tried an old free model may have a very different picture than someone using current paid models with agents.

## What I took away

- Before debating AI capability, check which models and tiers each side has used.
- Try current top models yourself periodically; impressions go stale fast.
- The 6B figure is Karpathy's rough estimate.
