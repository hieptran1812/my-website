## Summary

The post notes the SEC rate limits at 10 requests per second, or 600 per minute. It says its own service allows 1,000 per minute on Developer and unlimited on Pro, with structured financials and full filings.

## Background

Rate limits cap how many API calls you can make. Providers that repackage public data sell convenience, structure and speed.

## What I took away

- Check rate limits before building a data pipeline.
- This is a product advertisement.
