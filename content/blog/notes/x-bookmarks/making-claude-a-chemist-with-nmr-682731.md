## Summary

Anthropic published a Science Blog post, Making Claude a chemist. To manipulate a molecule, chemists first need its structure, and a main tool is NMR spectroscopy. Anthropic says Opus 4.7 matches, and on some tasks beats, dedicated NMR software.

## Background

NMR spectroscopy measures how atoms in a molecule respond to a magnetic field, giving signals that reveal structure. Interpreting those signals usually takes experts or specialist software.

## What I took away

- General models can now compete with specialist tools in some science tasks.
- The result is Anthropic's claim; read the post for task details.
