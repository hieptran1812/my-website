## Summary

The author says LLMs represent concepts as vectors and that taxonomies like organism, animal, bird appear as hierarchies in embedding space. Led by a student, the paper shows this comes from **co-occurrence statistics alone**. An arXiv link is included.

## Background

Co-occurrence means which words appear together in text. The finding suggests the hierarchy does not need special reasoning; simple statistics of the data can produce it.

## What I took away

- Embedding structure can come from data statistics, not just model design.
- Be careful reading deep meaning into geometry.
- Read the arXiv paper for the method.
