## Summary

Thinking Machines introduces Inkling 2 Small, scoring 31 on the AA Intelligence Index, a big jump from its predecessor. It is available for fine-tuning and inference on Tinker now, with a full weight release planned soon.

## Background

The AA Intelligence Index is an aggregate benchmark score from Artificial Analysis. Tinker is the company's fine-tuning platform. Weights will be released later.

## What I took away

- Weights are promised but not yet out.
- Fine-tuning access is available via Tinker.
- Compare index scores across models.
