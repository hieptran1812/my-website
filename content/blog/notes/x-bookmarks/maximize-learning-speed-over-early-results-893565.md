## Summary

@spicyofc says that in the first 1-2 years of learning a hard skill such as trading, you should maximize speed of learning rather than results. Long-term results are a byproduct of skill, so make skill go up as much as possible. The text is cut off.

## Background

Early results in noisy fields like trading are mostly luck, so they are bad feedback. Focusing on learning speed means getting many quick feedback loops. This applies to engineering and research too.

## What I took away

- Measure skill growth, not early outcomes.
- In noisy fields, results mislead early.
- Build fast feedback loops.
