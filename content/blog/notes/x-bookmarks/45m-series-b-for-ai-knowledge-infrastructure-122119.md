## Summary

The author says they raised a $45M Series B at a $500M valuation led by a16z and Salesforce Ventures. The money is to build the knowledge infrastructure for AI.

## Background

Knowledge infrastructure means systems that give AI access to company data in a usable form. Funding signals investor interest in this layer.

## What I took away

- Data access is a big AI bottleneck.
- Valuation is not proof of product quality.
- Track the space.
