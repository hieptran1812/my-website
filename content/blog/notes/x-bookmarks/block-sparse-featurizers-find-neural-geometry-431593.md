## Summary

The author says they found a way to discover neural geometry, completely unsupervised, with incredible results and structure inside. The quoted Goodfire post introduces Block-Sparse Featurizers (BSFs), which use multidimensional blocks instead of single directions to find concepts.

## Background

Many interpretability tools assume a concept is one direction. Blocks allow concepts that span several dimensions, such as shapes.

## What I took away

- Concepts may be geometric, not single directions.
- Unsupervised discovery scales better.
- Read the thread for results.
