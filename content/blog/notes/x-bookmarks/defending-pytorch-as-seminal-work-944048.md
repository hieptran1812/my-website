## Summary

The author says it is ridiculously wrong to claim PyTorch is only software that needs linear thinking and is not seminal. They note their own PhD was in AI systems and they would be thrilled to have created PyTorch. They point out it was published at NeurIPS and has 64K citations. The text is cut off.

## Background

There is a debate about whether systems and tooling work counts as research as much as new models. PyTorch is the main deep learning framework and underpins most modern AI research. Citation counts are one measure of influence.

## What I took away

- Tooling can have more impact than many model papers.
- Systems research deserves credit.
- Citations are one proxy for influence.
