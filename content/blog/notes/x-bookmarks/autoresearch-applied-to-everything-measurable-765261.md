## Summary

The author says autoresearch has been out for two weeks and the community is trying to apply it to everything with a measurable metric. They share some successful attempts in a six-part thread.

## Background

Autoresearch is an agent loop that changes code, measures a score and keeps improvements. The key requirement is a metric you can compute automatically.

## What I took away

- If you can measure it, you can loop on it.
- Choosing a good metric is the hard part.
- Follow the thread to see concrete use cases.
