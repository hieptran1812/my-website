## Summary

@RebellioMarket shares a quote framed as William O'Neil and his son in 1995: price can fool you for a day, but volume tells the truth. It says institutions leave footprints through volume and that learning to read it shows where real strength is.

## Background

Volume is the number of shares traded. The idea is that large buying by big investors shows up as higher volume. The attribution and framing here are as the post states; this is educational and not advice.

## What I took away

- Volume is often read together with price moves to judge strength.
- Be careful about quotes with unclear sourcing.
