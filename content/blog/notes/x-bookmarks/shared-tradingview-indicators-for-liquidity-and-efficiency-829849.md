## Summary

The author shares links to their TradingView indicators: Matt Liq Lines, Market Efficiency, MattSuite V3 and an Advanced Market Making Suite. The card identifies the author as mattlanders. They say have fun.

## Background

TradingView indicators are scripts that draw analysis on price charts. These are community scripts, and their usefulness is not shown in the post. This is not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Open scripts let you study how others build indicators.
- Test any indicator before relying on it.
- No performance claims are made in the post.
