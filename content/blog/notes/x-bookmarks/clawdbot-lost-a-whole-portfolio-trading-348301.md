## Summary

@kevinxu gave Clawdbot access to his portfolio with the instruction to trade it to $1M and not make mistakes. He says it ran 25 strategies, wrote 3,000+ reports, built 12 new algorithms, scanned every X post and traded 24/7. It lost everything, though he found the process beautiful.

## Background

An autonomous trading agent combines an LLM with tools to read news and place orders. Lots of analysis does not create an edge in markets. This is a cautionary example about giving an agent money and a vague goal.

## What I took away

- Activity and sophisticated reports do not equal profit.
- Do not give agents unbounded access to real money.
- Vague goals like do not make mistakes are not a risk control.
