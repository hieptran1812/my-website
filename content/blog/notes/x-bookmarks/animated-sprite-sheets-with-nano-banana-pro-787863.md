## Summary

The author says Nano Banana Pro together with Retro Diffusion turned their original characters into animated sprite sheets. They say they can now put their art in a video game and animate all 2000 characters, completing a two-year dream.

## Background

A sprite sheet is a grid of animation frames used in 2D games. Image models can now keep a character consistent across frames, which makes this possible. The result is the author's own report, shown in the media.

## What I took away

- Image models can now help produce consistent animation frames.
- Combining a general model with a pixel-art tool can fit game art needs.
- Check frame consistency yourself before using it in a game.
