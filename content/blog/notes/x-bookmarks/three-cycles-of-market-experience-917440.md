## Summary

The author says in the first cycle you make money in good times and lose it in bad times. In the second you make money in good times and keep most in bad times. From the third onward you make money in both. The quoted post says that during the last year they moved to cash, alternative assets such as plates and watches, property and commodities like gold and silver, with under 5% on-chain, and made money both up and down.

## Background

The idea is that risk management improves with experience of market cycles. These are personal anecdotes, not evidence of a method. This is educational context and not advice.

## What I took away

- Surviving bad periods is part of long-term results.
- Personal anecdotes are not proof.
- Not investment advice.
