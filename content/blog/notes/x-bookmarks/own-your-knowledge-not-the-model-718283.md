## Summary

The author praises a write-up by Hamza and agrees that companies should own their domain knowledge and learning loops, not obsess over owning the model. He asks whether your knowledge can transfer when a better model arrives. The quoted post says own your intelligence is a popular idea, but models will change, and the durable asset is knowledge every new model can inherit.

## Background

Many companies fine-tune their own models to own their AI. But models get replaced quickly, so a fine-tuned model can become outdated. Data, evaluations, feedback loops and documented know-how can be reused with each new model.

## What I took away

- Invest in data, evals and feedback loops that survive model changes.
- Ask if your setup can switch to a new model easily.
- Fine-tuned weights can be a depreciating asset.
