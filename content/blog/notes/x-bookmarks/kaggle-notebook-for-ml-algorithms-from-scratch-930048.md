## Summary

The author shares a Kaggle notebook with major ML concepts and algorithms written from scratch and explained, including supervised learning algorithms. The quoted post, by another learner, describes taking time and not rushing, and notes topics such as mini-batch gradient descent that one does not usually hand-write but is worth doing in practice.

## Background

Writing algorithms from scratch means coding them without a library that hides the details. It builds understanding of what each step does. Mini-batch gradient descent updates a model using small slices of data at a time.

## What I took away

- Implement algorithms yourself at least once to learn them.
- Slow and thorough beats rushing through concepts.
- Use the notebook as a reference while practicing.
