## Summary

Hesamation says to skip ML courses and use a repo with 300+ battle-tested case studies of how 80 companies, including Spotify, Netflix and Microsoft, designed ML systems. He says understanding systems in practice is a valuable skill.

## Background

Case studies show real tradeoffs such as data pipelines, serving and monitoring that courses often skip. The strong wording is the author's opinion, and courses still have value for fundamentals.

## What I took away

- Read real case studies to learn production ML design.
- Combine fundamentals from courses with practical examples.
