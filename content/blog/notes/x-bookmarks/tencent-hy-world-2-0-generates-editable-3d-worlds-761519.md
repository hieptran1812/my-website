## Summary

The author contrasts Genie3, which generates videos, with their work that generates usable 3D worlds. Tencent HYWorld 2.0 is an engine-ready world model. One image in gives a whole editable 3D world out, and it will be open sourced the next day.

## Background

A video world model makes frames you cannot edit. A real 3D scene can be loaded in a game engine and changed. That is the claimed difference.

## What I took away

- 3D output is more reusable than video.
- Open-source release helps testing.
- Claims are the author's.
