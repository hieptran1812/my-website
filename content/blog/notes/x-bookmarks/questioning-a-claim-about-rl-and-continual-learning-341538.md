## Summary

The author is confused by a Thinking Machines post on on-policy distillation. The post says RL only shapes behavior, cannot teach new knowledge well, and so cannot be enough for continual learning. The author thinks this seems plain wrong but admits they might be missing something.

## Background

Reinforcement learning (RL) trains a model using reward signals instead of fixed answers. Continual learning means a model keeps learning new things over time without forgetting old ones. On-policy distillation trains a student model on its own outputs, graded by a teacher. Whether RL can add knowledge or only reshapes behavior is an open debate.

## What I took away

- Treat strong claims about what RL can or cannot learn as debatable, not settled.
- Read the original On-Policy Distillation post before taking a side.
- Public disagreement from researchers is a good way to find open questions.
