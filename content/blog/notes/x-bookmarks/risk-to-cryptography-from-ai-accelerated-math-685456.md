## Summary

CZ jokes about the gap between his and Vitalik's math skills and recommends Vitalik's thread. The quoted post from Vitalik says no one should rush to move funds today, but the risks to cryptography from AI-accelerated math should be taken seriously, minimizing exposure to quantum-vulnerable and potentially AI-vulnerable cryptography.

## Background

Most crypto wallets rely on math problems that are hard to solve, like elliptic curve discrete logs. If AI helps discover new math shortcuts, some of these schemes could weaken, similar to the known threat from quantum computers.

## What I took away

- Crypto agility, the ability to switch algorithms, matters for long-lived systems.
- Vitalik explicitly says not to panic-move funds.
- Not financial advice; this is about security design.
