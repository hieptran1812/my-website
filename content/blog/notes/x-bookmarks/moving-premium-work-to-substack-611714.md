## Summary

@predict_addict says they are doing a Michael Burry and moving their most valuable work to Substack. They argue the internet is full of noise and that the real alpha is lost in the feed, so they want to build a community for the 1% of subscribers who care about the signal.

## Background

Writers often move from social feeds to newsletters to reach engaged readers and earn subscription income. It is a promotional announcement, and the 'alpha' language is marketing.

## What I took away

- Newsletters let writers own their audience.
- Judge paid research by its track record, not its marketing.
