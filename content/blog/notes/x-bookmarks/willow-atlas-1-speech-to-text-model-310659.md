## Summary

Willow introduces Atlas 1, a speech-to-text model for real-time dictation. It claims the model beats ElevenLabs, Deepgram, OpenAI and others by a wide margin, built on human-powered transcription infrastructure.

## Background

Speech-to-text turns audio into text, and accuracy is usually measured by word error rate. The comparison is the vendor's own claim.

## What I took away

- Vendor benchmarks need independent checks.
- Dictation needs low latency and accuracy.
- Human-labeled data can improve quality.
