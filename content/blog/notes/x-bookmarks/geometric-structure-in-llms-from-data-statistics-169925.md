## Summary

The author sarcastically quotes the line that LLMs are just stochastic parrots that memorize training data. The quoted thread says LLMs spontaneously form geometric manifolds, such as circles for months and spirals for timelines, and a new paper argues this comes from basic data statistics rather than deep learning dynamics.

## Background

A stochastic parrot is a critique that LLMs only repeat patterns. Researchers find that internal representations of cyclic concepts like months form circles. If statistics alone explain this, it says something about what structure data itself carries.

## What I took away

- Representations can be structured even without complex dynamics.
- Geometric structure does not by itself settle debates on understanding.
- Read the paper for the actual claim.
