## Summary

James Zou says introspection can greatly improve diffusion language models and congratulates the Together team on I-DLM being an oral spotlight at COLM 2026. The quoted post gives the talk details and says introspective consistency helps diffusion LMs match autoregressive quality while staying fast.

## Background

A diffusion language model generates text by refining many tokens in parallel over several steps, rather than one token at a time like autoregressive models. This can be faster, but quality has usually lagged.

## What I took away

- Diffusion LMs are an active alternative to autoregressive decoding.
- Read the paper to see what introspective consistency means in practice.
