## Summary

PatSnap Eureka promotes its Engineering product. It says it breaks down complex engineering challenges into root causes and generates structured, evidence-backed solution paths.

## Background

This is a vendor advertisement. Such tools combine patent and literature search with an LLM to suggest solutions.

## What I took away

- Ads describe features, not proven results.
- Try a trial on a real problem before trusting it.
- Check whether evidence is cited.
