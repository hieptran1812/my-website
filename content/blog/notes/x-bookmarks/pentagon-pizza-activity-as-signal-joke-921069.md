## Summary

The author says this index never fails to deliver. The quoted post reports an extreme spike in pizza activity near the Pentagon, with percentages for three pizza places and a DOUGHCON level of 4.

## Background

This is a long-running online joke that unusual late-night food orders near government buildings hint at big events. It is informal, not a reliable signal.

## What I took away

- Informal proxies can be entertaining but are weak evidence.
- Correlation without a mechanism is easy to over-read.
