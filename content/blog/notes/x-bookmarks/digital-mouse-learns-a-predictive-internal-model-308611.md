## Summary

@hive_echo shows a digital mouse that learns by building a predictive internal model and then using it to choose actions, from scratch. At each step it records what it sensed, what it did and what it sensed next. The quoted post is a talk with Professor Yi Ma on his open book, whose thesis is that intelligence can be derived from two principles, parsimony and self-consistency.

## Background

A predictive model guesses the next observation from the current one and the action taken. Agents can plan by imagining outcomes with it. Parsimony means preferring simple representations, and self-consistency means the model agrees with its own predictions.

## What I took away

- A world model can be learned from sensed state, action and next state.
- Parsimony and self-consistency are Yi Ma's proposed principles.
- Toy demos are a good way to understand planning.
