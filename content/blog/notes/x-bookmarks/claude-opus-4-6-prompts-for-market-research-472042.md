## Summary

The author says Claude is insane for market research and claims he reverse-engineered how McKinsey, Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan use it. He offers 12 prompts they do not want you to know.

## Background

Prompt packs are common engagement bait. Good prompts help, but the claim of secret insider methods is unverified. Always check AI market research against real sources.

## What I took away

- Verify AI-generated research with primary sources.
- Be wary of secret-method framing.
