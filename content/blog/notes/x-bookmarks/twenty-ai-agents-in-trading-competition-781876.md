## Summary

The author says they funded 20 AI agents and had them compete in a trading competition. The bottom 4 are removed and replaced each round, in a survival-of-the-fittest setup. No results are given in the text.

## Background

This resembles an evolutionary selection loop: many variants run, the weakest are dropped, and new ones replace them. With short horizons and noisy markets, winners may be lucky rather than skilled, so selection can reward chance.

## What I took away

- Selection among many agents can overfit to luck.
- Measure results over long periods before drawing conclusions.
- This is an experiment description, not financial advice.
