## Summary

The author says that just 3 weeks after launch, TokenSpeed already has support and adoption from Qwen and the PyTorch ecosystem, with no office hours or meetups. He ends with we can just build things. The quote is Qwen's post on its 580 tps record.

## Background

TokenSpeed is a new LLM inference engine. Fast adoption by a major model team is a strong signal, though it is the project's own framing.

## What I took away

- New open source tools can gain traction quickly with the right partners.
- Building and shipping beats promotion.
- Evaluate maturity before depending on a 3-week-old engine.
