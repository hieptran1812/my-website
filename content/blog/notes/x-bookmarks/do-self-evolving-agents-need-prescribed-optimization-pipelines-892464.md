## Summary

The author shares an arXiv paper titled Rethinking Self-Evolving Agents: Do We Still Need Prescribed Optimization Pipelines? The post contains only the title and link.

## Background

Self-evolving agents improve themselves over time. A prescribed pipeline means humans design the fixed steps for how the agent updates; the alternative is letting the agent decide how to improve itself.

## What I took away

- Read the paper for its answer and evidence.
- Consider how much of your agent's improvement process is hand-designed.
