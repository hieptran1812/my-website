## Summary

The author says to read their paper and stay tuned. The quoted Goodfire post says the most popular way to interpret AI misses the bigger picture: models think in curved shapes, but sparse autoencoders work with straight lines. It asks if they can still capture curved geometry and answers yes, but not how you might think.

## Background

SAEs break activations into a sum of straight-line features. If the real structure is curved, single features can miss it. The thread explores how groups of features can still reveal the curves.

## What I took away

- Feature geometry may be curved, not only linear.
- Read the thread for the clustering result.
- SAE results should be interpreted with care.
