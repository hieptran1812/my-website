## Summary

The authors claim the first experimental evidence of recursive self-improvement (RSI). They ran their autoresearch agent on improving the autoresearch agent itself for eight days. They report the result beats the harness they hand-tuned for two years, on held-out benchmarks.

## Background

Recursive self-improvement means a system improving the very process that improves it. Here, an AI research agent edits its own harness, the code and prompts that drive it. Held-out benchmarks are tests not used during optimization, which helps check for overfitting.

## What I took away

- Treat 'first evidence of RSI' as the authors' claim and look at the benchmarks.
- Automated harness search can beat long manual tuning.
- Held-out evaluation is essential when a system optimizes itself.
