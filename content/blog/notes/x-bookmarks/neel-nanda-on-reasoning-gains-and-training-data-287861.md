## Summary

Neel Nanda says the result is not very surprising but he is glad someone checked. The quoted paper asks how much AI reasoning gains are confounded by a training corpus that grew 10000x, and how much is local generalisation, meaning pattern-matching to hard-to-detect equivalent training data.

## Background

Benchmarks can look like reasoning when the model has seen similar problems in training. Testing this needs ways to find semantically equivalent data in huge corpora. It matters for judging real progress.

## What I took away

- Benchmark gains may partly be memorization of similar data.
- Contamination checks need to catch rephrased duplicates, not only exact matches.
