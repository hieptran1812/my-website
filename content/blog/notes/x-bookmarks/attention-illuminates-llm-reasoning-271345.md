## Summary

@LeYangco will present Attention Illuminates LLM Reasoning at ICML 2026. The quoted post says attention is a structured map of reasoning with a preplan-and-anchor rhythm, and aligning RL objectives with this rhythm gives more transparent results.

## Background

Attention shows which earlier tokens a model looks at when producing a token. Studying these patterns can reveal planning structure. RL objectives can then be shaped to match it.

## What I took away

- Attention patterns may expose a preplan and anchor rhythm.
- Interpretability findings can feed back into training objectives.
- Claims are from the authors; read the paper.
