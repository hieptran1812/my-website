## Summary

CAIS says Humanity's Last Exam was published in Nature last week. It claims model scores rose from under 5% to nearly 40% in just over a year and thanks Scale AI and 1000+ co-authors.

## Background

HLE is a hard benchmark of expert-level questions designed to stay difficult for AI. Rapid score gains show benchmarks saturate quickly. Tracking them helps policymakers.

## What I took away

- Hard benchmarks get saturated quickly.
- Scores are one signal of capability.
- Keep updating what counts as hard.
