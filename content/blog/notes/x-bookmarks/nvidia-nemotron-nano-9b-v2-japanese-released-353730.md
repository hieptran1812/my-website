## Summary

NVIDIA says Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2-Japanese reaches SOTA for models under 10B on Nejumi Leaderboard 4, with high-resolution Japanese understanding and advanced tool calling for edge use. It was built with Nemotron-Personas-Japan synthetic datasets. A Japanese post adds that it is commercially usable.

## Background

Small language models run on modest hardware. Synthetic personas data helps cover a language and culture. The leaderboard claim is NVIDIA's.

## What I took away

- Small language-specific models can beat bigger general ones locally.
- Synthetic data is used for non-English coverage.
