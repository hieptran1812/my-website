## Summary

DWF Labs posts a weekly market recap. It says BTC ETFs ended July with +$173M in cumulative flows and ETH ETFs with +$347M, reversing losing months in May and June. It also says the FOMC held rates at 3.50% to 3.75% for a fifth straight meeting, but the vote shifted from unanimous in June (the text is cut off).

## Background

ETF flows measure money moving into or out of exchange-traded funds that hold crypto. The FOMC is the US Federal Reserve committee that sets interest rates; split votes can signal a policy change ahead.

## What I took away

- Flows and central bank votes are context, not trading signals by themselves.
- Check the source data before drawing conclusions.
- This is educational context, not investment advice.
