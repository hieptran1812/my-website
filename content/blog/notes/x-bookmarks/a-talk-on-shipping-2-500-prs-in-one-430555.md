## Summary

The author says they shipped 2,500 PRs to production last month and shares a talk explaining how. It was meant for Cursor Compile in London, but they could not attend, so they posted it free on X. They suggest watching at 2x speed.

## Background

A PR (pull request) is a proposed code change reviewed before merging. Shipping thousands in a month implies heavy use of AI coding agents and automation. The details of the workflow are in the video.

## What I took away

- Watch the talk for concrete workflow ideas on agent-driven development.
- High PR counts mean little without review and quality practices; look for how they handle those.
- Consider how to split work into small, reviewable changes.
