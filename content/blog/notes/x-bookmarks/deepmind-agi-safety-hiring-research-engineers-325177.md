## Summary

The author is hiring at DeepMind AGI Safety for research engineers. The work is assessing catastrophic risks from frontier models, which informs safety cases and governance. Locations are London, SF and NYC, and both engineers and scientists are wanted.

## Background

A safety case is a structured argument that a system is safe enough to deploy. Frontier models are the most capable current models. Risk assessment involves testing what they can do that could be dangerous.

## What I took away

- Safety teams hire engineers, not only researchers.
- Safety cases link technical evaluations to governance decisions.
- Check the official posting for current openings.
