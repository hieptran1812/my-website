## Summary

The author proposes a four-step weekend project. Crawl papers from top venues, extract their limitations sections, put them into NotebookLM or Gemini, and ask questions to find unsolved problems.

## Background

Limitations sections list what a paper could not do, so they are a compact source of open problems. Long-context tools like NotebookLM can search and summarize many such passages at once. The answers still need human checking, since the model may combine or invent things.

## What I took away

- Limitations sections are a useful data source for research ideas.
- Pair a long-context tool with a focused corpus instead of the whole web.
- Verify any suggested open problem against the actual papers.
