## Summary

The author recommends following Sean for quality research on Wiener, Cybernetics, Phenomenology and Teleology. The quoted post is part 1 of how phenomenology gave us cybernetics, starting with Norbert Wiener's early life and his studies with Bertrand Russell.

## Background

Cybernetics studies control and feedback in machines and living things, and influenced AI and control theory. The history shows where ideas about feedback loops come from.

## What I took away

- Feedback and control ideas have a long intellectual history.
- History essays can deepen your view of modern AI concepts.
