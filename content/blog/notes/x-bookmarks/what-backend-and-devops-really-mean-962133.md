## Summary

The author lists what backend is not: a specific language, CRUD APIs glued together, only controllers, ORMs, or chasing new stacks. It is instead systems that stay correct under load and APIs that fail gracefully. The quoted post does the same for DevOps, saying it is not Kubernetes, Jenkins or Terraform but deployments without drama and problems caught before customers notice.

## Background

The point is that these roles are defined by outcomes such as reliability and predictability, not by specific tools. Tools change often, but the goals of correctness, safe deploys and early detection last.

## What I took away

- Judge your work by reliability outcomes, not by the tools used.
- Learn principles like graceful failure and safe deploys first, tools second.
