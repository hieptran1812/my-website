## Summary

The quoted post says that, although it seems too simple, a certain algorithm is the secret behind every RL environment and data company right now and is the algorithm for RSI. @michaelrbock jokes that many RL environment company CEOs texted him asking to delete it for giving away the alpha. The algorithm itself is not described in the text.

## Background

RL environment companies build tasks and graders that labs use to train models with reinforcement learning. RSI means recursive self-improvement, where AI improves its own training. The claim is the author's.

## What I took away

- Open the link to see the algorithm being discussed.
- Simple loops often underlie much of RL data work.
- Treat claims of secret algorithms with healthy skepticism.
