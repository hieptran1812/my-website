## Summary

@AlexanderKnigge calls the model insane and says its CLI renders LaTeX and handles differential geometry. The quoted post lists claimed specs: 30T MoE, 256 experts, 1M context, multimodal, beating Fable 5 on every benchmark.

## Background

MoE (mixture of experts) models route each token to a few expert sub-networks, so total size is large but compute per token is small. The post is hype-heavy and the specs come from social media claims, with a joke tone in nearby bookmarks.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of specs from hype posts.
- MoE size is not the same as active compute.
