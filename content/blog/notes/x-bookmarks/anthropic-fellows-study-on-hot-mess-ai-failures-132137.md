## Summary

The post shares new Anthropic Fellows research asking how misalignment scales with model intelligence and task complexity. It asks whether advanced AI will fail by pursuing wrong goals or fail unpredictably, like a hot mess. A link to the full write-up is included.

## Background

Misalignment means a model's behavior does not match what its designers intended. The research contrasts a model consistently pursuing a bad goal with one acting incoherently. Which failure mode is more likely changes what safety work should focus on.

## What I took away

- AI failure may look more like incoherence than a scheming plan.
- Task complexity may matter for how models fail.
- Read the linked write-up for details.
