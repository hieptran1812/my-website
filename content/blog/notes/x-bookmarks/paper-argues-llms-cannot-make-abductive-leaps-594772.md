## Summary

The author summarizes a new paper from DeepMind's Tom Zahavy, which argues LLMs cannot discover something like the next theory of relativity because "LLMs can't jump." The argument is that current AI masters induction (finding patterns) and deduction (applying rules) but lacks abduction, the creative leap to new explanations.

## Background

Induction generalizes from examples, deduction derives conclusions from rules, and abduction proposes the best new explanation for surprising facts. Many scientific breakthroughs are seen as abductive.

## What I took away

- Separate pattern-finding, rule-following and hypothesis creation when judging AI reasoning.
- This is one argument; a later bookmark in this batch shows the same author reconsidering it.
- Ask what kind of training could teach models to propose new hypotheses.
