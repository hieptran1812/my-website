## Summary

The author praises their scholars' work on improving Activation Oracles. They focus on making the tool qualitatively better, not just raising eval scores, since they want interpretability tools to be specific and reliable. The quoted post says AOs answer natural questions about activations but are vague and hallucinate, and identifies four fixes.

## Background

Activations are the internal numbers a model produces while running. An Activation Oracle is a model trained to describe them in words. If it hallucinates, its explanations cannot be trusted.

## What I took away

- Interpretability tools must be reliable, not just score well.
- Check how explanation tools are validated before trusting them.
