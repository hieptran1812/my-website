## Summary

The author lists their workflow for making these videos. They write the voiceover script, play it over songs until one fits, build a basic audio bed, add text and reference clips, and have a VA collect a folder of 400+ clips before generating more. The quoted post is a Risk more. Rise higher. Space x Kalshi video.

## Background

The workflow puts audio first and builds the picture around it. Preparing a big library of clips lets editors cut quickly. The example video is promotional content for the prediction market Kalshi.

## What I took away

- Start from the voiceover and music, then edit visuals to match.
- A large pre-collected clip library speeds up editing.
- Delegate clip collection to save creative time.
