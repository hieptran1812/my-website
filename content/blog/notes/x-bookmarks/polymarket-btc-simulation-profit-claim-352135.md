## Summary

The author claims a Chinese quant built a BTC price simulation engine with MiroFish and turned $2,000 into $166,000 in one trade, a 7,500% profit, with trades visible onchain on Polymarket. It links a wallet. These are unverified claims.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where positions are public. A single winning trade does not show a repeatable edge, and survivorship bias makes such stories misleading.

## What I took away

- One big win is not evidence of skill.
- Be skeptical of profit stories used to sell tools.
- Not trading advice.
