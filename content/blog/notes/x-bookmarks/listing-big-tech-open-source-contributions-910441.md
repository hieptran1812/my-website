## Summary

The author disputes the idea that companies like NVIDIA are anti open source. He lists NVIDIA projects such as NCCL, CUTLASS, Triton Inference, RAPIDS cuDF, Megatron-LM, TensorRT-LLM, Linux GPU kernel modules and Nemotron models with data and code, then starts on Microsoft's .NET and TypeScript. The quoted post says the poster does not want to ban open weight models but finds it interesting that some historically very anti open source companies now favor openness.

## Background

This is part of a debate about open models and which companies support them. Open source software and open weight models are related but different: one shares code, the other shares trained model parameters. Companies' motives for openness often include business strategy.

## What I took away

- Judge a company's openness by what it actually releases.
- Open source code and open weights are different kinds of openness.
- Business incentives often explain shifts in openness positions.
