## Summary

The author claims a Polymarket trader's arbitrage bot makes $110K per month. It does not trade direction but buys both Up and Down on the same market at once using a straddle. The post is cut off after that.

## Background

Buying both sides of a binary market can lock a gain if their combined cost is under $1. The profit figure is unverified, and real results depend on fees and fills. Treat it as a claim, not a method to copy.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of dollar-per-month claims without proof.
- Combined price under $1 is the core of this arbitrage idea.
- Costs and fill failures can remove the edge.
