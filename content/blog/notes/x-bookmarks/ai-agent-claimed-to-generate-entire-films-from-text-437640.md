## Summary

The author claims the first text-to-film AI agent is here. They say it can automatically generate an entire film, including script, storyboard, consistent characters, video, voice, lip-sync, LoRA and music, and promise a step-by-step tutorial.

## Background

Such agents chain several generative models: a language model for the script, image and video models for scenes, and speech and music models for audio. LoRA is a light fine-tuning method often used to keep a character's look consistent across shots.

## What I took away

- Treat 'first-ever' and 'wild' claims as marketing until you see the output quality.
- Character consistency across shots is the hard part of AI video pipelines.
- Multi-model pipelines are only as good as their weakest step.
