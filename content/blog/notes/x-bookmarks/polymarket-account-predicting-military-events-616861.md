## Summary

The author asks how a random Polymarket account predicted events such as US strikes on Venezuela and Iran, and notes his next bet is public. The quoted post claims a suspected military insider won 90K dollars on 9 military events and is now betting on US forces entering Iran.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people bet on event outcomes. Unusually accurate bets raise insider trading questions. These are claims in the posts, not verified facts.

## What I took away

- Prediction markets can leak information from informed participants.
- Be skeptical of insider claims without evidence.
- This is not betting advice.
