## Summary

Rork says Max replaces Xcode, installs on device in 1 click and publishes to the App Store in 2 clicks, powered by Swift, Claude Code and Opus. It claims it can build even Pokémon Go with AR and 3D.

## Background

Generating native apps by AI lowers the barrier. The one-shot claims are marketing.

## What I took away

- Test claims with a real app.
- Review generated code.
