## Summary

The quoted post introduces **Altar-1**, Aikido's first open-weight security model, pitched as frontier-grade defensive AI you can deploy yourself. Thom Wolf welcomes more open-weight security models with near-frontier defensive skills. He notes Altar-1 is a pruned and quantized version of GLM 5.3 small enough for one 4-H200 node.

## Background

Pruning removes less important weights or parts of a model to shrink it. Quantization stores weights in fewer bits to cut memory. Together they let large models run on less hardware, which matters for teams that want to keep security data in-house.

## What I took away

- Pruning plus quantization can make strong open models deployable on one node.
- Self-hosted security models keep sensitive data internal.
- Evaluate defensive models on your own threat scenarios.
