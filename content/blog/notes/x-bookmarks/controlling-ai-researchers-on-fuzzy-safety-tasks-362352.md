## Summary

@MiTerekhov announces a public Anthropic Fellows project. The labs plan to hand AI safety research to AIs, so the question is whether those AIs can be trusted. They explore how to control them on fuzzy tasks like writing research proposals, calling it a new direction in diffuse AI control.

## Background

AI control studies how to get useful work from a model even if it might act against you. Fuzzy tasks have no clear right answer, so errors or sabotage are hard to detect. That makes control harder than in tasks with checkable outputs.

## What I took away

- Delegating safety research to AI needs its own safeguards.
- Fuzzy tasks are a hard case for control.
