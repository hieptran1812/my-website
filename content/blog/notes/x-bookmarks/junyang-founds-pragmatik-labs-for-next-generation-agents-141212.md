## Summary

The author congratulates Junyang and Pragmatik Labs. In the quoted post, Junyang shares that he started a new company called Pragmatik (p7k) Labs in Shanghai, focused on research into next-generation agents across digital and physical worlds. He thanks Gaorong Ventures and HSG for co-leading the funding round, and Tencent.

## Background

Agents across digital and physical worlds covers both software agents that use computers and embodied agents like robots. New startups from experienced researchers are a signal of where talent and funding are moving.

## What I took away

- Agent research is drawing new startups and venture funding.
- Follow new labs to see what research directions they publish.
