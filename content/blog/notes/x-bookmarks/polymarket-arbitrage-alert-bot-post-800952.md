## Summary

The post says a trading bot called AlertPilots found a new arbitrage between Polymarket and Opinion, valued at $74, and that the market resolves soon. It looks like an automated alert and may be promotion for the bot.

## Background

Arbitrage means profiting from a price difference between two markets for the same outcome. Prediction markets can price the same event differently. Real arbitrage is often small and may disappear after fees, slippage or resolution risk, so such alerts should be read skeptically.

## What I took away

- Check fees, liquidity and resolution rules before believing an arbitrage claim.
- Alert bot posts can be marketing for a paid service.
- This is not trading advice.
