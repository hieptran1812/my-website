## Summary

The author says Karpathy left an AI running for 2 days to improve itself and it returned about 20 changes that worked, claiming AI is now its own best researcher. The quoted Karpathy post says the changes improved validation loss, were additive and transferred to larger models.

## Background

The system tuned a training setup, not itself in a broad sense. The tweets frame it as self-improvement, but it is automated experiment search on a defined metric.

## What I took away

- Distinguish tuning a training recipe from general self-improvement.
- The source post is the reliable detail; hype is commentary.
