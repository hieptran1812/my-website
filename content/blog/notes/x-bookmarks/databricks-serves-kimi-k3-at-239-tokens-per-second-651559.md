## Summary

The author says Databricks serves **Kimi K3** at 239 tokens per second and is now number one for Kimi K3 speed and latency on Artificial Analysis. He notes it is a 2.8T parameter model and the largest open-source model they have served.

## Background

Artificial Analysis is a site that benchmarks AI providers on speed, latency and price. Tokens per second measures output speed for a single request. Very large MoE models need many GPUs and careful parallelism to serve fast.

## What I took away

- Provider speed for the same model can vary a lot.
- Check both speed and accuracy, since fast serving can involve trade-offs.
- Leaderboard positions change often.
