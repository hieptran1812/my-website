## Summary

The author says they added Polymarket data to the public dataset: 400M+ trades going back to 2020, 36GB compressed, MIT licensed, free to download via Cloudflare R2.

## Background

Large open datasets let researchers study prediction market behavior and test models. An MIT license means you may use and modify it freely. R2 is Cloudflare's object storage.

## What I took away

- Open data lowers the barrier for market research.
- Check data quality before building on it.
- Past trades do not guarantee future results.
