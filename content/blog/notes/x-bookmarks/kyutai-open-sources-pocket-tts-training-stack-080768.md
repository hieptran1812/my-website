## Summary

Kyutai suggests a weekend project: train your own text-to-speech model from scratch on your GPU and run it on any device's CPU. They have open-sourced the full **Pocket TTS** training stack, including data pipeline, recipes and evals. The post is cut off while describing how well it learns.

## Background

Text-to-speech (TTS) models turn written text into audio. Small TTS models can run on ordinary CPUs, which suits phones and offline use. Having the full training stack lets you train on your own voice data or languages.

## What I took away

- Small TTS models can now be trained on a single GPU and run on CPU.
- Open training stacks are great for learning the full pipeline, not just inference.
- Start with the provided recipes and evals before customizing.
