## Summary

Neel Nanda thanks Welch Labs for an animated video about grokking, including a deep dive into his work reverse engineering a transformer that does modular addition. He especially likes the animations of 2D Fourier Transforms.

## Background

Grokking is when a model suddenly generalizes long after it has memorized training data. Reverse engineering the modular addition transformer found it uses Fourier-like features to compute answers. Visual explainers make this hard result easier to follow.

## What I took away

- Grokking shows models can learn clean algorithms after memorizing.
- Animated explainers are a good entry point into interpretability.
- Fourier features appear in small models doing modular arithmetic.
