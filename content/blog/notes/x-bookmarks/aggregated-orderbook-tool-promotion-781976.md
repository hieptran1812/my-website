## Summary

MarketLens responds to a post about visualizing 20 full-depth orderbooks, saying managing 20 separate ones is chaotic. They promote their multi-exchange aggregated orderbook. It is marketing.

## Background

An orderbook lists buy and sell orders by price. Aggregating combines exchanges into one view.

## What I took away

- Aggregation reduces clutter.
- This is a product pitch.
