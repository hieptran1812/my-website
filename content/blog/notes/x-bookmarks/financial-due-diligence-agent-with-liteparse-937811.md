## Summary

LlamaIndex says financial analysts spend about 70% of their time pulling numbers out of PDFs. They built a demo agent that ingests SEC filings and answers questions with exact citations highlighted on the original PDF page, in about 600 lines of Next.js, with no vector DB, just LiteParse. The link card is a how-to guide.

## Background

Citation highlighting maps answers back to page coordinates so users can verify them. LiteParse gives text with layout positions. The 70% figure is the post's claim.

## What I took away

- Exact citations build trust in document agents.
- You may not need a vector DB for a focused use case.
- Layout-aware parsing enables highlight-on-page features.
