## Summary

DWF Labs says real liquidity, products and outcomes tell the truth. The quoted post says crypto in 2026 is about balance sheets, yield and who survives when leverage gets wiped, according to DWF Labs.

## Background

A balance sheet shows assets and liabilities, so surviving a downturn depends on how much debt and leverage a firm holds. The claim is a market outlook from a firm with its own interests, not a fact. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Outlooks come from parties with their own interests.
- Leverage and solvency matter in downturns.
- Judge projects on fundamentals, not narratives.
