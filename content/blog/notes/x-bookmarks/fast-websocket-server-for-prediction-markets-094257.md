## Summary

After a month of work, the author's team at getdomeapi says its websocket server for prediction markets is now out of beta and open to the public. They describe it as the fastest.

## Background

A websocket keeps a connection open so data is pushed in real time instead of polled. Low latency matters for bots that react to market changes. The speed claim is the vendor's own.

## What I took away

- Real-time streaming is key for prediction market bots.
- Benchmark latency yourself before trusting a 'fastest' claim.
