## Summary

@sheriyuo shares good memories and encourages the person to make the most of their prime years, adding that at 16 they were gaming in a computer lab and at 21 had already developed tech the year before. The quoted post is a life update from someone who recently left Kimi Moonshot, calling the past year the best of their life and thanking supportive colleagues.

## Background

Researchers often move between AI labs, and departure posts give a glimpse of lab culture. Very young engineers are taking major roles at Chinese AI labs.

## What I took away

- Supportive teams are often what people value most in a job.
- Early-career people can contribute significantly at frontier labs.
