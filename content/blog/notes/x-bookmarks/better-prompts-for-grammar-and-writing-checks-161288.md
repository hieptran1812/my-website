## Summary

@HeyAmit_ tells people to stop asking ChatGPT to check my grammar and writing, saying bad prompts give bad results. They offer alternative prompts, which are in the attached content.

## Background

Specific prompts give better results because the model knows what to look for. For editing, you might state the audience, tone and what to keep. The post's examples are not in the text.

## What I took away

- Specific instructions beat vague ones.
- State audience and style for editing tasks.
- Open the post for the prompts.
