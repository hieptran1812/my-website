## Summary

The author says an era of "vibe quant trading" is starting. The quoted launch post for **Minara Strategy Studio** claims you can design strategies, backtest, optimize and go live in plain words, and says quant trading is solved. It is a closed beta.

## Background

Backtesting means running a strategy on past data to see how it would have done. Past results often look better than live results because of overfitting. This is a product promotion, so treat the claims as marketing.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of the claim that quant trading is solved.
- A good backtest does not guarantee live performance.
- Educational note only, not financial advice.
