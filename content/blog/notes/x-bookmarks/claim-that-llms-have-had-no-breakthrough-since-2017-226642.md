## Summary

The author claims there has been no breakthrough in LLMs for 9 years. They point out that Attention Is All You Need came out in June 2017 and applied sparse MoE in January 2017.

## Background

The Transformer architecture and sparsely-gated mixture-of-experts layers both date from 2017 and remain the base of most large models. Others would argue that later advances in scaling, RL post-training, and inference count as breakthroughs, so this is an opinion.

## What I took away

- Core architectures have been stable; much progress came from scale, data and training.
- Treat this as a provocative claim, not a settled fact.
- Ask what counts as a breakthrough before debating it.
