## Summary

LlamaIndex says AI document parsing has evolved past OCR to systems that understand documents like humans. In its latest blog post it explores what is changing, starting with zero-shot semantic understanding among other points.

## Background

OCR turns images of text into text but does not understand layout, tables or meaning. Newer systems use vision-language models to read structure and context. This matters for feeding PDFs to LLMs reliably.

## What I took away

- Plain OCR loses layout and table structure.
- Model-based parsers can handle new document types without templates.
- Test parsers on your own documents.
