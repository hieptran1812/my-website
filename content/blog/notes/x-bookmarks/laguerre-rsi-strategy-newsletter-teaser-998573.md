## Summary

The author promotes a Laguerre RSI as a smoother, faster and cleaner momentum indicator than the classic RSI. The full strategy, rules and code are behind a newsletter. The quoted post similarly promotes a Kalman filter trading strategy with a Python backtest.

## Background

RSI measures momentum on a 0 to 100 scale. Laguerre RSI uses a filter to make it smoother. A Kalman filter estimates a hidden trend from noisy prices. These are indicators, not guarantees.

## What I took away

- Smoother indicators lag differently, not necessarily better.
- Promotional posts hide details behind signups.
- Educational, not advice.
