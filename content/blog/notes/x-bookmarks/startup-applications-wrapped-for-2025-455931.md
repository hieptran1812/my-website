## Summary

@_tenZdhon_ shares their 2025 startup applications. They were rejected by YC three times, a16z speedrun, Z Fellows, Thiel Fellowship, Next36 and Nucleate, and accepted by GameOn, NormanESCH and MARs. They say they will continue building.

## Background

Many founders are rejected from top programs before getting traction. Sharing rejections openly shows the normal ratio of attempts to acceptances.

## What I took away

- Rejection is common even for strong applicants.
- Keep building while applying.
- Smaller programs can still help.
