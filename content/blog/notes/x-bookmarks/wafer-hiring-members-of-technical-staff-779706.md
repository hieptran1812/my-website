## Summary

Wafer is calling for strong engineers to join as Members of Technical Staff. The company claims it has grown revenue 30 times in three months. It wants people who want to work on very hard problems with little abstraction between them and the hardware.

## Background

Working with little abstraction from hardware usually means writing GPU kernels, low-level performance code and system software. Growth figures in hiring posts are self-reported claims.

## What I took away

- Low-level GPU performance skills are in demand.
- Treat growth claims in hiring posts as marketing until verified.
