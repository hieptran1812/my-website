## Summary

The author announces a work selected as an ICLR Oral on how models learn to associate tokens and build semantic concepts. They find that early-stage features in attention-based models can be written as compositions of three basis features.

## Background

Attention lets a model relate tokens to each other. Studying early training shows how word meaning relations first form. Finding a few basis features gives a simple picture of a complex process.

## What I took away

- Learning dynamics can be explained with few components.
- Early training reveals how semantics form.
- Read the paper for the three basis features.
