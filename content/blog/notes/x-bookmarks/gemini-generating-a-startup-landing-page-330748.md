## Summary

The author says Gemini made an entire landing page in a single shot and claims no AI model had designed a startup landing page like this before. They share the prompt used in the post.

## Background

One-shot generation means the model produces the whole result from a single prompt without follow-up fixes. Landing page design is a common test of how well a model handles layout, copy and code together. The quality claim here is the author's own.

## What I took away

- The prompt is the useful part; look at how it is structured.
- Treat one-shot demos as best cases, not typical results.
- Test with your own brief before trusting the claim.
