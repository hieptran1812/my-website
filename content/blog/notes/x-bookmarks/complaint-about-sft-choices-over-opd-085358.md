## Summary

The author says someone on the Microsoft AI team dislikes OPD, synthetic data and SFT on reasoning styles, yet likes SFT when OPD could be used. The quoted post expresses frustration that domain experts are not SFTed and that SFT is used for consolidation instead of OPD.

## Background

SFT trains on fixed examples, while OPD (on-policy distillation) trains on the student's own outputs scored by a teacher. Many see OPD as better at avoiding drift between train and test conditions.

## What I took away

- Opinionated critique, not a measured result.
- OPD is increasingly favored over plain SFT.
