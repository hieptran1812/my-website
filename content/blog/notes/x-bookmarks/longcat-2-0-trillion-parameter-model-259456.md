## Summary

@sheriyuo highlights the claim of the first trillion-parameter model trained end to end on a 50,000-GPU Chinese cluster. The quoted post introduces LongCat-2.0: 1.6T parameters, ~48B active, 1M context, built for agentic coding, with LongCat Sparse Attention.

## Background

Active parameters are used per token. Sparse attention makes 1M context affordable.

## What I took away

- Chinese compute is scaling large MoEs.
- Claims are from the release.
