## Summary

The author's takeaway is that interpretability tools like logit-lens, J-lens and steering methods can probe how physics knowledge and relationships are stored in LLMs. The quoted post says an LLM represents physical relationships in a form we can read and steer, and gives insight into post-training signals that help deeper generalization across materials science.

## Background

The logit lens projects intermediate activations into word predictions. Steering changes behavior by editing activations. Using them on science concepts shows whether models hold structured knowledge.

## What I took away

- Internal representations can be read and steered.
- Post-training may shape how principles generalize.
- Check the paper for specifics.
