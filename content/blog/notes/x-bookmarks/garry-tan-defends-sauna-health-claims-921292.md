## Summary

@garrytan says he told people sauna is awesome and tells haters to ignore it. The quoted post claims fifteen sauna sessions dramatically reduced environmental toxins, including a 65% drop in 2,4-D, as part of a self-experiment.

## Background

This is a personal health self-experiment, not a controlled study. Results from one person do not prove an effect. It is off-topic for most tech readers and is saved only as a bookmark.

## What I took away

- One person's self-tracking is not strong evidence.
- Be careful with health claims on social media.
