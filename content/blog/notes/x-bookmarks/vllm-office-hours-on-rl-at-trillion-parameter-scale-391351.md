## Summary

The speaker jokes that the talk will not beat its thumbnail and invites people to attend. The quoted post announces vLLM Office Hours: RL at 1T Scale, a prime-rl performance deep dive by Prime Intellect. It covers training trillion-parameter MoE models like GLM-5.1 on agentic RL, plus what is new in vLLM 0.25.

## Background

prime-rl is Prime Intellect's RL training framework. In RL training, an inference engine like vLLM generates the rollouts, so its performance directly affects training speed. Office hours are recurring public sessions for the community.

## What I took away

- RL training speed depends heavily on inference engine performance.
- Recordings of such sessions are good learning resources.
- Check vLLM release notes for version 0.25 changes.
