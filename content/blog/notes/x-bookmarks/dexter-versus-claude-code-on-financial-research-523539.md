## Summary

@virattt says he ran overnight tests of Dexter against Claude Code and Dexter came out ahead on complex financial tasks needing deep research. He reports it was 92% faster, 26% cheaper and 31% more correct. He uses Claude Code often, and the text is cut off at a key challenge for it.

## Background

Dexter is the author's own financial research agent. A specialized agent can beat a general coding agent on a narrow task. The numbers come from his own test, with no independent check.

## What I took away

- Specialized agents can beat general ones on narrow domains.
- Self-run benchmarks should be treated as a claim until reproduced.
