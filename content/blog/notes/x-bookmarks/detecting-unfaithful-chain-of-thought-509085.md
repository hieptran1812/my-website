## Summary

The author publishes their MATS application work and says they were selected for MATS 10.0 in Neel Nanda's stream. The project detects unfaithful chain-of-thought reasoning and suggests CoT forms a connected causal topology. It is the start of a thread.

## Background

Unfaithful CoT means the written reasoning does not reflect what the model actually used to get its answer. Detecting it matters for trusting model explanations.

## What I took away

- Written reasoning is not always the true reason.
- Small projects can be strong application work.
