## Summary

@MMT_Official_ responds to the claim that liquidation heatmaps do not work. They say that sentence reveals more about the user than the tool.

## Background

A liquidation heatmap shows where leveraged positions might be forced to close. Traders use it to see price levels with likely forced buying or selling. A tool is only as useful as how it is used. The post is a defense by a company that sells such a tool.

## What I took away

- A tool's value depends on how you use it.
- The author has a commercial interest.
- Test any indicator yourself.
