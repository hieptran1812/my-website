## Summary

Excalidraw announces upcoming Mermaid improvements: a proper code editor for manual edits, prominent errors with a possible auto-fix, and an improved renderer with fixed text wrapping and better styling support.

## Background

Mermaid is a text-based language for diagrams. Excalidraw can convert Mermaid text into hand-drawn style diagrams. Better errors and rendering make text-to-diagram workflows smoother.

## What I took away

- Text-to-diagram workflows are getting easier to debug.
- Auto-fix is early, per the authors.
