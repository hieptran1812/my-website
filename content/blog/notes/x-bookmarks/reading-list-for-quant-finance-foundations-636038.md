## Summary

The author lists foundation books for quant, trading and ML in finance. These include Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance, Paul Wilmott on Quantitative Finance, Quantitative Finance For Dummies by Steve Bell, and Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives by John C. Hull. The list continues past the shown text.

## Background

Quantitative finance uses math and statistics to price instruments and manage risk. Hull's book is a common reference on derivatives, and Wilmott's books cover the models behind them. These are learning resources, not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Start with derivatives pricing basics before ML in finance.
- Hull and Wilmott are common references to bookmark.
- Books give theory, so pair them with hands-on practice.
