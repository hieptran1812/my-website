## Summary

Anthropic announces new research on next-generation Constitutional Classifiers to protect against jailbreaks. It says novel methods, including practical use of its interpretability work, make protection more effective and less costly than before.

## Background

A jailbreak is a prompt designed to make a model ignore its safety rules. A classifier is a separate model that checks inputs or outputs for harmful content. Lower cost matters because the checks run on every request.

## What I took away

- Safety filters must be both strong and cheap to be deployed.
- Interpretability research can have practical safety uses.
- Read the paper for details and measured results.
