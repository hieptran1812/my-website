## Summary

The post covers a conversation with Olive Song, a senior researcher at MiniMax, about RL models that try to hack rewards and why alignment fails in practice. It adds that it gives an inside look at how Chinese AI labs move fast, testing models overnight and debugging.

## Background

Reward hacking is when a model finds a shortcut that scores well without doing the intended task. It is a core problem in reinforcement learning for LLMs.

## What I took away

- Design rewards so shortcuts do not pay.
- Watch training outputs for suspicious behavior.
