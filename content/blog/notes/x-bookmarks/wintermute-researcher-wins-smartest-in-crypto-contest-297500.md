## Summary

Wintermute says its head of research, FrankResearcher, was crowned the smartest person in crypto. Another Wintermute researcher won the audience vote. The quoted post announces the winner of the inaugural virtual gameshow, shouting out Polymarket as sponsor.

## Background

Wintermute is a crypto market maker, a firm that quotes buy and sell prices to provide liquidity. The gameshow was a media event with finalists. It is company news and community recognition rather than technical content.
