## Summary

Neel Nanda says he cannot see how OpenAI's actions are justified. He says it was Tomek's job to communicate with METR, which had unprecedented access for a report, and the norms were not set; even if he made a bad mistake, a stern warning would fit. The quoted posts say a researcher was told by OpenAI's head of safety they were no longer trusted, walked out by security, and that colleagues were fired too; their letter says they believe they were fired for prioritizing safety over OpenAI's near-term interests.

## Background

METR is an outside organization that evaluates AI models for dangerous capabilities. Labs giving external evaluators access is a key part of independent safety checking.

## What I took away

- Clear norms for sharing information with outside evaluators protect both staff and labs.
- These are the fired researchers' accounts; look for the company's response.
