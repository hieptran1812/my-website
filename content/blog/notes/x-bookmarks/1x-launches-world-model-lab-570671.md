## Summary

The author says they are going all in on world models and launching the 1X World Model Lab. Their bet is that you cannot fine-tune your way to AGI or to robots that operate in the physical world. General-purpose humanoids need models that, per the cut-off post, are built for that.

## Background

A world model predicts how the environment changes, often from video. For robots, it lets them imagine outcomes and learn from many examples without physical trials.

## What I took away

- Robotics labs are betting on world models.
- The strong claim about fine-tuning is the author's view.
