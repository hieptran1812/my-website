## Summary

The author says there are many excellent data engineering whitepapers. They added papers on git for data, a case for client protocols, and more AI-related ones to their curated list. They invite readers to pick a favorite from the linked page.

## Background

A whitepaper is a detailed technical report describing a system or idea, often from industry or research. Reading them is a way to see how real data systems are designed. A curated list saves time finding the good ones.

## What I took away

- Whitepapers are a good source of system design ideas.
- Bookmark the list and pick one paper to read at a time.
