## Summary

@alexcdot says his team found over 50 papers at NeurIPS 2025 with AI hallucinations. He says researchers from places like Google DeepMind, Meta, MIT and Cambridge allowed LLMs to generate content and that the slop is bad. The post is cut off after that.

## Background

A hallucination is when an LLM states something false, such as a fake citation. If authors do not check LLM text, errors enter the scientific record. The count is the author's claim and the method is not in the post.

## What I took away

- Verify citations and facts in LLM-written text.
- Peer review may miss fabricated references.
- Check the original report for how papers were flagged.
