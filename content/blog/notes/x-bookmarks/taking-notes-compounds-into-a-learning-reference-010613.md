## Summary

The author says taking notes is a habit with serious compounding returns. All their learning notes since entering the field live in their awesome-ml-sys tutorial, which they treat as a personal reference for quick lookups. The link card points to an RLHF system design page in that repository.

## Background

RLHF means reinforcement learning from human feedback, a way to tune language models using preference signals. Systems write-ups explain how training and serving pipelines are built. A public notes repo doubles as a learning resource for others.

## What I took away

- Keep running notes as you learn; they pay off over time.
- Publishing personal notes can help others and keep you honest.
- Use a single searchable place for quick lookups.
