## Summary

The author praises a piece of writing. They argue humans excel at forming good abstractions under uncertainty with missing pieces, and that machines will not catch up soon. They wonder how our brain, an easy-to-train model, does this.

## Background

An abstraction is a simplified concept that captures what matters and drops detail. Learning these from sparse, noisy data is a central question in AI and cognitive science. The claim about machines is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Abstraction from incomplete data remains an open research question.
- Treat the prediction about machines as opinion.
