## Summary

The author says someone turned Elon Musk's thinking framework into AI prompts, describing it as a billionaire engineer tearing apart your ideas and rebuilding them from physics. They share 15 prompts that they say changed how they solve problems. The prompts are not in the saved text.

## Background

First-principles thinking means breaking a problem into basic facts and rebuilding from there rather than copying existing solutions. Prompts can make an AI model critique ideas this way. The glowing claims are the author's.

## What I took away

- Prompts that force first-principles critique can sharpen ideas.
- Treat marketing language skeptically and test prompts yourself.
