## Summary

Meta says it tested its progress on automating AI research by entering **AIRA3**, the next generation of its autonomous AI research system, in a live Kaggle competition in June. The competition was run by NVIDIA and asked entrants to fine-tune a 30B Nemotron model to reason. The post is cut off before the results.

## Background

An autonomous research system is an AI agent that plans experiments, writes code, trains models and evaluates them with little human help. A live competition is a useful test because the tasks and leaderboard are external, so the agent cannot be tuned to a private benchmark. Fine-tuning means further training an existing model on new data to teach it a skill.

## What I took away

- Live external competitions are a stronger test of research agents than internal benchmarks.
- Fine-tuning for reasoning is now a task research agents are being asked to do end to end.
- Check the full post for how AIRA3 actually ranked before drawing conclusions.
