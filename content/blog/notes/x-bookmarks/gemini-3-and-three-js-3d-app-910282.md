## Summary

EHuanglu says they were blown away: Gemini 3 plus three.js produced a 3D product app controlled by hand gestures for rotation, scale and lighting, with zero coding. A tutorial with prompts and code is linked.

## Background

three.js is a JavaScript 3D library. LLMs can write such code, though results need testing.

## What I took away

- Prompts can produce interactive 3D demos.
- Verify the code.
