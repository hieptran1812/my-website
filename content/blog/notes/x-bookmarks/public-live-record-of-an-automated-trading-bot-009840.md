## Summary

The post shares a live record of one automated Pons runner on Robinhood Chain. It shows every trade, winners and losers, refreshed every minute, and says nothing is for sale or hidden.

## Background

An automated runner is a trading bot that places trades by rules. A public, live trade log is more transparent than screenshots of gains, but a short or selective record still says little about long-term results.

## What I took away

- Prefer full trade logs over profit screenshots when judging bots.
- Past results, even if transparent, do not predict future returns.
- This is not a recommendation to trade or use any bot.
