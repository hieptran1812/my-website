## Summary

The author writes for people who feel FOMO because they could be working on advocacy, policy, theory, empirical research or evals, whichever seems most urgent. They say many people feel the same and link a Substack post.

## Background

In fast-moving fields, there are many possible directions, and it is hard to know which matters most. Comparing yourself to all of them can cause stress and indecision.

## What I took away

- Feeling unsure about the best direction is common.
- Pick a path and make steady progress.
- Read the linked post for detailed advice.
