## Summary

The author says some YouTube videos are great for understanding language diffusion models. They call the channel by @juliarturc underrated, well structured and visually rich, which makes the topic easy to follow.

## Background

Language diffusion models generate text by starting from noise or masked tokens and refining them in steps, instead of predicting one token at a time. It is an alternative to standard autoregressive LLMs. Visual explanations help because the process differs from the usual approach.

## What I took away

- Use video explainers to get the intuition for diffusion language models.
- Compare diffusion decoding with left-to-right decoding.
