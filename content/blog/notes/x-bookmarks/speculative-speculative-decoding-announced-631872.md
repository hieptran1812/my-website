## Summary

The author announces Speculative Speculative Decoding (SSD), a new LLM inference algorithm that they claim is up to 2x faster than the strongest inference engines. It is a collaboration with Tri Dao and Avner May, with details in the thread.

## Background

Speculative decoding means a small model drafts several tokens and the big model checks them in one pass. SSD builds on it, per the name. The 2x figure is the author's claim.

## What I took away

- Read the thread for method and benchmarks.
- Compare against your serving baseline before adopting.
