## Summary

Screenpipe announces its tool that records and learns how you work, turning it into searchable memory, standard operating procedures and AI agents. It is open source and local-first, with 20K+ GitHub stars, 1,900+ forks and 130+ contributors.

## Background

Local-first means data stays on your machine by default rather than in a cloud. SOPs are written step-by-step procedures. Recording screen activity gives agents context about real workflows, but raises privacy concerns.

## What I took away

- Screen recording tools can capture sensitive data; check what is stored and where.
- Local-first design reduces but does not remove privacy risk.
- Mining your own workflows for SOPs is a practical use of AI memory.
