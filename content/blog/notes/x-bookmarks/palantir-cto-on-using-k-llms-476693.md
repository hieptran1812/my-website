## Summary

The CTO's line is to never use one LLM when you can use K-LLMs. The quote is a jab at Michael Burry's understanding of Palantir.

## Background

Using several models and combining answers can improve reliability.

## What I took away

- Ensembles reduce single-model errors.
- They cost more.
