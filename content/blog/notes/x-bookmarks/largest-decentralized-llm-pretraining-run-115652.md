## Summary

The author calls it the largest model ever trained through decentralized compute and says LLM training needs to decentralize soon. The quoted post says Covenant-72B, trained permissionlessly on Bittensor subnet 3, has 72B parameters and about 1.1T tokens over commodity internet, with no centralized cluster or whitelist.

## Background

Normal frontier training uses tightly connected data centers. Training over ordinary internet is hard because communication is slow, so this relies on methods that tolerate low bandwidth and changing participants.

## What I took away

- Decentralized pretraining at 72B scale is claimed as a first of its size.
- Low-bandwidth training methods are the key technical challenge.
- Treat the claims as the project's own until independently checked.
