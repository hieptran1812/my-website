## Summary

The authors say they ran an OpenClaw agent trading funding rate arbitrage for 20 days and that it generated more than 30% APR on a $10k account. They used LorisTools as the data layer and say the result beat their expectations. These are self-reported results.

## Background

Funding rate arbitrage in crypto perpetual futures tries to earn the periodic funding payment while holding offsetting positions to limit price risk. A short test window and small account do not show how it performs over time or in stress.

## What I took away

- Treat short-period, self-reported returns with skepticism.
- Annualizing 20 days of results can mislead.
- Consider fees, slippage, liquidation and exchange risk before trusting any strategy.
