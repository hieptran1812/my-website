## Summary

The post lists 9 AI research roles at labs in SF with salaries from $150K to $500K and 22 engineering roles at startups from $180K to $500K. It invites readers to text an account to get set up.

## Background

This is a recruiting promotion. Salary ranges are the poster's claims and the actual roles are not shown.

## What I took away

- Verify listings with the employers directly.
- Be careful sharing personal data with intermediaries.
- Salary ranges vary a lot.
