## Summary

The author says a bank analyst published a report showing Kalshi users lose money faster than sports gamblers. The author claims Kalshi first pressured the data provider to change its data. When that failed, Kalshi allegedly accused the provider and the analyst of conspiring to extort it.

## Background

Kalshi is a prediction market, where people trade contracts on the outcome of real events. Analysts often rely on third-party data providers to measure how users perform. The post is one side of a dispute and the claims are not verified here.

## What I took away

- Treat one-sided accounts of corporate disputes with caution until confirmed.
- Data providers can become pressure points when their data is unflattering to a platform.
- Reports on retail trader losses are useful context for anyone using prediction markets.
