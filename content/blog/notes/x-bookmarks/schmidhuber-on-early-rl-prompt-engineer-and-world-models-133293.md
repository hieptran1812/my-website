## Summary

The author says that 10 years ago he described an **RL prompt engineer**, which is adaptive chain of thought: an RL neural net learns to query its world model net for abstract reasoning and decision making. He notes it goes beyond his 1990 neural world model. The text is cut off.

## Background

A world model is a network that predicts what happens next in an environment. A controller can query it to plan. Chain of thought in LLMs is a modern relative of this idea. The priority claim is the author's.

## What I took away

- World models and chain of thought have long histories.
