## Summary

The author says the flow-based language model agenda continues and praises @ayhozade's work on reasoning through iterative denoising. The quoted post introduces Thinking with Looped Flows, which trains recurrent reasoning with local denoising and claims SOTA on ARC-AGI-1 and 2 among looped models.

## Background

Flow-based models learn to transform noise into data along a smooth path, similar to diffusion. Applying them to language and reasoning is an alternative to standard autoregressive models that predict one token at a time.

## What I took away

- Flow and diffusion ideas are spreading from images into language and reasoning.
- Watch this line of work as an alternative to autoregressive reasoning.
