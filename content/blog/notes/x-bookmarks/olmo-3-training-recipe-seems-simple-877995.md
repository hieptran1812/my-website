## Summary

The author watched a detailed video on OLMo 3 by Nathan Lambert and found the recipe extremely basic. He agrees that execution is the hard part, but finds it inspiring that training a SOTA or near-SOTA model is so easy and needs nothing special. The quoted post recommends Lambert's video.

## Background

OLMo is a fully open model family, with open data and training details. When the recipe is public, readers can see that results come from well-run standard steps rather than secret tricks. Still, running them at scale takes data quality, engineering and compute.

## What I took away

- Open recipes show that strong models rely on solid basics.
- Execution details and data quality are the hard part.
- Watch the OLMo 3 video to see a complete recipe.
