## Summary

Simile says it raised **$200M at a $2B valuation** in a Series B led by Greenoaks. Other participants include Index Ventures, Hanabi, A*, Bain Capital Ventures, CVS Health Ventures and Definition. The company states its mission is to simulate all eight billion people on earth accurately.

## Background

A Series B is a later funding round for a startup that has shown some traction. Simulating people usually means using AI agents to model how individuals or groups would respond to products, policies or messages. Accuracy of such simulations is hard to verify.

## What I took away

- Human simulation with AI is attracting large investment.
- Ask how such simulations are validated against real behavior.
- Funding size says little about whether the approach works.
