## Summary

The author praises work showing physics simulators can be scalable data generators for physical reasoning. The quoted post says Sim2Reason trains LLMs in virtual worlds governed by physics laws with zero human annotation, and claims a 5-10% zero-shot improvement on the International Physics Olympiad.

## Background

Simulators produce unlimited problems with known answers, so no human labeling is needed. Zero-shot means the model was not trained on the test task. The gain figure is the authors' claim.

## What I took away

- Simulators can give cheap, verifiable training data.
- Check how well simulated physics transfers to real tasks.
