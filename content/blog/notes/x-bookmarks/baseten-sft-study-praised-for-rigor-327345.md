## Summary

@JoshPurtell calls the study helpful and rigorous and is grateful to Baseten, as there is much confusion about basics. The quoted post says they fine-tune many customer models and varied one SFT lever at a time across dense and MoE models up to 235B on 4 customer tasks.

## Background

SFT is fine-tuning on example outputs. Controlled one-lever experiments give clean evidence. Confusion about basics is common.

## What I took away

- Use the study for SFT defaults.
- Controlled comparisons are rare and valuable.
