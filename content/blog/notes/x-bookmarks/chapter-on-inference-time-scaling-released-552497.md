## Summary

The author says chapter 4 on inference-time scaling is now available as weekend reading. The quoted post explains that inference scaling trades extra compute for better accuracy, and next to reinforcement learning it is among the most important ideas in today's LLMs, so the book gives it two chapters.

## Background

Inference-time scaling means spending more compute when answering, for example sampling multiple answers or thinking longer, to get better results. It differs from making the model bigger. Reasoning models rely on this idea.

## What I took away

- More test-time compute can improve accuracy.
- Inference scaling and RL are the two big levers in current LLMs.
- The book covers the topic in two chapters.
