## Summary

The post introduces GLM-4.7-Flash-Claude-Opus-4.5-High-Reasoning-Distill, a distilled GGUF model it says brings Claude-level intelligence to a compact package for developers who want high-performance AI without cloud costs.

## Background

Distillation trains a smaller model to imitate a larger one. GGUF is a file format for running models locally. Claude-level is a marketing claim and should be tested.

## What I took away

- Distilled local models trade quality for cost and privacy.
- Test such claims on your own tasks.
- Check licensing of distilled models.
