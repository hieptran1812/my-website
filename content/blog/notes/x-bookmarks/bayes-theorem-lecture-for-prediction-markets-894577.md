## Summary

The author pushes a free one-hour Stanford lecture on Bayes' Theorem. He says it is the math behind five formulas in an article about Bayesian trading on Polymarket.

## Background

Bayes' theorem updates a probability when new evidence arrives. In prediction markets you compare your updated belief to the market price. This is educational.

## What I took away

- Bayes' rule updates beliefs with evidence.
- Free lectures are a good start.
- Math skill does not guarantee trading profit.
