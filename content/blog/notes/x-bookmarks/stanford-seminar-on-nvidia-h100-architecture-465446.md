## Summary

The author shares a Stanford seminar on NVIDIA's H100 GPU. It covers Hopper streaming multiprocessors, the Transformer Engine, NVLink interconnects and strategies for optimizing HPC and AI workloads.

## Background

The H100 is NVIDIA's Hopper-generation data center GPU. Streaming multiprocessors are its compute units, the Transformer Engine adds FP8 support for transformer math, and NVLink is the fast link between GPUs.

## What I took away

- Understanding the hardware helps you reason about kernel performance.
- Watch it as background before studying newer Blackwell chips.
