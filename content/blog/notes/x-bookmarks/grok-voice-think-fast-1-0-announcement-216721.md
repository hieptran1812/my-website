## Summary

The post introduces Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0, a voice model built for complex multi-step workflows with snappy responses and high accuracy. It claims the top spot on the Tau Voice Bench and better handling of noise, accents and interruptions.

## Background

Tau Voice Bench tests voice agents on customer-service-style tool tasks. Noise and interruptions are common real-world problems. These are the vendor's claims.

## What I took away

- Voice models are now compared on agentic tasks.
- Test with your own audio conditions.
