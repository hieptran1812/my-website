## Summary

The author says their paper finds an explanation for why subliminal learning occurs, using steering vectors. The quoted post says it is simply steering vector distillation, and explains which traits transfer and why it fails across models.

## Background

Subliminal learning is when a model picks up a trait, like loving cats, from data that seems unrelated. A steering vector is a direction in activation space that pushes behavior. The paper links the transfer to copying such a direction.

## What I took away

- Data that looks neutral can carry hidden traits.
- Transfer seems to work only between related models.
