## Summary

The author says Rich Sutton reminded NeurIPS attendees that continual learning is needed to reach AGI. In the afternoon, Ali Behrouz presented a Google poster paper called **Nested Learning**, which offers new ideas toward continual learning. The author recorded the 40-minute talk to share.

## Background

Continual learning means a model keeps learning new things over time without forgetting old ones. Today's LLMs are mostly trained once and then frozen. Solving this would let models adapt after deployment.

## What I took away

- Continual learning is a recurring theme in current research.
- The recorded talk is a quick way to learn the Nested Learning idea.
- Ask how a model could learn after deployment without forgetting.
