## Summary

The author, writing in Vietnamese, says the quoted point explains why the short to medium term trend is down yet futures traders still love going long, even though wins are small and losses large. The quoted post claims exchanges earn most when prices fall, and that rising prices come from exchanges pumping and users feeling FOMO, leaving an anchor at high prices. It adds that altcoins peak at listing.

## Background

FOMO is fear of missing out, which pushes people to buy after prices rise. Anchoring is sticking to a past price as a reference. These are opinions about behavior and exchange incentives, not verified facts.

## What I took away

- Anchoring on past highs can keep traders in losing positions.
- Be wary of claims about exchange motives without evidence.
- Not advice to buy or sell.
