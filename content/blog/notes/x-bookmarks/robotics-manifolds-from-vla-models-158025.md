## Summary

The author says robotics manifolds and control based on them are coming soon from their lab. The quoted reply says they are looking at LIBERO manifolds and that VLAs seem to carve out surprisingly clean 3D-ish structure.

## Background

A VLA is a vision-language-action model that controls robots. LIBERO is a robot manipulation benchmark. A manifold view studies the shape of internal representations, which could allow control by steering within that shape.

## What I took away

- Geometry-based interpretability is spreading from language to robotics.
- Clean internal structure may enable new ways to control robot policies.
