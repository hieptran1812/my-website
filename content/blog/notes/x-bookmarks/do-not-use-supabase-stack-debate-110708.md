## Summary

The author posts a short 'Don't use Supabase'. The quoted post lists a low-cost app stack: Claude for coding, Supabase as backend, Vercel for deploying, Namecheap for domain, Stripe for payments, GitHub, Resend for emails, Clerk for auth, Cloudflare for DNS and PostHog. The author's reasons are not given.

## Background

The quoted list shows how a solo developer can ship a product mostly on free tiers. Supabase provides a hosted database and backend. The post does not explain why the author advises against it.

## What I took away

- Free tiers make small products cheap to launch.
- Ask for the reasoning behind a short 'don't use X' opinion.
- Check lock-in and scaling costs before picking a backend.
