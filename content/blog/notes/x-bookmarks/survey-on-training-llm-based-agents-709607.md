## Summary

The post shares a survey that reviews the end-to-end pipeline for training LLM agents, including environment setup, data and trajectory construction, and learning signals. It is by Zhang et al. in an ACL Anthology proceedings.

## Background

Training agents involves more than prompts: you need environments, trajectories of actions and reward signals. A survey helps map the field.

## What I took away

- Agent training has several pipeline stages.
- Use the survey as a map.
- Read the paper for details.
