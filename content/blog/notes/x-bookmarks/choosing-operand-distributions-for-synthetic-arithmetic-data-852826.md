## Summary

The author poses a question on synthetic data: to train a model on k-step arithmetic problems like a+b*c-d with operands from 1 to 100, which distribution should you use? Option A is sampling operands uniformly; option B starts with power law and the text cuts off.

## Background

Synthetic data is generated by a program instead of collected. The sampling distribution decides which examples the model sees most. A power law skews toward small numbers, like natural data, while uniform treats all values equally; this choice can change how well the model generalizes.

## What I took away

- The distribution of synthetic data matters as much as its quantity.
- Natural data often follows power laws; mimicking that may help.
