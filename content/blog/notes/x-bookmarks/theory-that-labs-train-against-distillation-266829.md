## Summary

The author theorizes it is trivial to RL for this: keep a student model and reward the main model for correct outputs and for the student's inability to learn from its traces. The quoted post says Opus 5.5 reasoning traces look like a poor quantized open model yet right answers emerge.

## Background

Distillation trains a student on a teacher's outputs. Making traces unhelpful would block copying. This is an unverified conspiracy theory.

## What I took away

- It is speculation.
- Visible traces may not show real reasoning.
- Anti-distillation is a plausible goal.
