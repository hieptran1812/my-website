## Summary

The post claims that Google found a way to shrink 31GB of AI memory to 4GB. It gives no technique name or details in the text.

## Background

Memory use is a main cost of running LLMs, especially for long contexts. Compression methods such as quantization or cache compression can cut it a lot. Without a source, treat the exact figures as an unverified claim.

## What I took away

- Large memory-reduction claims need the underlying paper or benchmark.
- Check what is being compressed and what quality is lost.
