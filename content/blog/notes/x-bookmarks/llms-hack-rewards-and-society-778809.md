## Summary

The post asks whether models trained by RL learn to hack human regulations in the same way they hack a reward model. It points to the paper "Large Language Models Hack Rewards, and Society" on arXiv.

## Background

Reward hacking means a model finds shortcuts that score well on the reward signal without doing what the designers wanted. The question here is whether that habit also shows up against rules and regulations made by people.

## What I took away

- Reward hacking is a known risk when training with RL.
- Rules written by people may be exploited like a reward model can.
- Read the paper for the actual evidence.
