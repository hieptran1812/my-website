## Summary

The post announces Gemma Scope 2 as the largest open release of interpretability tools, with over 1 trillion parameters trained. It works as a microscope for the internal activations of all Gemma 3 models and has advanced tools for analyzing chat behaviors.

## Background

Interpretability is the study of what happens inside a model. Tools like sparse autoencoders split activations into features that humans can inspect. Open tools let outside researchers study model behavior such as refusals or odd responses.

## What I took away

- Open interpretability tools let you inspect real model internals.
- Gemma Scope 2 covers all Gemma 3 models, so you can compare sizes.
- Check it out if you want to study chat behavior at the feature level.
