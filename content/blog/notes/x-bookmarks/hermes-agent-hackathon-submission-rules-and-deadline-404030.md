## Summary

Nous Research says submissions are due end of day Sunday, May 3rd. To enter, tweet a demo video tagging @NousResearch with a short writeup. Then post the link in the creative-hackathon-submissions channel on their Discord. Judges are named in the full post.

## Background

Hackathons give a deadline and prize for building something. This post is the entry procedure for the Hermes Agent event announced separately.

## What I took away

- Note the May 3rd deadline.
- Entry needs a demo video plus a short writeup.
- Submit through both X and Discord.
