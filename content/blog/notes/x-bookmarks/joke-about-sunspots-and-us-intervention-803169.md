## Summary

The author says the quoted post is their quant. The quoted post says it is working on a theory that US intervention in the Middle East correlates with the 11-year solar cycle of sunspot activity.

## Background

This is a satirical take on spurious correlation, where two unrelated series seem linked by chance. With few events and many possible series, such patterns are easy to find and mean little.

## What I took away

- Be wary of correlations found by searching many series.
- A claimed pattern needs a causal story and out-of-sample tests.
