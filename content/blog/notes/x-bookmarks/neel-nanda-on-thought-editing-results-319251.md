## Summary

The author finds the work on steering models by editing their chain of thought interesting. They are surprised how well it works relative to resampling. The quoted post introduces thought editing.

## Background

Resampling means regenerating outputs to get a different behavior. Editing the chain of thought targets the reasoning directly. A comment from an interpretability researcher adds credibility.

## What I took away

- Direct edits can beat resampling.
- Chain of thought is a useful intervention point.
- See the original thread for the settings.
