## Summary

@DavidRBellamy shares the team's pretraining and post-training data. The quoted announcement says K2 Horizon released 13 trillion pretraining tokens across 5 datasets and 24 subsets, plus post-training data-generation pipelines, with over 10 trillion tokens of synthetic data covering mathematics, reasoning and more.

## Background

Most open-weight models release weights but not training data. Releasing the data and the pipelines that create synthetic data lets others reproduce, audit and improve the training. Synthetic data is data generated by models, often used to teach math and reasoning.

## What I took away

- Open data releases make it possible to study what actually drives model quality.
- Synthetic data now makes up a large share of training mixes.
- Look at the pipelines, not just the datasets, to learn how the data was made.
