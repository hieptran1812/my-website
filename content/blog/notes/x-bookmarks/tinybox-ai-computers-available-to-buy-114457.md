## Summary

The tinygrad account reminds readers that two tinybox models are available to buy today, for bringing AI compute into a home or office.

## Background

tinygrad is a small open deep learning framework, and the tinybox is its company's prebuilt multi-GPU computer for running and training models locally.

## What I took away

- Local AI hardware is an option for teams wanting to avoid cloud costs.
- Compare total cost and power needs against renting GPUs.
