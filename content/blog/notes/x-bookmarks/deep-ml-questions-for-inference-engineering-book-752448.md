## Summary

The author says Deep-ML released its biggest collection yet, with over 100 questions tied to the Inference Engineering book by Philip, so you can read a chapter and then solve related questions. The quoted post says Inference Engineering launches today with a Baseten link.

## Background

Inference engineering is the practice of running models fast and cheaply in production. Practice problems alongside a book help turn reading into skill.
