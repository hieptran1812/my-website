## Summary

The authors say catastrophic forgetting has long challenged continual learning, but their new study found pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are surprisingly resistant. They report zero forgetting, or even positive backward transfer, is possible with simple methods.

## Background

Catastrophic forgetting is when a model loses old skills after learning new ones. VLA models take images and language and output robot actions. Positive backward transfer means learning new tasks improves old ones.

## What I took away

- Pretraining may make models more stable for continual learning.
- Simple methods can be enough in some settings.
- Check the paper for the setup before generalizing.
