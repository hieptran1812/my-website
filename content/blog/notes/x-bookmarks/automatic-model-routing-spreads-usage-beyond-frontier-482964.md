## Summary

The author says automatic behind-the-scenes routing, instead of a model picker, will redistribute value and usage toward many more models than frontier ones, especially open, small and cheaper models. It removes the cognitive load for users. The quoted post introduces Factory Router, claiming frontier performance with 25% lower cost.

## Background

A router chooses which model handles each request. If users stop picking models themselves, brand loyalty matters less and cost-quality balance matters more.

## What I took away

- Routing can cut cost without hurting quality, per Factory's claim.
- The market-shift argument is the author's prediction.
