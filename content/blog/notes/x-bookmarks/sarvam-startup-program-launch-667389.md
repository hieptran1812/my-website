## Summary

Sarvam AI announces the Sarvam Startup Program to power the startup ecosystem with high-quality models and tools. The program provides credits, priority support and more, per the post.

## Background

AI labs offer credits to attract developers to their models and APIs. For startups, credits lower the cost of experiments. Check the terms for eligibility.
