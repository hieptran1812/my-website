## Summary

@Teknium is excited that vLLM will support this with streaming audio. The quoted Qwen post answers questions about Qwen3-TTS. It says they are working with @vllm_project to enable streaming inference and that a real-time experience is coming soon.

## Background

TTS means text-to-speech. Streaming inference means audio is produced in chunks as it is generated, so playback can start before the whole clip is ready. That lowers latency for voice assistants and live apps.

## What I took away

- Streaming is key to real-time voice applications.
- Serving engines like vLLM are extending beyond text.
- Support is described as coming, not yet released.
