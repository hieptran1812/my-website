## Summary

TensorTonic announces its final module covering information theory fundamentals. Topics include Shannon entropy, KL divergence, information gain in decision trees, mutual information, cross entropy loss, GAN training, Jensen-Shannon divergence and perplexity.

## Background

Entropy measures uncertainty in a distribution. KL divergence measures how different two distributions are. Cross entropy loss and perplexity are the standard way language models are trained and scored, so these ideas underlie much of ML.

## What I took away

- Cross entropy and KL divergence are tightly linked.
- Perplexity is a direct function of cross entropy.
- Information theory connects trees, GANs and language models.
