## Summary

The author calls backtesting versus walk-forward analysis the greatest challenge in algorithmic trading. They point to a free article by @SurmountInvest that they say covers real details nobody talks about.

## Background

A backtest tests a strategy on past data. Walk-forward analysis repeatedly fits on one period and tests on the next unseen period, which better mimics live trading. It helps reduce overfitting, where a strategy only looks good because it was tuned to history.

## What I took away

- A good backtest does not prove a strategy will work live.
- Walk-forward testing checks the strategy on data it has not been tuned on.
- Overfitting is a main risk in strategy research.
