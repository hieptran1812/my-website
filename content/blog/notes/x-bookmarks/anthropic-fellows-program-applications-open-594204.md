## Summary

@NeelNanda5 says past Anthropic Fellows impressed him and the program seems worth applying to for safety or interpretability research, like an internship with a good conversion rate. The quoted post opens applications for rounds starting May and July 2026, offering funding, compute and mentorship for four months.

## Background

A fellowship gives researchers and engineers time and resources to work on real safety projects. The conversion rate claim is the author's impression.

## What I took away

- Applications are open for May and July 2026 starts.
- The program offers funding, compute and mentorship.
- Good option for entering safety research.
