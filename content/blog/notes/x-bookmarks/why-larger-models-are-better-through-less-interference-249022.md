## Summary

The author is glad to see this work out and says it concretizes intuitions about the effects of scaling. The quoted post says larger models are better because of data-induced competition for resources (neurons), shown through formal analysis, idealized tasks and real pretraining.

## Background

The idea is that with few parameters, different pieces of knowledge overwrite each other. More neurons mean less interference so rare facts are retained.

## What I took away

- Scale helps by reducing interference between updates.
