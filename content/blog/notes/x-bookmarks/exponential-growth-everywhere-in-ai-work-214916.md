## Summary

The author lists the exponential graphs they stare at every day, such as revenue, PRs, tokens, compute, incidents and their resting heart rate. They are replying to a post saying Anthropic engineers on average ship 8x as much code per quarter as in 2021-2025.

## Background

The joke is that faster output also grows the load of reviews, incidents and alerts. More code shipped does not remove the work of operating it.

## What I took away

- Faster code output raises downstream load such as review and incidents.
- The 8x figure is the quoted post's claim.
