## Summary

The author claims JP Morgan and top quant firms use computer vision to trade stocks and that Wall Street scans time series as images. They introduce the Gramian Angular Field (GAF), a transformation that turns time series into images. The thread continues beyond the saved text.

## Background

GAF encodes a series as a matrix using angles, so image models like CNNs can learn from it. Whether it beats ordinary time-series models is debated, and the claim about firms using it is the author's, not verified here. Another bookmark describes an advisor being skeptical about it.

## What I took away

- GAF converts a time series into an image for vision models.
- Check claims about what top firms use.
- Compare against simple baselines before adopting it.
