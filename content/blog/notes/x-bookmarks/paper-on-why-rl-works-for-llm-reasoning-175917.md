## Summary

@yacinelearning recommends the paper as interesting. The quoted post says it analyzes RL training across eight models, including Qwen, LLaMA and vision-language models, and finds that aha moments are not random but markers of something deeper.

## Background

RL for reasoning trains LLMs with rewards for correct answers. Aha moments are points where the model suddenly changes its reasoning. The post does not state the exact finding.

## What I took away

- Aha moments may reflect structure in training dynamics.
- Analyzing many models gives stronger evidence.
- Read the paper for the details.
