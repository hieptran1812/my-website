## Summary

The author shares The Ultimate List of AI Neolabs for May 2026. They define a neolab as a pre-revenue scale startup working on long-term AI breakthroughs, usually with a $1B+ valuation, and say there are now 63 of them.

## Background

Neolabs are research-first companies funded on the promise of future breakthroughs rather than current sales. A list like this helps track who is hiring and where funding goes.

## What I took away

- Large funding is flowing to pre-revenue research labs.
- Such lists are useful for tracking the field, but the definition is the author's.
