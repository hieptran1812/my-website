## Summary

@dchaplot announces that Tinker is now open to everyone. New additions are vision support with Qwen3-VL, a new model Kimi K2 Thinking (1T params), and OpenAI API-compatible inference. The quoted post from the Tinker announcement says the same: general availability, advanced vision input models, Kimi K2 Thinking, and a simpler way to sample from models.

## Background

Tinker is a service for fine-tuning open models without managing your own training infrastructure. An OpenAI API-compatible endpoint means existing code written for the OpenAI API can call the tuned model with few changes. Vision support lets models take images as input.

## What I took away

- OpenAI-compatible inference makes it easier to plug a tuned model into existing tools.
- Managed fine-tuning services lower the setup cost for post-training experiments.
- The announcement says you can start training within minutes.
