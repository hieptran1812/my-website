## Summary

The author calls it another great technical write-up on training an LLM from scratch, covering architecture, pre-training, post-training and data composition. The quoted post releases Mellum2, a 12B A2.5B MoE pre-trained on about 11T tokens and post-trained with RLVR, with base, SFT and RL checkpoints.

## Background

12B A2.5B means 12 billion total parameters with 2.5 billion active per token. RLVR trains with automatically checkable rewards.

## What I took away

- Full-recipe write-ups are great learning material.
- Checkpoints at each stage are released.
