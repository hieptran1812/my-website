## Summary

The author implemented Olmo 3 from scratch in a standalone notebook and says it is a good way for coders to read the architecture at a glance. The quoted post praises Olmo models for being fully transparent with detailed technical reports and mentions a 100-page report with a side-by-side comparison.

## Background

Reading code is often clearer than reading a paper for understanding how a model is built. Fully open models share data, code and training details, not just weights. That makes them useful for learning.

## What I took away

- Reimplementing a model in a notebook is a strong way to learn its architecture.
- Fully open models make training choices easier to study.
- Look at the notebook to compare Olmo 3 with other architectures.
