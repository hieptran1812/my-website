## Summary

The author claims Anthropic dropped a prediction market trading bot structure making $300 to $1,500 a day at a 68.4% win rate, hidden in a 33-page guide to building Claude skills. The quoted post just shares the real guide link. The profit claims are unverified and look promotional.

## Background

A skills guide explains how to package instructions for Claude. Linking it to profit numbers is a hook; the guide itself is about skill building.

## What I took away

- Separate the real resource from the sensational claim.
- Be skeptical of daily profit and win rate numbers.
- The guide is the useful part.
