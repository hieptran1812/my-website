## Summary

TensorTonic says all its coding problems now come with interactive visualizations. The goal is to help learners understand each problem before solving it. The example shown is positional encodings from the transformer paper.

## Background

Positional encodings are signals added to token embeddings so a transformer knows word order, since attention alone ignores position. Seeing them animated is often easier than reading the formula.

## What I took away

- Visualizing a concept first can make coding it easier.
- Positional encodings are a good topic to learn interactively.
