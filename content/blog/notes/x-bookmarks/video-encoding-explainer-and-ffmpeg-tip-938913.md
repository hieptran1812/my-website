## Summary

The author shares an ImageKit blog to understand video encoding. The quoted post gives a pro tip: install ffmpeg locally and you will not need 90% of the online tools you use daily, such as compressing a video in seconds without ads.

## Background

ffmpeg is a free command-line tool that converts, compresses and edits audio and video. Video encoding is how raw video is compressed with codecs. Local tools avoid uploading files to websites.

## What I took away

- ffmpeg can replace many online converters.
- Learn codecs and bitrates to compress sensibly.
- Local tools keep your files private.
