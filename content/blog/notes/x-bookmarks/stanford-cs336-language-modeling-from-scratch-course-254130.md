## Summary

The author recommends Stanford's CS336 course, "Language Modeling from Scratch". They say it is a deep technical dive into building LLMs. They argue that mastering the whole stack, from hardware efficiency to scaling laws, matters as frontier models become more closed.

## Background

Scaling laws describe how model quality improves as you add data, parameters and compute. Knowing the full stack means understanding data, training code, and the hardware it runs on. A course that covers all of it helps engineers who want to build models, not only use them.

## What I took away

- CS336 is a candidate course for learning the full LLM stack.
- Hardware efficiency and scaling laws are core skills, not side topics.
- Building from scratch teaches more than calling an API.
