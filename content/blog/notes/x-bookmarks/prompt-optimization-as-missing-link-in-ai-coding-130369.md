## Summary

Chris Potts shares a clip from his practice run to give context for a slide. The quoted post shows the slide from the Bay Area DSPy meetup, saying that in the age of AI coding, software engineering strikes again, and prompt optimization is the missing link for helping people discover latent requirements.

## Background

DSPy is a framework for programming LLMs and automatically optimizing prompts. Latent requirements are needs that people have but have not stated. The idea is that optimizing prompts against examples reveals what you really want.

## What I took away

- Prompts can be optimized against examples rather than hand-written.
- Unstated requirements are a main source of AI coding failures.
