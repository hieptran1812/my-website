## Summary

The author says bear markets create the best opportunities. The quoted post says everyone called the last 12 months the worst bear market, yet it cites growth in Morpho RWAs (33x), prediction market volume (17x), perp DEX volume (4x) and stablecoin active addresses (almost double). These are the quoted author's claims.

## Background

Price and usage can move separately. Prices may fall while real activity such as trading volume and active addresses grows. The post argues usage is a better signal than price. This is commentary, not advice.

## What I took away

- Compare price with usage metrics before judging a market.
- Numbers in the quote are the author's claims; verify them independently.
