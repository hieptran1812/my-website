## Summary

The author jokes that they can beat the compiler and shows a resume from 2008-9 when they were 17. The quoted post shares a college resume and says they try to be the best at what they do and prefer working with people who feel the same. It also says you can complain about the world or create something of your own.

## Background

Beating the compiler means hand-writing low-level code that runs faster than what the compiler produces. It is a sign of strong performance engineering skill. The post is personal and light-hearted.

## What I took away

- Early deep technical projects stand out on a resume.
- Low-level optimization is a valued skill.
- Build something of your own instead of complaining.
