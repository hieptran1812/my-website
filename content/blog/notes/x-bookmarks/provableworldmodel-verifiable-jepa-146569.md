## Summary

@AbdelStark describes ProvableWorldModel, the first provable JEPA world model, letting you prove robot decisions without re-running inference. The quoted post says anyone can verify on a laptop CPU or phone, in milliseconds, that the committed model ran as claimed.

## Background

A world model predicts how the environment changes. Provable inference uses cryptographic proofs so others can check the computation. Verification is much cheaper than rerunning.

## What I took away

- Verifiable inference lets third parties trust model runs.
- Verification can be fast on small devices.
