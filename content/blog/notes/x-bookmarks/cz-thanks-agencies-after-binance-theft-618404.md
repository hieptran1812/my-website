## Summary

The quoted Secret Service post says it continues to investigate the theft of more than $6 billion in cryptocurrency from Binance Global and that, within minutes, investigators worked with industry partners to identify and freeze a significant portion. @cz_binance thanks the Secret Service, agencies and industry partners, saying their speed helped contain the damage and reduce ripple effects.

## Background

Freezing stolen crypto means exchanges and stablecoin issuers block funds tied to the hack so they cannot be moved or cashed out. Fast coordination can recover a big share of losses.

## What I took away

- Speed of response matters a lot in crypto theft recovery.
- Public-private cooperation is part of crypto security today.
- Exchange custody carries real hack risk.
