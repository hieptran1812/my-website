## Summary

Skyler Miao quotes the MiniMax announcement that M2.5-HighSpeed is live at 100 TPS, a 3x speed-up. It was launched 48 hours after M2.5 and is aimed at agent applications.

## Background

TPS means tokens per second, how fast a model writes output. Agents run many sequential steps, so faster generation shortens total task time and lowers waiting cost.

## What I took away

- Speed matters for agents with long loops.
- Compare tokens per second together with price and quality.
