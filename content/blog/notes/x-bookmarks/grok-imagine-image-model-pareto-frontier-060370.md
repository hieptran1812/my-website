## Summary

Elon Musk thanks the Grok Imagine team. The quoted post says xAI's Grok-Imagine-Image model is Pareto-optimal in Image Arena, meaning it gives the highest Arena score at its price point, and improves the frontier in the mid-price tier.

## Background

A Pareto frontier shows the best quality for each price. A model on it is not beaten by any cheaper model with the same or better score. The claim comes from the quoted source.

## What I took away

- Compare models on quality per price.
- Arena scores are based on human votes.
- Check your own use case.
