## Summary

The author says if this works well we are looking at a new era and praises Anemll. The quoted post says SWA TBQ compression works at 3, 3.5 and 4 bit, needed an algorithm change, and more quality comparisons are planned. It could help on ANE.

## Background

SWA is sliding window attention. TBQ is a compression method named in the post. ANE is Apple's Neural Engine. Quality tests are still pending.

## What I took away

- Early results, not proven.
- Wait for quality comparisons.
- Compression helps on-device.
