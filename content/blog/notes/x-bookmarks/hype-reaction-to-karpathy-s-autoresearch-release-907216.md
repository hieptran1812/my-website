## Summary

The author claims Karpathy dropped an AI that picks architectures, tunes hyperparameters, commits code and runs experiments in a loop, and predicts degrees and PhDs will be useless in 6 months. The quoted Karpathy post describes a minimal single-GPU, one-file, about 630-line repo derived from nanochat.

## Background

The repo automates experiment loops on a small training setup. The prediction about degrees is the author's opinion and goes well beyond what the quoted post says.

## What I took away

- Check the original post against the hype.
- Automating experiments is not the same as replacing researchers.
