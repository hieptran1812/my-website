## Summary

@willcb says this may be the most noteworthy continual learning paper in a long time, because most similar work is fancy RL-flavored context distillation. The quoted post says Zyphra studies whether LLMs can learn forever from new data. The first obstacle is plasticity loss, and they derive a scaling law for its onset.

## Background

Continual learning means a model keeps learning from new data over time. Plasticity loss means it gradually becomes worse at learning new things. A scaling law predicts when this starts as size or data grows.

## What I took away

- Plasticity loss may block lifelong learning.
- A scaling law would let you predict when it starts.
