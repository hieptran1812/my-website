## Summary

The author shares notes titled Mastering PyTorch: From Linear Regression to Computer Vision, written as part of their journey to master ML. They cover the building blocks and fundamentals learned through exercises and projects.

## Background

Starting from linear regression teaches the training loop that every deep model uses: forward pass, loss, backward pass and update. Writing notes helps cement learning.

## What I took away

- Build up from simple models to understand the core loop.
- Write your own notes as you learn.
