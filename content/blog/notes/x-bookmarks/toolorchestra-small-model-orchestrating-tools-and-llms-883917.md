## Summary

The author calls **ToolOrchestra** from NVIDIA a cool work. It is an 8B model trained on calling tools and other LLMs to answer queries. They say it is a good demo of what frontier small language models (SLMs) will be about in 2026.

## Background

An orchestrator model decides which tool or larger model to call for each part of a task. A small model doing the routing can cut cost, since big models are used only when needed.

## What I took away

- Small models can be useful as routers and coordinators.
- Consider delegating to larger models only when needed.
