## Summary

The author calls interleaved thinking a game-changer. They built a small deep research agent and say the results are impressive: the agent is more efficient at reasoning over multiple steps, which gives huge leverage for self-improving agents. The linked repo uses MiniMax M2.1.

## Background

Interleaved thinking means the model reasons between tool calls, not just once at the start, so it can update its plan after each result. This suits research agents that search and read repeatedly. The efficiency claim is the author's.

## What I took away

- Let the model think after each tool result.
- Try the open repo to see the pattern in practice.
