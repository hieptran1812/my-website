## Summary

The author thanks them for the free money glitch, quoting a post that says 5-minute up or down crypto markets on Polymarket are live, powered by Chainlink.

## Background

Other posts here claim bots exploit gaps in these new markets. Chainlink is an oracle network that supplies price data.

## What I took away

- New markets can have pricing gaps early on.
- Gaps close quickly; this is not advice.
