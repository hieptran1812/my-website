## Summary

Sakana AI introduces KAME, a tandem architecture for enhancing knowledge in real-time speech-to-speech conversational AI, accepted at ICASSP 2026. The post asks whether a speech AI can think deeply without pausing to process, and links to a blog and paper.

## Background

Speech-to-speech systems must reply fast, which limits how much reasoning they can do. A tandem design pairs a fast speaking model with a deeper knowledge component. Details are in the linked paper; the post text is cut off.

## What I took away

- Real-time voice AI trades off latency against depth of reasoning.
- Splitting fast and slow components is a common design idea.
