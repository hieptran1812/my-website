## Summary

The post says CSRankings counts publications in top conferences to rank professors and universities, which encourages quantity over quality. The authors propose impactrank.org, a new ranking that tries to measure quality instead of quantity.

## Background

CSRankings ranks computer science departments by counting papers at major venues. Counting papers can reward many small papers. An impact-based ranking tries to credit work that others actually use or build on.

## What I took away

- Counting papers rewards volume.
- Metrics shape researcher behavior.
- Check the site for how quality is measured.
