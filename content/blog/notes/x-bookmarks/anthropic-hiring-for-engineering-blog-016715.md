## Summary

The author announces that Anthropic is hiring an editor-writer-engineer to own its Engineering Blog. The post says the blog is one of Anthropic's most widely read publications and shares lessons about building AI for engineers and technical founders. A link goes to the job board.

## Background

Engineering blogs share practical lessons from real systems. The role mixes writing, editing and technical skill, which is a niche career path for engineers who write well.

## What I took away

- Technical writing is a real career path for engineers.
- Writing well about hard-won lessons is a valued skill.
- See the job link for the actual requirements.
