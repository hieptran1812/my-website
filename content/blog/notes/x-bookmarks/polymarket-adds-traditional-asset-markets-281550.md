## Summary

Polymarket says users can now get exposure to US equities, major indices and commodities such as gold and natural gas through new markets. The quoted post says they cover index ETFs, single-name up/down and daily close contracts using Pyth Pro data.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market platform. Pyth is an oracle network supplying price data. These contracts pay by whether prices move up or down. This is information, not a recommendation.

## What I took away

- Oracles supply price feeds to onchain markets.
- Prediction platforms are expanding to traditional assets.
- Understand contract rules before trading.
