## Summary

The author says Jev-like decision models are good at real-time games, though they found many problems during development, and calls the demo worth trying. The quoted post says they let Jev play Minecraft and cannot beat it, with 24 ms decisions versus about 200 ms human reactions, and invites people to join the server.

## Background

Real-time games need fast decisions under changing conditions, making them a good test for decision models. Speed alone can give an AI a big edge over humans.

## What I took away

- Fast reaction time can make an AI seem stronger than its reasoning is.
- The author notes real problems remain; demos hide them.
