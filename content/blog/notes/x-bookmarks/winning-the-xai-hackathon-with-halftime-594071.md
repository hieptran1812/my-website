## Summary

@krishgarg says they won the xAI hackathon by making ads for X Videos, introducing Halftime. It is targeted ad generation using AI that is meant to feel like part of the movies and shows, built with two teammates.

## Background

Hackathons are short events where teams build prototypes. Halftime, as described, generates ads that blend into video content. It is a hackathon project, so it is not necessarily a shipped product.

## What I took away

- Hackathon wins show a fast prototype of an idea, not a finished product.
- AI-generated ads that blend into content raise both business and disclosure questions.
