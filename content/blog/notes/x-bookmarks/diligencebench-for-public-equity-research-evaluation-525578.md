## Summary

The author announces DiligenceBench, a rubric-based eval for public-equity research. They report that Meta Muse Spark 1.1 tops the finance harness at 57.4%, followed by GLM 5.2, Sonnet 4.6 and GPT-5.6 Sol. A second observation about what strong models benefit from is cut off.

## Background

Rubric-based evals score answers against a checklist of what a good analysis should contain, rather than a single correct answer. Equity research means analyzing public companies for investment decisions, a task with long documents and judgment calls.

## What I took away

- A top score of 57.4% suggests this kind of financial research is far from solved.
- Rubric evals depend heavily on rubric quality; check how they were written.
- Benchmark results describe model capability, not investment advice.
