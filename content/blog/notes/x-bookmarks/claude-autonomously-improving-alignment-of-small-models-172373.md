## Summary

Anthropic shares new Fellows research asking whether Claude can autonomously align other AIs. They gave Claude 48 hours and one GPU to improve the alignment of small models; it proposed methods, then trained and tested the models itself. Anthropic says it worked surprisingly well, and the linked article is titled Automated researchers can reliably mitigate alignment failures.

## Background

Alignment means making a model behave as intended, for example being honest and avoiding harmful actions. Automated alignment research uses AI agents to do parts of that research, which could help safety work keep pace with capability gains.

## What I took away

- AI agents may be able to do useful safety research under tight compute budgets.
- Small models make cheap test beds for automated research ideas.
- Read the article for which alignment failures were tested and how success was measured.
