## Summary

Modal announces the winner of its best use of Modal challenge at an AI research hackathon, a general framework for recursively self-improving RL to prevent reward hacking and produce stronger policies. The quoted post says the team trained AIs to play Rocket League, Street Fighter and Tetris with agents that supervise training and spot failures.

## Background

Reward hacking is when a policy exploits the reward instead of the intended goal. Having agents supervise training could catch such failures early. It is a weekend project result.

## What I took away

- Agents can supervise RL training runs.
- Reward hacking is a core RL risk.
- Hackathons give quick prototypes.
