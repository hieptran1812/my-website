## Summary

The author wrote a long article for traders with longer attention spans on price action and market structure. It covers how price moves through order flow, ranging and trending structures, live trade examples and extra resources.

## Background

Price action means reading price moves directly, and market structure describes patterns like trends and ranges. The article is educational content from the author and not advice.

## What I took away

- Learn how order flow drives price before relying on patterns.
- Examples help only if you test them yourself.
