## Summary

The author thanks Raymond Feng for sharing at the AIE conference how their team thinks about **continual post-training**. The linked talk is titled Learning on the Job: The Future of Post-Training, from Applied Compute.

## Background

Post-training is the stage after pretraining where a model is tuned with fine-tuning or reinforcement learning to follow instructions and do tasks well. Continual post-training means the model keeps learning from new data or feedback while it is being used, instead of being trained once and frozen.

## What I took away

- Continual learning from deployment feedback is an active direction for post-training.
- Watch the talk to see how a company frames learning on the job for models.
- Keep in mind the risks of continual updates, such as regressions and forgetting.
