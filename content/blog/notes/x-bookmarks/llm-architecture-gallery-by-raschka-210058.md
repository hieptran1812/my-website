## Summary

The author says they finally put together a new LLM Architecture Gallery that collects the architecture figures all in one place, with a link to his site.

## Background

Comparing architecture diagrams side by side shows how models differ in attention, normalization and expert layers. It is a handy reference when reading model papers.

## What I took away

- Use the gallery to compare model designs quickly.
- Bookmark it as a reference for new releases.
