## Summary

The author quotes a line saying every day they try to obtain more compute to pass on to users, and that they are sorry it may take some time but will acquire as much as they can. The author adds you heard the man.

## Background

Compute limits constrain how many requests an AI provider can serve, which shows up as usage limits. Labs sign deals for more GPUs to relieve this.

## What I took away

- Usage limits of AI services are often set by available compute.
- New compute deals can change limits quickly.
