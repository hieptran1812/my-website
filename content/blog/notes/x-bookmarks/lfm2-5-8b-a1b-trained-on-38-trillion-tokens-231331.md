## Summary

The author summarizes the release as an **8B MoE with 1B activated, trained on 38 trillion tokens** for local and agentic workflows. The quoted announcement lists 1.5B active parameters, 128K context and a hybrid MoE architecture, for phones, laptops, PCs and robots.

## Background

Training a small model on a very large number of tokens is called overtraining. It helps small models punch above their size. Note the two posts give slightly different active parameter counts (1B versus 1.5B).

## What I took away

- Small models trained on many tokens can be strong for their size.
- Active parameter count drives inference cost in MoE models.
- Verify exact specs on the model card when posts disagree.
