## Summary

The author says one gift of crypto to quants is that data is mostly free. They say there is no better time to be an aspiring quant. They advise scraping high quality tick data and investigating what patterns might exist, including by searching for patterns others describe.

## Background

Tick data is the record of every trade or quote. Traditional markets charge for it, while crypto exchanges often provide it for free. Testing your own ideas on real data is a hands-on way to learn. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Free tick data lowers the barrier to learning quant research.
- Form a hypothesis, then test it on data.
- Beware of overfitting to past patterns.
