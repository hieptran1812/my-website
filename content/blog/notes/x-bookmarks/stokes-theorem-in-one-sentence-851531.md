## Summary

The post states Stokes' theorem: the line integral of F around a closed curve C equals the surface integral of the curl of F over any surface S bounded by C. It says boundary circulation equals the net swirl inside.

## Background

Curl measures local rotation of a vector field. Stokes' theorem says adding up that tiny rotation across a surface equals the circulation around its edge. It generalizes Green's theorem to 3D and underlies electromagnetism.

## What I took away

- The edge circulation equals the total swirl inside.
- Any surface with the same boundary gives the same answer.
- Good picture: tiny rotations cancel inside, leaving the boundary.
