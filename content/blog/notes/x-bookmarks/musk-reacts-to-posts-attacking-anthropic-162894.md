## Summary

Elon Musk posts two exclamation marks over quoted posts. They claim Anthropic's AI will turn against humanity, its safety team is a joke and its values are political. The second says AI could go rogue unless it is built to understand the universe.

## Background

These are opinion claims about a company's safety approach, made without evidence in the saved text. They reflect a public debate about how to align AI and who decides its values.

## What I took away

- Separate opinion from evidence in AI safety debates.
- Look for published methods and evals rather than slogans.
- Competitor commentary may be biased.
