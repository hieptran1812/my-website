## Summary

Karpathy says he tried Gemini 3 through early access. He first warns that public benchmarks can be gamed, and that avoiding this depends on the discipline and self-restraint of a team that is strongly incentivized to do otherwise. The text is cut off before his remarks on the model.

## Background

Benchmarks are fixed test sets, and a team can tune a model toward them, even by accident. That makes high scores less trustworthy as a sign of general ability. Hands-on use and private tests are a common check.

## What I took away

- Treat public benchmark scores with caution.
- Hands-on testing on your own tasks tells you more.
- Incentives can distort how results are reported.
