## Summary

@radamar jokes that you can forget diffusion and only need a bunch of "swings". The quoted post introduces Un-0, a first model: an image generator whose backbone is made of coupled oscillators instead of a conventional neural network.

## Background

Coupled oscillators are many simple units that swing and influence each other, like linked pendulums. Using them as a model backbone is an unconventional alternative to layers of standard neurons. It is an early idea, so results should be checked in the original post.

## What I took away

- Non-standard backbones are being tried for generation.
- Treat this as an early research direction.
