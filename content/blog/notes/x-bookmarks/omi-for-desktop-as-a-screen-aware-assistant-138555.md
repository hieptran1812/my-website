## Summary

The author spent 4 months building Omi for Desktop, a life architect that sees your screen, hears conversations and suggests what to do next. They call it a second brain that pays attention. It is open source and local.

## Background

Always-on assistants raise privacy concerns, so running locally matters. They need constant screen and audio capture to give context.

## What I took away

- Local and open source helps trust.
- Think about privacy before always-on capture.
- Context is what makes assistants useful.
