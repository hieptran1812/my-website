## Summary

The author shares their ICLR 2026 oral paper, in the top 1-1.5%, as slow-cooked research on how transformers learn semantic associations between tokens such as bird and flew during training. It quotes the co-author's post about early features being compositions of three basis features.

## Background

Semantic association means the model learns that some words go together in meaning. Understanding how this appears in training helps explain why language models work.

## What I took away

- Training dynamics are a growing research area.
- Top oral papers are good reading lists.
- See the linked paper for the method.
