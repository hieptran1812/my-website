## Summary

@chddaniel jokes that a billion-dollar potential is within reach if you just get 50,000,000+ users. The quoted post expresses amazement that Linktree is a billion-dollar company for links in a tree.

## Background

Linktree lets people put several links on one page. The joke is that a simple product can reach a high valuation when it has a large user base. It is a humorous take, not an analysis.

## What I took away

- Distribution and user base can matter more than product complexity.
- Humor posts are not business analysis.
