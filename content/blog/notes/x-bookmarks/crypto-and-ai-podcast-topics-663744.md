## Summary

The author says they enjoyed recording a podcast with @thatdegenvc. Topics include Pantera's investment in Surf, the rise of x402 and crypto x AI trends, neobanks, undercollateralized lending, and privacy solutions like TEEs and private AI. The quoted post announces biweekly research podcasts hosted with @SurfAI.

## Background

x402 is a payments idea that uses the HTTP 402 status code so machines can pay for services. TEEs are secure hardware areas that keep data private while code runs. These topics sit where crypto and AI meet.

## What I took away

- x402 and TEEs are two terms to follow in crypto x AI.
- Podcasts can be a quick way to scan a fast-moving area.
- Treat investment commentary as opinion.
