## Summary

@youwillmakemaps describes a task: grab weather radar data by API, turn it into fake clouds on a Mapbox map, and add shadows and offset so they look like they float. They say GPT 5.1 Codex answered that it would take months, while Gemini 3 Pro said sure and delivered. They conclude they do not understand LLMs.

## Background

Different models can respond very differently to the same prompt, including how they judge effort. This is a single anecdote and not a benchmark, so it does not show which model is better in general.

## What I took away

- Try the same task on more than one model.
- One anecdote is not a ranking.
