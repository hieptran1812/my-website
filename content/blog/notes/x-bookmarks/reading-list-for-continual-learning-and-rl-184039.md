## Summary

alphaXiv says 2026 will be the year of continual learning and RL for LLMs. They compiled a reading list of their users' favorite papers to help people start the year.

## Background

Continual learning means a model keeps updating from new data without forgetting old skills. RL for LLMs trains models using reward signals.

## What I took away

- Use the list as a starting point.
- Continual learning and RL are expected to be hot areas.
