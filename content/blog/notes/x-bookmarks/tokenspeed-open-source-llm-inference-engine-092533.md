## Summary

Together AI welcomes the preview of the TokenSpeed inference engine under an MIT license. The quoted post says TokenSpeed offers TensorRT LLM level performance and vLLM level usability, built by a lean team in two months.

## Background

An inference engine runs trained models to serve users. TensorRT LLM is known for speed and vLLM for ease of use. Matching both would be a useful combination, but the performance claims are the team's own.

## What I took away

- New open engines compete on speed and usability.
- Benchmark the engine on your own workload before switching.
