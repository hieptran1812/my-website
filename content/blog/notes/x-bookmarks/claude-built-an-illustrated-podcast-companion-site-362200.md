## Summary

The author asked Opus 5.5 to make an interactive website companion for the Acquired podcast's Home Depot episode. He says it came out nicely, with watercolor illustrations all drawn by Claude, and shares links to the artifact and the episode.

## Background

Artifacts are self-contained web pages an LLM can generate and share. This example shows models producing designed, illustrated content, not just text answers.

## What I took away

- Try LLMs for companion pages to long content like podcasts or reports.
- Open the link to judge the quality yourself.
