## Summary

The author shares Week 1 of a Stanford course, Frontier Systems, with a timestamped outline. Topics include a four-bottlenecks framework, why uncertainty creates opportunity and why compute is not yet a commodity.

## Background

A commodity is a uniform good traded at one price. Compute is not, because chips, networking, power and software differ a lot. The lecture frames AI scaling around bottlenecks.

## What I took away

- Compute supply has several distinct bottlenecks.
- Uncertainty in demand creates opportunity.
