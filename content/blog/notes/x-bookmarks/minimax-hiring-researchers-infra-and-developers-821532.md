## Summary

The author says MiniMax is hiring researchers, infrastructure engineers and developers. They invite people to DM or email, and note that the team will be at NeurIPS this week.

## Background

Conferences like NeurIPS are common places for AI labs to recruit. Reaching out to the team in person or by message can be more effective than a cold application.

## What I took away

- Conferences are a good moment to meet hiring teams.
- Reach out directly when a team posts open roles.
