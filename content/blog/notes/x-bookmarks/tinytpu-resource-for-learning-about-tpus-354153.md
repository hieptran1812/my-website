## Summary

The author says that for people just finding out about TPUs, the quoted post is a great educational resource. The quoted post describes TinyTPU, an open-source ML inference and training chip built over a summer that does end-to-end inference and training entirely on chip.

## Background

A TPU (tensor processing unit) is a chip designed for the matrix math used in neural networks. Building a small one teaches how such hardware works: systolic arrays, memory and control. A small project like this is for learning, not comparable to Google's TPUs.

## What I took away

- A small open-source chip is a good way to learn accelerator design.
- TPUs specialize in matrix multiplication.
