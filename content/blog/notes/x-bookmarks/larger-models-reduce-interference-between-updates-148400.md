## Summary

The author is excited about the paper. They show that with more parameters, larger models see less interference between updates, so they retain memories of rarely seen samples and learn the tail of the distribution. The quoted post says scaling benefits trace to data-induced competition for neurons.

## Background

The tail of a distribution is rare facts or cases. Small models overwrite them while learning common ones, and larger models have room to keep both.

## What I took away

- Scale helps rare knowledge most.
- Interference is a useful lens on model capacity.
