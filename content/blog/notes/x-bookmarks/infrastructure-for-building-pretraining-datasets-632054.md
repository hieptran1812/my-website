## Summary

The author says building pretraining datasets requires infrastructure, and shares a blog post about how to do that. The details are at the link.

## Background

Pretraining data comes from huge web crawls that must be filtered, deduplicated and processed. That needs distributed compute and storage, not just scripts. Data quality strongly affects model quality.

## What I took away

- Dataset building is an engineering problem at scale.
- Filtering and deduplication need real infrastructure.
- Read the blog for specifics.
