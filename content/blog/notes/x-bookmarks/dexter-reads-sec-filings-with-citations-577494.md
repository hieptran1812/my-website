## Summary

The author says Dexter can read SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K), find important parts and answer with citations. Coverage is claimed to be 20,000 stocks over 30 years, and the code is open source.

## Background

SEC filings are official company reports: 10-K is annual, 10-Q quarterly, 8-K for events. Retrieval with citations lets you check where an answer came from. This is a research tool, not investment advice.

## What I took away

- Citations make financial AI answers checkable.
- Open source lets you inspect how filings are parsed.
- Always verify against the original filing.
