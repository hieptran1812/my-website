## Summary

The quoted post says it heard some frontier models are a 48-layer transformer looped twice, and introduces DeepLoop, a method for depth scaling that makes looped transformers stable and scalable. The author, @willdepue, says he does not think Mythos is a universal transformer, but that the rumor alone convinces people to make the idea work. He calls this hyperstition: false rumors leading to real research progress.

## Background

A looped or universal transformer reuses the same layers several times instead of stacking new ones, so it gets more compute per token without more parameters. Hyperstition means an idea that becomes true because people believe it and act on it.

## What I took away

- Rumors about closed models can still point researchers at useful directions.
- Looped transformers trade parameter count for repeated compute; stability is the main challenge.
- Do not treat rumors about frontier architectures as facts.
