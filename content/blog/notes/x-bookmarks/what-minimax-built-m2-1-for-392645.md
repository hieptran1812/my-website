## Summary

The author says M2.1 was built for two things: real, hard-core engineering that is reliable in production, and vibe coding where users focus on ideas and outcomes while the model handles details. The focus areas begin with multi-language coding, not just Python, and the list is cut off.

## Background

Vibe coding means describing what you want and letting an AI write the code. Multi-language support matters because production codebases use Java, C++, Go and more, not only Python.

## What I took away

- Coding models are being tuned for languages beyond Python.
- Production reliability and fast prototyping are both targets.
