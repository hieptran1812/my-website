## Summary

The author promotes a free all-in-one page of Jane Street resources for traders and urges saving it. The post text gives no details on contents.

## Background

Jane Street is a quantitative trading firm that publishes puzzles, talks and technical writing. Aggregated lists can be useful but quality varies. This note is educational and not advice.

## What I took away

- Verify what a resource list actually contains.
- Prefer primary sources from the firm itself.
- Study materials are not trading recommendations.
