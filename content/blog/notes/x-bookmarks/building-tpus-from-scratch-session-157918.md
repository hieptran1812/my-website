## Summary

Himanshu recommends an interesting session by a Waterloo undergraduate on building TPUs from scratch at small scale. The quoted post asks whether a low-cost DIY AI cluster at home is possible and whether one can build their own GPU or chips.

## Background

A TPU is a chip designed for matrix math in neural networks. Building a tiny version teaches how accelerators work, even if it does not compete with real hardware.

## What I took away

- Small hardware projects teach how AI chips work.
- Curiosity questions can lead to useful learning.
