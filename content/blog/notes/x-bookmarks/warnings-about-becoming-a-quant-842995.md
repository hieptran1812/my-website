## Summary

The author says everyone romanticizes quant life, with claims like 1.4m dollar average pay at Jane Street, and asks whether you want this life, showing a day schedule. The quoted post argues you will not become a quant because of lack of information, since MIT, Harvard and Stanford content is free.

## Background

Quant roles apply math and programming to trading. Pay figures in viral posts are unverified claims. The posts focus on the effort and fit required.

## What I took away

- Ask whether you want the daily life, not just the pay.
- Free resources exist; discipline is the bottleneck.
- Verify salary claims with reliable sources.
