## Summary

The authors say emergence in bigger LLMs is not magic but a consequence of scaling laws plus how real text mixes skills, which they call slingshot generalisation. The quoted post says decomposing loss curves into learned quanta is a favorite research area.

## Background

Emergence is when abilities appear suddenly at larger scale. Explaining it with smooth scaling laws would make capabilities more predictable.

## What I took away

- Emergence may be predictable.
- Loss curves may decompose into discrete skills.
