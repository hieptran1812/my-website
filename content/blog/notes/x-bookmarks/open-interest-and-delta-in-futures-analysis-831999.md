## Summary

The author says open interest and delta are powerful when combined to analyze market sentiment and spot possible reversals or continuations in futures and options markets. He defines open interest as the total number of open contracts. The text is cut off.

## Background

Open interest counts contracts that are still open, so rising OI means new positions are being added. Delta here means the net difference between aggressive buys and sells. Together they show whether price moves come with new positions or with closing ones. This is educational and not advice.

## What I took away

- Open interest shows whether positions are opening or closing.
- Combine it with order flow for context.
- No indicator predicts reversals reliably.
