## Summary

@TianyuPang1 shares UniRL, an RL infra for unified multimodal models, with FlowDPPO for diffusion and flow-matching models and DRPO for LLMs and VLMs. The quoted post says one RL loop covers all of these.

## Background

Diffusion and language models usually need separate RL setups. A shared infrastructure simplifies this.

## What I took away

- One RL stack for several model types.
