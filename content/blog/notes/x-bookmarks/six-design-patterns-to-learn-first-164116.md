## Summary

The author says that if you want to start with design patterns, begin with six: Factory, Singleton, Observer, Builder, Strategy and Decorator. They say these are the ones you are most likely to code and be asked about in interviews.

## Background

Design patterns are reusable solutions to common software design problems. Factory creates objects, Observer notifies subscribers of changes, Strategy swaps algorithms, and Decorator adds behavior. Knowing the names helps in discussions and interviews.

## What I took away

- Learn these six patterns by writing a small example of each.
- Know when not to use a pattern, for example Singleton hides global state.
