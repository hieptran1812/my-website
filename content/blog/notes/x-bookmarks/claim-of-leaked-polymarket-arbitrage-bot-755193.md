## Summary

The author says a Polymarket arbitrage bot leaked on GitHub, free to set up, and claims fresh wallets make over $50k per day on 15-minute BTC markets. The quoted post describes a terminal assistant for short-term Bitcoin up or down markets.

## Background

Arbitrage means exploiting price differences for low-risk profit. Claims of huge easy profits are common in promotions and scams. The quoted tool is described as decision support.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of claims of huge easy profits.
- Never run unknown trading code with real keys.
- Not trading advice.
