## Summary

@h100envy says an Alibaba engineer who leads Qwen explained the future of open agent models in 25 minutes, better than expensive courses, and lists the loop: pretrain, SFT, RLHF, tool use, multimodal, ship a family.

## Background

The listed stages are the standard path to a modern model: pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning from human feedback, then tool use. The comparison to paid courses is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- The standard pipeline is pretrain, SFT, RL, tools, multimodal.
- Treat bold comparisons with paid courses skeptically.
- Find the original talk.
