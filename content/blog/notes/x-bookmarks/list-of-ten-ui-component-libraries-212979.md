## Summary

The author says you are NGMI if you do not use these 10 UI libraries and lists sites such as patterncraft.fun, cult-ui.com, tailark.com, smoothui.dev, motion-primitives.com, kokonutui.com, magicui.design and ui.aceternity.com. The list is cut off.

## Background

These are component libraries for building polished web interfaces, often with animation. The tone is hype; you do not need all of them. NGMI means not going to make it.

## What I took away

- Component libraries speed up frontend work.
- Pick one or two that fit your stack instead of trying all.
