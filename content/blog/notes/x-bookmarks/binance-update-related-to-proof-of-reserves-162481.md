## Summary

@cz_binance writes that there is a new update and that it seems mostly related to PoR, proof of reserves. The post gives no further details.

## Background

Proof of reserves is a way for a crypto exchange to show it holds enough assets to cover customer balances, often using cryptographic proofs. It became more important after exchange failures and, per other bookmarks, a large Binance hack.

## What I took away

- Proof of reserves helps users check an exchange's solvency.
- Short posts like this need the linked update for context.
- Verify reserve reports through independent sources when possible.
