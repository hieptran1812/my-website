## Summary

Musk says China is rapidly reducing dependence on oil and quotes a post saying this is a bigger deal than people think. That post cites official data that 12% of China's vehicles are now EVs and fuel sales fell 5.7% in 2025.

## Background

As more vehicles are electric, demand for gasoline and diesel falls. The figures here come from the quoted post and are not verified.

## What I took away

- Check original official data behind such figures.
- EV adoption affects fuel demand.
