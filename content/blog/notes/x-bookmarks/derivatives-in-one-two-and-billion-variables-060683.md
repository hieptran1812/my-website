## Summary

The author wrote a roughly 1000-word article explaining derivatives for one, two and a billion variables, and says no one has explained them this way before. The link card is titled Derivatives in One, Two, and Billion Variables.

## Background

A derivative measures how a function changes when its input changes. With many variables it becomes a gradient, which is what neural network training uses. Going from one variable to a billion is the core of backpropagation.

## What I took away

- The gradient is the multi-variable version of the derivative.
- Training a neural net means following gradients over billions of parameters.
- A short read to refresh the basics.
