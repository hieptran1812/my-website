## Summary

The author says he placed #1 twice at the MLSys 2026 x NVIDIA FlashInfer AI Kernel Generation Contest on the DeepSeek Sparse Attention track. He says his AI agent standalone beat every human competitor. The quote is the contest talk announcing results and FlashInfer-Bench, an agent-oriented benchmark engine for production kernels.

## Background

Kernels are low-level GPU programs. FlashInfer is a library of fast attention kernels. An agent that writes kernels needs a benchmark that checks correctness and speed. The claim is the author's.

## What I took away

- Agents are becoming competitive at kernel writing.
- Benchmarks with correctness checks are needed for agent kernels.
- Read the contest write-up for the agent design.
