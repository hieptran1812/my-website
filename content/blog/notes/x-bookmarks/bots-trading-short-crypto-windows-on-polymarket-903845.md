## Summary

@ascetic0x calls a quoted thread an excellent breakdown and says they keep seeing similar bots on Polymarket, with their number growing. The quoted thread describes one account that turned 15-minute crypto prediction windows into a mechanical profit engine using momentum lag, and compares it to Jane Street. The author also recommends following another account that they describe as an analyst with a 90%+ win rate.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on outcomes of events. A short-window market asks something like whether Bitcoin goes up or down in 15 minutes. A momentum-lag idea means the market price reacts a little slower than the underlying asset. Claims like a 90%+ win rate are self-reported and should be checked.

## What I took away

- Automated bots are increasingly active in short-window prediction markets.
- Win-rate claims on social media are unverified and can mislead.
- This is an educational observation, not a trading recommendation.
