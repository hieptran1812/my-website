## Summary

The author drafted a post on betting size and the Kelly Criterion as described by Euan Sinclair. They invite readers to read it and say what else they would like to see.

## Background

The Kelly criterion is a formula for the fraction of capital to wager given your edge and odds, aiming to maximize long-run growth. Full Kelly can be very volatile, so people often use a fraction. This is educational and not advice.

## What I took away

- Position sizing matters as much as having an edge.
- Kelly depends on accurate estimates of edge, which are hard to get.
