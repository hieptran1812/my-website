## Summary

The author says open, pluralistic scientific superintelligence leads to the best future and is excited to work with Tyler again. The quoted post from Tyler announces joining Prime Intellect as Head of Scientific AI, arguing that a billion copies of one agreeable mind would not be scientifically fruitful and that scientific AI should be weird and nonconsensus.

## Background

The argument is that science advances through diverse and contrarian ideas, so many varied AI models may help discovery more than one dominant model.

## What I took away

- Diversity of models and approaches may matter for AI-driven science.
- Watch Prime Intellect's open science work.
