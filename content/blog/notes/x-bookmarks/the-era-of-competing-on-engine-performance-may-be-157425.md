## Summary

The author says that for the past two years inference providers competed mainly on engine performance. They believe that era is ending. The post does not say what replaces it.

## Background

Inference providers host models and sell API access. Engine performance means speed and cost per token from the serving software. If engines converge in speed, providers would need to compete on other things such as reliability, accuracy, features or price.

## What I took away

- As open engines mature, raw speed may stop being a strong differentiator.
- Ask what else matters when choosing a provider: accuracy, uptime, context limits, support.
- This is one person's view; the full argument may be in the replies.
