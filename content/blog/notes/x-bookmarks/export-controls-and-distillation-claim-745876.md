## Summary

@teortaxesTex says this is why export controls are needed to stop fast-following. The quoted post claims 2.3M Claude Fable 5 reasoning traces were distilled into Qwen3-4B with 100% self-consistency at 512 samples, 0.00 bits output entropy, and zero hallucination variance.

## Background

Distillation trains a small model on a larger model's outputs. The quoted numbers are extraordinary and are the claimants' own. Zero entropy would mean the model always outputs the same thing, which is suspicious.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of perfect scores and zero entropy.
- Distillation policy is a live debate.
- Verify claims before drawing conclusions.
