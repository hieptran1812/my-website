## Summary

The post says Stanford researchers studied what happens when you reduce the size of the LLM inside a multimodal model. They found the part that suffers most is vision, and perception collapses.

## Background

Multimodal models combine a vision encoder with an LLM. Smaller LLMs are cheaper, so the finding warns that cutting the language part can hurt seeing, not only language.

## What I took away

- Do not assume small LLM backbones only reduce text skill.
- Test perception tasks when shrinking a multimodal model.
