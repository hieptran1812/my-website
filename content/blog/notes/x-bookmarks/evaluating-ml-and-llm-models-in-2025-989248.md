## Summary

The post asks how to evaluate an ML or LLM model in 2025. It says precision, recall and F1 were once the main way to judge a model and were popularized by Kaggle competitions. The text is cut off before it reaches its main point.

## Background

Precision is the share of predicted positives that are correct, and recall is the share of real positives that were found. F1 combines the two. These work well for classifiers but fit open-ended LLM output poorly, which is why LLM evaluation uses other methods.

## What I took away

- Know precision, recall and F1, but expect them to be insufficient for LLM output.
- Evaluation choices should match the task.
