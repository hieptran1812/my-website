## Summary

The author, who interviewed at Google, shares 10 must-know dynamic programming patterns with LeetCode-style examples, starting with 1D DP. The quoted post lists 10 graph algorithms such as DFS, BFS, topological sort and Dijkstra.

## Background

Dynamic programming solves problems by combining answers to overlapping subproblems. Learning patterns lets you map new problems onto known ones.

## What I took away

- Learn patterns, not individual problems.
- Pair DP with core graph algorithms.
