## Summary

The author says Epoch is fundraising and calls it one of the best orgs for understanding and forecasting AI progress. The quoted post lists investigations like AI adoption by role and region, data supply bottlenecks, worldwide compute buildout, and whether inference costs keep falling.

## Background

Epoch AI studies trends in compute, data and AI capabilities. Its public datasets are widely used for forecasting. The listed questions show what the field wants to measure.

## What I took away

- Epoch's data is a good reference for AI trends.
- The listed questions are a good research agenda.
- Consider supporting open research orgs.
