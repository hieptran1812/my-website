## Summary

The author says he is happy about support from the Chinese community and notes a cultural difference in how people follow his stock-picking logic. The quoted Chinese post profiles him, claiming a 4502.45% return in 2026 and 25 public positions that rose 100% to 1000%. These are self-reported or promotional claims.

## Background

Returns this large are rare and hard to verify. Public track records are often selected after the fact. This is not investment advice.

## What I took away

- Ask for verified, audited records before trusting return claims.
- Extreme returns often reflect survivorship or high risk.
- Learn the reasoning, not just the picks.
