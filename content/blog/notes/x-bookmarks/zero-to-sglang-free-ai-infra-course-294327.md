## Summary

The authors say the zero-to-sglang written course is done after more than three months. It is free and meant as a solid path into AI infra. They encourage learners to become open-source contributors, and it is hosted on GitHub.

## Background

SGLang is an open-source framework for serving LLMs efficiently. A course built around it teaches how inference systems work, including scheduling and batching. Contents are at the repository.

## What I took away

- A structured path into inference infra is now free.
- Contributing to SGLang is a practical next step.
- Check the repo for prerequisites.
