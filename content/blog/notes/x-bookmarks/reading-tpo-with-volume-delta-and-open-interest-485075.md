## Summary

The author shows how they read a TPO chart. Adding a volume-delta profile can reveal divergences at extremes, such as strong positive delta at the top while price cannot move higher, which they call trapped buyers. They add open interest and net longs and say those zones are usually retested.

## Background

TPO is a market profile chart showing time spent at each price. Volume delta is buy volume minus sell volume. The idea is that heavy buying with no price progress may mean buyers are stuck. This is an educational description of one view, not advice.

## What I took away

- Combine price profile with volume delta to look for divergence.
- Open interest can add context about positioning.
- Claims like zones usually get retested should be tested on data.
