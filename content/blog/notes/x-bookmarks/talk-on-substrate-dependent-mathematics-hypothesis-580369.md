## Summary

The author recommends a short talk, Substrate-dependent mathematics hypothesis, by Olaf Witkowski on Dr. Levin's channel. They say every video from that symposium is great. The quoted post invites readers to talks in an ongoing symposium on Platonic space, with new talks weekly.

## Background

The talks explore whether mathematical structures exist independently of physical things, an idea often called Platonic space. The symposium is hosted by Michael Levin's thoughtforms.life. It is philosophical and speculative research.

## What I took away

- A symposium series with weekly talks is available.
- The topic is philosophy of mathematics and biology.
- Treat it as ideas to explore, not settled results.
