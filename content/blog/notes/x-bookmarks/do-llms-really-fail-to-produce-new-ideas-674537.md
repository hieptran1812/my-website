## Summary

@Hesamation argues that new ideas are not out of distribution but come from recombination, abstraction, analogy and search, like the Wright brothers combining birds, bicycles, wings and engines. The quoted post says it is mathematically true that LLMs cannot come up with novel ideas because training reduces loss.

## Background

The debate is about whether models trained to fit data can create something new. Recombination can produce novelty even from known parts. Both views here are opinions.

## What I took away

- Novelty can arise from combining known pieces.
- Be skeptical of absolute claims in either direction.
