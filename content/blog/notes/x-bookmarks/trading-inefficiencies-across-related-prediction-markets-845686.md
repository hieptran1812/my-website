## Summary

The author shares a guide on how to spot and trade inefficiencies across related prediction markets. They call it a must-read for people trading Polymarket.

## Background

Related markets, such as ones on the same event, should have consistent prices. When they do not, there may be an inefficiency. This note is educational and not advice.

## What I took away

- Related markets should obey logical price constraints.
- Apparent gaps may be costs, not opportunities.
- Read the guide critically.
