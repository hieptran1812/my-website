## Summary

@elonmusk says necessity is the mother of invention and calls the Tesla AI team hardcore. The quoted post claims Tesla patented a mathematical cheat code that lets cheap 8-bit chips run 32-bit AI models. The claim is from the quoted account.

## Background

Bit width is the precision of numbers. Lower precision like 8-bit is faster and cheaper but less accurate. Methods to keep accuracy at low precision are common in quantization. The quoted post is promotional.

## What I took away

- Low-precision compute saves cost.
- Patent claims need careful reading.
- Hype headlines can overstate novelty.
