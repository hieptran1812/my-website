## Summary

@devpost announces the Google DeepMind Gemini Hackathon for building with Gemini 3. It focuses on reasoning and multimodal projects, from experiments to full prototypes. It lists $100K in prizes and a deadline of Feb 9.

## Background

A hackathon is a time-limited build event with prizes. Multimodal means the model handles more than text, such as images or audio. Events like this are a low-risk way to try a new model and get feedback.

## What I took away

- Deadline is Feb 9 per the post.
- Hackathons are a quick way to learn a new model API.
- Check the official page for rules.
