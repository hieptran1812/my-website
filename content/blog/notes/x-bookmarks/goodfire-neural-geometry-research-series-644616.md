## Summary

Goodfire says neural networks might speak English but think in shapes. They say understanding neural geometry is key to understanding how models work and to debugging and controlling them precisely. They are releasing a series of posts on this agenda.

## Background

Neural geometry studies how representations are arranged in a model's internal space. If concepts correspond to shapes, steering and debugging can be done with geometric tools.

## What I took away

- Think of representations as structures, not just feature lists.
- Follow the series for concrete methods.
