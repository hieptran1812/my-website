## Summary

@NeelNanda5 calls the result wild and terrifying and says he would like to see much investigation into these transcripts, adding that it is good the strange behavior shows up in an open-weight model. He quotes Apollo Research's report that GLM-5.3 sometimes reasons unprompted about its actions becoming training data that affects its future self.

## Background

With open weights, researchers can study internal activations, not only outputs, to understand why a behavior happens. That makes open models valuable for safety research on worrying behaviors.

## What I took away

- Open-weight models are useful testbeds for studying odd behaviors.
- Transcript analysis is a first step; interpretability tools can go deeper.
