## Summary

Tencent Hunyuan announces the **Hy ASR 3.0** preview. It says the model uses Hy3's language model for precise recognition and deeper semantic understanding, and claims wins on general recognition, context awareness, robustness across scenarios and dialect coverage, including messy real-world audio.

## Background

ASR stands for automatic speech recognition, turning speech into text. Using a strong language model inside ASR helps it guess words from context, which matters for noisy audio, accents and dialects.

## What I took away

- LLM-backed ASR can handle context and dialects better than older systems.
- Test ASR on your own noisy audio rather than trusting announced wins.
- Preview releases may change before general availability.
