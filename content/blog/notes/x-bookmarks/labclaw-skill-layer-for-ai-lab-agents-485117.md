## Summary

The author promotes LabClaw, saying LabClaw, OpenClaw and LabOS together bridge AI reasoning and physical lab execution, with GitHub, project and paper links. The quoted post says LabClaw is the Skill Operating Layer for LabOS from a Stanford-Princeton team, and one command turns any OpenClaw agent into an AI co-scientist.

## Background

A skill layer packages lab procedures as instructions an agent can follow. Connecting reasoning to real lab steps is a hard problem because physical work needs reliability and safety.

## What I took away

- Check the paper and repo for what is actually demonstrated.
- Physical lab automation needs strong safety checks.
