## Summary

The author releases version 2 of the **LLM Evaluation Guidebook**, now updated, nicer to read and with interactive graphics, hosted on Hugging Face. She says she is taking a sabbatical to hike with her dogs and will be back at Hugging Face in December 2026.

## Background

LLM evaluation is how we measure whether a model is good at tasks. It is hard because benchmarks can be gamed and metrics can mislead. A guidebook gives practitioners a shared set of practices.

## What I took away

- Bookmark the guidebook as a reference on evaluation.
- Learn how benchmarks and metrics can mislead.
- Evaluation skill is as valuable as training skill.
