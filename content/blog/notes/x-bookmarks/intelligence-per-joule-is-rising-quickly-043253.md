## Summary

The author says intelligence-per-joule is increasing quickly because both models and chips are improving and the gains compound. However, demand for inference is growing even faster. The post links an arXiv paper with collaborators from Hazy Research and quotes a post saying intelligence per joule will keep improving.

## Background

Intelligence per joule measures how much useful model capability you get for a unit of energy. Better models give more capability per computation, and better chips give more computation per joule, so the two improvements multiply. Rising demand can still increase total energy use, an effect similar to the Jevons paradox.

## What I took away

- Efficiency gains from models and hardware multiply rather than add.
- Cheaper inference tends to increase total usage, so total energy can still rise.
- Read the linked paper for how the metric is defined and measured.
