## Summary

@KrishnaswamyLab announces an invited talk at the Non-Euclidean Foundation Models and Geometric Learning workshop at NeurIPS. The talk is on geometry-aware generative models for scientific data generation, with two arXiv papers linked.

## Background

Geometry-aware models take into account the shape or structure of the data space, such as manifolds, instead of treating data as flat vectors. This is useful for scientific data such as cells or molecules, which often have non-flat structure.

## What I took away

- Scientific data often has structure that ordinary models ignore.
- The linked papers are the place to start for details.
