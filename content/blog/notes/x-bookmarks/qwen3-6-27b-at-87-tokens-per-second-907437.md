## Summary

The author reports **87 tokens per second with Qwen3.6 27B** on a consumer AMD graphics card, not a server GPU. They call it the best local model and say it runs faster than paid cloud models. They conclude that the future of AI companies does not look so dominant.

## Background

Tokens per second measures generation speed. Running a 27B model on a consumer card usually needs quantization. The comparison with cloud models is the author's opinion and depends on the cloud service and load.

## What I took away

- Consumer GPUs can now run strong open models at good speeds.
- Check quantization and context length behind any speed claim.
- Local inference weakens the moat of closed API providers, at least for some tasks.
