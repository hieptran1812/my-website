## Summary

The author says many YC startups got wiped and the real moat is infra and the models, not wrappers. The quoted post is Anthropic's Cowork announcement. It is an opinion.

## Background

Wrapper startups build thin products on top of model APIs and are vulnerable when model makers ship similar features.

## What I took away

- Thin wrappers are vulnerable.
- This is opinion, not fact.
