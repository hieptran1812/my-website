## Summary

The author spoke to a school board about AI in schools. A highlight is that you will never be able to detect AI use in homework, and that AI detectors do not really work. The quoted post shows Gemini Nano Banana Pro solving exam questions inside the exam page image, with doodles and diagrams. The rest of the list is cut off.

## Background

AI detectors guess whether text was machine-written and are unreliable. Image models that can read and answer exam pages make cheating on take-home work easier. Schools may need to rely on in-person work instead.

## What I took away

- AI detectors are unreliable, so do not base grading on them.
- Assessment may need to move to in-person settings.
- Image models can now solve questions shown as pictures.
