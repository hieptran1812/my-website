## Summary

The author says Larry Wasserman blogged about statistics for a short time. They claim it has the highest signal-to-noise ratio of all quantitative data science blogs, with Ben Recht's blog coming close. The blog has been inactive since 2013 but, in the author's view, has not aged.

## Background

Signal-to-noise ratio here means how much useful content there is compared to filler. Statistics and machine learning share many foundations, so older writing on statistical thinking is often still useful. Blogs like this can teach concepts that outlast trends.

## What I took away

- Old, well-written statistics material can stay useful for a decade or more.
- Add the Normal Deviate blog to a reading list for statistics and machine learning basics.
- Ben Recht's blog is named as a comparable high-quality source.
