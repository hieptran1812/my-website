## Summary

@atomic_chat_hq says Mistral OCR 4 converted a photo of a handwritten calculus exam into clean LaTeX, taking 5.1s and costing $0.09, with formulas reportedly exactly right. The quoted post introduces Mistral OCR 4, with bounding boxes, block classification and inline confidence scores in 170 languages.

## Background

OCR (optical character recognition) turns images of text into digital text. LaTeX is a markup used for math. Bounding boxes and confidence scores let downstream tools locate and trust or flag each piece.

## What I took away

- Handwritten math can be converted to LaTeX in seconds.
- Confidence scores help route uncertain parts to review.
