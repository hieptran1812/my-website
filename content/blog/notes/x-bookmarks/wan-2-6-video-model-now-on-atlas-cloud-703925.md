## Summary

Atlas Cloud says Wan 2.6 is live on its platform. It lists multi-shot narrative with smart segmentation and no character hallucination, 15-second duration, and advanced video reference that clones look and tone for solo or duo actors. These are the vendor's own claims.

## Background

Wan is a family of video generation models. Multi-shot means one prompt can produce several connected scenes while keeping characters consistent. Reference features let the model copy the style or appearance of a given clip. Longer clips matter because most video models produce only a few seconds.

## What I took away

- Character consistency across shots is a key weakness of video models.
- Vendor claims like zero hallucination should be tested on your own prompts.
- Longer clip length makes storytelling use cases more practical.
