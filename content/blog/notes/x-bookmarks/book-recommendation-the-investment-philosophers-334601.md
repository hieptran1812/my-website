## Summary

The author says they loved The Investment Philosophers by Ethan Everett. They say it is good for readers who like thinking about why and how people think. They found its explanations of human nature in philosophical terms useful.

## Background

The post is a reading recommendation, not a technical piece. Books about how people think can help investors understand behavior such as bias and herd action, which affects markets.

## What I took away

- Add the book to a reading list if investor psychology interests you.
- Understanding human behavior is part of understanding markets.
