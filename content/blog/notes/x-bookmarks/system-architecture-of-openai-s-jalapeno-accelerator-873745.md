## Summary

The author praises a small account whose articles are very detailed and well chosen, and complains the X algorithm underrates it. The quoted post shares an advanced system architecture breakdown of OpenAI's **Jalapeno** inference accelerator. It goes beyond raw FLOPs into the surrounding network and how AI added value.

## Background

An inference accelerator is a chip built to run trained models efficiently. Raw FLOPs say little about real performance; memory, interconnect and network design often decide how fast large models run across many chips.

## What I took away

- Look past peak FLOPs to networking and memory when judging AI chips.
- Small expert accounts can be great sources; follow good writers, not only big ones.
