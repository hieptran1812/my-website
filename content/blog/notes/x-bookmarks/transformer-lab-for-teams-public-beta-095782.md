## Summary

The author announces the public beta of Transformer Lab for Teams, described as the open-source OS for modern AI research. It aims to replace fragmented workflows and legacy scripts with a unified engine.

## Background

Research teams often juggle scripts for training, evaluation and tracking. A unified platform standardizes these. It is open source so you can inspect it.

## What I took away

- Unified tooling reduces glue code.
- Try the beta against your own pipeline.
- Check how it handles compute management.
