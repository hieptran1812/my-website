## Summary

@NeelNanda5 says this was one of his favourite parts of Anthropic culture. He describes a scheme where people get 2.5X more equity if they agree to donate half of it, which he says filters for people who care about doing good. The quoted post says this was a large reason they chose Anthropic over OpenAI in March 2021 and calls it costly signaling.

## Background

Costly signaling means showing a real commitment by accepting a real cost, so the signal is hard to fake. Here the cost is giving away part of the equity. The idea is that people who only want money would not accept the deal.

## What I took away

- Costly signals are harder to fake than stated values.
- Hiring and culture design can use incentives that filter for motivation.
- These are personal accounts, not verified details of the company's pay scheme.
