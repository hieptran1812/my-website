## Summary

@jacobrodri_ reacts with surprise to a quoted claim. The quoted post says the author found a Turkish app studio making $270M per year with 29 apps.

## Background

Mobile app studios often run many apps with subscriptions and paid user acquisition. Revenue figures shared in viral posts are often estimates from third-party tools, not reported numbers.

## What I took away

- Treat viral revenue figures as unverified estimates.
- A portfolio of many apps is a known business model, but results vary widely.
