## Summary

Ion Stoica says high-quality video generation now runs on hardware you already own. The quoted posts describe a planned short-drama pipeline with FreeVideo and FreeToken, and FreeVideo itself, which runs MiniMax H3 and Video DeltaNet in as little as 8GB VRAM and 16GB RAM.

## Background

VRAM is GPU memory, usually the limiting factor for large models. 8GB is common on consumer cards, so this target makes local video generation widely accessible.

## What I took away

- Try FreeVideo if you have a mid-range GPU.
- See the related FreeVideo post in this batch.
