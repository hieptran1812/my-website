## Summary

Lochan says many people in AI have watched 3Blue1Brown but do not realize the videos are made with code, not edited. He recreated a backpropagation animation with Manim, with help from Blackbox AI. The post is cut off.

## Background

Manim is a Python library for making math animations, created for 3Blue1Brown. Making an animation of backpropagation forces you to understand each step of the algorithm.

## What I took away

- Manim lets you build math animations in code.
- Teaching by building a visual is a good way to learn.
