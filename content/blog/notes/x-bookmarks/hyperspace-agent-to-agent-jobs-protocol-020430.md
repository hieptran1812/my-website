## Summary

The post announces v4.1.0 of an Agent-to-Agent Jobs Protocol: your agent can post a job to the Hyperspace network and another agent picks it up, executes it and delivers the result without human involvement, for example summarizing 20 ML papers. The quoted post describes making Karpathy's autoresearch loop generic, with a distributed swarm solving plain-English problems.

## Background

This is a marketplace idea for agents: tasks are posted and picked up by other agents. Trust, verification and cost are open questions the post does not address in the shown text.

## What I took away

- Agent-to-agent job markets need verification of results.
- Plain-English problem posting lowers the barrier to using swarms.
