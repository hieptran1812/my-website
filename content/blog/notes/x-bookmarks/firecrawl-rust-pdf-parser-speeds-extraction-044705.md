## Summary

Firecrawl announces a new Rust-based PDF parser that it says is 3x faster and gives cleaner data for RAG and AI search. It has three modes: Fast, Auto and OCR, aimed at academic papers and regulatory filings.

## Background

PDF parsing is hard because layout is complex. OCR reads text from scanned images. Clean parsed text improves retrieval-based answers.

## What I took away

- Parsing quality limits RAG quality.
- The 3x number is the vendor's claim.
