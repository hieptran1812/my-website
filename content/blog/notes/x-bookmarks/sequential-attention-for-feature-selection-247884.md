## Summary

Google Research says feature selection is a fundamental NP-hard problem, meaning it is hard to solve perfectly and quickly for large datasets. It introduces Sequential Attention, but the text is cut off before describing it.

## Background

Feature selection picks the most useful inputs for a model. NP-hard means no known fast exact method, so practical methods use approximations.

## What I took away

- Feature selection is hard in theory but approximations work.
- Fewer features can mean smaller, faster models.
- Read the blog for the method.
