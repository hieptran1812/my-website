## Summary

The author quotes a line comparing this to an ML researcher rediscovering Kaiming init and RMSNorm: the autosearcher rediscovered ListNet, a listwise vs pairwise ranking loss. The quoted post introduces Autosearcher, a distributed search engine built with the autoresearch pattern.

## Background

ListNet is a known learning-to-rank method that scores whole lists instead of pairs. Rediscovery shows the agent's loop found a known good idea, though not a new one.

## What I took away

- Rediscovering known methods is a sanity check for agent search.
- Rediscovery is not the same as novel discovery.
- The claim comes from the author's own experiment.
