## Summary

The author praises a developer who is designing and training a TTS model, a codec model and an audio upscaler from scratch. The quoted post says local TTS models are slow or robotic, so he open-sourced MiraTTS, claimed to generate 100 seconds of realistic audio in 1 second, with code and weights linked.

## Background

TTS means text-to-speech. A codec turns audio into compact tokens a model can handle, and an upscaler improves audio quality. The speed figure is the creator's claim, so test it locally.

## What I took away

- Open TTS models are getting fast enough for local use.
- Audio pipelines often combine a TTS model, codec and upscaler.
- Verify the speed claim on your own hardware.
