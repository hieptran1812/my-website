## Summary

@LiorOnAI recommends a video on why Transformers are taking over CNNs in computer vision. The post gives no details of the argument.

## Background

CNNs use local filters and were long dominant for images. Vision Transformers split images into patches and use attention. With enough data they scale well. The video is the source of the reasoning.

## What I took away

- Attention-based models now compete in vision.
- Scale and data matter for the switch.
- Watch the video for the full argument.
