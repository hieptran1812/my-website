## Summary

The author says the GB300 is the best AI computer. One quoted post congratulates SpaceX and Anthropic on a compute partnership powered by 220,000+ NVIDIA GPUs in Colossus 1. Anthropic says the deal, with other compute deals, let it raise usage limits for Claude Code and the API.

## Background

GB300 is an NVIDIA GPU system for AI. Colossus 1 is a large GPU cluster. More compute lets providers serve more users, which shows up as higher limits.

## What I took away

- Compute partnerships quickly translate into usage limit changes.
- Rival labs can still share infrastructure.
