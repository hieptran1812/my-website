## Summary

On day 234 of a GPU programming series, the author studies **Designing AI Chip Hardware and Software**, a self-published document by Bjarke Hammersholt Roune shared by a friend, and notes the author's background on TPUs and compilers. The quoted day-233 post covers Reiner Pope's talk Chip design from the bottom up, highlighting sections such as FPGAs versus ASICs.

## Background

AI chips like GPUs and TPUs are designed around matrix math and memory bandwidth. FPGAs are reconfigurable chips, while ASICs are fixed custom chips that are faster but costly to design. Understanding chip design helps explain why kernels and models are shaped the way they are.

## What I took away

- Knowing chip design basics makes GPU programming choices easier to understand.
- Daily learning logs are a good way to stay consistent on hard topics.
- Both the document and the talk are worth adding to a hardware reading list.
