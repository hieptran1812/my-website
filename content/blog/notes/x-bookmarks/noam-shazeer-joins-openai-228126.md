## Summary

@himanshustwts says OpenAI has Noam himself and lists Shazeer's contributions: Attention is All You Need, Sparse MoE, Switch Transformer, Multi Query and more. The quoted post is Shazeer saying he will join OpenAI, calling it a difficult decision and expressing pride in the Google team.

## Background

Shazeer co-authored the Transformer paper and key work on MoE and multi-query attention. Talent moves between labs shape the field. This is reported from the posts.

## What I took away

- Key architecture authors are moving between labs.
- Many core LLM ideas trace to a few people.
