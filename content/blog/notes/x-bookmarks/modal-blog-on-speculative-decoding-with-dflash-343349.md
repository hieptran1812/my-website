## Summary

@EthosVentures calls the Modal writeup great and says spec decoding is the future of open-source inference, adding it is bullish for $ZERO, a token. The quoted post announces six more DFlash speculators for Alibaba Qwen 3.x, with over 1k output tokens per second for 3.5 122B-A10B on a B200.

## Background

Speculative decoding has a small model draft tokens that the big model verifies in one pass, so output is faster. The token comment is the author's opinion, not advice.

## What I took away

- Speculators are available for Qwen 3.x models.
- Treat investment views as opinions.
