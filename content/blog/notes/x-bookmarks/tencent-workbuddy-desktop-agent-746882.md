## Summary

The author says Tencent seems to be betting big on OpenClaw. The quoted post introduces WorkBuddy, an AI native desktop agent for multi-type tasks with built-in skill templates for coding, documentation, research, data analysis and automation, no project setup and quick IM connection.

## Background

Desktop agents act on your computer to complete tasks. Skill templates give them prebuilt procedures for common jobs.

## What I took away

- Large companies are shipping OpenClaw-style agents.
- Skill templates lower the barrier for non-technical users.
