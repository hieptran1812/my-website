## Summary

The author says inference optimization has moved up the abstraction ladder. It is less about handwriting the fastest kernels and more about how well you define the optimization context, I/O, constraints and testing environment for AI agents.

## Background

As coding agents get better at writing kernels, the human job shifts to specifying the problem: inputs and outputs, performance targets, and correctness tests. This is often called harness engineering.

## What I took away

- Good benchmarks and correctness tests are what make agent-written kernels trustworthy.
- Kernel knowledge still matters for writing the spec and judging results.
