## Summary

@GuandaoYang gives three reasons for the name Functional Attention: functions instead of tokens become first-class in attention, it is inspired by functional maps from geometry processing, and it works. The quoted post is the ICML 2026 paper introducing it as going from pairwise affinities to functional correspondences.

## Background

Standard attention scores pairs of tokens. Functional maps come from geometry processing and relate functions on two shapes. The paper applies that idea to attention so it operates in function space.

## What I took away

- Attention can be framed beyond pairwise token matching.
- Geometry ideas are being imported into attention design.
