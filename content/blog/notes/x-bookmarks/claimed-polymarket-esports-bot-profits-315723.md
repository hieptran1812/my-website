## Summary

The author claims $111 turned into $230K in three months, a 23,000% return, from League of Legends and Dota 2 markets on Polymarket. They call it the most overlooked edge and say a bot quietly farmed esports.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market. Posts with extreme return claims and no verifiable data are common promotion tactics and often involve survivorship bias or outright exaggeration. This is not trading advice.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of huge return claims without audited proof.
- Ask what risks, costs and failed attempts are not shown.
