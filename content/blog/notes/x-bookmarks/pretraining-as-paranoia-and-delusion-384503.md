## Summary

@eliebakouch jokes that pretraining is the art of being paranoid 90% of the time and completely delusional the other 10%. He quotes @zxytim's post listing experimental setup factors to consider before trusting architecture ablation results.

## Background

Pretraining runs are expensive, so teams check every detail obsessively, yet they must also make bold bets on unproven ideas. The quoted post highlights how many factors can skew ablation results.

## What I took away

- Double-check experimental setups before trusting results.
- Big training decisions still require bold bets despite uncertainty.
