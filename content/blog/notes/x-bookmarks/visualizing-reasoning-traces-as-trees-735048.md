## Summary

@ivanfioravanti praises a visualization of models thinking and rethinking before reaching an answer. The quoted post says the author asked 8 models the same tricky probability question and rendered each reasoning trace as a tree, with each pivot like But or Wait, actually as a new branch.

## Background

Reasoning models write long thinking traces, and phrases like Wait mark self-correction. Rendering the trace as a tree makes the structure easier to compare across models.

## What I took away

- Visualizing traces reveals how often models backtrack.
- Comparing models on the same question shows different reasoning styles.
- Tree views are a cheap debugging tool for reasoning.
