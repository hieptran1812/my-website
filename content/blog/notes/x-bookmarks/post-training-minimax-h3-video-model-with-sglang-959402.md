## Summary

The author says they will soon share how they post-train **MiniMax H3** using the SGLang framework. The quoted post declares the post-training era for video models has begun.

## Background

Post-training is extra training after pretraining to improve specific behaviors, such as with fine-tuning or RL. It has transformed text LLMs, and the claim is that video models are now entering the same phase. SGLang is mainly a serving engine, so using it for post-training rollouts is notable.

## What I took away

- Video models may now benefit from the same post-training methods as LLMs.
- Serving engines are often reused for generating rollouts during RL.
- Watch for the write-up to see the actual recipe.
