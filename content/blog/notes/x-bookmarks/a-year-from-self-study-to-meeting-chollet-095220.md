## Summary

@eigenron says that a year ago they started teaching themselves deep learning with @fchollet's book, and now they talked to him about world models at a YC x ARC party. They say life can change in a year if you keep at it. The quoted post is a chapter-2 study note on the book: tensors, tensor operations, gradient descent and MNIST from scratch.

## Background

Deep Learning with Python is a popular beginner book. Chapter 2 covers tensors, gradient descent and a first MNIST classifier. World models are models that learn to predict how an environment changes.

## What I took away

- Consistent self-study over a year can open real doors.
- Working through a book chapter by chapter with notes is a solid way to start.
