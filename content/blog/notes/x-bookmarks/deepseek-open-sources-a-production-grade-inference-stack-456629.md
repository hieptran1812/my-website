## Summary

@superalesha notes that the creator of QLoRA said DeepSeek's inference system is on par with OpenAI's and better than what Anthropic runs, at least at their scale, and stresses that it is Apache 2.0 licensed. He argues that until now a production stack at that level existed only inside a few companies. The quoted post makes the comparison claim directly.

## Background

An inference stack is the software that serves a model to users: batching, caching, parallelism and scheduling. Apache 2.0 is a permissive license that allows commercial use. Comparisons to closed stacks are opinions, since those stacks are not public.

## What I took away

- A permissive license makes a production inference engine usable by anyone.
- Treat comparisons with closed systems as informed opinion, not measurement.
- See the DeepInfer note in this batch for the release itself.
