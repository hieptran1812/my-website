## Summary

The author says that in two years the firm went from unprofitable to top 10%, top 1%, and with the upcoming Mark3.5 aims to be a top 0.1% hedge fund. He calls the fund a proof of technology and says AlphaFund Labs is building what SSI is trying to build. The quoted post jokes that knowing what SSI is trying to do marks someone on the inside of the SF ML scene.

## Background

SSI is Safe Superintelligence, an AI lab. The performance rankings are the author's claim with no data. Treat them as marketing until audited.

## What I took away

- Self-reported fund rankings are not verified performance.
- Hedge funds are sometimes pitched as a stepping stone to AI ambitions.
