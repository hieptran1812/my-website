## Summary

Stas Bekman notes that his ML Engineering book is on Prime Intellect's suggested reading list for RL and distributed training roles. The quoted post praises the company for including a reading list, saying it reflects the culture, and starts the list with Designing Data-Intensive Applications and Programming Massively Parallel Processors.

## Background

Distributed training spreads model training across many GPUs and machines. Books on data systems and parallel programming give the base knowledge needed for that work.

## What I took away

- Use job-post reading lists as a curriculum even if you are not applying.
- Programming Massively Parallel Processors is a standard CUDA text.
