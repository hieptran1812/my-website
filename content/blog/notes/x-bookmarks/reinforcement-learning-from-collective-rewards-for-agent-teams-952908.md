## Summary

@GenReasoning introduces Reinforcement Learning from Collective Rewards (RLCR), which trains language models as agent organisations that share a message board and a workspace. They use it to train Avalon-M1-Mini, a 120B model, and study its behavior, reporting several interesting findings (the post text is cut off).

## Background

In reinforcement learning, a model is rewarded for good outcomes and updated to make them more likely. Collective rewards mean a group of agents is rewarded for the team's result rather than each agent's own. This targets multi-agent systems where several model instances coordinate on a task.

## What I took away

- Training agents together on a shared goal is different from training one agent at a time.
- Shared communication channels like a message board are part of the training setup.
