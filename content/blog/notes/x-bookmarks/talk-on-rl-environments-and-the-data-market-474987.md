## Summary

The author recommends listening to a speaker who shares secrets on RL environments. The quoted post asks how much a good RL task sells for and what makes a good one, and says the talk was given at the SGLang Summit 2026.

## Background

RL environments are tasks with automatic scoring that a model practices on. Labs buy them from vendors, so there is a market. Quality matters because a poor environment can teach the wrong behavior.

## What I took away

- RL tasks are now a traded commodity.
- Task quality determines what the model learns.
- Watch the talk for pricing and quality criteria.
