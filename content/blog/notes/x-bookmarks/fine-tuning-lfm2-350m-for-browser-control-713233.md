## Summary

Pau Labarta Bajo shares a step-by-step article on fine tuning a small language model for browser control. The linked Substack post is about fine tuning LFM2-350M.

## Background

Fine tuning adapts a pretrained model to a task with extra training on examples. Small models are cheap to run and can work well when the task is narrow, like clicking and typing in a browser.

## What I took away

- Small models can be fine tuned for narrow agent tasks.
- Follow the article for a concrete workflow.
