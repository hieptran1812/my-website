## Summary

The author says to repeat after him that this is not what a Kalman filter is used for, reacting to a quoted post about missiles, robots and traffic. The quoted post says quants use the Kalman filter to predict future observations of hidden variables and shows how to use it in Python without the explosions.

## Background

A Kalman filter estimates a hidden state, such as true position or a latent price level, from noisy measurements. It updates its estimate step by step as new data arrives. In finance it is used for things like estimating hedge ratios or trends.

## What I took away

- A Kalman filter tracks a hidden variable from noisy data.
- Techniques from engineering often transfer to quant finance.
- Try it in Python on a simple spread or trend series.
