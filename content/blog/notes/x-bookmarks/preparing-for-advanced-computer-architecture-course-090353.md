## Summary

The author says he was a top student in his undergraduate Computer Architecture class, but took it in his sophomore year. He is now reviewing the material with a Coursera course to prepare for Advanced Topics in Computer Architecture.

## Background

Computer architecture covers how processors, memory and caches work. Reviewing the basics before an advanced class avoids gaps. It is a useful foundation for understanding GPUs and performance.

## What I took away

- Review fundamentals before advanced courses.
- Online courses can fill gaps from earlier years.
