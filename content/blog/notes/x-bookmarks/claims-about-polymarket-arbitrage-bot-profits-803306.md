## Summary

The author claims programmers make $10k to $200k per month on Polymarket. His first example is arbitrage bots that buy YES and NO when the combined price is under $1, such as 48 cents plus 49 cents for 97 cents, locking a 3 cent profit per dollar. He names a trader called distinct-baguette; the thread is cut off.

## Background

If YES and NO both cost less than $1 together, buying both guarantees $1 at resolution. Real trades face fees, partial fills and fast competitors. The income claims are unverified.

## What I took away

- The YES plus NO under $1 idea is simple but crowded.
- Treat income claims with skepticism.
- Fees and fill risk decide real profit.
