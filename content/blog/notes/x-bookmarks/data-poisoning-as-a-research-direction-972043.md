## Summary

The author proposes data poisoning as a valuable direction. He says his team is not working on it and has not done serious research, so his view should be taken with a grain of salt. He asks for pointers to existing research. The text is cut off.

## Background

Data poisoning means inserting harmful or misleading examples into training data to change model behavior. It matters because models train on web-scale data that is hard to audit.

## What I took away

- Data poisoning is an open area with room for new work.
- The author invites existing references.
- Training data provenance is a safety issue.
