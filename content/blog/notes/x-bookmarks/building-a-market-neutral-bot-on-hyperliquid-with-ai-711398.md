## Summary

The author says AI tools make financial markets feel like an addictive video game. In a few hours they set up a VPS in Tokyo, connected to Hyperliquid's WebSocket feed, and built a 24/7 market-neutral bot trading deviations between SNDK markets. The quoted post says a new venue, Entropy, with ex-Citadel and ex-Optiver staff, could challenge TradeXYZ, and notes its backend returns a pointsMultiplier field hinting at a points program.

## Background

Market-neutral means the bot holds offsetting positions so overall market moves matter less; it profits from price differences between related markets. A VPS close to the exchange reduces latency. Points programs reward users with possible future tokens, which can draw speculative activity.

## What I took away

- AI tools make it fast to build trading bots, which also makes it fast to lose money.
- Market-neutral does not mean risk-free; spreads can widen and fees add up.
- This is a description of an experiment, not a trading recommendation.
