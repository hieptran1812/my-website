## Summary

@hexapode says hundreds of researchers, amateurs and their agents are collaborating to push the frontier of math, calls it the future of research, and invites people to a San Francisco event. The quoted update reports progress on an integer multiplication community auto-research effort, with a constant going from 2^-182 to 0.001734815770, and says a free model, opencode's Space Bunny Max, set the record four times in a row.

## Background

Auto-research means AI agents run experiments or search for proofs and constructions with little human input. Open community projects let many people try ideas in parallel and share results on a leaderboard. Integer multiplication algorithms are a classic area of complexity theory.

## What I took away

- Open leaderboards plus agents can make fast progress on well-defined math targets.
- Free models can compete when the task is well scoped.
- Check how results are verified in such community projects.
