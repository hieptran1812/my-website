## Summary

The author says that after Hugging Face, **Unsloth** has done the most to make deep learning accessible. They like the Qwen3.5 models, even the 2B and 4B sizes, and say the 0.8B one is easy to fine-tune. The quoted Unsloth post says you can run RL on Qwen3.5-2B locally with 8GB VRAM, using vision GRPO to learn math problems.

## Background

Reinforcement learning (RL) trains a model by rewarding good outputs instead of showing it correct answers. GRPO is an RL method that compares several sampled answers to the same prompt. Needing only 8GB VRAM means a consumer GPU or free Colab is enough to try it.

## What I took away

- RL fine-tuning of small models is now possible on a single consumer GPU.
- A ready notebook is the fastest way to learn GRPO by doing.
- Small Qwen3.5 sizes (0.8B to 4B) are good test beds for cheap experiments.
