## Summary

@simon_zhai says every time he loses faith in academic RL research, Andrea Zanette's work comes up. He quotes Zanette's post on unifying all phases of foundation model training into a single algorithm.

## Background

Academic labs often have less compute than industry, so impactful work tends to be about ideas and frameworks. Unified training proposes one algorithm in place of separate training stages.

## What I took away

- Academic research can still shape how large models are trained.
- Follow researchers whose work consistently offers new frameworks.
