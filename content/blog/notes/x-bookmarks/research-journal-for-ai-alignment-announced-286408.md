## Summary

The author announces, with several co-founders, a forthcoming research journal for AI alignment. Geoffrey Irving, Marcus Hutter, Scott Aaronson and Victoria Krakovna have agreed to form the initial advisory board.

## Background

Alignment research aims to make AI systems behave as intended. A dedicated journal gives it a venue for peer review.

## What I took away

- A dedicated alignment journal is coming.
- Watch for submission details.
