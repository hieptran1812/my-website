## Summary

The post says Tencent released Universal Audio Tokenizer on Hugging Face. It is a compact single-codebook model that combines general audio perception and linguistic alignment for Audio-LLM integration.

## Background

An audio tokenizer turns sound into discrete tokens that a language model can read and write. A single codebook keeps the token stream simple.

## What I took away

- Tokenizers are the bridge between audio and LLMs.
- Single codebook means simpler modeling.
