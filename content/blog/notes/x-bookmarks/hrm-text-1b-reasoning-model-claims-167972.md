## Summary

The author says HRM-Text outperforms all previous Transformers and Looped Transformers across language and reasoning benchmarks with a fraction of params, FLOPs and data, with expressive and stable latent reasoning and large effective depth. The quote introduces it as an ultra-lean 1B model trained on just 40B structured tokens.

## Background

Looped transformers reuse layers repeatedly to get more effective depth without more parameters. HRM-style models reason in a latent loop. The outperformance claim is the authors' and needs independent checks.

## What I took away

- Small models with latent reasoning loops are an active direction.
- 40B tokens is tiny compared with typical pretraining.
- Verify claims on the benchmarks used.
