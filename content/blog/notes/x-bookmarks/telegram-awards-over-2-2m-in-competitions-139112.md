## Summary

Pavel Durov says Telegram awarded over $2,222,000 in one month to people in coding, design, AI and content creation. The linked card adds that $222,000 was distributed this week among winners of two recent competitions.

## Background

Platforms run paid contests to attract developers and creators to build on them. These are an option for engineers to earn money and build a portfolio.

## What I took away

- Platform contests can be a way to get paid for side projects.
- Read the rules and terms before entering.
