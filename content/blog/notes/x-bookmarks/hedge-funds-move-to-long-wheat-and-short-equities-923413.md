## Summary

The author writes in Vietnamese that hedge funds are shifting to long wheat and recalls the coffee rally of 2022 to 2024. The quoted post says hedge funds shorted global equities heavily in March, at a 13-year high, mostly through ETFs and indexes as a market hedge.

## Background

Going long means betting on a price rise and short means betting on a fall. Positioning data shows how funds are leaning, but it does not predict outcomes.

## What I took away

- Positioning is not a prediction.
- Educational only, not advice.
- Check the source data.
