## Summary

The post recommends an essay by Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic. It says his picture is that around 2027 a new nation appears whose citizens are tens of millions of AI agents, smarter than people and fast.

## Background

This is a commonly discussed scenario of many capable AI agents acting at scale. It is a forecast, not a fact.

## What I took away

- Treat forecasts as scenarios.
- Read the original essay.
