## Summary

The author notes that AI progress has led many people to think the Riemann hypothesis could be solved soon. Meanwhile, they recommend a 40-page presentation by Alain Connes titled The Riemann Hypothesis: Past, Present (and likely Future; the text is cut off).

## Background

The Riemann hypothesis is a famous unsolved problem about where the zeros of the Riemann zeta function lie, with deep links to the distribution of prime numbers. Alain Connes is a Fields Medal winning mathematician who has worked on it for decades.

## What I took away

- Learn the real difficulty of a problem before guessing how soon AI will solve it.
- Expert overviews are a good entry point to famous open problems.
