## Summary

The quoted post wonders why OpenAI spends so many GPUs on hard math problems instead of using its strongest models to search for a better architecture than the Transformer. @hyhieu226 says, admitting the opinion may not age well, that there is no better architecture than the Transformer and that attention and MoE variations are just for efficiency while hardware lags behind.

## Background

The Transformer uses attention to let every token look at others. Variants like sparse attention and Mixture-of-Experts reduce compute or memory. The argument is that these change cost, not the basic capability.

## What I took away

- Separate architecture changes for capability from those for efficiency.
- Hardware limits shape which architectures are practical.
- This is an opinion; keep it in mind when evaluating new architectures.
