## Summary

The author and Karina Nguyen announce a new course on Full-Stack Post-Training, RL and RSI at UC Berkeley. Berkeley students who want to learn about the frontier are encouraged to enroll next semester.

## Background

Post-training means shaping a pretrained model with SFT and RL. RSI most likely stands for recursive self-improvement. The course is aimed at students, and the post gives no syllabus.

## What I took away

- Post-training is becoming a formal curriculum topic.
- Berkeley students can enroll next semester.
- Look for public materials if you are not enrolled.
