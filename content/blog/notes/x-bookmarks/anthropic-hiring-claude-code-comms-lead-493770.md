## Summary

The author writes 'Join our team' and quotes a post saying they are hiring someone to lead Claude Code comms at Anthropic. The role is not for someone who wants an old playbook. The candidate must be a Claude Code super user, understand developers and dev tools, and have great taste.

## Background

Comms means communications: how a product is explained to the public. The post shows that this role values hands-on product use as well as writing skill.

## What I took away

- Developer-tool roles often value deep product use.
- Technical communication is a career path next to engineering.
