## Background

Randomized linear algebra uses random sampling or sketching to approximate large matrix computations quickly. It is useful in machine learning where matrices are huge and exact answers are costly.

## What I took away

- Randomness can speed up large matrix computations with small error.
- Free lecture notes are a good source for the underlying theory.
