## Background

SGD is stochastic gradient descent, which updates weights using small random batches. Some theory views the noise in SGD as sampling from a distribution over solutions, similar to Bayesian inference. This is one theory among several about generalization.

## What I took away

- Noise in SGD may act as a form of regularization.
