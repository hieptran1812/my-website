## Summary

The author says the autonomous AI agent age is here, quoting that Base44 Superagent can run 24/7 and act across tools: sending emails, browsing, following up with leads, monitoring competitors and managing calendars. The quoted launch post says agents come with managed infrastructure, security by default and one-click integrations.

## Background

Unlike chatbots that wait for prompts, autonomous agents run on their own and use tools. Managed infrastructure means the vendor handles hosting and setup. This is promotional language from the product side.

## What I took away

- Autonomous agents need permissions limits and monitoring.
- Treat marketing claims as claims until tested.
