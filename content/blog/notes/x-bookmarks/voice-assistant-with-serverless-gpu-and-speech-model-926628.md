## Summary

The author shares an example for building a voice assistant. It uses serverless GPU compute from Modal and a speech-to-speech model from Liquid. A link points to a Liquid4All cookbook example called voice-chat on GitHub.

## Background

A speech-to-speech model takes spoken audio in and produces spoken audio out, often with lower delay than chaining separate speech recognition, text model and voice synthesis. Serverless GPU compute means you pay only while your code runs and do not manage servers. Together they make a voice app easier to prototype.

## What I took away

- Serverless GPUs reduce setup work for demos.
- Speech-to-speech models can simplify a voice pipeline.
- The linked cookbook is a ready starting point.
