## Summary

The author says that some lists prove none of the Instagram quants are actually quant. The post does not include the lists in its text; they are likely in attached media at the link.

## Background

Quant refers to quantitative finance roles that use math, statistics and programming for trading and risk. Many social media accounts use the label loosely. The post is a jab at that gap.

## What I took away

- Judge quant advice by evidence and track record, not by the label.
- Open the link to see what lists the author means.
- Real quant work is usually less flashy than social media suggests.
