## Summary

The author says his team at Delphi Digital released its Year Ahead reports, their most popular each year, as three deep dives on Macro, Apps and Infra. The team spends November and December focused on predicting the next year.

## Background

Year-ahead reports are forecasts from a research firm, not guarantees. They are useful for seeing which themes analysts consider important.

## What I took away

- Use outlook reports to learn themes, not to predict prices.
- Read the Macro, Apps and Infra sections for different angles.
