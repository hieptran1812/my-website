## Summary

The quoted post from LeCun says LLMs accumulate declarative knowledge, which is why they need many parameters and much memory, and that this can be replaced by understanding, which is what JEPA world models aim for. @Arjunjain says he was trained in Vapnik's school of kernels and bounds that saw neural nets as alchemy, but LeCun changed his mind at NYU, and now LeCun has brought a proof, as his old school always asked.

## Background

JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) learns by predicting representations of missing parts of the input rather than raw pixels or tokens. Vapnik's school is statistical learning theory, focused on formal guarantees. A proof here means a theoretical argument, not just experiments.

## What I took away

- World models aim to compress knowledge into understanding instead of memorization.
- Theory and empirical deep learning are converging in some areas.
- Read the actual proof before accepting broad claims about it.
