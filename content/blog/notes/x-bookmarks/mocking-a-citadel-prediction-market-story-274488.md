## Summary

The author says Olympic gold medalists in larping, mocking the quoted post. The quoted story claims a Citadel intern at a party said the firm has a four-factor model for prediction market contracts that enters when all align.

## Background

Larping means pretending. Posts with secret insider strategies are often invented to get attention.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of insider stories.
- Verify before copying a strategy.
- Educational only, not advice.
