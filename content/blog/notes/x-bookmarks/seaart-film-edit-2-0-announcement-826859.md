## Summary

SeaArt introduces SeaArt Film Edit 2.0. It lists multiple reference fusion, stable consistency, professional text rendering and enhanced realism. More details are in a follow-up thread.

## Background

Reference fusion means combining several input images, for example characters or styles, into one generated result. Consistency means the same subject looks the same across outputs, which matters for video and film work. This is a product announcement, so the claims are the vendor's own.

## What I took away

- Consistency across frames is a key problem for AI video tools.
- Treat product launch claims as marketing until you test them yourself.
