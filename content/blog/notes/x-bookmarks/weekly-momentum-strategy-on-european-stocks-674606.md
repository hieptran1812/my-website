## Summary

The author has researched systematic weekly rebalancing strategies on European single stocks. They say a simple approach works well: volatility-adjusted 12-month cross-sectional momentum on a restricted universe, with a trend filter to step aside in drawdowns, as a good diversifier.

## Background

Cross-sectional momentum buys recent winners and avoids losers relative to peers. Volatility adjustment scales returns by risk. A trend filter reduces exposure during market declines. Claims of working well are the author's.

## What I took away

- Momentum plus a trend filter is a classic design.
- Educational only, not advice.
