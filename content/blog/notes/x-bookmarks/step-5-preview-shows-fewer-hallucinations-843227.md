## Summary

The quoted post says an Artificial Analysis result suggests Step 5 Preview tends to have fewer hallucinations and more reliability. @sheriyuo responds that he is waiting for another step, a play on the model name.

## Background

Hallucination is when a model states false information confidently. Benchmarks that measure it help pick models for tasks where accuracy matters.

## What I took away
