## Summary

The author reminds people who got laid off or are searching that they are hiring Senior and Staff+ engineers at ClickHouse. They want people who have built distributed data infrastructure and know Kubernetes, and ask them to DM.

## Background

ClickHouse is a database for fast analytics. Distributed data infrastructure means systems spread across many machines. Kubernetes is a common platform for running them.

## What I took away

- Distributed systems and Kubernetes skills remain in demand.
- Direct outreach to hiring managers can be effective.
