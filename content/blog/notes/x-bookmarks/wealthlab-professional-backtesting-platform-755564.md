## Summary

The post introduces WealthLab as a professional backtesting and strategy development platform for systematic traders. The link card says it offers portfolio backtesting with real index rebalancing and walk-forward validation.

## Background

Backtesting platforms replay history to test a rule set. Walk-forward validation trains on one period and tests on the next, which reduces overfitting. Realistic index rebalancing avoids survivorship bias.

## What I took away

- Use walk-forward validation to reduce overfitting.
- Check that backtests handle index changes realistically.
