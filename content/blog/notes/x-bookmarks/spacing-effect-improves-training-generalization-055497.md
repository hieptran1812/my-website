## Summary

DAIR.AI says researchers took inspiration from the spacing effect in biological memory. They report that putting spaced intervals between training sessions significantly improves generalization in artificial systems. The post links to the paper.

## Background

The spacing effect is the finding that people remember better when study is spread over time instead of crammed. Applying this to machine learning means pausing between training phases. The result is the paper's claim and should be checked against the details.

## What I took away

- Ideas from human learning can transfer to machine training schedules.
- Read the paper for the actual setup before applying it.
