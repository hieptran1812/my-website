## Summary

NVIDIA says its CVPR 2026 paper **LocateAnything** is trending number one on Hugging Face. It is a vision-language detection model that rethinks bounding box prediction, aimed at agents and robots that need to quickly pinpoint where things are. The post text is cut off.

## Background

Detection models find objects and return bounding boxes. Vision-language versions take text prompts to find anything described. Speed matters for robots that act in real time.

## What I took away

- Open-vocabulary detection is key for robots and agents.
- Trending on Hugging Face signals interest, not quality.
- Read the paper for how bounding boxes are predicted.
