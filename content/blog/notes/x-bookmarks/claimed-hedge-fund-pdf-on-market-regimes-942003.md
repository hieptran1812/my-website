## Summary

The post claims hedge funds have a secret for detecting market regimes and that a 37 page PDF reveals everything. It is a promotional teaser with no details in the text.

## Background

A market regime is a period with a distinct behavior, such as high volatility or a trend. Methods such as hidden Markov models try to detect regimes. Claims of secrets are usually marketing.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of secret-method claims.
- Regime detection is a real topic, but no method is a guarantee.
- This is not trading advice.
