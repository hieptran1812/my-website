## Summary

The post shares lecture notes for MIT's Game Theory 2024 course (14.126) by Muhamet Yildiz and Alexander Wolitzky. The notes are on MIT OpenCourseWare.

## Background

Game theory studies how decision makers choose when outcomes depend on each other. It is used in economics, markets, auctions and multi-agent AI. This is a graduate-level course.

## What I took away

- MIT OpenCourseWare offers free graduate-level material.
- Game theory helps with markets, auctions and multi-agent systems.
- Expect a heavy math background.
