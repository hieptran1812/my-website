## Summary

@GaotangLi quotes the line Three ways to the unverifiable: Rubrics as Rewards, Generative Reward Models, Process Rewards, and says their paper instantiates the exact same idea.

## Background

Unverifiable tasks have no automatic correct answer, so rewards must come from rubrics, a judge model, or step-level scores. The three named approaches are common ways to provide that signal.

## What I took away

- Rubrics, generative judges, and process rewards all address tasks without a checker.
- Compare the approaches on your task.
- See arXiv 2605.10899.
