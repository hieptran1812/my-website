## Summary

@elonmusk replies Recursive Self-Improvement to a quoted story. The quoted post says a retired NASA engineer who worked on the Space Shuttle for 25 years was hired by SpaceX at 62 and called his first week the most disorienting of his career, because at NASA a design change needed 3 meetings, 2 committees and a review board.

## Background

Recursive self-improvement usually refers to an AI improving itself; here it is used loosely for an organization that improves itself quickly. The story contrasts process-heavy and fast-iteration engineering cultures.

## What I took away

- Heavy review processes trade speed for caution; both have costs.
- Stories like this are anecdotes, not evidence about which approach is safer.
