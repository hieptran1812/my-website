## Summary

Raschka says he does not think it is deeply unprofitable. It hurts an individual who pays for API use, but for a company at scale it is peanuts. He says it would only be deeply unprofitable if every user got free API use, which he doubts they would do.

## Background

This replies to a claim that OpenClaw, an open agent tool, loses money. Raschka's point is that cost depends on who pays and at what scale. A fixed subscription or limited usage keeps losses bounded.

## What I took away

- Profit depends on scale and who pays, not on raw usage.
- Look at the pricing model before judging a product.
