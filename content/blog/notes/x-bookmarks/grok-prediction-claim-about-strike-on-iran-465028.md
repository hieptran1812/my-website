## Summary

Elon Musk writes that predicting the future is the best measure of intelligence. He quotes a post claiming Grok accurately predicted the date of a US and Israel strike on Iran, saying it was not a lucky guess. These are claims from the posts, not independently checked.

## Background

A single correct prediction is weak evidence of forecasting skill. Judging a predictor needs many predictions, stated probabilities and comparison with a baseline.

## What I took away

- One hit does not prove forecasting ability.
- Ask for the prediction record and timestamps before trusting such claims.
