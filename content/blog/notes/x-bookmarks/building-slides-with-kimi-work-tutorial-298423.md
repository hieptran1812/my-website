## Summary

Moonshot AI shares the first tutorial for building slides with Kimi Work. It says Kimi Slides handles the whole process: clear structure and research powered by **Kimi K3**, cohesive design with charts and SmartArts, and editable output ready to download.

## Background

AI slide tools combine research, outlining and layout generation into one flow. SmartArt is the diagram feature in Microsoft Office, so editable output means you can refine the deck in standard office tools.

## What I took away

- Editable output matters more than polish for real work decks.
- Check AI-researched facts in slides before presenting them.
- Use such tools for first drafts, then refine by hand.
