## Summary

The post mocks the advice "don't hire for credentials" by noting the person it is about is a 17 year old MIT dropout who did IOI and interned at Jane Street and Facebook AI Research.

## Background

IOI is the International Olympiad in Informatics. The point is that skipping a degree can still come with very strong achievements, so it may not be a typical case.

## What I took away

- Success stories of dropouts often hide strong signals.
- Evidence of ability matters more than the label.
