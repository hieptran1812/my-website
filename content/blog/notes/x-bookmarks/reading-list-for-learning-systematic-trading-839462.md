## Summary

The author lists what they consider the best long-form introductory trading materials. These include the TLAQ and RW-Pro courses from Robot Wealth, articles by @systematicls, articles by @0xfdf (now removed), and books by @__paleologo and @AgustinLebron3. The card describes sysls as someone who worked at large hedge funds running systematic processes.

## Background

Systematic trading means rules-based strategies that are tested on data instead of decided by gut feeling. The list is a personal opinion about learning resources. It is educational material and not advice to trade.

## What I took away

- A mix of courses, articles and books gives different angles on quant trading.
- Some listed material has been removed, so availability varies.
- This is a reading list, not a recommendation to trade.
