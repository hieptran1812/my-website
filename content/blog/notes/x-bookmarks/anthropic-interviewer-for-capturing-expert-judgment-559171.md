## Summary

The author says your best people cannot document their expertise because they do not know what they know until asked. They describe an interviewer built to reach peer status so experts share judgment patterns they would only tell a colleague, and they wrote a blog on its architecture. The quoted post announces **Anthropic Interviewer**, a tool for understanding people's views on AI, available at claude.ai/interviewer as a week-long pilot.

## Background

Tacit knowledge is know-how people use without being able to state it. Interview-style questions can pull it out. An AI that asks good follow-up questions could make that capture cheaper.

## What I took away

- Experts often reveal knowledge only in response to good questions.
- Capture knowledge through conversation instead of asking for documents.
- The blog on the interviewer's architecture is worth reading for design ideas.
