## Summary

@vr4300 claims that applying category theory is the way to advance state-of-the-art artificial intelligence research. The post gives no evidence or examples.

## Background

Category theory is a branch of math that studies structure and the relationships between things, such as how objects map to each other. Some researchers use it to describe how neural network components compose. Whether it will drive AI progress is debated, so this is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Treat this as a strong opinion, not an established result.
- If curious, look for concrete papers that apply category theory to machine learning before judging it.
