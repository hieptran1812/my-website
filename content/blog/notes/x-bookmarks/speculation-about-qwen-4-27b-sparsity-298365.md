## Summary

The author says if Qwen 4 27B is sparse, functional versions will run on 8GB VRAM or mobile quickly. The quoted posts speculate that Qoduck is Qwen4-27B, estimating about 35GB in Q8, 480 tps and an extreme yapper. Another claims Qwen-4-Flash results are shockingly good.

## Background

Sparse models activate only part of their weights, which lets offloading fit them on small devices. Q8 means 8-bit quantization. All of this is rumor and speculation.

## What I took away

- Sparsity plus quantization drives local deployment.
- These are unverified rumors.
- Wait for official releases.
