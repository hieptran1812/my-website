## Summary

The author says they made +$1.23m in 2025, with $215k in the first half and $1.02m in the second half. They call it their second consecutive seven-figure year. They say they always believed they could be consistently profitable but never imagined this result.

## Background

This is a self-reported result with no audit or details on risk. Trading results are often uneven, as the split between the two halves shows. Posts like this show survivorship bias, because people who lose rarely post.

## What I took away

- Self-reported returns cannot be verified from a post.
- The uneven halves show how variable trading results can be.
- Do not treat a single success story as a method to copy.
