## Summary

The author recommends the ETH zurich route for AI research: train models that use a single GPU and finish in under a minute. He names PufferLib as a good example and says the more models you train, the more you learn. The quote is a Japanese post about a model that finished training in one minute.

## Background

Fast experiments give quick feedback, so you can try many ideas. PufferLib is a fast reinforcement learning library. Small scale ideas often transfer partly to larger scale.

## What I took away

- Keep experiments under a minute to iterate often.
- Single-GPU setups lower the barrier to research.
- Number of experiments run is a key driver of learning.
