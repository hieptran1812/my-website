## Summary

Aakash Gupta says Karpathy built the networks behind coding assistants, taught deep learning and ran AI at Tesla, so his feeling of being dramatically behind is telling. The quoted post by Karpathy says the profession is being dramatically refactored, since the code a programmer writes is increasingly sparse. He feels he could be 10X more powerful if he could string together the new tools properly.

## Background

Karpathy means that AI coding tools change what a programmer does, and that learning to combine agents, prompts and tools well takes effort. The 10X figure is his own estimate.

## What I took away

- Even top experts feel the pace of change in AI coding.
- Learning to orchestrate AI tools is now a skill in itself.
