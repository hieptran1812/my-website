## Summary

@yunta_tsai says people think ML projects are 99% training but in reality they are 50% evaluation, 40% data cleaning, 8% integration and 2% training. The first two set the noise floor and the model cannot lower it. The text is cut off.

## Background

The noise floor is the error caused by bad labels or messy data. A better model cannot beat that limit. The percentages are a rough rule of thumb.

## What I took away

- Invest in evaluation and data quality first.
- The split is an estimate, not data.
