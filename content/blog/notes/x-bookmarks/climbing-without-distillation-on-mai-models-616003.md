## Summary

The author says climbing with no distillation, like the big boys do, was fun, and invites readers to the tech report. The quoted post announces seven new MAI models, starting with MAI-Thinking-1, strong on reasoning and SWE tasks.

## Background

Distillation trains a model on a stronger model's outputs. Avoiding it means the model's skills come from its own pretraining and RL.

## What I took away

- Training without distillation is presented as a point of pride.
- Read the tech report for the journey.
