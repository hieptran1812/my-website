## Summary

Allium Labs describes an agentic copy trader that scans Solana for high net PnL smart wallets, filters for human-like behavior and syncs your portfolio to match them, in a fully autonomous loop.

## Background

Copy trading mirrors another wallet's trades. Past profit does not guarantee future results and copying adds delay and risk. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Past wallet profits are weak signals.
- Automation can magnify losses.
