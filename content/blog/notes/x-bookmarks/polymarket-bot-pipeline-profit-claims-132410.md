## Summary

The author says PICO has become a fully autonomous pipeline and claims they can build a bot and make up to $10k a month. The quoted post describes a solo dev building a Polymarket trading bot for small bankrolls using GPT-5-nano in AutoResearchClaw, Qwen 2.5 in MiroFish, and Opus 4.6 plus Codex.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on event outcomes. Automated bots can lose money as easily as they can make it. Income claims like this are unverified and often promotional.

## What I took away

- Treat profit claims as marketing until verified with audited results.
- Small automated trading setups carry real risk of loss.
- This is educational context, not advice.
