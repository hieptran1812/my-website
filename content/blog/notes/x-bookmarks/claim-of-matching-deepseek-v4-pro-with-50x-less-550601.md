## Summary

The quoted post says frontier pretraining is seen as a big-lab game, so their only path is algorithmic efficiency. They claim their recipe matches DeepSeek V4 Pro's pretrain with 50x less compute, about half the FLOPs of GPT-3 or around $0.5M on GB200. The author replies only with what, signaling surprise.

## Background

Algorithmic efficiency means better data, architecture or optimizers that reach the same quality with less compute. Matching a frontier model at a fraction of the cost would be a big deal, so such claims need careful checking of how matches is measured.

## What I took away

- Treat large efficiency claims as the authors' until independently checked.
- Ask which benchmarks define matching a model.
- Efficiency gains are the main path for teams without huge clusters.
