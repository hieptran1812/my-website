## Summary

@tom_doerr links a GitHub repository that simulates trading strategies with real market data. The post gives no further details.

## Background

Paper trading means testing strategies without real money. Using real data gives realistic results but does not model all costs like slippage. It is a safe way to practice.

## What I took away

- Test strategies on paper before real money.
- Check whether costs are modeled.
- See the repo to judge quality.
