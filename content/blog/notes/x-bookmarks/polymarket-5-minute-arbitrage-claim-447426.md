## Summary

The author says a Clawdbot setup exploits brief glitches in 5-minute BTC and ETH contracts where Yes plus No costs under $1, buying both. It claims 8,894 trades and about $150K earned without human input.

## Background

In a binary market, Yes and No should add to $1. If they add to less, buying both locks a payoff of $1, called arbitrage. Such gaps are usually tiny and fast, and the numbers here are the author's claim.

## What I took away

- Arbitrage needs speed, fees and fill risk to be checked.
- Treat unverified profit totals with caution; this is not advice.
