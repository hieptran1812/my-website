## Summary

The author announces a Thursday livestream with Alex Zhang on the latest RLM results, covering compositional generalization, why it is worth fostering and what current harnesses lack. The quoted post says transformers struggle with tasks they were not trained on and proposes that the harness should generalize through composition, noting a strong property when training RLMs on tasks with shared structure.

## Background

Compositional generalization means solving new problems by combining known skills in new ways. RLMs (recursive language models) let a model break tasks into parts and call itself on them, so the harness structure can supply the composition the model lacks.

## What I took away

- Harness structure can help models generalize beyond their training.
- Tasks with shared structure benefit most from compositional approaches.
