## Summary

The post shares a MachineLearningMastery article titled 'The Journey of a Token: What Really Happens Inside a Transformer'. The post has no further commentary.

## Background

A token is a small piece of text, like a word or word part, that a language model reads. Inside a transformer it is turned into a vector, mixed with other tokens by attention, and passed through layers to predict the next token. Following one token end to end is a good way to learn the architecture.

## What I took away

- Tracing a single token is a clear way to learn how transformers work.
- Good to read before deeper topics like attention variants or KV caching.
