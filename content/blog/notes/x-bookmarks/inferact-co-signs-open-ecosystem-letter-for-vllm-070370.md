## Summary

vLLM says that on behalf of its community, **Inferact** signed the open letter and calls for building an open ecosystem. The quoted Inferact post says it was founded to grow vLLM into the leading open source inference engine. Its goal is making open-weight AI faster, cheaper and accessible across hardware and clouds.

## Background

This refers to an open letter shared by NVIDIA's CEO about why open models matter. Inferact is a company built around vLLM. Inference engines are key to making open-weight models practical to deploy.

## What I took away

- Open inference engines are central to the open model ecosystem.
- Industry letters signal alignment but do not change code by themselves.
- Track which companies back open models with actual releases.
