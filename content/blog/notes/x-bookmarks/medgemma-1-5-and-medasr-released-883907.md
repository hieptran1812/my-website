## Summary

Google Research announces MedGemma 1.5, small enough to run offline and better on 3D imaging such as CT and MRI, and MedASR, a speech-to-text model for medical dictation. Both are on Hugging Face and Vertex AI.

## Background

Open medical models let developers build healthcare tools locally, which helps with privacy. They still need careful validation before clinical use.

## What I took away

- Small open models can run offline.
- Medical use needs validation.
