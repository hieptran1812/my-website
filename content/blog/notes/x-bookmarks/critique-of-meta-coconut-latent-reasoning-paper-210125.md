## Summary

The author says Meta published a paper claiming LLMs can think without words and reason in latent space instead of English. After reading all 18 pages and the appendix, they say the results section tells a very different story than the abstract, and promise to explain what the Coconut hype leaves out.

## Background

Latent reasoning means a model passes hidden vectors between steps instead of writing out chain-of-thought text. It could be faster but harder to inspect. Abstracts often highlight best cases.

## What I took away

- Read results sections, not just abstracts.
- Latent reasoning trades interpretability for possible efficiency.
- Look for the thread details before judging.
