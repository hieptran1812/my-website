## Summary

The author shares the **LLM Inference Handbook** from Modular as another good resource. The quoted post, by the same author, shares Google DeepMind's book How to Scale Your Model and notes that inference on constrained systems such as cars is an ongoing challenge that needs this kind of understanding.

## Background

These are free online references. The scaling book explains how to reason about compute, memory and communication when training and serving large models on accelerators. The inference handbook focuses on serving models efficiently.

## What I took away

- Bookmark both as reference texts for inference and scaling work.
- Constrained devices make first-principles performance reasoning more important.
