## Summary

The author says Claude built them a market making engine after reading an article, and claims it collected $1,429 in spread in the first 24 hours. The post says it does not care who wins and only needs the gap between bid and ask. The text is cut off.

## Background

Market making means posting both buy and sell prices and earning the difference (the spread). Real risks include price moves against your inventory and fees. A one-day profit figure says little about long-run results.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of short-window profit claims, especially without details.
- Market making has inventory and adverse-selection risk.
- This is educational context only, not advice.
