## Summary

The post says we are in the era of local AI orchestration. Gemma 4 evaluates a scene and calls a segmentation model, finding 64 vehicles, then 23 white ones. It all runs offline on a laptop.

## Background

Segmentation means marking which pixels belong to which object. Here a language model decides which tool to call and with what request. This pattern is called tool orchestration.

## What I took away

- Small local models can drive vision tools.
- Chain follow-up requests with a language model.
- Offline means private and cheap.
