## Summary

@TeksEdge calls Nex-N2-Pro the new king of open source and lists win rates over Kimi-K2.6, MiniMax, GLM-5.1 and DeepSeek-V4-Pro. He says to test to verify. The quote introduces Nex-N2 as an agentic family with adaptive thinking.

## Background

Small test counts, such as 8 or 13 tasks, give noisy results. The author suggests verification.

## What I took away

- Look at sample sizes.
- Verify before trusting leaderboards.
