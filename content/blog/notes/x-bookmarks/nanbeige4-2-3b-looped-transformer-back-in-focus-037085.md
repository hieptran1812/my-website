## Summary

The quoted Nanbeige post says GPT-6 Astra and an article by @rasbt brought attention back to **Nanbeige4.2-3B**. It is built on a looped transformer, and they claim it consistently beats larger models like Qwen3.5-9B and Gemma4-12B on key benchmarks. The author says Nanbeige is really good and hopes for stronger open looped models.

## Background

A looped transformer runs the same layers multiple times per token, adding depth and compute without adding parameters. This can make small models punch above their size, at the cost of more compute per token.

## What I took away

- Looped models trade extra compute for fewer parameters.
- Compare models at equal compute, not just equal size.
- Watch for more open looped models.
