## Summary

The author quotes the claim best open-weight LLM by a US company and says it is actually a Chinese LLM fine-tuned by a US company. The link goes to the original announcement by @drishanarora.

## Background

Open-weight means the model weights are public. Many models are built by fine-tuning another group's base model, so where a model comes from can be unclear. Marketing claims about origin may leave out the base model.

## What I took away

- Check the base model behind any fine-tuned release.
- Origin claims in launch posts can be misleading.
- Fine-tuned does not mean trained from scratch.
