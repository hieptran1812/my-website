## Summary

Opennote announces Papers, an AI-native academic writing suite. Its assistant Feynman is said to help from finding references to peer review without leaving the editor.

## Background

Academic writing involves finding citations, drafting and review. An AI-integrated editor aims to put these steps in one place. Output should still be checked, especially references.

## What I took away

- Check every AI-suggested citation yourself.
- Integrated tools reduce context switching.
- Evaluate it against your own workflow.
