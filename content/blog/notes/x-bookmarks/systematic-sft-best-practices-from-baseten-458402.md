## Summary

@amiruci says post-training is still more art than science and calls the author an experienced artist. The quoted post says Baseten fine-tunes many customer models and varied one SFT lever at a time across 2 model families, dense and MoE up to 235B, on 4 real-world customer tasks.

## Background

SFT is supervised fine-tuning on examples. Changing one variable at a time shows which choices matter. The study uses customer-like tasks, so its findings are practical.

## What I took away

- Test fine-tuning choices one at a time.
- Read the study for practical defaults.
