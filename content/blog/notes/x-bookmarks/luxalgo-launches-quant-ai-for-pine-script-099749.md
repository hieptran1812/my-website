## Summary

LuxAlgo announces Quant, which it calls the first AI to build indicators and strategies like a TradingView wizard. It uses five years of LuxAlgo development and says it builds, reverse engineers and auto-debugs Pine Script indicators. The post is cut off.

## Background

Pine Script is TradingView's scripting language. An AI assistant can draft code, but strategies still need honest backtesting.

## What I took away

- AI can speed up indicator coding.
- First of its kind is a marketing claim.
