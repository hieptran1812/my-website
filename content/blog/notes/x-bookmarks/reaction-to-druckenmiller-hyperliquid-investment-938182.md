## Summary

@elonmusk replies with one word, interesting, to Stanley Druckenmiller's post. Druckenmiller says he is an investor in trade(xyz), which is partnering with ICE to bring 24/7 perpetual futures on Hyperliquid to the US.

## Background

Perpetual futures are derivatives without an expiry date, common in crypto. Bringing them to US users via a partnership with ICE, a major exchange operator, is notable regulatory and market news.

## What I took away

- Celebrity reactions add attention, not information.
- This is news, not investment advice.
