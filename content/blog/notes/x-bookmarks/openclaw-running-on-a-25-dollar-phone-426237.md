## Summary

The author says ClawPhone is alive. They installed OpenClaw on a $25 phone with full hardware access and say it is a cool form factor for agents, though they have not explored everything yet.

## Background

Phones come with sensors, cameras, a microphone and a modem. Giving an agent full access to them is powerful but risky for privacy and security.

## What I took away

- Cheap phones can be dedicated agent devices.
- Full hardware access needs careful limits.
- Use a separate device and accounts for experiments.
