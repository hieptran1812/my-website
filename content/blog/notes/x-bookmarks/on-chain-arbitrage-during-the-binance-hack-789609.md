## Summary

@0xNaiXi says that during the hour Binance was hacked, a textbook arbitrage window appeared on-chain and someone made $3.47 million in one hour. He scraped on-chain data for a recap, starting with the hot wallet being hacked around 4:24 AM Beijing time on October 30. The rest is in the thread.

## Background

Arbitrage means profiting from price differences for the same asset across markets. During a crisis, prices can diverge briefly between exchanges and on-chain markets. Hot wallets are exchange wallets kept online for fast withdrawals.

## What I took away

- Market dislocations create brief price gaps; studying them is educational.
- On-chain data allows anyone to reconstruct events after the fact.
- This is a recap, not a strategy to copy.
