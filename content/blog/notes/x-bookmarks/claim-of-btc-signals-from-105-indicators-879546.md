## Summary

The author says they backtested 10 years of Bitcoin data against 105 financial indicators. They claim only a handful of signals predict BTC 4 to 6 weeks out and promise to list them.

## Background

Testing 105 indicators on one price history invites false positives, since some will look predictive by chance. This is called multiple-comparison bias or data snooping. The result is the author's claim and not verified here.

## What I took away

- Testing many indicators finds spurious winners by chance.
- Out-of-sample testing is needed before trusting a signal.
- This is not trading advice.
