## Summary

The author urges trying Grok Voice for customer support. The quoted post says Grok Voice Think Fast 1.0 handles complex workflows with speed and accuracy, even in hard-to-hear environments, from multi-step troubleshooting to high-volume tool calls.

## Background

Voice agents answer calls and can use tools like order lookups. Noisy audio and long workflows are hard for them. These are vendor claims.

## What I took away

- Test voice agents in noisy, multi-step cases.
- Treat vendor performance claims as unverified.
