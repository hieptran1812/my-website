## Summary

The author calls the Kimi K2.5 tech report beautiful. The quoted post lists joint text-vision training with 15T tokens, zero-vision SFT to activate visual reasoning, and Agent Swarm with PARL for parallel sub-agents, up to 4.5x lower latency.

## Background

SFT is supervised fine-tuning. PARL is the reinforcement method named for orchestrating sub-agents. The numbers are from the tech report.

## What I took away

- Read the report for training recipes.
- Parallel sub-agents cut latency.
- Text-only SFT can activate vision.
