## Summary

ZachXBT graphed the 48.5% SIREN cluster on BSC and says the addresses link to several obscure DWF-affiliated tokens, such as LADYS, RACA and TOMO.

## Background

Onchain analysis follows wallet links to find concentration and insiders. High supply concentration is a risk for price manipulation.

## What I took away

- Check supply concentration before trusting a token.
- Onchain links are evidence, not proof.
- Beware pump patterns.
