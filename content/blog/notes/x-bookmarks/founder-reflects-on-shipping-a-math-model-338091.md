## Summary

The author says it is the first day he has had time to breathe since joining Y Combinator. He recalls being told in August to drop everything and move to SF, and three months later releasing a SOTA math model together with Nous. He feels lucky to be doing his life's work.

## Background

SOTA means state of the art, the best known result on benchmarks. The post gives no model name or numbers, so the claim is the author's own. It shows how fast a small startup can ship a model.

## What I took away

- Small teams can ship competitive models in about three months.
- The post gives no benchmarks, so verify the claim elsewhere.
