## Summary

@oliviscusAI says you can parse any document with one 1.7B parameter model called dots-ocr. It claims one system handles text, tables, formulas, images and PDFs in 100+ languages. It says there is no separate OCR pipeline or task-specific models.

## Background

OCR turns images of text into text. Traditional pipelines chain separate steps for layout, tables and formulas. A single vision-language model simplifies that. Test it on your own documents.

## What I took away

- One model replaces a multi-step pipeline.
- Small models can be practical for local use.
- Test on your own documents before trusting claims.
