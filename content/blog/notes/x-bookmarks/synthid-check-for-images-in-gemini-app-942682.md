## Summary

With the launch of Nano Banana Pro, Gemini users can upload an image into the Gemini app and ask whether it was generated by or edited with Google AI. The check uses SynthID, Google's digital watermarking technology.

## Background

SynthID embeds an invisible watermark in AI-generated content that can be detected later. It only detects Google's own watermark, so it cannot flag images from other tools. It is one tool among others for provenance.

## What I took away

- SynthID only detects Google AI images, not all AI images.
- A missing watermark does not prove an image is real.
- Provenance tools are still partial.
