## Summary

The author refers back to their earlier post on the paper "LLMs Can't Jump," which argued LLMs lack the abductive leaps needed for breakthroughs like relativity. They say a new idea might change your mind: the missing piece is not more text tokens but a shift to Joint (the text is cut off, likely a joint embedding approach). The quoted post is the earlier summary of the DeepMind paper by Tom Zahavy.

## Background

Joint embedding approaches, such as JEPA-style models, learn by predicting abstract representations rather than the next word. Some researchers think this kind of learning could support the world models needed for creative reasoning.

## What I took away

- The debate on AI creativity is open; hold arguments on both sides loosely.
- Scaling text alone may not be the only path to new kinds of reasoning.
