## Summary

The author is hiring research interns in Singapore with visa sponsorship available. Candidates should have hands-on research experience in agentic systems, coding, environment simulation or post-training. The minimum internship length is six months.

## Background

Environment construction means building simulated tasks where an AI agent can act and receive feedback, which is needed for training agents with reinforcement learning. These skills are in demand as labs focus on agent post-training.

## What I took away

- Hands-on research in agents or RL environments is a sought-after skill.
- Check details such as visa terms and duration directly with the poster.
