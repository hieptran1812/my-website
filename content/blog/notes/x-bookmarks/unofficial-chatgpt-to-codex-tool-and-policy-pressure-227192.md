## Summary

The quoted post promotes an unofficial GitHub tool that claims to turn ChatGPT into unlimited Codex use. The author says the tragedy of the commons will push OpenAI's policy closer to Anthropic's every day.

## Background

The tragedy of the commons is when individuals overuse a shared resource until it is restricted for everyone. Workarounds that abuse flat-rate plans often lead providers to tighten usage limits.

## What I took away

- Using unofficial tools to bypass limits can break terms of service.
- Abuse of generous plans tends to make them stricter for all users.
