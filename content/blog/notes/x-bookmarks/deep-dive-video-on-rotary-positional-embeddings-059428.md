## Summary

The author notes that a quoted post got many views and asks whether people want more such deep dives. The quoted post is a YouTube "brain dump" explaining Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) in transformers.

## Background

Transformers need to know token order. RoPE does this by rotating the query and key vectors by an angle that depends on position. It is used in many modern LLMs.

## What I took away

- RoPE is a core piece of modern transformer design.
- Video explainers can be a quick way in.
- Feedback on views shows demand for deep dives.
