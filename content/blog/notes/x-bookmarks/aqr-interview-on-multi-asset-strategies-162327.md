## Summary

The author calls it a great interview with AQR's Jordan Brooks and links to the AQR Insights page. The topic, from the link title, is multi-asset strategies and asset allocation.

## Background

Multi-asset investing spreads capital across asset classes such as stocks, bonds and commodities. AQR is a quantitative asset manager that publishes research on these methods.
