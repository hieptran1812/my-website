## Summary

LG AI Research introduces EXAONE 4.5, its first open-weight Vision Language Model. It combines text and visual understanding for real-world challenges and is described as a step toward Physical Intelligence.

## Background

Open-weight means the model files can be downloaded. A vision language model reads images and text together.

## What I took away

- A new open VLM is available.
- Check blog and benchmarks.
- Open weights allow local use.
