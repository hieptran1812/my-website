## Summary

The author claims remote work is cooked, since AI can do any job done through a screen better. The quoted post introduces Griffin, described as the first model to pass a video Turing test: 48% of people who talked to it live thought it was human, versus under 3% for previous systems, and it ranks first on an NVIDIA full-duplex video benchmark.

## Background

A Turing test checks whether people can tell a machine from a human. Full-duplex means the model can listen and speak at the same time, like a real conversation.

## What I took away

- Seeming human in a short chat is not the same as doing a full job.
- Realistic video avatars raise impersonation and fraud risks.
