## Summary

The author introduces Economy of Minds, where LLM agents with different capabilities learn to cooperate through economic transactions, rewarding each other for solving tasks together. The quoted post describes a population of agents that each have limits such as partial tools, partial observations and finite context.

## Background

Multi-agent systems usually rely on a fixed orchestration design. Here the agents use a market-like exchange to decide who helps whom, which could let the structure adapt on its own.

## What I took away

- Market-style incentives are one way to coordinate agents.
- Read the paper for results and limits.
