## Summary

The author says Anthropic's data team automated 95% of business analytics queries with Claude. The blog covers evals, ablations and online validation. The quoted post covers best practices for skills, data foundations and evaluations when building data analysis agents.

## Background

Data analysis agents write queries and interpret results. Evals compare outputs against known answers, and ablations remove parts to see what matters.

## What I took away

- Good data foundations and evals matter as much as the model.
- The 95% figure is Anthropic's own claim.
