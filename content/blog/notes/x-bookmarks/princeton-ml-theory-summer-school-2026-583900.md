The post announces mini-courses at the 2026 Princeton ML Theory Summer School, taught by Subhabrata Sen, Lenaic Chizat, Sinho Chewi, Elliot Paquette, Elad Hazan and Surya Ganguli. It runs August 3 to 14, 2026, and applications are due by March 31.
