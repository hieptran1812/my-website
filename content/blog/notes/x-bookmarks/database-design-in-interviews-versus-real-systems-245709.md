## Summary

@VKazulkin shares an article by Himanshu Singour titled How We Actually Design Databases in Interviews vs. When Building Real Systems.

## Background

Interview designs are simplified and clean, while real systems face messy constraints, migrations and changing needs. Knowing the gap helps in both settings.

## What I took away

- Interview answers are not production designs.
- Real systems evolve with changing requirements.
- Open the article for the specific differences.
