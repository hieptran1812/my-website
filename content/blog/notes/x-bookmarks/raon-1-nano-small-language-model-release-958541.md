## Summary

The author announces **Raon-1 nano**, their first small language model trained from scratch, released under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face. They describe it as an E1.5B model with state-of-the-art-level accuracy on instruction following, context following and several tool-use benchmarks.

## Background

A small language model (SLM) is a compact LLM that can run cheaply on modest hardware. Training from scratch means it was not fine-tuned from another model. Apache 2.0 allows commercial use. The accuracy claims are the author's own.

## What I took away

- Small models trained from scratch with permissive licenses are useful for local use.
- Check benchmark claims against your own tasks.
- Instruction and tool-use ability matter most for small agent models.
