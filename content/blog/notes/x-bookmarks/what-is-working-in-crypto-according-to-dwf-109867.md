## Summary

The author says the only things working in crypto are tradefi listings, market activations and prediction markets, which drive volume and engage traders. They add that the best deals are done when blood is on the streets, but it is hard because people do not join.

## Background

Tradefi listings put traditional financial assets on crypto venues, and prediction markets let people trade on event outcomes. This is one market participant's opinion, not advice.

## What I took away

- This is an opinion from a market maker, not a recommendation.
- Be skeptical of broad claims about what works in markets.
