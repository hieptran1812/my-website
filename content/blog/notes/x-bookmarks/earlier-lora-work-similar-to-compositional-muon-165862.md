## Summary

The author read the Compositional Muon research and says it is nice. They note they did something similar on LoRA factors long ago and link an arXiv paper. The quoted post introduces Compositional Muon, which treats composed attention circuits like QK^T and OV together.

## Background

LoRA adds small low-rank matrices to a model for cheap fine-tuning. Each adapter is a product of two factors, so the same idea of optimizing composed matrices applies there.

## What I took away

- Optimizing products of matrices as a unit appears in more than one setting.
- Check the linked paper for the LoRA version.
