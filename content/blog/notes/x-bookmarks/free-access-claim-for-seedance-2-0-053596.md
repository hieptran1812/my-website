## Summary

The post claims Seedance 2.0 is free, calling it a China model that produces realistic 1080P video, with no credit card or sign up needed. It then teases how to access it. The wording is promotional.

## Background

Posts like this often drive traffic to third-party sites. Free no-signup claims should be checked, since links may be unofficial or unsafe.

## What I took away

- Verify claims of free access on official sources.
- Be careful with links that ask for no sign up but promise a lot.
