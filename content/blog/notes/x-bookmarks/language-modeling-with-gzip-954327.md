## Summary

@snwy_me calls the post incredible. The quoted post says any compression tool can be contorted into language modeling, and gzip can generate text that somewhat resembles Shakespeare, with a short write-up linked.

## Background

Compression and prediction are linked: a good compressor predicts well. Using the compressor's cost to sample next tokens gives a crude language model.

## What I took away

- Compression equals prediction.
- A fun way to explain language modeling.
