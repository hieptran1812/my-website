## Summary

Nicholas Chen says he tried this material a year ago and got lost quickly. After working in two production codebases, the content feels familiar and interesting, and he recommends it. The quoted post announces an open-source system design course on GitHub.

## Background

System design covers how to build services that scale and stay reliable, including topics like distributed systems and architecture. Such material is easier to absorb once you have seen real systems.

## What I took away

- Revisit hard material after getting more experience.
- Practical exposure makes theory stick.
