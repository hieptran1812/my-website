## Summary

The quoted post says you can try Gemma at about 2,000 to 4,000 tok/s using an inference engine produced by an AI swarm, with no speedup for video. The author adds that the swarm worked for one hour and produced an engine running at about 1500 tok/s on a single B200.

## Background

An AI swarm here means many coding agents working together to write software. An inference engine is the program that runs a model efficiently on hardware. Tokens per second measures generation speed. These are the author's claims about agent-written, model-specific engines.

## What I took away

- Agent-generated, model-specific engines are an emerging trend worth watching.
- Treat speed claims carefully and look for the exact workload and hardware.
- Note the limits stated, such as no speedup for video.
