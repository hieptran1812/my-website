## Summary

The author says Hollywood is terrified of AI because of a new AI Relight tool on Higgsfield. It can simulate a film studio and change the source light direction, colour, exposure and softness. They claim AI is taking over film studios and show how to use it.

## Background

Relighting changes how a scene is lit after it has been shot. Traditionally this needs studio setups or heavy post-production. The claim about Hollywood is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- AI tools can change lighting in already-captured footage.
- Treat the sweeping claims about studios as opinion.
- Try the tool on a small clip to judge real quality.
