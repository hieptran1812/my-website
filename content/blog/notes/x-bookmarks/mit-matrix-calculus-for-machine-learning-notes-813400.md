## Summary

The post shares MIT's Matrix Calculus for Machine Learning and Beyond (January IAP 2023) lecture notes. A YouTube playlist of the videos is also linked.

## Background

Matrix calculus extends derivatives to vectors and matrices, which is how gradients are computed in neural networks. It helps you understand backpropagation beyond formulas. The course is free.

## What I took away

- Matrix calculus underlies gradient-based training.
- Free notes and videos are available.
- Work through examples by hand.
