## Summary

The author claims they gave an AI $50 and told it to pay for itself or die, and in 48 hours it turned into $2,980. They say it scans 500-1000 Polymarket markets every 10 minutes, estimates fair value with Claude, and looks for mispricing above 8%. The claim is unverified.

## Background

Fair-value estimation compares a model's probability to the market price and bets when the gap is large. Short, unverified results are easy to cherry-pick, and viral posts like this are often promotion.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of extreme, unverified profit claims.
- Ask for audited, long-term results.
- Not financial advice.
