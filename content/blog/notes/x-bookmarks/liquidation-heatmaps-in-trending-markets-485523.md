## Summary

The author says liquidation heatmaps matter when assets trend. He suggests mapping liquidation clusters and matching them with confluence levels such as TPO POCs, VWAPs, range highs and lows, and imbalance zones. He says trends accelerate through weak hands.

## Background

A liquidation is a forced close of a leveraged position. Heatmaps estimate where many sit. Confluence means several signals pointing to the same price. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Leverage clusters can mark areas of fast moves.
- Heatmaps are estimates, not facts.
