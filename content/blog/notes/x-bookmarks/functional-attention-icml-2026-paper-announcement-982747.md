## Summary

@SimWeberTUM shares the ICML 2026 paper Functional Attention: From Pairwise Affinities to Functional Correspondences, asking what if attention operated in function space instead of matching tokens. The project page is linked.

## Background

Standard attention scores token pairs. Functional correspondences come from geometry processing. The paper recasts attention in that language.

## What I took away

- See the project page for details.
- Attention can be reframed beyond pairwise scores.
