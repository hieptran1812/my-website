## Summary

The quoted post announces GPT-5.6, with Sol as the flagship and Terra and Luna as cheaper options, good at coding, knowledge work, cybersecurity and science with fewer tokens and lower cost. The author highlights a line saying that during internal testing, average daily output tokens per active researcher were more than twice the highest level seen for GPT-5.5.

## Background

Output tokens per user is a rough proxy for how much people rely on a model. A jump in usage suggests the model was useful enough that researchers used it much more.

## What I took away

- Usage metrics can signal real adoption beyond benchmarks.
- These numbers come from the vendor; treat them as their claim.
- Model families with tiers let you match cost to task difficulty.
