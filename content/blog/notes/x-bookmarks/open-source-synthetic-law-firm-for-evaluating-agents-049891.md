## Summary

Harvey announces it is open-sourcing a synthetic law firm built with Engram Lab. The dataset contains over 100M tokens of work product from 250+ synthetic matters across 46 clients and about 10k files. They built it to evaluate how well agents can search and understand a firm's past work.

## Background

Synthetic data here means realistic but made-up documents, so the environment can be shared without exposing real client data. Evaluation environments like this let teams measure agent search and retrieval on large, messy document collections that look like real enterprise data.

## What I took away

- Realistic synthetic corpora are a way to benchmark agents without privacy risk.
- Enterprise agent quality depends heavily on search over large, varied file sets.
- Consider building a synthetic version of your own domain data for evals.
