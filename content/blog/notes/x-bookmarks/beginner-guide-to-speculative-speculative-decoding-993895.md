## Summary

The author wrote a beginner-to-advanced article after reading the speculative speculative decoding (SSD) paper. It starts from basics, introduces needed terms and why they work, and ends with a technique that algorithmically speeds up LLMs by 2x, as claimed.

## Background

Speculative decoding means a small model drafts several tokens and the big model checks them in one pass. SSD extends this idea further, per the post. The 2x figure is the author's description.

## What I took away

- Learn standard speculative decoding first.
- This article is a gentle path into the SSD paper.
- Check measured speedups in the paper.
