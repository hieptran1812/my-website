## Summary

The author says he hooked autoresearch to a peer-to-peer astrophysics researcher agent that gossips and collaborates with other agents. The steps are to learn to train an astrophysics model, train one, use it to write papers, and more. The quoted post is Karpathy's autoresearch repo announcement.

## Background

Peer-to-peer means agents talk directly to each other without a central server. Here they share findings while running on their own machines.

## What I took away

- Autoresearch can be extended to a networked, domain-specific setup.
- Results of such networks are still the author's claims.
