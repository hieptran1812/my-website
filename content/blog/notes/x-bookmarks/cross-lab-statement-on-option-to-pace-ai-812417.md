## Summary

Neel Nanda says he signed a statement because AI is progressing fast with incentives to go as fast as possible despite risks. He says coordinating a change of pace may be needed but will be hard and needs preparation, so keeping the option is good, and he is glad this is consensus across labs. The quoted post says that at some point AI acceleration in frontier development may be so high that the world will need to pace AI advancement.

## Background

Pacing AI means deliberately slowing or coordinating the rate of frontier model development. Doing this needs agreements, monitoring and institutions that take time to build. The argument is about preparing the ability to slow down, not necessarily slowing down now.

## What I took away

- Preparing an option is different from committing to use it.
- Coordination between labs is hard and needs groundwork in advance.
- Watch how such statements turn into concrete mechanisms.
