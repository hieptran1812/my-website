## Summary

The author says they contribute to Cardano on-chain activity with a set of strategy bots, mainly DEX arbitrage. The bots have run for a long time, and the post shows a snapshot from the last few minutes. Strategy logic, execution, monitoring and tooling are all self-built.

## Background

A DEX is a decentralized exchange running on a blockchain. DEX arbitrage means profiting from a price gap between pools or venues. Building the whole stack yourself gives control but also requires handling fees, failures and competition from other bots.

## What I took away

- On-chain bots need strategy, execution and monitoring all working.
- Arbitrage margins are thin and competitive.
- This is a personal showcase, not trading advice.
