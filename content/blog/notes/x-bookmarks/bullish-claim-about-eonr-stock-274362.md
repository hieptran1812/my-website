## Summary

The author says he posted about $EONR again and is **massively long**. He claims that if the team hits its growth projections, returns could be 10-25x in the coming years.

## Background

This is a promotional stock call. The return figures depend entirely on the company hitting its own projections, which is uncertain. Posts like this are opinion, not analysis.

## What I took away

- Treat 10-25x claims as the author's opinion, not evidence.
- Check whether the author holds the position he promotes.
- Ask what the projections are based on before believing them.
