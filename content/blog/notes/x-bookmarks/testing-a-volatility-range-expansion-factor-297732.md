## Summary

The author shares a test chart for a factor called the **Volatility Range Expansion Factor**. They say the logic is intuitive: true market volatility is not just the day's high-low difference but also needs to consider something more, where the post is cut off.

## Background

A factor is a measurable stock characteristic used to rank stocks and predict returns. Range-based volatility measures use high, low and often the previous close; true range, for example, includes overnight gaps. A test chart usually shows returns of portfolios sorted by the factor.

## What I took away

- Volatility measures that include gaps can capture risk that high-low misses.
- A single test chart is not proof; check costs, out-of-sample periods and robustness.
- This is educational, not a trading recommendation.
