## Summary

@EKortukov shares research saying white-box methods to control LLMs often use contrastive input pairs, but this misses rich uncertainty information in representations. Using it allows steering with fewer side effects. The post starts a thread.

## Background

Steering adds a direction to activations to change behavior. Contrastive pairs contrast, for example, honest and dishonest prompts. Uncertainty-aware methods use the spread, not just the mean.

## What I took away

- Steering side effects may be reduced with uncertainty.
- Look at the thread for the method.
