## Summary

@finiteopencover writes a satirical list of an interview where the intern was told to read two papers on open-weight models but gave an incoherent summary and took 40 seconds to name a model released on Hugging Face the day before. The quoted post says the author has five hours of interviews tomorrow.

## Background

Open-weight models are models whose weights are public. Interviewers often check whether candidates follow the field. The story is a joke about how hard it is to keep up.

## What I took away

- Be ready to explain models you have used.
- Follow new releases to keep up.
- Prepare short summaries of papers you read.
