## Summary

The author says the optimization loop behind Polymarket arbitrage bots like gabagool22 uses Adaptive Frank-Wolfe for tractability and Bregman projection for guaranteed profit. They claim understanding it puts you ahead of 99% of noise and point to an article.

## Background

Frank-Wolfe is an optimization algorithm for constrained problems, and Bregman projection finds the nearest point in a set under a certain distance. Claims of guaranteed profit are promotional.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of guaranteed profit claims.
- The math is real but profits are not assured.
- Not trading advice.
