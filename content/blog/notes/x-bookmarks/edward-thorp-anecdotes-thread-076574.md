## Summary

The author praises Edward Thorp and lists claims: he invented card counting in blackjack, is 93 years old yet looks 60, predicted Buffett's rise and Madoff's fall, and ran a fund for 20 years with only 3 months down. A thread of anecdotes follows.

## Background

Thorp is a mathematician who applied probability to gambling and later to markets. He is often cited as an early example of quantitative investing. The specifics in the post are the author's claims.

## What I took away

- Probability and bet sizing underlie both card counting and investing.
- Consistent risk control mattered more than big wins in his story.
- Verify specific figures before repeating them.
