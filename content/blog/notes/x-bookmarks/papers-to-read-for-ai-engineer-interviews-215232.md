## Summary

The author lists papers to read for AI engineer interviews: Attention Is All You Need, LoRA, PEFT, Vision Transformers, VAE and GANs, with more in the full post.

## Background

LoRA is a low-rank way to fine-tune big models cheaply. PEFT means parameter-efficient fine-tuning. VAEs and GANs are generative model types.

## What I took away

- Learn the transformer paper first.
- LoRA and PEFT are practical.
- Know both generative families.
