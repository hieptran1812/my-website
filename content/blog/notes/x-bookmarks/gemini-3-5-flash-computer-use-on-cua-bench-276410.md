## Summary

@ivanfioravanti says CUA feels like magic in Hermes Agent and plans a video on Reachy Mini. The quoted thread reports early access to Gemini 3.5 Flash native Computer Use, with the highest mean reward of any frontier model tested on Cua-Bench at 0.267, on KiCad tasks no model fully solves, at Flash speed and cost.

## Background

Computer use means a model controls a screen with clicks and typing. Cua-Bench scores such agents on tasks, and mean reward is the average score. A 0.267 mean shows the tasks remain hard.

## What I took away

- Computer-use agents are improving but KiCad tasks are far from solved.
- Fast, cheap models can still lead on agent benchmarks.
