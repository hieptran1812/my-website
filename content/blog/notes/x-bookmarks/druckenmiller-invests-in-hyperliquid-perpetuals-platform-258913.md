## Summary

The quoted post from Druckenmiller says he is an investor in trade(xyz), which is partnering with ICE to bring 24/7 perpetual futures on Hyperliquid to the US, and that he plans to use the product. @emilyyvuong, writing in Vietnamese, describes him as a Wall Street legend backing Hyperliquid: once George Soros's right hand, part of the 1992 short of the British pound that made over $1B, and known for his long record at Duquesne Capital.

## Background

Perpetual futures are derivatives with no expiry date that track an asset's price, popular in crypto. ICE is a major exchange operator. A well-known investor's backing is news, not a reason to trade.

## What I took away

- Famous investor endorsements are not trading advice.
- Perpetual futures carry leverage risk; learn how funding rates work.
- Traditional finance and crypto platforms are increasingly linking up.
