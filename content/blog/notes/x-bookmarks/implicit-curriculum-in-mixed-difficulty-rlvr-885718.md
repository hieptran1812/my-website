## Summary

@yuhuang42 asks whether RLVR models can find their own frontier, and says the paper proves mixed-difficulty RL induces an implicit curriculum: easier tasks become learnable first and pull harder tasks into reach.

## Background

RLVR is reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards. A curriculum orders tasks from easy to hard. The claim is that no manual ordering is needed if the data mixes difficulties.

## What I took away

- Mixing task difficulty may be enough to get curriculum benefits.
- Easy tasks help unlock hard ones.
- This is a theoretical claim from the authors.
