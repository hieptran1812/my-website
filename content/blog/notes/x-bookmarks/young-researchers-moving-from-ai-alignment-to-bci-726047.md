## Summary

The author says the smartest young people in SF worked on AGI and alignment 5 to 10 years ago and are working on brain-computer interfaces today, and praises Naomi as part of a wave of young talent in BCI. In the quoted post, the researcher says she resigned from OpenAI to join Conduit as a founding researcher, training models to non-invasively read the human mind, and shares thoughts on what telepathy could look like by 2035.

## Background

A brain-computer interface (BCI) reads signals from the brain to control devices or decode intent. Non-invasive means without surgery, for example using sensors on the scalp. Machine learning is used to turn noisy brain signals into useful outputs.

## What I took away

- Talent flows can hint at which fields are about to grow.
- BCI combines neuroscience, hardware and ML, so skills from AI transfer.
- Read the linked essay for the concrete roadmap toward 2035.
