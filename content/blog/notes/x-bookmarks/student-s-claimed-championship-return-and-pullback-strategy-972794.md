## Summary

The post says Hong Kong student Martin Luk won the stock category of the 2025 U.S. Investing Championship with an annual return of 969.8%. It says his most used strategy is the pullback buying method, and then cuts off.

## Background

A pullback strategy means buying a stock in an uptrend after it temporarily drops, betting the trend resumes. Trading contest results reflect one year and often very high risk, so they say little about repeatability.

## What I took away

- Extreme one-year returns usually come with extreme risk; survivorship bias is strong in contests.
- Study the strategy logic, not the headline number.
- This is educational context, not a recommendation.
