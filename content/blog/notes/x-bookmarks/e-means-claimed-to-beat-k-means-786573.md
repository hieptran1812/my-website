## Summary

The author cheers that classic machine learning is back. The quoted post introduces **e-means**, claimed to beat k-means in 10x fewer epochs with order-of-magnitude wall-clock speedups. It is released in FAISS, needs no tuning beyond k, and is said to be robust across scales and datasets.

## Background

k-means is a basic clustering algorithm that groups points around k centers. FAISS is a library for vector search. A faster, tuning-free variant would help large-scale indexing. Speedups are the authors' claims.

## What I took away

- Classic algorithms can still be improved.
- FAISS users may get this directly.
- Benchmark on your own data.
