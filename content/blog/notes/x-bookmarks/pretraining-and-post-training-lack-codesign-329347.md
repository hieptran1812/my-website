## Summary

The author says they are surprised there is not more codesign between pretraining and post-training. They joke that companies really do ship their org charts.

## Background

Pretraining builds the base model on huge text data, and post-training (SFT, RL) shapes its behavior. In many labs these are separate teams. The remark echoes Conway's law, where a product mirrors the structure of the organization that made it.

## What I took away

- Team boundaries can limit technical choices across training stages.
- Ask whether pretraining choices could be tuned for later RL or fine-tuning.
- Conway's law applies to model development too.
