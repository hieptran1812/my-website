## Summary

The author says Jane Street is looking to hire great hybrid writers-and-engineers. As the firm grows, communication becomes more important for internal and external audiences.

## Background

A writer-engineer can explain technical work clearly and also build things. Such roles exist because complex firms need clear documentation and messaging.

## What I took away

- Strong writing is a career edge for engineers.
- Technical communication roles can sit inside engineering teams.
