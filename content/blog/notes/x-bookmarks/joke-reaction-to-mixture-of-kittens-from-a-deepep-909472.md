## Summary

The author jokes from the point of view of DeepEP, implying it has been badly beaten. The quoted Cursor post open-sources **Mixture-of-Kittens (MoK)**, an MoE training megakernel for NVL72 that fuses all MoE communication and compute into one deterministic kernel and claims up to 2.37x over the strongest public baselines.

## Background

DeepEP is DeepSeek's open-source library for expert-parallel communication in MoE models, a common baseline. The joke suggests MoK's claimed speedups outpace such existing tools.

## What I took away

- DeepEP is a standard baseline for MoE communication; compare new tools against it.
- Benchmark claims should be checked on your own cluster and model shapes.
