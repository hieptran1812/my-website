## Summary

@jeremyphoward says the answer is yes, kinda. After a few minutes of clicking like on posts he liked and show less like this on others, his feed changed. The quoted post asks whether this is true.

## Background

Recommendation feeds learn from signals like likes and negative feedback. A quick burst of feedback can shift what you see. Users have more control than many assume.

## What I took away

- Feedback buttons do affect the feed.
- A few minutes of curation can help.
- This is one person's report.
