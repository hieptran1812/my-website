## Summary

@ar0cket1 asks how they make such great visuals. The quote introduces Compositional Muon, extending Muon from individual matrices to compositions like QK^T and OV, since the loss sees those products.

## Background

Muon is an optimizer working on matrices. In attention, the product matters, not each weight alone.

## What I took away

- Optimize in terms of compositions.
- Visuals aid learning.
