## Summary

The post says most traders guess support and resistance, but the market cares about where real trading happened. It claims volume profile shows this exactly and asks readers to comment VOL to receive a DM guide.

## Background

A volume profile shows how much was traded at each price level. High-volume zones may act as reference areas. The comment-for-DM format is lead generation.

## What I took away

- Volume profile is a tool to see traded volume by price.
- Beware of comment-to-DM funnels.
- Educational only.
