## Summary

The author shares a new blog on experiments with fine-grained credit assignment using an agentic judge. It covers effects on model performance and reasoning quality and some thoughts on RL science.

## Background

Credit assignment means deciding which steps in a long output deserve reward. A judge agent can score parts of a trajectory instead of only the final outcome.

## What I took away

- Finer credit can improve RL for reasoning.
- Judges can be agents.
- Read the blog for the findings.
