## Summary

David Ha shares a blog on how AI can generate **playable Minecraft worlds**. He notes generative AI has handled images, video and text, and asks about interactive 3D environments; the team trained models on billions of cubes. The quoted post announces Dream-Cubed: Controllable Generative Modeling in Minecraft by Training on Billions of Cubes, with a blog, paper and code.

## Background

Voxels are 3D pixels, like Minecraft's blocks. Generating structured, playable 3D spaces is harder than images because the result must be coherent and usable, not just look right. This connects to world models, which aim to generate environments agents or people can interact with.

## What I took away

- 3D voxel worlds are a tractable testbed for generative 3D research.
- Controllability matters as much as quality for usable generated content.
- Paper and code are open, so the approach can be studied directly.
