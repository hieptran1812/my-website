## Summary

The author recommends an X account that helps catch up on many podcasts and interviews. The quoted post shares Dylan Patel of SemiAnalysis saying **speed is the moat** in AI inference. He says getting there runs through new compute like Groq and Cerebras, KV-cache offload and networking optimization, noting most infrastructure is optimized for high throughput.

## Background

A moat is a lasting competitive advantage. In inference, speed means low latency per user, which differs from throughput (total tokens served). KV-cache offload moves cached attention data to cheaper memory, and Groq and Cerebras build specialized chips for fast inference.

## What I took away

- Summary accounts can save time but check the original source for nuance.
- Latency and throughput need different hardware and software choices.
- Consider whether your product needs fast responses or cheap bulk tokens.
