## Summary

The author announces open-sourcing **GenOffice**, a free, full-featured AI office suite for PC and Mac, and invites others to build it together. The quoted post says it is ad-free with the usual editing tools, and that it started with one engineer, one week and $10,000 in tokens.

## Background

An AI office suite is a document, spreadsheet and slide editor with AI features built in. The note about $10,000 in tokens refers to the cost of using AI coding models to build the software.

## What I took away

- AI coding tools are lowering the cost of building large apps.
- Claims of being the first or full-featured are the authors' claims; try it yourself.
- Open-source projects built fast may need care around maintenance and security.
