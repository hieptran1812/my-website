## Summary

The author announces Energy-Based Reasoners and says bye to looped reasoning. They claim EBRs use 345x less inference compute and 2.6x fewer training FLOPs than the best baselines, and are 7x to 43x more sample efficient than the best looped baselines. The text is cut off.

## Background

Energy-based models assign a score to candidate outputs and search for low-energy answers. They offer another way to spend compute on reasoning. These numbers are the authors' claims.

## What I took away

- Energy-based approaches compete with looping.
- The big multipliers are unverified claims.
- Look for independent replication.
