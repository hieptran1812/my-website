## Summary

@suchenzang jokes that Chinese is peak token efficiency. The quoted post argues that in the human-observable limit these models will all speak Chinglish, since most highly educated digital workers use English or Chinese, and compact translations of four-word idioms (chengyu) give efficiency.

## Background

Token efficiency means expressing an idea in fewer tokens, which saves cost and time. Reasoning models are rewarded for short, correct chains, which may push them toward compact words from any language. This is a speculative prediction.

## What I took away

- Reasoning traces may mix languages for compactness.
- This is a prediction, not a measured result.
