## Summary

The author introduces **Simile** and calls simulating human behavior one of the most consequential and technically difficult problems of our time. They say they raised $100M from Index, Hanabi, A* BCV and several named individual investors. The post does not describe the product.

## Background

Simulating human behavior means building models that predict how people would act or respond, for example in surveys or product decisions. LLM-based agents are one way to try this. The hard part is checking that the simulated people match real ones.

## What I took away

- A large funding round signals investor interest in human-behavior simulation.
- The post gives no product details, so judge it by later results.
- Ask how any simulation is validated against real people.
