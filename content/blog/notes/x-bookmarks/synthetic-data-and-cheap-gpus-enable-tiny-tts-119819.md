## Summary

The author calls the work wonderful and says we can do much with synthetic data and 10 dollars of GPUs. The quoted post is the tiny-voca announcement of a 4.5M parameter, 18 MB text-to-speech model that never heard a real human voice.

## Background

The comment is about low-cost training. If synthetic data works, small teams can build specialized models without large budgets.

## What I took away

- Cheap compute can be enough for small models.
- Synthetic data lowers collection costs.
