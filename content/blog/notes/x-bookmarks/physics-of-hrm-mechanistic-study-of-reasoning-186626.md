## Summary

@ZimingLiu11 asks whether reasoning models really think or secretly guess. They did a mechanistic study of HRMs and show a surprising failure on extremely simple Sudokus and grokking. They say Physics of HRM boosts accuracy from 55% to 97%.

## Background

HRM is a hierarchical reasoning model, a small model for puzzle-like tasks. Grokking is when a model suddenly generalizes long after fitting training data. A mechanistic study looks inside the model to explain behavior. The numbers are the authors' claim.

## What I took away

- Look inside models to check if they reason or guess.
- Simple tasks can expose failures.
- Understanding mechanisms can guide fixes.
