## Summary

@H0wie_Xu says Ying Sheng, who was hired to build infrastructure at xAI, left to democratize frontier-lab AI infrastructure and, as founder and CEO of @radixark, just launched #Miles. The quoted post is the RadixArk launch message from the team, started by core developers of SGLang and related projects.

RadixArk is a company built around the SGLang ecosystem and open training tools like slime and AReaL. The post does not describe what Miles is in detail, so check the launch post for specifics.
