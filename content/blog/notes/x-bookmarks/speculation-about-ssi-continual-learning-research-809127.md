## Summary

The author thinks there are now enough clues to guess at **SSI's** secret research, with the top guess being a brain-inspired way for AI to learn continually. They note today's frontier models learn mostly during training (text cut off). The quoted SSI post announces a long-term partnership with NVIDIA, which is making a substantial investment that lets SSI 10x its compute in 12 months because its research is worth scaling.

## Background

SSI is Safe Superintelligence, the company co-founded by Ilya Sutskever. Continual learning means a model keeps learning new knowledge after deployment without forgetting old knowledge, which current models do poorly. The guess is the author's speculation, not a confirmed fact.

## What I took away

- Treat outside guesses about secret research as speculation.
- Continual learning is widely seen as a key missing capability.
- A 10x compute increase signals the company believes its approach scales.
