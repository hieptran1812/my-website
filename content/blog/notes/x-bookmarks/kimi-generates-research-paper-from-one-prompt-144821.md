## Summary

The Kimi account says one prompt gives a publication-ready research paper with academic charts. It claims Kimi handles professional formatting, LaTeX formulas and figures, and can produce a Word file by chatting.

## Background

Formatting help is useful, but a generated paper still needs checks on facts, citations and data. Publication-ready is the vendor's claim.

## What I took away

- Use AI for formatting, verify the content.
- Check every citation.
- Marketing claims need testing.
