## Summary

The author claims a Polymarket trader grew **$15 to $3M** using a consistent timing model across crypto, politics, tech and macro. They cite 114 predictions and 90% accuracy. These are the author's claims and are not verified in the post.

## Background

Posts like this are common on social media and can be survivorship bias, where only winners get shared. Wallet histories can be real, but they do not show that results will repeat.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of dramatic wallet stories.
- Survivorship bias hides the many who lost.
- Not advice.
