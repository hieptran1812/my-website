## Summary

The author says autoresearch starts the era of disposable models. They claim labs that cannot automate their own R&D will be outrun, and that the moat is the speed of the automated experimentation loop, not talent. They cite minimax 2.7 as built from an autoresearch-like process. The text is cut off.

## Background

Autoresearch means an AI agent runs experiments in a loop: change code, test, keep what improves. If models are cheap to retrain, each one becomes disposable. These are the author's opinions and predictions.

## What I took away

- Fast, automated experiment loops can matter more than any single model.
- Measurable metrics are what make a loop automatable.
- Treat the 'talent no longer matters' claim as opinion.
