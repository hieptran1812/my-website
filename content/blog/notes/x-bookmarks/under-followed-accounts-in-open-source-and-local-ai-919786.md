## Summary

The author lists five people he thinks are under-followed in the open source and local AI space. He plans to recommend five people to follow in this space every Sunday going forward.

## Background

Local AI means running models on your own hardware instead of using cloud APIs. Following practitioners who share experiments is a good way to keep up with fast-moving tools and techniques.

## What I took away

- Curated follow lists can help find good sources quickly.
- Check whether recommended accounts share hands-on, verifiable work.
- Look for the weekly recommendations if this area interests you.
