## Summary

The author says they gave their Clawdbot a 36GB Polymarket dataset with 400M+ trades and plans to use the Simmer SDK AI Divergence skill, suggesting it might become a prediction machine. The quoted post says the dataset is MIT licensed and free via Cloudflare R2. The post is promotional.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where people trade on event outcomes. Historical trade data can be used to study prices, but past data does not guarantee future profit. Calling an agent a prediction machine is a marketing claim.

## What I took away

- Open datasets are useful for research and backtesting.
- Be skeptical of promises that data alone makes an agent profitable.
- Not financial advice.
