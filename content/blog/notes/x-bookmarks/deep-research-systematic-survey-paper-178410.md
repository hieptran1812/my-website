## Summary

The post recommends the survey Deep Research: A Systematic Survey. It covers a 3-stage roadmap for deep research systems, how they differ from RAG, key components (query planning, information acquisition, memory management, answer generation) and optimization such as prompting and supervised fine-tuning.

## Background

Deep research systems are agents that plan searches, read many sources and write a report. Unlike simple RAG, they run multiple steps. A survey gives a map of the design choices.

## What I took away

- Deep research means planning, searching, memory and writing.
- Use the survey's component list as a design checklist.
