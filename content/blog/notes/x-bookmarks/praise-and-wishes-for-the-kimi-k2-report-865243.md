## Summary

@dejavucoder says the Kimi K2 technical report is a work of art given its dense information and good references. They wish it expanded on the large-scale agentic data synthesis pipeline for tool use learning and say they still do not understand the infra section.

## Background

A technical report describes how a model was built and trained. Agentic data synthesis means generating practice tasks and tool-use traces so the model learns to use tools. These reports are valuable because they reveal training methods.

## What I took away

- Technical reports are a good way to learn how frontier models are trained.
- It is normal to not understand every section; mark gaps and revisit.
