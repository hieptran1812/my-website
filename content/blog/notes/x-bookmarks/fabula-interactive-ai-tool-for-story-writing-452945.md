## Summary

Google Research introduces Fabula, an interactive writing tool that helps authors structure and refine stories. It was co-designed with 42 expert writers. The demo shows how convergent iteration supports creativity and is at the Google booth at CHI2026.

## Background

CHI is a major human-computer interaction conference. Co-designing with writers means the tool was shaped by its users.

## What I took away

- Co-design with real users improves tools.
- AI can help structure not just generate.
- See the demo for details.
