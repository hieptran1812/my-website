## Summary

The author says they are releasing self-contained lecture notes that explain flow matching and diffusion models from scratch, going from zero to state of the art, with a link to arXiv. The quoted post announces the MIT 2026 course, teaching the full stack of modern image, video and protein generators in theory and practice, with videos.

## Background

Diffusion and flow matching are the main methods behind modern image and video generators. A course covering both theory and practice helps you understand them rather than just use them.

## What I took away

- A free MIT course plus notes is a strong starting point for generative modeling.
- The same methods apply to images, video and proteins.
- Pair the theory notes with code exercises.
