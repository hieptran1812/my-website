## Summary

The author says their paper was accepted at ICLR 2026 and that they are now scaling **set RL** to LLMs for maths and coding reasoning. The quoted post explains that policy gradient methods often collapse, failing to explore broadly and converging to narrow, exploitable behaviors, which hurts generalization and test-time scaling.

## Background

Policy gradient methods update a model to make rewarded actions more likely. If they stop exploring, the model may settle on a few behaviors. Better exploration may help reasoning models gain more from extra compute at test time.

## What I took away

- Exploration collapse is a known weakness of policy gradient RL.
- Watch for results applying set RL to LLM reasoning.
