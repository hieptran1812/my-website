## Summary

@gabriberton is happy the paper was called the wildest CVPR 2026 result. The quoted post says a frame needs one token, not 1,024, and DeltaWorld gets better predictions with over 35x fewer parameters and 2,000x fewer FLOPs, per the post.

## Background

World models predict future video. Tokens are compact units of input. Using one per frame cuts compute greatly. Figures are the quoted claim.

## What I took away

- Heavy compression can still work for prediction.
- Check the paper for tradeoffs.
