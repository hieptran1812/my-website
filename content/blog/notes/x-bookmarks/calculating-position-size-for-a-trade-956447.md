## Summary

@spicyofc says this is how a trader can calculate how much size to put on a trade. The method is likely in the attached image.

## Background

Position sizing often links the amount risked to a stop distance, for example risk per trade divided by the distance to the stop. It controls losses. This is educational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Size positions from risk per trade, not from conviction.
- The stop distance affects the size.
- Open the link to see the specific method.
