## Summary

The post says autonomous trading agents are running on @b402ai smart wallets. It gives no further detail in the text.

## Background

A smart wallet is a crypto wallet controlled by code, so an agent can be given limited permissions to act. Autonomous agents that trade money carry real risk if they are wrong or exploited. This is a product mention, not advice.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of agents that handle real funds without clear limits.
- Look for spending caps and permission controls on smart wallets.
- The post has little detail, so verify claims before relying on them.
