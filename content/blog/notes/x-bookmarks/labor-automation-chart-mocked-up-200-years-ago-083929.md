## Summary

The author mocks up how Anthropic's new labor automation chart would have looked 200 years ago. They say the outer ring would be almost unrecognizable: computer and math was nonsensical, and medicine and law were tiny and barely professionalized.

## Background

The point is that job categories change a lot over time, so a snapshot of occupations today may not predict future work. It is a perspective on automation forecasts.

## What I took away

- Occupation categories evolve over decades.
- Be cautious reading static automation charts as forecasts.
