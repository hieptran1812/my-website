## Summary

The author has been thinking about continual learning for long-running agents and running toy experiments with MLX. They say the status quo of prompt compaction plus recursive sub-agents is remarkably effective, and suggest it may go a long way. The rest of the post is cut off.

## Background

Continual learning means a model keeps learning over time without forgetting. For agents, a cheaper alternative is compacting context into summaries and delegating to sub-agents. MLX is Apple's machine learning framework.

## What I took away

- Context compaction and sub-agents are a strong baseline.
- Try simple approaches before changing model weights.
- Check the full post for the author's conclusions.
