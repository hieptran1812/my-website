## Summary

@0xSero says Nex is onto something. The quoted post claims that after analyzing weights, Rio 3.5 is about 0.6 times Nex N2 Pro plus 0.4 times Qwen 3.5, and that it even introduces itself as Nex.

## Background

Weight merging averages the parameters of several models. If the claim is true, Rio 3.5 would be a merge rather than a new model. This is the quoted team's claim and the next bookmarks treat it with humor.

## What I took away

- Treat the claim as unverified.
- Weight analysis can reveal model lineage.
