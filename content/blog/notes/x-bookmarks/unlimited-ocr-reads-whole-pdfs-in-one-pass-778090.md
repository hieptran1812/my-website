## Summary

The author recommends an account as the best on X to learn about AI and open source. The quoted post claims China open-sourced Unlimited-OCR, a 3B parameter model that runs locally and reads a 100-page PDF in a single pass instead of chopping it into pages.

## Background

OCR extracts text from images or scans. Most tools process pages separately and can lose context across pages. Reading everything in one pass would keep structure but needs long context. The claim is from the quoted post.

## What I took away

- Page-by-page OCR can lose document context.
- Test claims on your own documents.
- A 3B model is small enough to run locally.
