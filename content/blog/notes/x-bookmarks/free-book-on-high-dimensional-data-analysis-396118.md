## Summary

The author shares a 730 page PDF of High-Dimensional Data Analysis with Low-Dimensional Models by Wright and Ma. The preface is by E. Candes, and the book is hosted at book-wright-ma.github.io.

## Background

Many real datasets are high-dimensional but have low-dimensional structure, such as sparsity or low rank. The book covers the math for exploiting that structure. It connects to compressed sensing and modern representation learning.

## What I took away

- Low-dimensional structure in high-dimensional data is a core idea.
- Free resource for rigorous math background.
- It is long, so pick chapters that match your needs.
