## Summary

The author says they advise against trading crypto markets on **Polymarket**. They claim the effective leverage on the 15-minute markets is about **1000x**.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market where you buy shares that pay out if an event happens. On a very short market, a small price move can swing the share price a lot, which acts like leverage. High leverage means small errors can wipe out a position quickly.

## What I took away

- Treat very short-horizon bets as high-leverage even when no loan is involved.
- The 1000x figure is the author's claim, so check the math yourself.
- Size positions assuming you could lose the full amount.
