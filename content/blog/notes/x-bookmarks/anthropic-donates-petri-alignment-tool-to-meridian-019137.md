## Summary

Anthropic says it is donating Petri, its open-source alignment tool, to Meridian Labs so development can continue independently. With Meridian it also released a major update improving the adaptability, realism and depth of Petri's tests.

## Background

Alignment tools test whether models behave as intended. Moving a tool to an independent group can make results more credible, since the model maker does not control the tests.

## What I took away

- Independent stewardship adds trust to safety evaluation tools.
- Open-source alignment tests let others reproduce audits.
