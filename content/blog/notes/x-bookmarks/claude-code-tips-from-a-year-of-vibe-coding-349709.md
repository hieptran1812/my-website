## Summary

@0xPaulius says they spent 365 days vibe coding, months researching, and shipped 10+ apps with 30k+ users and $100k+ generated as a solo developer. They are sharing their best Claude Code cheats. The numbers are their own claims.

## Background

Vibe coding means building software by prompting an AI and accepting its code without writing everything by hand. Claude Code is a coding agent in the terminal. The post is a thread of practical tips, and the revenue figures are unverified.

## What I took away

- Practical workflows from heavy users can be a shortcut to learning tools.
- Treat revenue and user numbers in self-promotional posts with caution.
