## Summary

The post introduces Jemini Deep Research, a system that generates custom reports about the Epstein case. Each run makes 50+ searches across the archive to write a heavily cited, encyclopedia-length article.

## Background

Deep research systems run many searches and then compose a long cited answer. Citations help check claims, but readers should still verify sources, especially on sensitive subjects.

## What I took away

- Check citations in generated reports.
- Deep research tools suit large document archives.
