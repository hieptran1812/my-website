## Summary

The post says a gpt5 raw CoT leak method was achieved internally. It gives no details or evidence in the text.

## Background

CoT means chain of thought, the step-by-step reasoning a model writes before answering. Labs often hide the raw version. A claim about leaking it should be treated carefully without evidence.

## What I took away

- Unsupported claims about model leaks should be treated as unverified.
- Raw reasoning traces are often hidden by providers on purpose.
- Ask for evidence before drawing conclusions.
