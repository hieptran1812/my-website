## Summary

@ZhijingJin praises Rada Mihalcea as an amazing researcher and mentor whom students and collaborators enjoy working with. The quoted post is Rada's announcement that she is elected a fellow of ACL, thanking collaborators and students.

## Background

ACL is the Association for Computational Linguistics, the main community for NLP research. Fellow is an honor for major contributions. Mentorship is part of what the post highlights.

## What I took away

- Good mentorship is valued in research communities.
- Recognition posts show who shapes a field.
- Consider mentors when choosing a lab.
