## Summary

The author says social media frames AI debate as skeptics who think LLMs are doomed versus fanatics who think superintelligence is imminent. They say leading researchers actually say something more nuanced. One quoted post says scaling the current approach keeps improving results and will not stall, but something important will stay missing. Another quoted post summarizes an Ilya Sutskever podcast: superintelligence in 5-20 years, current scaling stalling, and a return to research.

## Background

Scaling means making models bigger and training them on more data and compute. The debate is whether that alone leads to human-level or superhuman AI. The quoted summaries are second-hand and partly cut off.

## What I took away

- Views of leading researchers are more nuanced than the two camps suggest.
- Scaling can keep helping and still leave gaps such as continual learning.
- Check original interviews rather than short summaries.
