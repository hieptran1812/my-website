## Summary

The author says this is how companies should hire: forget leet code and show you can solve real problems. The quoted post says Unsloth made 5 challenges and a score of 47 points earns an offer of $500K/year plus equity, with no experience or PhD needed. It lists a Founding Engineer role at 47 points and an ML Engineer at 32 points.

## Background

Instead of algorithm interviews, the company asks candidates to complete real tasks, such as converting nf4 / BnB 4-bit quantized weights. This tests the actual skills needed. Pay figures here are from the quoted post.

## What I took away

- Real-problem challenges can replace puzzle interviews.
- Building public skills in areas like quantization can open doors.
- The pay figures are the company's stated offer.
