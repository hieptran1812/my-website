## Summary

Unsloth shares a beginner guide by NVIDIA on fine tuning LLMs with Unsloth. It covers training methods LoRA, FFT and RL, when and why to fine tune, data and VRAM needs, and how to train locally on DGX Spark, RTX GPUs and more.

## Background

LoRA trains small add-on weights so fine tuning needs less memory. FFT is full fine-tuning of all weights. VRAM is GPU memory, which limits what you can train locally. The guide is a practical entry point.

## What I took away

- LoRA is the cheapest way to start fine tuning.
- Estimate data and VRAM needs before you begin.
