## Summary

The author jokes about Anthropic's CEO seeing a Chinese competitor release a 40-minute masterclass. The quoted post says the Kimi founder gave a 40-minute talk on the architecture behind a $20B valuation and calls it the fastest way to learn to build AI agents.

## Background

Kimi is a family of models from Moonshot AI. The quoted post mentions a topic list starting with optimization and linear attention. The valuation and quality claims are the posters' own and are not verified here.

## What I took away

- Founder talks can be a quick way to learn design choices behind a model.
- Be careful with hype framing such as masterclass and fixed valuations.
