## Summary

The author says the AI hedge fund has a Nassim Taleb agent that finds big upside bets, avoids fragility and buys what thrives in crisis. It is the third macro agent after Druckenmiller and Jhunjhunwala styles.

## Background

Persona agents prompt an LLM to reason like an investor. These are experimental tools, not advice.

## What I took away

- Personas encode investing styles in prompts.
- Do not treat agent output as advice.
- Backtest before trusting.
