## Summary

The author claims China released an AI employee that runs 100% locally and can research, code, build websites, create slide decks and generate videos. It comes with its own computer and is 100% open source. The post gives no name in the text.

## Background

Such agents combine a model with tools and a sandboxed computer to complete tasks end to end. The post does not name the project, so the claims are unverified. Another bookmark links DeerFlow from ByteDance, which may be related.

## What I took away

- Verify 'does everything by itself' claims with a small test.
- Running locally helps privacy but may need strong hardware or model access.
