## Summary

The author says JEPA is bigger than vision and that JEPA-Anything explores predictive representation learning across seven domains, from biology to weather, as a step toward general world models. The quoted post describes one world model working across molecules, cells, fluids, patients, robots and weather, with links to paper and code.

## Background

JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) learns by predicting the representation of a hidden part of the input from the visible part, rather than reconstructing raw data. A world model predicts how a system evolves.

## What I took away

- JEPA-style training may transfer well beyond images.
- Check how results compare to domain-specific baselines.
