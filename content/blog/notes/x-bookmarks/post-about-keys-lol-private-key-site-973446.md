## Summary

The author describes a website called keyslol that stores every private key that ever has been or could be generated, so anyone could find and steal assets. The text is cut off before the conclusion.

## Background

Private keys are huge random numbers, so the possible space is too large to scan. A site showing keys does not mean assets are at risk. Safety comes from random generation.

## What I took away

- Key space size makes guessing unrealistic.
- Weak randomness is the real risk.
- Never share private keys.
