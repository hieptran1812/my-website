## Summary

The post asks how to price an RWA asset when its market is closed, a core challenge for RWA perps platforms. It names two approaches, safety-first, which halts trading and freezes, and one prioritizing availability.

## Background

RWA means real-world assets like stocks put on a blockchain. Perps are perpetual futures with no expiry. When the exchange closes there is no live price, so platforms must choose between stopping and trading on estimates.

## What I took away

- Safety and availability trade off.
- Closed-market pricing is hard.
- Educational, not advice.
