## Summary

The author shares a detailed article for traders who want to study on a Saturday. It covers five major stages from beginner to profitable trader, the problems experienced at each stage and the solutions.

## Background

Trader education posts often describe a personal path. They reflect one person's experience and may not generalize. Most beginners lose money, so treat such content as reading, not a promise.

## What I took away

- Personal journey articles are one data point, not a rule.
- Read with skepticism about claims of profitability.
- This is not a recommendation to trade.
