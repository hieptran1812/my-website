## Summary

The author reacts with simply: 1. What. The quoted post says researchers have long sought materials that sort electrons by spin while their magnetism cancels, and that in 3 days, 90+ Opus 5.5 agents helped uncover two room-temperature candidates in simulations: YBaMnFeO5 and KV[Cr(CN)6].

## Background

Materials that sort electrons by spin with no net magnetism (often called altermagnets) could be useful for spintronics. Simulation results are candidates, which still need lab confirmation.

## What I took away

- Many parallel agents can speed up search in scientific simulation.
- Simulated candidates are not confirmed discoveries.
