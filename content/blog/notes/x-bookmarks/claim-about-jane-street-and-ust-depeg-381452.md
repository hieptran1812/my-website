## Summary

The author claims Jane Street purposely dumped 80M UST on Curve after Terra withdrew 150M UST to migrate liquidity, with the sole goal of depegging UST. This is the author's allegation.

## Background

UST was Terra's algorithmic stablecoin that lost its peg in 2022. A depeg means the price fell away from one dollar. Causes of the collapse are debated, and this post is one accusation.

## What I took away

- Treat allegations as unproven.
- Check on-chain data and court records.
