## Summary

Elon Musk says the letter has his full support and that Jensen is right. The quoted post is Jensen Huang's first post, sharing a letter NVIDIA signed on why open models matter. It says AI will transform every industry, and that open models strengthen safety and cybersecurity, speed innovation and diffusion, and enable sovereignty.

## Background

Open models are AI models whose weights are published for anyone to run and modify. Sovereignty here means countries being able to run and control their own AI. Supporters argue openness helps security research and competition, while critics worry about misuse.

## What I took away

- Major tech leaders are publicly aligning on open models.
- Consider both the benefits and risks claimed for openness.
- Note the role of hardware vendors, who gain when more people run models.
