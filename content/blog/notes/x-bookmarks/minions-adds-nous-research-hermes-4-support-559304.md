## Summary

In a daily ship update, the author says Minions now supports the Nous Research inference API. Their latest **Hermes-4** models can be used as supervisor LMs in Minions.

## Background

Minions is a setup where a small local model works with a larger supervisor model. Adding a new provider lets users choose which model plays the supervisor role.

## What I took away

- Supervisor and worker model pairings can cut cost.
- Provider support in tools makes models easy to swap.
