## Summary

Karpathy says he finally read a great post and recognizes the pattern: something that sounds wrong is declared good actually because of a galaxy brain reason. He says such reasoning is the best way to justify anything while feeling good about it. He links the quoted post, Galaxy brain resistance.

## Background

Galaxy brain reasoning is a chain of clever steps that ends in a conclusion you wanted anyway. The danger is that the argument feels sophisticated, so you stop questioning it. Resistance means designing your thinking so it cannot easily be bent this way.

## What I took away

- Be suspicious when an argument makes a bad-sounding conclusion feel clever.
- Ask whether the reasoning would still hold if you disliked the conclusion.
- Read the linked essay for the full idea.
