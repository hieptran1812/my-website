## Summary

@srush_nlp says he is fascinated by Stanford CS 312, Deep Learning Alchemy, Fall 2026. He likes that it puts the burden of theory construction on the student and their Claude, which he finds transformative and surprisingly challenging. He muses that maybe knowledge in ML was never that critical.

## Background

Alchemy is a nod to the criticism that deep learning is driven by trial and error rather than theory. The course asks students, with AI help, to build their own explanations of observed phenomena.

## What I took away

- Building your own theories with AI help may teach more than memorizing facts.
- The course site has materials worth browsing.
- AI assistants change what knowledge is worth memorizing.
