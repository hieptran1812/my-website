## Summary

@georgeyw_ starts from the line that neural networks are grown, not programmed, and says they are working to change that. Mechinterp studies how models generalize beyond training data by looking at the internal structure that results. They introduce patterning as the dual: given a desired structure, find the data that produces it.

## Background

Mechanistic interpretability tries to understand what a trained network computes inside. Patterning flips the direction: instead of reading structure out of a trained model, you choose the structure and work back to the training data. If it works, it would give more control over what models learn.

## What I took away

- Interpretability goes from data to structure; patterning goes from structure to data.
- Control over training data could become a way to shape model behavior.
- Treat it as a research proposal until the details are checked.
