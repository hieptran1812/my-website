## Summary

@novasarc01 says current agents confuse information access with learning: they search a corpus and reconstruct answers, but none of that work changes the representation governing the next search. The quoted post frames continual learning as developing expertise from only a corpus of documents.

## Background

Retrieval gives facts per query but does not improve the model. Learning would update its internal state. The quote calls this machine studying.

## What I took away

- Search is not the same as learning.
- Agents may need ways to consolidate what they read.
