## Summary

The author says many developers' hard work does not lead to career growth because they work on a problem nobody cares about. They say to stop being a coder and become a problem solver, which means finding product-market fit. This reacts to a video from someone else.

## Background

Product-market fit means building something enough people actually want. The point is that effort is rewarded when it targets real demand.

## What I took away

- Check that people want the problem solved.
- Skill without demand does not grow a career.
