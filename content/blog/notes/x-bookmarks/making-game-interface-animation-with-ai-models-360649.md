## Summary

The author explains how to create this kind of video game interface animation with GPT Image 2 and Seedance 2. They say GPT Image 2 is good at details and interface design and allows reworking an image multiple times. The quoted post says someone made their own AAA game with these tools.

## Background

Image models are now good at readable UI designs, and video models can animate them. The AAA label is a showcase claim, since the output is visuals.

## What I took away

- Iterating on one image repeatedly is a useful workflow.
- Treat AAA game claims as concept visuals.
