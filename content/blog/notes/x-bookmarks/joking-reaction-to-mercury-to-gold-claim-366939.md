## Summary

@skyiszen jokes that gold is finished, reacting to a quoted company post. The company says it turned mercury into stable gold at a rate beating CERN's record by 10x and enriched isotopes in a plasma centrifuge, beating a 40-year Soviet record.

## Background

The company's claims are described in more detail in another note in this batch. Making gold in a lab is not new; the question is cost and scale.

## What I took away

- A lab result does not mean gold prices will change.
- Treat market jokes as jokes, not financial analysis.
