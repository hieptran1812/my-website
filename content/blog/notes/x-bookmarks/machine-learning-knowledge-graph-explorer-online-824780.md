## Summary

The author put his knowledge graph explorer online. The quoted post describes a hierarchical graph of ML where lower nodes are fundamental, upper nodes advanced, yellow edges show prerequisites and blue edges show concepts built on gradient descent.

## Background

A knowledge graph maps how concepts depend on each other. It helps you plan what to learn first.

## What I took away

- Use prerequisite edges to plan study.
- Gradient descent sits near the base.
- Explore the graph to find gaps.
