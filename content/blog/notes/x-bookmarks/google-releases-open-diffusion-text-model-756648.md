## Summary

@neural_avb is excited about an open-source diffusion text model. The quoted post says DiffusionGemma brings text diffusion research to Gemma 4 and claims up to 4x faster inference by generating blocks of text at once.

## Background

Normal LLMs predict one token at a time. A diffusion model refines a block of text in parallel, which can cut latency. The 4x is Google's claim.

## What I took away

- Parallel generation is a different speed route.
- Quality tradeoffs need testing.
