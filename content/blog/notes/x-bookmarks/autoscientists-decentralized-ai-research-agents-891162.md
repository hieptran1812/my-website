## Summary

The post says AI scientists are starting to do science, not just answer questions or run workflows. AutoScientists is a **decentralized team of AI agents** that generate hypotheses, design experiments, write code, test ideas, analyze failures and revise strategy. The text is cut off.

## Background

Decentralized means there is no single planner assigning tasks; agents coordinate among themselves. Long-running experimentation needs agents that learn from failures, not just execute steps.

## What I took away

- Agent teams are being applied to full research loops.
- Failure analysis and strategy revision are the hard parts.
