## Summary

The author calls it one of the best terminals so far and says prediction market frontends have not solved discoverability, since too many categories cause info overload. Recon lets you filter markets by trader. The quoted post says it tracks the smartest wallets in every category.

## Background

Discoverability means finding the markets worth looking at. Following successful wallets is a form of copy-based research. Past wallet performance does not guarantee future results. This is not advice.

## What I took away

- Filtering by trader is one answer to market overload.
- Past results of a wallet do not predict future ones.
