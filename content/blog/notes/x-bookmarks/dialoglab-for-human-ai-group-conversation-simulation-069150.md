## Summary

Google Research argues that realistic conversational research needs a rethink of fully autonomous agents. It introduces **DialogLab**, an open-source prototyping framework that uses human-in-the-loop control to simulate human-AI group conversations. The text is cut off.

## Background

Human-in-the-loop means a person can steer or approve parts of an agent's behavior instead of letting it run alone. For simulating group chats, this keeps conversations realistic and controllable.

## What I took away

- Full autonomy is not always best for simulation.
- Human steering can improve realism and control.
- Check the open-source release if you study multi-party dialogue.
