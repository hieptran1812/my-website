## Summary

Sundar Pichai announces Nano Banana Pro, which he calls SOTA for image generation and editing with more advanced world knowledge, text rendering, precision and controls. It is built on Gemini 3 and is described as really good at complex infographics.

## Background

SOTA means state of the art. Text rendering inside images has long been hard for image models. The claims come from Google's CEO and are promotional.

## What I took away

- Better text rendering makes images more usable for infographics.
- SOTA claims should be checked on your own use cases.
- The model is built on Gemini 3.
