## Summary

The author shares a custom BTC bot built by the_smart_ape for Polymarket's 15-minute Up or Down markets. He claims it is risk-free arbitrage: it catches moments when one side drops sharply, buys it and then hedges. The quoted post says the bot watches only the first 2 minutes of each round, configurable.

## Background

In an Up/Down market, the two sides together should price near $1. If one side drops fast, buying it and later the other side can lock a profit, but only if both fills happen. Calling it risk-free is the author's claim and ignores fill risk and fees.

## What I took away

- Hedged strategies still carry execution risk.
- Be skeptical of any strategy labeled risk-free.
- Short windows and configurable rules make strategies easy to backtest.
