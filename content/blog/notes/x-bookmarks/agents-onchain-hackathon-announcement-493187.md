## Summary

KeeperHub announces an online hackathon called Agents Onchain, about agents that act on chain. The prize pool is 5,000 USD, it opens July 27, and registration is open.

## Background

Onchain means actions recorded on a blockchain, such as sending tokens or calling smart contracts. Agents acting onchain can execute transactions automatically, which raises security questions about keys and permissions.

## What I took away

- Hackathons are a low-cost way to explore agent plus blockchain ideas.
- Agents with transaction ability need strict limits on keys and spending.
- Verify hackathon organizers and terms before registering.
