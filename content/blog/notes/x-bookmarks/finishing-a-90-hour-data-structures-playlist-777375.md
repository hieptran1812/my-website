## Summary

The author finished the Data Structures and Algorithms playlist by @pmavrin: 61 videos, about 90 hours, presented by an ICPC World champion. They had taken lectures with similar content 14 years ago, and still found it a nice refresher.

## Background

Data structures and algorithms are the base of most programming interviews and performance work. Revisiting them periodically helps even experienced engineers. The post is a personal recommendation.

## What I took away

- Fundamentals are worth revisiting even after many years.
- A long course can be done at a steady pace across weeks.
- The playlist is a candidate resource if you want a structured refresher.
