## Summary

The author jokes that they shipped this project while having a fever. The quoted post says you can now understand **Kimi K3's core architecture** by implementing it yourself in PyTorch.

## Background

Kimi K3 is a large open model from Moonshot. Reimplementing a model's architecture in a small, readable form is a common way to understand new components such as new attention types. PyTorch is the most widely used deep learning framework.

## What I took away

- Building a model piece by piece is one of the best ways to learn its design.
- Educational reimplementations are a good starting point before reading a full tech report.
- Look for such projects when a new architecture is released.
