## Summary

@Cramer___ thinks the software engineer role is evolving into a product engineer. LLM tools have made writing code easier, so the real bottleneck is deciding what to build and how the user experience should work. They say they have been spending more of their time on that.

## Background

If code is cheap to produce, the scarce skill is judgment about product and design. Engineers who understand users and trade-offs become more valuable. This is the author's opinion.

## What I took away

- Invest in product thinking and UX judgment.
- Implementation speed is less of a differentiator now.
- Spend time on deciding what to build.
