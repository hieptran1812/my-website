## Summary

Neel Nanda says that if the account is factually correct and not missing context, it is a completely unacceptable reason to fire someone. The quoted post is from a former OpenAI researcher who says they and two safety colleagues were fired last week, given only one reason: accessing an executive's email.

## Background

Disputes about how AI labs treat safety staff matter because these teams are meant to check risky decisions. Public accounts like this are one side of the story.

## What I took away

- Note the conditional: Nanda's judgment depends on the facts being complete.
- Follow up for OpenAI's side before forming a firm view.
