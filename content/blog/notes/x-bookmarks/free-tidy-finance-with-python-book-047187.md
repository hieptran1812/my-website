## Summary

@quantscience_ shares the Tidy Finance with Python book, which is available for free. It is a primer on financial data, asset pricing, modeling and machine learning, and portfolio optimization.

## Background

Tidy Finance teaches empirical finance with code. Asset pricing studies why assets earn the returns they do, and portfolio optimization picks asset weights. It is educational material rather than trading advice.

## What I took away

- A free hands-on book is a good entry point to quantitative finance.
- Work through the code to see how data and models fit together.
