## Summary

Goodfire announces Silico with example requests: replicate J-space on GLM 5.2, train a reward model and run RL to reduce hallucinations, and show how a model makes cancer predictions. They say using it is like having a team of AI researchers ready to run such experiments. A private beta is open.

## Background

These examples mix interpretability (understanding internals), post-training (reward models and RL) and applied analysis. A platform that runs them end to end combines an agent with model access, compute and tooling.

## What I took away

- Research platforms bundle agent, compute and model access; check what each part contributes.
- Example prompts show intended use, not proven results.
- Other bookmarks react to this launch; compare views.
