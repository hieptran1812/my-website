The author thanks @_akhaliq for featuring their work and points to the project page danceopd.github.io. The quoted post is titled **DanceOPD On-Policy Generative Field Distillation**. The post text gives no further technical detail.

## Background

On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student model on its own generated outputs while a teacher model supervises them. This avoids the mismatch of training only on teacher samples. The title suggests the idea is applied to generative models such as image or video generators.

## What I took away

- On-policy distillation is being applied beyond text LLMs.
