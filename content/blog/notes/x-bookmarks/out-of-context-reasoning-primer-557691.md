## Summary

The author calls out-of-context reasoning one of the most fascinating developments in the science of how LLMs work. He recommends a primer by Owain Evans, one of the main discoverers of the phenomenon, as a great introduction.

## Background

Out-of-context reasoning means a model uses facts learned in training, not shown in the prompt, to draw conclusions. It matters for safety since models can act on knowledge that is not visible in their input.

## What I took away

- Models can reason from training data without it appearing in the prompt.
- The primer is a good starting point for the topic.
