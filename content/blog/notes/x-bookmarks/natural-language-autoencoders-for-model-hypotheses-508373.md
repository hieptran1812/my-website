## Summary

The author says this is very cool work and a strong new tool for hypothesis generation about weird model behaviors. The quoted Anthropic post introduces Natural Language Autoencoders that translate Claude's activations into human-readable text.

## Background

Hypothesis generation means suggesting possible explanations that you then test. Readable text from activations can point researchers to what to investigate, though it does not prove the cause.

## What I took away

- Use readable activation summaries as leads, then verify with experiments.
