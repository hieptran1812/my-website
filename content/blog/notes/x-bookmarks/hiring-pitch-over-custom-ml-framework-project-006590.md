## Summary

The author says the project is impressive and invites the builder to work with them this summer. The quoted post describes a 12M parameter LLM trained on a custom framework with a Rust backend and CUDA kernels.

## Background

Strong public projects often lead to job offers. Writing kernels, a tokenizer and a transformer from scratch shows depth.

## What I took away

- Public build projects get noticed.
- Depth beats certificates.
- Share your work.
