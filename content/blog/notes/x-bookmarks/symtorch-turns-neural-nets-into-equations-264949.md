## Summary

The post asks whether you know what your trained model has learned and introduces **SymTorch**, a library that translates deep learning models into human-readable equations. A short demo video is attached.

## Background

Symbolic regression finds a math formula that approximates a model's behavior. This can make a model easier to inspect, though the formula is only an approximation.

## What I took away

- Equations can make model behavior easier to inspect.
- Check how well the formula matches the original model.
