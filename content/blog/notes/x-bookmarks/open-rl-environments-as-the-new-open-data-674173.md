## Summary

Thom Wolf says releasing many high-quality open-source RL environments is the most impactful thing to push the open frontier, the RLVR equivalent of sharing pretraining data. The quoted post praises a team that will release about 7k RL training data items and the framework behind a top-6 model on Artificial Analysis. It adds they shipped the model and tech report less than a week after the final RL run started.

## Background

RLVR means reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards, where answers can be checked automatically, such as math or code tests. An RL environment is the task plus the checker that gives rewards. As post-training matters more, good environments become as valuable as good pretraining data.

## What I took away

- RL environments and verifiers are now a key shared resource for open models.
- Releasing the training framework along with the model helps others reproduce results.
- Consider building and sharing environments for your own domain.
