## Summary

The author says Strategy #17 is on their Substack. The quoted preview describes a trend-following gold system traded live for years with a 44% win rate and 2.3 Risk/Reward, which does not win often but pays when it does.

## Background

Trend following buys rising markets and sells falling ones. A low win rate can still profit if wins are larger than losses. The figures are the author's claims.

## What I took away

- Win rate alone says little.
- Check risk to reward.
- Educational only, not advice.
