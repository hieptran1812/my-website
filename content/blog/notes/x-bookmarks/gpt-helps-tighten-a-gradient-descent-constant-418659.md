## Summary

The author shares this as another example of AI helping with math, in optimization theory. In the quoted post, a researcher explains that in 2019 they and Yura Malitsky wrote a paper on adaptive estimation of gradient Lipschitzness in gradient descent, tightened the constant from 1/2 to 1/sqrt(2) in 2023, and could not tell if it could reach exactly 1. The text then says GPT finally helped, though it is cut off.

## Background

The Lipschitz constant of the gradient bounds how fast the gradient can change, and it sets a safe step size for gradient descent. Adaptive methods estimate it on the fly. Tightening a constant means proving a stronger, more precise guarantee.

## What I took away

- AI models are starting to help close small but real gaps in proofs.
- Long-open technical questions are good test cases for AI math assistants.
- Results still need human checking and peer review.
