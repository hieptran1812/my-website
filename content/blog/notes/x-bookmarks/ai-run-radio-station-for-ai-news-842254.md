## Summary

The author says the first fully AI-run radio station on X broadcasts AI news around the clock. It tracks signals from GitHub, HuggingFace, OpenRouter, X, HN and YouTube and turns them into a feed for builders and founders. The quoted post adds that AI Twitter produces hundreds of posts an hour and the station plays ambient music between updates.

## Background

This is an aggregation agent: it collects updates from many sources and rewrites them as audio. It addresses information overload. Quality depends on how accurately the AI summarizes what it finds.

## What I took away

- Agents can turn scattered feeds into a single digest.
- Check summarized news against sources for anything important.
