## Summary

@thejayden says that after 200k+ views, people are already replicating the strategy with bigger profit margins. They created a free Telegram group for Polymarket quants to learn, share and debate, and may make it private if it gets too crowded.

## Background

Polymarket is a prediction market. Posts that promote a group by pointing to profits and urgency are common in crypto. The claims about profit margins are unverified.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of 'alpha' groups that rely on hype and crowding.
- Strategies that get copied widely tend to lose their edge.
- This is not trading advice.
