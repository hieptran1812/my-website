## Summary

The author invites prediction-market fans to try the **Forecastathon**, which moves past binary prediction into continuous time-series forecasting. They describe it as simple, structured and focused. The link card is titled The Forecastathon - Where Models meet Markets.

## Background

Binary markets ask yes or no questions. Continuous forecasting asks you to predict a whole series of numbers, which tests models more finely. Competitions like this give a way to score forecasting skill.

## What I took away

- Forecasting contests give honest feedback on your models.
- Continuous targets are harder than yes/no ones.
