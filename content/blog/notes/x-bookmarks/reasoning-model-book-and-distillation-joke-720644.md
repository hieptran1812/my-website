## Summary

@Michaelzsguo jokes that Dario claims Chinese models distilled from Anthropic, and says the book explains how, then says just kidding and that he is buying it to learn fine-tuning small specialists. The quoted post announces Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch), 440 pages on inference scaling, RL and distillation.

## Background

Distillation trains a smaller model on a larger model's outputs. The book teaches these techniques from scratch with code.
