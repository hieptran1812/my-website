## Summary

The author is joining Google DeepMind to build world models and interactive media experiences. This means dropping out of Stanford GSB and stepping down as co-student body president. They say they still value an MBA.

## Background

World models generate interactive environments that respond to actions, with uses in games, media and robotics. Big labs are hiring for these products as the area grows.

## What I took away

- World models are a growing hiring area at major labs.
- Career moves into AI often come from non-traditional paths.
