## Summary

@slowdownisha quotes @rasbt: hundreds of LLM papers appear monthly proposing techniques, but one of the best ways to see what works is to look at the pre-training and post-training pipelines of state-of-the-art models.

## Background

Papers often show isolated tricks. Real model reports show which ones were combined and kept. Reading them gives a filter on hype.

## What I took away

- Prefer model technical reports over single-trick papers.
- What shipped is better evidence than what was proposed.
