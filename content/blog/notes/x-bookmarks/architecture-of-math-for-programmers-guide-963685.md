## Summary

freeCodeCamp shares a guide by Tiago on the architecture of math. It argues math is about finding complex patterns in the world and shows ways to use mathematical concepts in programming beyond what is taught in school.

## Background

Programmers use math in graphics, algorithms, ML and data work. Seeing math as pattern-finding makes it easier to apply to code than seeing it as arithmetic.

## What I took away

- Look for patterns in problems before writing code.
- Read the guide for practical examples.
