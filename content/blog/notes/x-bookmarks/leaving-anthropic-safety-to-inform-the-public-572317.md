## Summary

The author left Anthropic's safety team two weeks ago and explains why. They say AI companies are racing to build machines much smarter than humans and we may not survive this. They want to work from outside to make sure the public is informed; the rest is cut off.

## Background

Several safety researchers in this batch moved from labs to outside roles. Their reasoning is that public awareness and independent oversight can influence how labs and governments act.

## What I took away

- A trend of safety researchers moving outside labs is visible in this period.
- Read the full post for their specific concerns.
