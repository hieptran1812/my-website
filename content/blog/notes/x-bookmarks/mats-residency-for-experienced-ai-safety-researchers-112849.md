## Summary

@NeelNanda5 quotes the description of the MATS Residency, a paid 6 to 24 month role for experienced researchers and professionals pursuing independent AI safety research, and says he is glad MATS is trying new ways to support promising work. The quoted MATS post says the technology is outpacing our ability to understand it and that they are funding exceptional talent.

## Background

MATS is a well-known AI safety research training program. A residency for experienced people helps mid-career professionals switch into safety research without joining a lab.

## What I took away

- Mid-career people now have funded paths into AI safety research.
- Check MATS for eligibility and application timelines.
