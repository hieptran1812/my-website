## Summary

@ivanfioravanti shares a video of LM Studio MLX v.1.8.5 with Codex and qwen3.6-35b-a3b at 6bit on an M5 Max 128GB at normal speed, using the command codex --oss -m qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b.
