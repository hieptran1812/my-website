## Summary

The author says Fei-Fei Li shares a similar view to Yann LeCun on LLM limits. She argues language is a purely generated signal, unlike a 3D world that follows physics. The quoted post repeats LeCun's comparison between about 30 trillion words of LLM training text and what a 4-year-old perceives.

## Background

The claim is that intelligence grounded in the physical world needs more than text. This motivates world models and spatial intelligence research. They are opinions, not proofs.

## What I took away

- Grounding in physical reality is a recurring critique of text-only LLMs.
- Two prominent researchers share this view.
- Treat it as a research direction, not a settled result.
