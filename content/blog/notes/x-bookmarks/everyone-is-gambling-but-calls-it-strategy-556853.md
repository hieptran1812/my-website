## Summary

The author jokes that they realized everyone is just gambling but calling it a strategy. It is a short, humorous remark about trading culture.

## Background

A real strategy should have a tested edge, defined risk and enough trades to rule out luck. Without that, results can be chance dressed up as skill.

## What I took away

- Ask whether a strategy has a measurable edge or just a lucky streak.
- Backtests and sample size help tell skill from chance.
