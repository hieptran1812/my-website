## Summary

The author is happy to receive the Best Paper Award at NeurIPS 2025 for the **Artificial Hivemind** paper. The quoted post says different models give the same thoughts, a case of mode collapse that persists even across mixed ensembles of models.

## Background

Mode collapse means a model gives a narrow range of similar answers instead of diverse ones. The paper's claim is that this happens across many different models, not only one. That matters for creativity and for using ensembles to get varied ideas.

## What I took away

- Different LLMs may give very similar answers to open-ended prompts.
- Ensembling models may not give as much diversity as expected.
- Read the paper if you rely on LLMs for brainstorming.
