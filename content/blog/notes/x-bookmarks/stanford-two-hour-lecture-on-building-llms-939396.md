## Summary

The author shares a 2-hour Stanford course on how to build LLMs from scratch and says it can save years of learning. The timestamps include LLM tokenization, how LLMs decode prompts, the training pipeline and LLM architecture.

## Background

Tokenization splits text into the units a model reads. Decoding is how the model picks output tokens. The training pipeline usually covers pretraining on large text, then post-training to follow instructions.

## What I took away

- A single lecture gives an overview, not mastery; follow up with hands-on work.
- Use the timestamps to jump to the parts you need.
- The claim of saving two years is the author's framing.
