## Summary

@inductionheads says the same author, @behrouz_ali, who just introduced the HOPE model, which people call the next Transformer, also wrote Titans. The quoted post describes Titans as a Google architecture that gives language models a long-term memory, using an MLP that updates while the model runs.

## Background

Titans uses a small neural network as memory that is updated at inference time, to handle long context. 'Next Transformer' is hype from social media. These designs are research proposals, not yet established replacements.

## What I took away

- Titans and HOPE come from the same author and share the memory idea.
- Be careful with claims that a new architecture is the next Transformer.
