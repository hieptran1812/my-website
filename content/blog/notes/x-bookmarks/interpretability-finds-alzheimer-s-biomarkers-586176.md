## Summary

@NeelNanda5 says it is cool to see interpretability finding novel scientific knowledge and hopes it is the first of many. The quoted post announces a novel class of biomarkers for Alzheimer's detection, found using interpretability with @PrimaMente. It promises an explanation of how they did it and how interpretability can power discovery in digital biology.

## Background

A biomarker is a measurable sign of a disease. Interpretability methods look inside a trained model to see what it has learned. The idea is that a model trained on biological data may learn useful patterns that humans have not named, and interpretability can extract them.

## What I took away

- Interpretability can be a tool for scientific discovery, not only safety.
- Models can encode knowledge that people have not yet articulated.
- Read the thread to see how the claim was validated.
