## Summary

The author is amazed that their browser can do this in 00.38 seconds. They say Supertone did well with the Supertonic instant TTS open-source model, and a demo is on Hugging Face.

## Background

TTS means text-to-speech. Running in the browser means the model runs on your own device with no server. The 0.38 second figure is the author's observation from one demo.

## What I took away

- Small TTS models can run fast on-device.
- Try the Hugging Face demo to judge quality.
- Browser inference protects privacy.
