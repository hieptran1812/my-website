## Summary

The author says the real reason they built the site was to check updated Bitcoin charts on the road while keeping their Bitcoin node running safely at home. They are glad others enjoy it too and link wickedsmartbitcoin.com.

## Background

A Bitcoin node is software that validates the blockchain. Running one at home and reading data from it remotely means you do not rely on third-party data. The post is a personal project note.

## What I took away

- Personal needs can drive useful side projects.
- Running your own node avoids trusting outside data.
