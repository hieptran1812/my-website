## Summary

@a16zfrontier quotes a line saying every other capital-intensive commodity, such as oil, real estate and energy, has tools to price, hedge, finance and trade risk, while compute has almost none.

## Background

Commodity markets use futures, swaps and benchmarks so buyers and sellers can manage price risk. GPU compute is a huge cost but lacks such instruments. The idea is educational context, not financial advice.

## What I took away

- Compute pricing is volatile and has few hedging tools.
- Financial instruments for compute are an open idea.
