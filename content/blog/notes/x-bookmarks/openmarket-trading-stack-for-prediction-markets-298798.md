## Summary

OpenMarket announces a new way to see prediction markets with a complete trading stack from visualization to execution. It says this unlocks a different view of how money positions before odds move. The text is cut off before details.

## Background

A prediction market lets people trade on the outcome of events, with prices acting as odds. Tools that show positioning claim to reveal flows before prices change. This is a product announcement, not advice.

## What I took away

- Product launches describe what the company claims, not proven results.
- Prediction market tools focus on visibility into positioning.
- Evaluate risks and fees before using any trading tool.
