## Summary

The author says you can write for two hours and produce one paragraph you delete tomorrow. He argues the cause is six cognitive processes fighting for the same brain, and offers a way to separate them.

## Background

Writing mixes idea generation, structuring, drafting and editing. Doing them at once overloads working memory. Splitting them into stages is a common technique.

## What I took away

- Separate brainstorming, outlining, drafting and editing.
- Do not edit while drafting.
