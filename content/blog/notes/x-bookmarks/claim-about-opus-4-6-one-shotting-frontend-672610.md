## Summary

The author says it is so over for frontend devs and that Claude Opus 4.6 can one-shot any prompt, inviting prompts in the comments. This is a promotional claim.

## Background

One-shot means a single prompt produces the result. Models can build UI prototypes quickly, but real frontend work includes accessibility, maintenance and integration.

## What I took away

- Hype posts overstate what demos show.
- Prototypes are not production apps.
- Test on realistic tasks.
