## Summary

The author announces CC Mirror, which they call the best way to use the GLM 4.7 and MiniMax M2.1 coding plans. Listed features are full model support, preconfigured tools, custom themes, isolation from CC and enhanced prompts. It starts with npx cc-mirror.

## Background

Coding plans are subscriptions for using certain models in coding tools. A wrapper can point a Claude Code style client at other models. Best way is the author's claim.

## What I took away

- Wrappers let you try other models in a familiar agent interface.
- Isolation avoids interfering with your main setup.
- Evaluate on your own tasks.
