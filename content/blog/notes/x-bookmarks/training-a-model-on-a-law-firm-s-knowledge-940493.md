## Summary

Harvey says it partnered with Engram Lab to train a model that learns from a firm's accumulated knowledge. They built a 100M token synthetic law firm with about 10k documents across 250+ matters and 46 clients, and the model internalized this knowledge in two ways. The quoted post introduces their first research blog, Understanding a Law Firm through Study, about agents trained with native memory.

## Background

Most enterprise AI uses retrieval: searching documents at question time and pasting them into the prompt. Native memory instead trains the knowledge into the model's weights. Synthetic data lets researchers test this without exposing real confidential client documents.

## What I took away

- Training knowledge into weights is an alternative to retrieval worth comparing.
- Synthetic datasets are a safe way to test ideas on sensitive domains like law.
- Ask how such a model handles updates when firm knowledge changes.
