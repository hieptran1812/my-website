## Summary

The post says $15,400,000,000 worth of Bitcoin and Ethereum options expire today. No other detail is given.

## Background

An option is a contract giving the right to buy or sell an asset at a set price by a set date. Large expiries can bring short-term price swings, since traders hedging those positions may adjust their holdings. This is informational, not advice.

## What I took away

- Large option expiries are watched because they can affect short-term volatility.
- Headline notional values do not tell you direction.
