## Summary

Sam Altman says this is the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while, giving transparency into pretraining progress and hoping for agreement on common limits. The quoted post says OpenAI argues in Perplexity and Intelligence for shared limits based on perplexity and other measures, with progress beyond them gated on safety ability.

## Background

Perplexity measures how well a model predicts text, with lower being better. Using it as a shared limit would give labs a common measurable bar. Whether limits are agreed is uncertain.

## What I took away

- Perplexity may be proposed as a safety-linked metric.
- The proposal depends on other labs agreeing.
- Read the original post for the full argument.
