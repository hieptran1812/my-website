## Summary

The authors deploy Claude Code in an autoresearch loop to find novel jailbreaking algorithms, which beat 30+ existing GCG-like attacks with hyperparameter tuning. They say incremental safety research can now be automated.

## Background

GCG is a known optimization-based jailbreak attack. Autoresearch lets an agent propose, test and keep improvements. Automation helps both red teams and defenders.

## What I took away

- Agents can automate safety research.
- Attack automation raises defense stakes.
- Check the paper for benchmarks.
