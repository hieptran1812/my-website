## Summary

Sam Altman posts the topic chain-of-thought monitorability and links a card titled Evaluating chain-of-thought monitorability. The post has no further text.

## Background

Chain of thought is the reasoning text a model writes before answering. Monitorability asks whether reading that text lets us detect bad behavior. If models reason in readable language, monitors can catch problems, which matters for safety.

## What I took away

- Readable reasoning can act as a safety signal.
- Training choices might make reasoning less monitorable, so it needs measuring.
- Read the linked evaluation for the details.
