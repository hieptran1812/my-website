## Summary

The author announces a thread on how to build a simple algorithmic trading strategy with a **472% return** using Python. The text gives no details of the strategy.

## Background

Backtest returns that look this high are often caused by overfitting, ignoring costs, or testing on the same data used to design the strategy. A headline number says little about future results.

## What I took away

- Be skeptical of large backtest returns.
- Ask about costs, out-of-sample tests and drawdowns.
- Educational note, not advice.
