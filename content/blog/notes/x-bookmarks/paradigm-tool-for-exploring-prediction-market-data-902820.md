## Summary

The author announces a new tool from Paradigm for exploring prediction market data. They bet users will find markets they did not know existed.

## Background

Prediction markets let people trade on the outcomes of events. There are many markets across platforms and finding them is hard. A data exploration tool helps with discovery and research.

## What I took away

- Discovery tooling can reveal the breadth of prediction markets.
- Open data exploration helps independent analysis.
- Try the tool and judge its coverage yourself.
