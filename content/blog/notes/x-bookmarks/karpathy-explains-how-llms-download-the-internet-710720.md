## Summary

MIT CSAIL shares a video of Andrej Karpathy breaking down how LLMs like ChatGPT download the Internet. It is described as a beginner-friendly explanation.

## Background

LLMs are first pretrained on very large amounts of text collected from the web, which teaches them to predict the next word. This stage gives them most of their knowledge. The video is an easy introduction to that idea.

## What I took away

- Pretraining on web text is the first stage of building an LLM.
- This is a good beginner resource to share with teammates.
- Open the link to watch the full video.
