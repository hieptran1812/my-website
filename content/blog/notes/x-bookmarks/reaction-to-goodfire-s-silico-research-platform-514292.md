## Summary

The quoted post from Goodfire describes Silico, a platform that runs research tasks like replicating J-space on GLM 5.2, training a reward model and running RL to reduce hallucinations, or explaining how a model makes cancer predictions; a private beta is open. The author calls it one of the first pieces of software he thinks will change ML research. He asks how much harnessing is needed, whether models can just do this, and whether it relies on a Goodfire-specific training stack.

## Background

AI research agents are systems that plan and run experiments with code, data and models. Interpretability work in particular needs access to model internals, which requires specialized tooling around the model.

## What I took away

- Ask how much of an agent's capability comes from the harness versus the model.
- Research automation tools are early; judge them by real experiments, not demos.
- Several bookmarks cover Silico; compare insider and outsider views.
