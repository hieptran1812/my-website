## Summary

The author points to a new resource on Apple ANE. The quoted post announces The Apple Neural Engine Inference Book, a practitioner's guide with converters, Swift runtimes and validated model manifests. Every model in the repo runs fully on the Neural Engine with no GPU or CPU fallback.

## Background

The Neural Engine is Apple's dedicated chip for machine learning on its devices. It is efficient but hard to target, so guides are valuable.

## What I took away

- Useful if you deploy models on Apple devices.
- Verified full-ANE execution is the notable claim.
