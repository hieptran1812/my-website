## Summary

The author says NVIDIA released Nemotron Speech ASR, an open source transcription model built for low-latency use cases like voice agents. They show a voice agent built with it, citing 24ms transcription finalization and a total voice-to-voice time under a stated limit. The text is cut off.

## Background

ASR (automatic speech recognition) turns speech into text. Voice agents chain ASR, an LLM and text-to-speech, so every stage adds delay. Fast transcription is key to natural conversation.

## What I took away

- Latency across the whole voice chain decides how natural an agent feels.
- Open ASR models give you control over deployment.
- Latency figures are from the author's demo.
