## Summary

The author claims an open-source LLM is as good as Opus 4.6 with 20 times cheaper inference. They say Kimi K2.5 and MiniMax M2.5 are open-source MoE models competing with proprietary offerings. Kimi K2.5 is listed as 1T total parameters with 32B active per token.

## Background

Mixture of experts (MoE) models have many parameters but only activate a subset for each token, which keeps compute low. The quality and cost claims are the author's and should be checked against independent benchmarks.

## What I took away

- MoE models separate total size from per-token compute.
- Treat headline quality-and-cost claims as unverified until benchmarked.
- Active parameters matter more than total for inference cost.
