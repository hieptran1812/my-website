## Summary

@elonmusk replies that it is one way to pay for all those GPUs. He is quoting a post that claims Grok 4.20 is topping live trading platforms with roughly 10 to 12 percent returns, about 3 to 4 times the S&P 500 over the same period. The quoted claim is unverified.

## Background

Live trading results from one short period say little about skill, since luck and market conditions matter a lot. The quote is an author's claim and the reply is a joke, not a confirmation. Claims about model returns need audited data and long time spans.

## What I took away

- Short-term returns are weak evidence of skill.
- A joke reply is not an endorsement of the claim.
- Be skeptical of benchmarks that are not audited.
