## Summary

Neel Nanda says the **GDM AGI Safety** team is hiring on all subteams, with roles in London, the Bay Area and other locations. He says there is a lot of work to reduce risks from AGI at Google, but the team is bottlenecked on people. He invites anyone who wants to help to apply.

## Background

GDM is Google DeepMind, Google's main AI research lab. AGI safety work tries to make sure very capable AI systems behave as intended and do not cause serious harm. Teams like this cover areas such as interpretability, alignment and evaluation of dangerous capabilities.

## What I took away

- Safety teams at large labs say talent, not ideas, is their main bottleneck.
- If interested in AI safety work, check the open roles directly rather than waiting for a perfect fit.
- Roles exist across several subteams, so different backgrounds may be useful.
