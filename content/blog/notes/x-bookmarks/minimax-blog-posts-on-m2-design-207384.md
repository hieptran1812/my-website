## Summary

Elie Bakouch shares the three existing MiniMax blog posts in case people missed them. They cover why M2 ended up as a full attention model, aligning to what, and what makes good reasoning data.

## Background

Full attention means every token looks at all earlier tokens, which is costly for long input. Many labs test cheaper alternatives, so hearing why a lab stayed with full attention is useful. Details are in the posts.

## What I took away

- Read the blog on full attention to learn the tradeoffs behind M2.
- Reasoning data quality is a key theme for post-training.
