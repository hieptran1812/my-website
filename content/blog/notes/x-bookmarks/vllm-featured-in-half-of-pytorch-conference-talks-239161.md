## Summary

The vLLM project says it was mentioned in about half of the PyTorch Conference 2025 talks, roughly 53 of 117. Earlier they expected only 5 dedicated talks. The quoted post listed topics such as easy and fast serving, an open-source post-training stack and AMD GPU support via Triton.

## Background

vLLM is an open-source engine for running LLMs efficiently. Being named in many talks shows how central it has become for inference, and for RL training stacks that need fast generation.

## What I took away

- vLLM is widely used beyond plain serving, including post-training.
- Conference mention counts are a rough signal of adoption.
- The numbers are the project's own count.
