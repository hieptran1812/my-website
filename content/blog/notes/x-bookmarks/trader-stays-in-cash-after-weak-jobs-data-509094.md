## Summary

The author praises a trader as sharp and balanced. The quoted post says jobs data was horrible, price gapped down to where puts were exited the day before, and the trader stayed in cash because sometimes the best position is no position.

## Background

A gap is when price opens far from the prior close. Holding cash means choosing not to take a trade. This is a description of one trader's approach, not advice.

## What I took away

- Not trading is a valid decision.
- Plan around data releases that cause gaps.
- This is educational context, not a recommendation.
