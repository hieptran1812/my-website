## Summary

The post tells how Chen-Ning Yang and Robert Mills introduced a new idea in 1954. The idea is that a force may not be just a push or pull, but can come from geometry. The text is cut off after that.

## Background

Yang-Mills theory is a framework in which forces arise from symmetries of a space, described with gauge fields. It underlies the modern Standard Model of particle physics. The link between forces and geometry is why the idea mattered so much.

## What I took away

- Forces can be viewed as geometric structure (gauge symmetry).
- Good background reading if you meet gauge ideas in physics or geometry-flavored ML.
