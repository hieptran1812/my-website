## Summary

The post says the paper was accepted as an ICML 2026 Spotlight. It notes reasoning in LLMs has improved largely by chaining local steps, but asks if that is the whole story, since humans sometimes make leaps across domains through analogy. They design a synthetic task to study this. The text is cut off.

## Background

Analogy means mapping structure from one domain to another. A synthetic task lets researchers control what the model has seen, which makes it clearer whether it truly transfers ideas.

## What I took away

- Reasoning may need more than step-by-step chains.
- Synthetic tasks isolate specific abilities.
- Read the paper for what small models can do.
