## Summary

The author says Jane Street has interesting blogs, though each would take a week to understand. They list posts on group theory for positional encodings and attention, reverse engineering a neural network, designing with Claude Code, and visualizing piecewise linear neural networks.

## Background

Jane Street is a trading firm that publishes technical writing. Positional encodings tell a transformer where each token sits in the sequence, and group theory is one way to reason about them.

## What I took away

- Quant firm blogs can be a good source of deep ML material.
- Bookmark the four linked posts for later reading.
