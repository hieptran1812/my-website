## Summary

The author, a quant fund founder and former Bridgewater quant, addresses a common question: why go through YC and raise VC money instead of just launching a fund. They argue this is not the relevant question and that it makes a lot of sense for this kind of company. The full reasoning is in a linked post.

## Background

A traditional fund raises money from investors to trade and earns fees and a share of profits. A venture-backed company instead sells equity to build a business, such as a technology platform. The two models have different incentives, timelines and regulation.

## What I took away

- Think about whether a quant effort is a fund or a technology company; the funding path differs.
- Read the linked post for the actual argument.
