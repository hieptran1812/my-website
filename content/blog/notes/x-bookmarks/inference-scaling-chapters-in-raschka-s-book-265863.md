## Summary

Raschka says inference scaling lets us trade extra compute for better modeling accuracy. He says that next to reinforcement learning it is one of the most important concepts in today's LLMs, so his book will cover it in two chapters. He has just finished the first. The text is cut off.

## Background

Inference scaling means spending more compute when answering, for example by sampling several answers or reasoning longer, to get better results. It differs from making the model bigger. Reasoning models rely on this idea.

## What I took away

- More compute at answer time can improve accuracy.
- Inference scaling and reinforcement learning are central to current LLMs.
- A dedicated book chapter is coming for each part.
