# Nano Banana Hackathon (Sep 2025): winners synthesis

- **Slug:** `banana`. **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana
- **Dates:** 2025-09-05 19:30 UTC to 2025-09-08 06:59 UTC (metadata), a roughly **48-hour** build sprint. Winners: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/discussion/609783
- **Entrants:** 826 teams, 868 competitors, 827 submissions (metadata). The host says "over 800" submissions and **489 qualified** for deep review (bank §13).
- **Prize pool:** "$400,000" in credits (metadata): $250k Gemini API, $100k ElevenLabs, $50k Fal. The metadata clarification misspells Fal as "Tal Credits". Per the tracks API:
  - Each of the **50 Overall winners** receives "$5,000 in Gemini API Credits + $1,000 in Fal credits, and 11 million (approx. US $2,000) ElevenLabs Credits".
  - **Special Technology Prize - Fal:** "$5k Fal Credits".
  - **Special Technology Prize - ElevenLabs:** "22M ElevenLabs Credits (6 months of Scale, approx. value $4,000)".
- **Rubric:** Innovation/"Wow" 40%, Technical execution 30% (use of "consistency, fusion, editing"), Impact 20%, Presentation 10%. Mandatory: Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, a video of 2 minutes or less, a **live public demo URL** ("not just a GitHub link or notebook"), and a **Gemini integration writeup of 200 words or fewer**.
- **Bank coverage:** `kaggle-llm-writeup-bank-part4.md`, H2 "## 13.". It covers judging and themes only, with no per-project detail and no special-prize winners, so every section below is new.
- **Scope choice (explicit):** the host lists "50 Top Projects ... in no particular order". The table lists **all 50 in the host's announcement order** (609783). Per-winner synthesis covers **the first 20 in that order** (`wu/banana/`), which include **both special-prize winners**: #5 SingleShot3D (Fal) and #12 Sozofix (ElevenLabs).
- **Section length (explicit):** the rules capped the Gemini-integration writeup at **200 words**, so most writeups are 160 to 400 words, though some ran longer. Sections here are deliberately **about 100 to 250 words**. Where a writeup is thin, linked GitHub READMEs and demo pages were fetched for facts. Team numbers are marked "(writeup)".
- **Judge remarks:** unlike the Gemini 3 thread, 609783 gives a **one-line judge rationale per winner**, quoted in each section as "Judges".
- **What set winners apart (host, 609783):** "Presentation Quality" (a clear, persuasive video and demo) and "Potential Impact" (who uses it and why): "while many submissions were dazzling on the what and how, the winning projects also mastered the so what and for whom."

## Per-winner synthesis (#1 to #20 in host order)

### #1 The Banana Board: Your AI Electronics Tutor (David Fernandez)
**Judges:** "Transforms STEM education by converting complex circuit design into an interactive, conversational, and visual learning experience with AI-powered analysis and editing."

**What it does.** A conversational circuit canvas built on three features:
1. **Text-to-schematic** with Gemini 2.5 Flash Image Preview.
2. **Visually grounded editing:** the user submits the current circuit image, **highlights or circles a region** and gives a command ("Replace this bulb with an LED", "Make this a 350Ω resistor"). Nano Banana edits that region and preserves the rest. This is an **iterative-edit** loop on the previous output.
3. **Conversational analysis:** Gemini 2.5 Flash reasons over the diagram image to compute current, power and battery life.

**Stack.** React plus FastAPI, with a [live demo on Vercel](https://thebananaboard.vercel.app/). The writeup runs about 900 words, over the 200-word guideline.

**Eval.** It claims "accurately performs multi-step reasoning ... correct current, power, and battery life calculations" (writeup) without any test set. The roadmap lists Arduino code generation, Live API voice tutoring, JSON prompting for consistent component naming, and simulation.

**Links.** [Video](https://youtu.be/z96MzHKhaLc). Domain: STEM education.

### #2 Surgical Visualisations (Surgical Banana: Arav31, kilojoles)
**Judges:** "Addresses a critical medical need by providing photorealistic surgical outcome previews on a patient's own image, leveraging AI for precise, empathetic, and medically plausible edits."

**What it does.** The patient uploads a photo and picks a procedure. The system then **generates a detailed procedure-specific edit prompt** and Nano Banana applies a **localized, region-specific inpainting edit** while leaving the rest of the face unchanged. The cleft-lip example prompt specifies closing the gap, a symmetrical Cupid's bow, nose-base alignment, a thin faded scar along the philtral column, and "Ensure the rest of the face remains completely unchanged". Hair transplant is the other demo case.

The design relies on identity **consistency** (same person, only the region changes) and low latency so that several options can be explored "during consultations".

**Eval.** None; there is no clinician review of plausibility. The demo images credit two plastic-surgery clinic galleries as sources.

**Links.** [AI Studio app](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apps/drive/13yq-spKhNCMyoVNloWS5bwn5dbEiRHUq), [video](https://youtu.be/Cr6uaTF5o-E). Domain: medical / patient communication.

### #3 RealityFlux (Auravana), thin writeup supplemented from GitHub
**Judges:** "Streams a live camera feed through AI to 'rewrite reality' in real-time, showcasing dynamic AR transformation and immersive user experiences."

**Writeup (about 160 words).** Each tap or prompt becomes a **masked inpaint request**: the current frame, plus an "on-device segmentation mask", plus the instruction. The writeup claims Flash Image returns in "under two seconds" and that **successive frames are chained as references** to lock lighting and perspective while the camera moves. A **Fusion mode** inserts new 3D-looking objects (a "chrome panther", "hovering neon kanji").

**GitHub README ([cschanhniem/realityflux](https://github.com/cschanhniem/realityflux), MIT) adds and partly contradicts:**
- The camera runs at 640x480, and frames are downsized to **512x288** for Gemini.
- Uses `@google/genai` v1.17.0 with browser-side calls, plus Gemini speech-to-text for voice prompts.
- Exposes `generateImage`, `editImage` and `fuseImages(img1, img2, prompt)`.
- A **rate-limit queue of ≤20 images/min with 3 s delays**, so "real time" means a few seconds per frame, not video rate.
- ElevenLabs is "a placeholder", not integrated.
- **No on-device segmentation model and no frame-chaining logic are named in the README.**

**Eval.** None.

**Links.** [Demo](https://banana-hackathon.pages.dev/), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I8HjjaENhX4). Domain: AR / creative.

### #4 Post Compose (Pose Compose) (Mert Gulsun)
**Judges:** "Solves a universally relatable problem by seamlessly fusing individual shots into photorealistic group photos, precisely matching lighting, perspective, and shadows."

**What it does.** Separate individual photos become **one photoreal group shot**, with Gemini 2.5 Flash Image Preview at every step:
- Subject isolation (hair edges and proportions preserved).
- **Identity and style consistency across multiple inputs** (faces, clothing, lighting).
- Natural-language pose, gaze and micro-expression edits.
- **Multi-image fusion and relighting** into a chosen scene: perspective correction, colour-temperature match, directional shadows, contact points, occlusion.
- World-knowledge scaling against landmarks and horizon alignment.
- Aspect-ratio layouts for Instagram, LinkedIn and print.
- **Iterative refinement without masks** ("move Alex closer", "make lighting warmer", "add soft shadow under feet").

**Eval.** None. **Links:** [live site](https://www.posecompose.com/), [video](https://youtu.be/9Kxpz15_lWE). It is the clearest single example of the judges' "fusion of 3 to 5 inputs" theme. Domain: consumer photography.

### #5 SingleShot3D: Digital twin from a single image (G Welinder; author Gustav Frøbert Welinder): **Special Technology Prize, Fal**
**Judges:** "Generates perfectly consistent multi-view product images from a single input, streamlining 2D-to-3D pipelines through technical innovation for 3D asset creation."

**Key trick, "generate-then-split".** Nano Banana is prompted to output a **2x2 orthographic grid** (front, back, left, right) **in one atomic generation**. The app then cuts the composite into four views. One call gives four mutually **consistent** views at lower latency, because the views share one canvas. Users can **regenerate any single quadrant via inpainting**.

**High-fidelity mode.** A staged pipeline first generates a canonical front view. Once the user confirms it, that view anchors the other three sides.

**3D conversion (the Fal special prize).** The user chooses among three **Fal.ai** multi-view 2D-to-3D engines: **Hunyuan Turbo** (fast), **Hunyuan Standard** (quality) and **Tripo3D** (photoreal). The output downloads as a .zip.

**Eval.** None. **Links:** [AI Studio app](https://ai.studio/apps/drive/1kZEUXzHDrwyun4vAqTpZOyg2CdbqBAxe), [video](https://youtu.be/bDFnv_5ZdP8). Domain: e-commerce / 3D assets.

### #6 Visual Artificer: AI-Powered Image Re-imagination (AndreasCo1)
**Judges:** "Empowers users to transform their photos into unique works of art using creative AI 'spells' that can be shared, fostering community and artistic discovery."

**What it does.** Upload a photo and pick a **"spell"**, which is a curated, hand-crafted prompt for a style such as "3D Hologram" or "Pixel Art". `gemini-2.5-flash-image-preview` returns the restyled image. Logged-in users can write and save custom spells, and there is a community gallery and a credits system. The stack is React, TypeScript and Tailwind on Cloud Run.

**Honest scope (writeup).** "Google oauth login not implemented yet": each login grants 5 mock credits, and gallery sharing and multi-save are "not implemented yet". This is the simplest technical design among the first 20 (single-image restyle via a prompt library) and won on UX and the community concept.

**Eval.** None. **Links:** [Cloud Run demo](https://visual-artificer-1067963245297.us-west1.run.app/), [video](https://youtu.be/d95-gQniLqM). Domain: creative / consumer.

### #7 ForgeOne: Pro Mode Revolution (Giulio Leone)
**Judges:** "Implements an AI system that critiques and refines its own creative output against professional scorecards, setting a new standard for automated, quality-controlled creative workflows."

**Problem.** "The first AI image is magic. The fiftieth client edit is a nightmare". Professionals need consistency across revisions.

**Closed loop, "Orchestrate, Generate, Validate & Correct":**
1. **Orchestrate:** `gemini-2.5-flash` acts as project director. It decomposes a request into a task plan (e.g. "complete brand" becomes Logo Design, Color Palette Definition, Brand Applications), each mapped to one of **10 professional modes**.
2. **Generate:** a "Prompt Intelligence" layer enriches each task prompt with industry terminology before calling `gemini-2.5-flash-image-preview`.
3. **Validate and Correct:** `gemini-2.5-flash` scores each output on a **scorecard** (Prompt Compliance, Text Quality, Technical Quality, Professional Compliance, each out of 100). If a score falls below threshold (example: "Text Quality: 40/100"), an **Intelligent Retry** diagnoses the failure, writes a **`delta_prompt`** and regenerates, repeating until the threshold is met.

**Why it matters for the series.** It is the only one of the first 20 with an **LLM-as-judge self-correction loop** on the image output.

**Eval.** No numbers on how often the loop converges or how many retries it takes.

**Links.** [Vercel demo](https://forgeone.vercel.app/), [video](https://youtu.be/zglsegeT2-M). Domain: professional creative production (branding, architecture).

### #8 Life Machine: The Canvas of Fate (sonderone)
**Judges:** "Creates a personalized, visually evolving narrative from a user's life story ... using changing art styles to reflect emotional states and choices."

**What it does.** A roughly 10-minute **life-simulation game**. The player uploads a selfie and basic information. **Gemini** writes branching story events from each choice (the text model is not named), and **Nano Banana** renders each life stage as a painting of the player. The **art style evolves** with emotional state and circumstances. The player's identity is kept **consistent** across ages and styles while the style shifts on purpose. The game is inspired by the non-AI "Life Simulator".

**Practical note (writeup).** The author apologises that the shared Gemini key may not work and that judges may need their own key, a common failure mode of 48-hour demos.

**Eval.** None. **Links:** [Cloud Run](https://life-machine-the-canvas-of-fate-291434781720.us-west1.run.app), [AI Studio](https://ai.studio/apps/drive/1hjywm8pi2M24I16IwI2hIH6S6t7WiVGS), [video](https://youtu.be/EJpbO9gEe7A). Domain: games / interactive narrative.

### #9 NanoStudio (Powerhouse; author Mrutyunjay Patil)
**Judges:** "Provides a comprehensive, professionally polished AI-powered product mockup studio with advanced prompt engineering for identity preservation and efficient batch processing."

**What it does.** E-commerce product mockups:
- **Reference-image consistency** via a `generateImageWithReference()` call, plus an explicit **identity-preserving prompt template**: "Preserve the exact product identity ... Do not alter shape, proportions, material, texture, branding, or logo placement. [Scene] [Aspect ratio] Use professional lighting and composition."
- **Scene Weaver:** **15+ JSON-schema templates** (360° views, studio packs, colorways, lifestyle), 8 curated scenes plus custom prompts, and 1:1, 16:9 and 4:5 outputs.
- **Magic Edit:** natural-language **iterative editing** with a 0 to 100% strength slider, a before/after slider and an **edit history with rollback**.
- **Batch logic:** 2 images for a single scene, 1 per scene for several.
- **Quota monitor:** 20 images/min and 200/day.

**Stack.** Next.js 15, React 18, Turso (LibSQL) BLOB storage and Vercel.

**Eval.** None. **Links:** [Vercel](https://nanostudio.vercel.app/), [video](https://youtu.be/Pko1Qufcr9c). Domain: e-commerce.

### #10 FrameFusion.ai (Lawrence)
**Judges:** "Executes an ambitious and professionally executed end-to-end 'storyboard-to-video' system that effectively solves the critical 'character drift' problem for video."

**Pipeline ("CinemaFlow").** There are two phases:
1. **Nano Banana** generates a **style- and character-consistent storyboard**: style lock, per-panel regeneration that keeps the "visual DNA", and multi-character/environment composition.
2. **Veo 3** animates each panel into an **8-second clip with native audio** and natural-language camera direction.

**Numbers.** The "95% consistency rating", "10x faster" and "80% cost reduction" are **marketing claims with no method**. The writeup derives them from vendor statistics ("70% faster generation", "40 million videos"), so **do not cite them as results**. The writeup reads as a launch press release, with no implementation detail on how consistency is enforced (e.g. reference-image passing).

**Links.** [Video](https://youtu.be/ImVIA_DK_bg), [Cloud Run app](https://framefusion-ai-anime-video-builder-868118654119.us-west1.run.app). Domain: animation / video content.

### #11 The Daily Banana (SummitSat)
**Judges:** "Elevates the comic creator genre with a sophisticated 'analyze, generate, refine' pipeline, intelligently processing reference images to ground the story plan before generating visuals."

**Pipeline (writeup):**
1. **Analyze:** uploaded reference photos plus tags (e.g. "Senator Higgins") go to Gemini, which describes them ("bald man with thick glasses in pinstripe suit"). That description **grounds the story plan** in visual facts before any panel is drawn.
2. **Generate:** every panel request carries the **reference images plus the text**, so characters stay identical across the strip (**consistency via reference passing**).
3. **Refine:** an existing panel, the user instruction and the original references are combined in one multimodal request for targeted **iterative edits** that keep coherence.

**Eval.** None. A short writeup (about 240 words) but a clear method. **Links:** [Cloud Run](https://the-daily-banana-comic-generator-381169979880.us-west1.run.app), [video](https://youtu.be/YE1k6TV4cug). Domain: comics / storytelling.

### #12 Sozofix, AI DIY assistant (RairoRapha): **Special Technology Prize, ElevenLabs**
**Judges:** "Combines sustainability with a voice-first AI assistant and visually consistent repair guides in a multi-service app, showcasing a holistic vision for DIY and repair."

**What it does.** Upload a photo of a broken item or spare materials. **`gemini-2.5-flash`** writes a project plan (title, tools, steps), and **Nano Banana** iteratively generates step images that **preserve the user's own object** across the sequence ("If you upload your chair, every repair step shows *your* chair").

**The ElevenLabs integration that won the special prize:**
- "Alfred", a real-time voice agent on the **ElevenLabs Conversational AI API** (`@elevenlabs/react` SDK). The team switched to it after native browser APIs proved unstable for calls.
- **AI memory:** **`gemini-2.0-flash-lite`** summarises past call transcripts into a **"pre-call briefing"** that is passed to the ElevenLabs agent as dynamic variables, along with the user profile and the current project.

**Other models and services.** Narration uses **DeepGram TTS**, chosen over `gemini-2.5-flash-tts-preview` because of latency; they claim **60% faster** guide generation (writeup). Experimental AR uses **TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD** plus Three.js overlays driven by parsing the step text.

**Stack.** React, Vite, a Flask orchestrator and Firebase.

**Lessons (writeup).** "Trust SDKs", isolate services one at a time, and read the docs: one overlooked line in the ElevenLabs docs caused their biggest connection bug.

**Eval.** None. **Cross-hackathon note:** RairoRapha is also one of the 50 Gemini 3 winners (#49, Equation Whisperer).

### #13 Hephaestus (One_Horizon: Sahil Tanna, tayyab, Nikunj3004_)
**Judges:** "Transforms 2D blueprints into immersive, explorable, photorealistic 360° walkthroughs, solving a key visualization problem in design."

**Three-stage Nano Banana pipeline (`gemini-2.5-flash-image-preview`):**
1. **Image plus text to image:** a simple or **handwritten blueprint** plus a style prompt ("minimalist design") produces a detailed top-down floor plan. The user then **iteratively edits** it (furniture, materials).

3. **Stitching:** multiple perspectives are passed as inputs and composed into a **single equirectangular 360° panorama**. The writeup claims the model "understands the spatial relationships" between views; seam quality is not measured.

### #14 Whisper of Guardian (suhmieum)

### #15 AnimaBanana (Anton Begehr)
**Judges:** "Converts static character images into fluid, multi-frame animation sequences with high style consistency and precise pose editing ... in a sophisticated AI Character Animation Studio."

**What it does.** Upload a character image, describe a motion ("waving hand", "jumping"), and get a **9-frame animation** with real-time preview. The main pieces:
- **Planning:** the motion prompt is turned into **JSON pose descriptions per frame**. The writeup does not name the planning model.
- **Style lock:** the original image is the consistency reference for every frame.
- **Hierarchical "divide-and-conquer interpolation":** generate key poses first, then fill frames in between.
- "Surgical" pose edits to specific body parts.
- **Natural-language per-frame correction**, which is iterative edit.
- Automatic transparency and background preservation.
- Real-time **cost estimation** and batching.

**Stack.** React, TypeScript, the Google GenAI SDK and Vite. The [GitHub repo](https://github.com/abegehr/AnimaBanana) is the AI Studio template export (needs `GEMINI_API_KEY`). Its README adds nothing about the frame algorithm.

## Consensus vs divergence
- **Table stakes:**
  - A live URL plus a short video. The host screened on both, and 609783 says what separated winners was "Presentation Quality" and "Potential Impact", the "so what and for whom".
  - A named real user: patient, AAC child, technician-equivalent DIYer, e-commerce seller.
  - Consistency as the thing that makes the use case work.
- **The dominant technical pattern** is **reference-image passing plus iterative re-editing**: keep the user's own face, object or character fixed, and change scene, pose or step (#2, #4, #9, #11, #12, #15, #17, #18).
- **Clever single-call tricks beat orchestration in some winners:** #5's 2x2 grid gives four consistent views in one call. #13 stitches multiple renders into one equirectangular panorama.
- **Orchestration winners** match the host's theme 5 ("Gemini Flash for structured JSON scripts, then Nano Banana, then ElevenLabs"): #7 (plan, generate, judge, retry), #12 (plan, images, voice agent with memory), #17 (images, Seedance video, ElevenLabs audio), #15 (JSON pose plan, frames).
- **There are no places among the 50.** The only differentiated awards are the two special prizes, and both went to the most multi-service builds.
- **Divergences:**
  - *Self-critique loop vs human-in-the-loop refinement:* [ForgeOne](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/writeups/forgeone-pro-mode-revolution) automates quality control with a Flash judge and `delta_prompt` retries. Most others (e.g. [Post Compose](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/writeups/simplifying-group-photos-with-post-compose), [Daily Banana](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/writeups/the-daily-banana)) leave refinement to the user.
  - *TTS choice:* [Sozofix](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/writeups/sozofix-ai-diy-assistant) dropped `gemini-2.5-flash-tts-preview` for DeepGram over latency, while [Quicktoons](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/writeups/quicktoons-web-based-animation-software) used ElevenLabs for audio.
  - *"Real time":* [RealityFlux](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/writeups/realityflux) markets live AR, but its own README throttles to ≤20 images/min with 3 s delays.
